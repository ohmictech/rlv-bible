Masters, give your slaves what's right and fair, because you know that you also have a Master in the sky. 
Keep at your praying, staying awake in it with thanksgiving, 
and pray for us too, that God may open a door for our message, so that we can speak the secret of Christ, the very thing I'm in chains for. 
Pray that I make it plain, as I should. 
Live wisely toward those outside, buying out every chance you get. 
Let your talk always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you know how to answer each person. 
Tychicus will tell you everything about me. He's a truly loved brother, a trustworthy servant, and a fellow slave in the Lord. 
I'm sending him to you for this very reason, so that he may find out how you're and encourage your hearts, 
I'm sending him with Onesimus, our trustworthy and truly loved brother, who's one of you. They'll tell you everything that's going on here. 
Aristarchus, who's in prison with me, greets you, and so does Mark, Barnabas' cousin. You have already received instructions about him: if he comes to you, welcome him. 
Jesus, who's called Justus, also greets you. These are the only ones of the circumcision who work with me for God's reign, and they have been a comfort to me. 
Epaphras, one of your own number and a slave of Christ Jesus, greets you. He's always struggling for you in his prayers, that you may stand mature and fully convinced in everything God wants. 
I can testify about him that he has worked very hard for you, and for those in Laodicea and Hierapolis. 
Luke, the truly loved doctor, and Demas greet you. 
Greet the brothers and sisters in Laodicea, and Nymphas, and the gathering that meets in his house. 
When this letter has been read among you, have it read in the gathering at Laodicea too, and be sure that you also read the letter from Laodicea. 
Tell Archippus, "See to the service you were given in the Lord, and make sure you carry it out." 
I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Favor be with you. Amen.
