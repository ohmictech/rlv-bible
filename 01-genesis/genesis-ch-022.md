Some time later, God tested Abraham. He said to him, "Abraham!" Abraham answered, "Here I am." 
God said, "Take your son, your only son, Isaac, the one you love, and go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I will show you." 
Abraham got up early in the morning, saddled his donkey, and took two of his young servants with him, along with his son Isaac. He cut wood for the burnt offering and set off for the place God had told him about. 
On the third day Abraham looked up and saw the place in the distance. 
Abraham said to his servants, "Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there and worship, and then we will come back to you." 
Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and put it on his son Isaac. He carried the fire and the knife himself, and the two of them walked on together. 
Isaac said to his father Abraham, "Father?" Abraham answered, "Here I am, my son." Isaac said, "We have the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for the burnt offering?" 
Abraham said, "God himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering, my son." And the two of them walked on together. 
When they came to the place God had told him about, Abraham built the altar there and arranged the wood on it. Then he tied up his son Isaac and laid him on the altar, on top of the wood. 
Abraham reached out and took the knife to kill his son. 
But the messenger of God called to him from the sky, "Abraham! Abraham!" Abraham answered, "Here I am." 
The messenger said, "Don't lay a hand on the boy and don't do anything to him. Now I know you respect God, because you haven't held back your son, your only son, from me." 
Abraham looked up and saw a ram behind him, caught in a thicket by its horns. He went and took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering instead of his son. 
Abraham named that place "God Will Provide". That's why people still say today, "On God's mountain, it will be provided." 
The messenger of God called to Abraham from the sky a second time, 
and said, "I have sworn by myself, says God, because you have done this and haven't held back your son, your only son, 
I will bless you richly and make your offspring as many as the stars of the sky and as the sand on the seashore. Your offspring will take over the gates of their enemies. 
And all the nations of the earth will be blessed through your offspring, because you have obeyed me." 
Then Abraham went back to his servants, and they all set out together for Beersheba. And Abraham lived at Beersheba. 
Some time later Abraham was told, "Milcah has also had children by your brother Nahor: 
Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of Aram, 
Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph, and Bethuel." 
Bethuel was the father of Rebekah. Milcah had these eight sons by Nahor, Abraham's brother. 
His concubine, whose name was Reumah, also had Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.
