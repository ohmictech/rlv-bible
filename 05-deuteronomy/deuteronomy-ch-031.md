Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel. 
He said to them, "I am a hundred and twenty years old today. I can't lead you out and bring you in anymore. God has said to me, 'You won't cross this Jordan.' 
Your God himself will cross over ahead of you. He will destroy these nations ahead of you, and you will take their place. Joshua will cross over ahead of you, as God has said. 
God will do to them what he did to Sihon and Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, when he destroyed them. 
God will hand them over to you, and you will do to them everything I have commanded you. 
Be strong and brave. Don't be afraid or terrified of them, because your God himself goes with you. He won't fail you or leave you." 
Moses called Joshua and said to him in front of all Israel, "Be strong and brave, because you will go with this people into the land God swore to their fathers to give them, and you will bring them into it as their inheritance. 
God himself goes ahead of you. He will be with you. He won't fail you or leave you. Don't be afraid. Don't lose heart." 
Moses wrote down this law and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of God's covenant, and to all the elders of Israel. 
Moses commanded them, "At the end of every seven years, at the set time in the year of cancellation, at the Feast of Shelters, 
when all Israel comes to appear before your God in the place he will choose, read this law out loud in front of all Israel, so they hear it. 
Gather the people together, the men, the women, the children and the foreigners inside your towns, so they can hear it and learn to fear your God and be careful to do all the words of this law, 
and so that their children, who have never known, may hear and learn to fear your God, as long as you live in the land you are crossing the Jordan to take." 
God said to Moses, "Look, the day of your death is near. Call Joshua and stand together at the Tent of Meeting, and I will commission him." Moses and Joshua went and stood at the Tent of Meeting. 
God appeared at the Tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood at the entrance to the Tent. 
God said to Moses, "Look, you will die and join your ancestors. Then this people will get up and go sleeping around after the strange gods of the land they are going into, and they will leave me and break my covenant, which I have made with them. 
Then my anger will burn against them in that day, and I will leave them, and I will hide my face from them, and they will be swallowed up, and many bad things and troubles will come on them. Then in that day they will say, 'Haven't these bad things come on us because our God isn't among us?' 
I will certainly hide my face in that day because of all the evil they have done by turning to other gods. 
So write down this song for yourselves and teach it to the Israelites. Put it in their mouths, so that this song can be a witness for me against the Israelites. 
When I have brought them into the land I swore to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey, and they have eaten and stuffed themselves and grown fat, then they will turn to other gods and serve them and despise me and break my covenant. 
When many bad things and troubles have come on them, this song will testify against them as a witness, because it won't be forgotten from the mouths of their descendants. I know what they are thinking and what they are doing today, even before I have brought them into the land I promised them." 
So Moses wrote down this song that same day and taught it to the Israelites. 
He commissioned Joshua son of Nun and said, "Be strong and brave, because you will bring the Israelites into the land I swore to them, and I will be with you." 
When Moses had finished writing the words of this law in a scroll, right through to the end, 
Moses commanded the Levites who carried the ark of God's covenant, 
"Take this scroll of the law and put it beside the ark of the covenant of your God, so that it can be there as a witness against you. 
I know how rebellious you are and how stiff your neck is. If you have been rebellious against God while I am still alive with you today, how much more will you be after I die? 
Gather all the elders of your tribes and your officials to me, so I can speak these words in their hearing and call the sky and the earth to witness against them. 
I know that after my death you will ruin yourselves completely and turn off the road I have commanded you, and bad things will happen to you later on, because you will do what is bad in the sight of God and make him angry by what your hands have made." 
Then Moses recited the words of this song from beginning to end in the hearing of the whole assembly of Israel.
