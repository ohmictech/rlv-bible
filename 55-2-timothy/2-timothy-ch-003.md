But know this: in the last days hard times will come. 
People will love themselves and love money, they will be boastful and look down on others, they will speak slander, they won't listen to their parents, they will be unthankful and irreverent, 
they will have no family love, they will be unforgiving, they will be slanderers, they will have no self-control, they will be savage, and they won't love what is good, 
they will be traitors and reckless and swollen with conceit, loving pleasure more than loving God, 
they will keep up the outward form of devotion to God while denying its power. Turn away from people like that. 
Some of them slip into houses and take captive foolish women who are loaded down with mistakes and pulled this way and that by all kinds of cravings, 
always learning but never able to come to know the truth. 
Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, these men oppose the truth too. Their minds are rotted, and as far as the trust goes, they have failed the test. 
But they won't get far, because everyone will see how foolish they are, just as it happened with those two. 
But you have followed my teaching, my way of life, my purpose, my trust, my patience, my love, and my staying power, 
and the persecutions and hard times that happened to me at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. I put up with those persecutions, and the Lord rescued me out of them all. 
And in fact, everyone who wants to live with devotion to God in Christ Jesus will be hunted down. 
But bad people and impostors will get worse and worse, leading others astray and being led astray themselves. 
But you must stay with what you have learned and been sure of, knowing who it was you learned it from. 
From the time you were a baby you have known the set-apart writings, which are able to make you wise for rescue through the trust that is in Christ Jesus. 
Every writing is breathed out by God and useful for teaching, for showing people their fault, for setting them right, and for training in rightness, 
so that the person who belongs to God may be complete, ready for every good deed.
