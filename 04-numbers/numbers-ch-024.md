When Balaam saw that it pleased God to bless Israel, he didn't go off to look for omens as he had at the other times, but turned his face toward the wilderness. 
Balaam looked up and saw Israel camped by their tribes, and the Spirit of God came on him. 
He spoke his oracle: "This is the saying of Balaam son of Beor, the saying of the man whose eyes are open, 
the saying of one who hears God's words, who sees what the Almighty shows, who falls down with his eyes open. 
How good your tents are, Jacob, and your homes, Israel! 
They spread out like valleys, like gardens beside a river, like aloes God has planted, like cedars beside the water. 
Water will flow from his buckets, and his seed will be in many waters. His king will be higher than Agag, and his kingdom will be lifted up. 
God brought him out of Egypt. He has the strength of a wild ox. He will devour the nations against him, break their bones and pierce them with his arrows. 
He crouched and lay down like a lion, like a lioness. Who will dare to wake him? Everyone who blesses you is blessed, and everyone who curses you is cursed." 
Balak's anger burned against Balaam, and he clapped his hands together and said, "I called you to curse my enemies, and now you have blessed them three times. 
So get out of here and go home. I meant to honor you greatly, but God has kept you from that honor." 
Balaam said to Balak, "Didn't I tell the messengers you sent me, 
'Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I couldn't go beyond God's word to do good or bad on my own. I will say only what God says'? 
Now I am going back to my people. Come, I will tell you what this people will do to your people in the days to come." 
He spoke his oracle: "This is the saying of Balaam son of Beor, the saying of the man whose eyes are open, 
the saying of one who hears God's words and knows what the Most High knows, who sees what the Almighty shows, who falls down with his eyes open. 
I see him, but not now. I see him, but not close. A star will come out of Jacob, and a scepter will rise out of Israel. It will crush the foreheads of Moab and break apart all the sons of Sheth. 
Edom will become a possession, and Seir, his enemy, will become a possession, while Israel shows its strength. 
One from Jacob will rule, and will destroy what is left in the city." 
Then he looked at Amalek and spoke his oracle: "Amalek was first among the nations, but in the end it will be destroyed." 
He looked at the Kenites and spoke his oracle: "Your home is strong, and your nest is set in the rock, 
but Kain will be burned up when Asshur takes you captive." 
He spoke his oracle: "Alas, who can live when God does this? 
Ships will come from the coast of Kittim, and they will humble Asshur and humble Eber, but they too will be destroyed." 
Then Balaam got up and went back home, and Balak went his own way.
