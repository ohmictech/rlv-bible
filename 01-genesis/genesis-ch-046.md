Israel set out with everything he had and came to Beersheba, where he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. 
God spoke to Israel in visions that night and said, "Jacob, Jacob!" He answered, "Here I am." 
God said, "I'm the God of your father. Don't be afraid to go down into Egypt, because I'll make a great nation of you there. 
I'll go down with you to Egypt, and I'll bring you back up again. Joseph's own hands will close your eyes." 
Jacob left Beersheba, and the sons of Israel carried their father Jacob, their little ones and their wives in the wagons Pharaoh had sent for him. 
They took their livestock and everything they had acquired in Canaan and came into Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him, 
his sons and grandsons, his daughters and granddaughters, all his offspring with him. 
These are the names of the children of Israel who came into Egypt, Jacob and his sons. Reuben was Jacob's firstborn. 
The sons of Reuben: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi. 
The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, whose mother was a Canaanite woman. 
The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari. 
The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez and Zerah. Er and Onan died in Canaan. The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul. 
The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puvah, Iob and Shimron. 
The sons of Zebulun: Sered, Elon and Jahleel. 
These were the sons of Leah, born to Jacob in Paddan Aram, along with his daughter Dinah. That was thirty-three in all. 
The sons of Gad: Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi and Areli. 
The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah and their sister Serah. The sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel. 
These were the sons of Zilpah, the servant Laban gave to his daughter Leah, and she bore them to Jacob. That was sixteen in all. 
The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife: Joseph and Benjamin. 
Joseph had Manasseh and Ephraim in Egypt, born to him by Asenath, the daughter of Potiphera, the priest of On. 
The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim and Ard. 
These were the sons of Rachel born to Jacob. That was fourteen in all. 
The son of Dan: Hushim. 
The sons of Naphtali: Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer and Shillem. 
These were the sons of Bilhah, the servant Laban gave to his daughter Rachel, and she bore them to Jacob. That was seven in all. 
Everyone who came with Jacob into Egypt, his own descendants apart from the wives of his sons, came to sixty-six in all. 
Joseph had two sons born to him in Egypt, so the whole household of Jacob that came into Egypt was seventy in all. 
Jacob sent Judah on ahead to Joseph, to ask the way to Goshen, and they came into the land of Goshen. 
Joseph had his chariot made ready and went up to Goshen to meet his father Israel. When he appeared before him he threw his arms around his neck and wept there for a long time. 
Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die, since I've seen your face and know you're still alive." 
Joseph said to his brothers and to his father's household, "I'll go and tell Pharaoh, 'My brothers and my father's household, who were in Canaan, have come to me. 
They're shepherds. They've kept livestock all their lives, and they've brought their flocks and herds and everything they own.' 
When Pharaoh calls you in and asks what you do for a living, 
tell him, 'We've kept livestock since we were young, and so did our fathers.' Then he'll let you live in Goshen, because Egyptians can't stand shepherds."
