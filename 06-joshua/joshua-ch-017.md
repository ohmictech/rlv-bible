This was the land allotted to the tribe of Manasseh, since he was Joseph's firstborn. Machir, Manasseh's firstborn and Gilead's father, was a fighter, so he received Gilead and Bashan. 
This land went to the rest of the Manassites, clan by clan: the clans of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher and Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, clan by clan. 
But Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, had no sons, only daughters. Their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah. 
They came to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun and to the leaders and said, "God commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers." So as God had commanded, Joshua gave them an inheritance among their father's brothers. 
Ten shares fell to Manasseh, in addition to the land of Gilead and Bashan east of the Jordan, 
because Manasseh's daughters received an inheritance among his sons. The land of Gilead belonged to the rest of Manasseh's sons. 
The border of Manasseh ran from Asher to Michmethath, which is east of Shechem, and on south to the people of En Tappuah. 
The land of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah itself, on Manasseh's border, belonged to the Ephraimites. 
The border went down to the brook of Kanah, south of the brook. These towns belonged to Ephraim, though they lay among the towns of Manasseh. Manasseh's border was on the north side of the brook and ended at the sea. 
The land south of the brook was Ephraim's and the land north was Manasseh's, with the sea as its border. They reached Asher on the north and Issachar on the east. 
Inside Issachar and Asher, Manasseh had Beth Shean and its towns, Ibleam and its towns, the people of Dor and its towns, the people of Endor and its towns, the people of Taanach and its towns, the people of Megiddo and its towns, and the three hill towns. 
But the Manassites couldn't drive out the people of those towns, and the Canaanites kept living in that land. 
When the Israelites grew strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, but they never drove them out completely. 
The descendants of Joseph said to Joshua, "Why have you given us only one lot and one share of land, when we are a numerous people and God has blessed us so much?" 
Joshua answered, "If you are so numerous, go up into the forest and clear land for yourselves there in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, since the hill country of Ephraim is too small for you." 
The descendants of Joseph said, "The hill country isn't enough for us, and all the Canaanites living in the valley have iron chariots, both those in Beth Shean and its towns and those in the valley of Jezreel." 
Then Joshua said to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, "You are a numerous people with great strength, so you won't have just one lot. 
The hill country will be yours. Even though it is forest, you will clear it, and its farthest edges will be yours, because you will drive out the Canaanites, even though they have iron chariots and are strong."
