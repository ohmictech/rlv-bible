"You, son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, 'The God says: "Look, I'm against you, Gog, prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal. 
I'll turn you around, will lead you on, and will cause you to come up from the uttermost parts of the north; and I'll bring you onto the mountains of Israel. 
I'll strike your bow out of your left hand, and will cause your arrows to fall out of your right hand. 
You'll fall on the mountains of Israel, you, and all your hordes, and the peoples who are with you. I'll give you to the ravenous birds of every sort and to the animals of the field to be devoured. 
You'll fall on the open field, for I have spoken it," says God. 
"I'll send a fire on Magog and on those who dwell securely in the islands. Then they'll know that I'm God. 
"'"I'll make my holy name known among my people Israel. I won't allow my holy name to be profaned any more. Then the nations will know that I'm God, the Holy One in Israel. 
Look, it comes, and it'll be done," says God. "This is the day about which I have spoken. 
"'"Those who dwell in the cities of Israel will go out and will make fires of the weapons and burn them, both the shields and the bucklers, the bows and the arrows, and the war clubs and the spears, and they'll make fires with them for seven years; 
so that they'll take no wood out of the field, and not cut down any out of the forests; for they'll make fires with the weapons. They'll plunder those who plundered them, and rob those who robbed them," says God. 
"'"It'll happen in that day, that I'll give to Gog a place for burial in Israel, the valley of those who pass through on the east of the sea; and it'll stop those who pass through. They'll bury Gog and all his multitude there, and they'll call it 'The valley of Hamon Gog'. 
"'"The house of Israel will be burying them for seven months, that they can cleanse the land. 
Yes, everyone in the land will bury them; and they'll become famous in the day that I'll be glorified," says God. 
"'"They'll set apart men of continual employment who will pass through the land. Those who pass through will go with those who bury those who remain on the surface of the land, to cleanse it. After the end of seven months they'll search. 
Those who search through the land will pass through; and when anyone sees a man's bone, then he'll set up a sign by it, until the undertakers have buried it in the valley of Hamon Gog. 
Hamonah will also be the name of a city. Thus they'll cleanse the land."' 
"You, son of man, God says: 'Speak to the birds of every sort, and to every animal of the field, "Assemble yourselves, and come; gather yourselves on every side to my sacrifice that I sacrifice for you, even a great sacrifice on the mountains of Israel, that you can eat meat and drink blood. 
You will eat the flesh of the mighty, and drink the blood of the princes of the earth, of rams, of lambs, and of goats, of bulls, everyone fatlings of Bashan. 
You will eat fat until you're full, and drink blood until you're drunk, of my sacrifice which I have sacrificed for you. 
You will be filled at my table with horses and charioteers, with mighty men, and with everyone of war," says God.' 
"I'll set my glory among the nations. Then all the nations will see my judgment that I have executed, and my hand that I have laid on them. 
So the house of Israel will know that I'm their God, from that day and forward. 
The nations will know that the house of Israel went into captivity for their guilt, because they trespassed against me, and I hid my face from them; so I gave them into the hand of their adversaries, and they all fell by the sword. 
I did to them according to their uncleanness and according to their rebellion. I hid my face from them. 
"So God says: 'Now I'll reverse the captivity of Jacob and have mercy on the whole house of Israel. I'll be jealous for my holy name. 
They'll forget their shame and all their trespasses by which they have trespassed against me, when they dwell securely in their land. No one will make them afraid 
when I have brought them back from the peoples, gathered them out of their enemies' lands, and am shown holy among them in the sight of many nations. 
They'll know that I'm their God, in that I caused them to go into captivity among the nations, and have gathered them to their own land. Then I'll leave none of them captive any more. 
I won't hide my face from them any more, for I have poured out my Spirit on the house of Israel,' says God."
