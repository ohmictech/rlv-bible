Then God's word came to me, saying, 
"You shall not take a wife, neither shall you have sons or daughters, in this place." 
God says concerning the sons and concerning the daughters who are born in this place, and concerning their mothers who bore them, and concerning their fathers who became their father in this land: 
"They'll die grievous deaths. They won't be lamented, neither will they be buried. They'll be as dung on the surface of the ground. They'll be consumed by the sword and by famine. Their dead bodies will be food for the birds of the sky and for the animals of the earth." 
God says, "Don't enter into the house of mourning. Don't go to lament. Don't bemoan them, for I have taken away my peace from this people," says God, "even loving kindness and tender mercies. 
Both great and small will die in this land. They won't be buried. Men won't lament for them, cut themselves, or make themselves bald for them. 
Men won't break bread for them in mourning, to comfort them for the dead. Men won't give them the cup of consolation to drink for their father or for their mother. 
"You shall not go into the house of feasting to sit with them, to eat and to drink." 
The God of Armies, the God of Israel says: "Look, I'll cause to cease out of this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride. 
It'll happen, when you tell this people all these words, and they ask you, 'Why has God pronounced all this great bad against us?' or 'What's our guilt?' or 'What's our wrong we have done against our God?' 
then you shall tell them, 'Because your fathers have forsaken me,' says God, 'and have walked after other gods, have served them, have worshiped them, have forsaken me, and haven't kept my law. 
You have done bad more than your fathers, look, you each walk after the stubbornness of his bad heart, so that you don't listen to me. 
So I'll cast you out of this land into the land that you haven't known, neither you nor your fathers. There you'll serve other gods day and night, for I'll show you no favor.' 
"Therefore look, the days come," says God, "that it'll no more be said, 'As God lives, who brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;' 
but, 'As God lives, who brought up the children of Israel from the land of the north, and from all the countries where he had driven them.' I'll bring them again into their land that I gave to their fathers. 
"Look, I'll send for many fishermen," says God, "and they'll fish them up. Afterward I'll send for many hunters, and they'll hunt them from every mountain, from every hill, and out of the clefts of the rocks. 
My eyes are on all their ways. They aren't hidden from my face. Their guilt isn't concealed from my eyes. 
First I'll recompense their guilt and their wrongdoing double, because they have polluted my land with the carcasses of their detestable things, and have filled my inheritance with their abominations." 
God, my strength, my stronghold, and my refuge in the day of affliction, the nations will come to you from the ends of the earth, and will say, "Our fathers have inherited nothing but lies, vanity and things in which there's no profit. 
Should a man make to himself gods which yet are no gods?" 
"Look, I'll cause them to know, this once I'll cause them to know my hand and my might. Then they'll know that my name is God."
