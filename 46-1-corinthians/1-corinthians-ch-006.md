Dare any of you, having a case against his neighbor, go to law before the unright, and not before the set-apart ones? 
Don't you know that the set-apart ones will pass verdicts on the world? And if the world is judged by you, are you not fit to decide the smallest cases? 
Don't you know that we will pass verdicts on messengers? How much more on things that belong to this life? 
If then you have cases about things that belong to this life, do you set as judges those who count for nothing in the gathering? 
I say this to shame you. Isn't there even one wise person among you who could decide between his brothers and sisters? 
But brother goes to law with brother, and that before unbelievers! 
So it is already a complete failure on your part that you have lawsuits with one another. Why not rather let yourselves be wronged? Why not rather let yourselves be cheated? 
No, but you yourselves do wrong and defraud, and that against your brothers. 
Or don't you know that the unright will not be given charge of God's reign? Don't be led astray. Neither those who sleep around, nor idol worshipers, nor those who cheat, nor soft men, nor men who lie with men, 
nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor slanderers, nor swindlers, will be given charge of God's reign. 
Some of you were like that. But you were washed. You were set apart. You were made right in the name of the Lord Jesus and in the Spirit of our God. 
"All things are permitted for me," but not all things are helpful. "All things are permitted for me," but I will not be brought under the power of anything. 
"Foods for the belly, and the belly for foods," but God will bring both it and them to nothing. But the body is not for sleeping around, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body. 
Now God raised the Lord, and will also raise us by his power. 
Don't you know that your bodies are members of Christ? Should I then take the members of Christ and make them members of a woman who sleeps around? May it never be! 
Or don't you know that the one joined to a woman who sleeps around is one body? For, "The two," he says, "will become one flesh." 
But he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit. 
Run from sleeping around! "Every mistake a person makes is outside the body," but the one who sleeps around misses the mark against his own body. 
Or don't you know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, 
for you were bought with a price. Therefore give God weight in your body and in your spirit, which belong to God.
