Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, each took his censer, and put fire in it, and laid incense on it, and offered strange fire before God, which he hadn't commanded them. 
Fire came out from before God, and devoured them, and they died before God. 
Then Moses said to Aaron, "This is what God spoke of, saying, 'I'll show myself holy to those who come near me, and before everyone I'll be glorified.'" Aaron held his peace. 
Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron, and said to them, "Draw near, carry your brothers from before the sanctuary out of the camp." 
So they came near, and carried them in their tunics out of the camp, as Moses had said. 
Moses said to Aaron, and to Eleazar and to Ithamar, his sons, "Don't let the hair of your heads go loose, and don't tear your clothes, so that you don't die, and so that he won't be angry with all the congregation; but let your brothers, the whole house of Israel, bewail the burning which God has kindled. 
You shall not go out from the door of the Tent of Meeting, lest you die; for the anointing oil of God is on you." They did according to the word of Moses. 
Then God said to Aaron, 
"You and your sons aren't to drink wine or strong drink whenever you go into the Tent of Meeting, or you'll die. This shall be a statute for the eras throughout your generations. 
You're to make a distinction between the holy and the common, and between the unclean and the clean. 
You're to teach the children of Israel all the statutes which God has spoken to them by Moses." 
Moses spoke to Aaron, and to Eleazar and to Ithamar, his sons who were left, "Take the meal offering that remains of the offerings of God made by fire, and eat it without yeast beside the altar; for it's most holy; 
and you shall eat it in a holy place, because it's your portion, and your sons' portion, of the offerings of God made by fire; for so I'm commanded. 
The waved breast and the heaved thigh you shall eat in a clean place, you, and your sons, and your daughters with you: for they're given as your portion, and your sons' portion, out of the sacrifices of the offerings for wholeness of the children of Israel. 
They shall bring the heaved thigh and the waved breast with the offerings made by fire of the fat, to wave it for a wave offering before God. It shall be yours, and your sons' with you, as a portion for the eras, as God has commanded." 
Moses diligently inquired about the goat of the offering for wrongdoing, and, look, it was burned. He was angry with Eleazar and with Ithamar, the sons of Aaron who were left, saying, 
"Why haven't you eaten the offering for wrongdoing in the place of the sanctuary, since it's most holy, and he has given it to you to bear the guilt of the congregation, to make things right for them before God? 
Look, its blood wasn't brought into the inner part of the sanctuary. You certainly should have eaten it in the sanctuary, as I commanded." 
Aaron spoke to Moses, "Look, today they have offered their offering for wrongdoing and their burnt offering before God; and such things as these have happened to me. If I had eaten the offering for wrongdoing today, would it have been pleasing in God's sight?" 
When Moses heard that, it was pleasing in his sight.
