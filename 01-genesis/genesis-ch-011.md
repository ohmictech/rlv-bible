The whole earth had one language and one way of speaking. 
As they traveled east, they found a plain in the land of Shinar, and they lived there. 
They said to one another, "Come, let's make bricks and bake them thoroughly." They used bricks instead of stone, and tar instead of mortar. 
They said, "Come, let's build ourselves a city, and a tower whose top reaches to the sky, and let's make a name for ourselves, or we'll be scattered over the whole earth." 
God came down to see the city and the tower that the human race was building. 
God said, "Look, they're one people, and they all have one language, and this is only the beginning of what they'll do. Nothing they plan will be out of their reach now. 
Come, let's go down and confuse their language there, so they won't understand each other's speech." 
So God scattered them from there over all the earth, and they stopped building the city. 
That's why its name was called Babel, because there God confused the language of the whole earth. From there God scattered them over all the earth. 
This is the account of the generations of Shem: Shem was one hundred years old when he fathered Arpachshad, two years after the flood. 
After he fathered Arpachshad, Shem lived five hundred more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Arpachshad was thirty-five years old when he fathered Shelah. 
After he fathered Shelah, Arpachshad lived four hundred three more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Shelah was thirty years old when he fathered Eber. 
After he fathered Eber, Shelah lived four hundred three more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Eber was thirty-four years old when he fathered Peleg. 
After he fathered Peleg, Eber lived four hundred thirty more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Peleg was thirty years old when he fathered Reu. 
After he fathered Reu, Peleg lived two hundred nine more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Reu was thirty-two years old when he fathered Serug. 
After he fathered Serug, Reu lived two hundred seven more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Serug was thirty years old when he fathered Nahor. 
After he fathered Nahor, Serug lived two hundred more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Nahor was twenty-nine years old when he fathered Terah. 
After he fathered Terah, Nahor lived one hundred nineteen more years and fathered other sons and daughters. 
Terah was seventy years old when he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran. 
Now this is the account of Terah's family. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran fathered Lot. 
Haran died in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans, while his father Terah was still alive. 
Abram and Nahor both married. Abram's wife was named Sarai, and Nahor's wife was named Milcah, the daughter of Haran. Haran was also the father of Iscah. 
But Sarai couldn't have children. She had no child. 
Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot the son of Haran, and his daughter-in-law Sarai, the wife of his son Abram. They set out from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. They came to Haran and lived there. 
Terah lived two hundred five years in all, and then he died in Haran.
