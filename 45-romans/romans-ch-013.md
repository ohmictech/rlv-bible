Let every life be obedient to the higher authorities, for there's no authority except from God, and those who exist have been appointed by God. 
So he who resists the authority resists the plan of God; and those who stand against bring judgment on themselves. 
Rulers aren't a terror to the good deed, but to the bad. Do you desire to have no fear of the authority? Do that which is good, and you'll have praise from the authority, 
because he's a servant of God to you for good. If you do bad, be afraid, for he doesn't carry the sword for nothing; he's a servant of God, one who brings heat on the one who does bad. 
So you need to be in obedience, not only because of the heat, but also for the sake of your conscience. 
That's why you pay taxes, because they're servants of God's service, continually doing this very thing. 
So give everyone what is due to them: if you owe taxes, pay taxes; if customs, then customs; if respect, then respect; if honor, then honor. 
Don't be in debt to anyone, except to love one another; for he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law. 
The commandments, "You shouldn't cheat," "You shouldnt't murder," "You shouldnt't steal," "You shouldnt't covet," and whatever other commandments there are, are all summed up in this saying, namely, "You will love your neighbor as yourself." 
Love doesn't harm a neighbor. Love is the fulfillment of the law. 
Do this, knowing that it's already time for you to awaken out of sleep, for rescue is now nearer to us than when we first trusted. 
The night is far gone, and the day is near. Let's throw off the deeds of darkness, and let's put on the armor of light. 
Let's walk properly, like it's daytime; not in wild parties and drunkenness, not in sleeping around and shameless acts, and not in strife and jealousy. 
But wear the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no room for the flesh, for its desires.
