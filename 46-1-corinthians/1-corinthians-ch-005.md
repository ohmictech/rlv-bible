It is actually reported that there is sleeping around among you, and sleeping around of a kind that is not even named among the nations, that a man has his father's wife. 
You are arrogant, and you didn't mourn instead, so that the one who did this might be removed from among you. 
For my part, though absent in body but present in spirit, I have already passed a verdict, as though I were present, on the one who did this. 
In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when you are gathered together, with my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, 
you are to hand such a one over to the Adversary for the wrecking of the flesh, so that the spirit may be made whole in the day of the Lord Jesus. 
Your boasting is not good. Don't you know that a little yeast leavens the whole lump? 
Clean out the old yeast, so that you may be a new lump, just as you really are bread without yeast. For indeed Christ, our Passover, has been sacrificed. 
Therefore let's keep the feast, not with old yeast, nor with the yeast of ill will and malice, but with the bread without yeast of sincerity and truth. 
I wrote to you in my letter to have nothing to do with those who sleep around; 
yet not at all meaning the people of this world who sleep around, or the greedy and swindlers, or idol worshipers, because then you would have to leave the world. 
But as it is, I wrote to you not to keep company with anyone who is called a brother but is someone who sleeps around, or is greedy, or an idol worshiper, or a slanderer, or a drunkard, or a swindler. Don't even eat with such a person. 
For what do I have to do with passing a verdict on those outside? Don't you pass a verdict on those inside? 
But God passes a verdict on those outside. "Put the malicious man out from among yourselves."
