But I determined this for myself, that I would not come to you again in sorrow. 
If I make you grieve, then who will make me glad but he who is made to grieve by me? 
And I wrote this very thing to you, so that when I came I wouldn't have sorrow from those I ought to be glad about, having confidence in you all that my joy would be shared by all of you. 
Out of much hard times and deep pain I wrote to you with many tears, not to make you grieve, but so that you might know the love I have so overflowing for you. 
But if any has caused sorrow, he has caused sorrow not to me, but in part (that I not press too heavily) to you all. 
The penalty everyone gave him is enough for such a one; 
so that instead you should rather release him and comfort him, so that he isn't swallowed up by too much sorrow. 
So I beg you to make your love for him firm. 
To this end I also wrote, that I might know the proof of you, whether you are obedient in all things. 
Now I also release whomever you release anything. If I have released anything, I have released it for your sakes in the presence of Christ, 
so that we aren't taken advantage of by the Adversary, for we are not unaware of his schemes. 
Now when I came to Troas for the Good News of Christ, and when a door was opened to me in the Lord, 
I had no relief for my spirit, because I didn't find Titus my brother, so I said goodbye to them and went on to Macedonia. 
But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ, and makes the sweet smell of knowing him known through us in every place. 
We are the sweet smell of Christ to God, among those who are being made whole and among those who are being broken up: 
to the one a stench from death to death, to the other a sweet aroma from life to life. Who is sufficient for these things? 
We are not like so many, selling the word of God for profit. But out of sincerity, from God, in God's sight, we speak in Christ.
