By David. Praise God, my soul! All that's within me, praise his holy name! 
Praise God, my soul, and don't forget all his benefits, 
who forgives all your mistakes, who heals all your diseases, 
who buys back your life from destruction, who crowns you with loving kindness and tender mercies, 
who satisfies your desire with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's. 
God executes righteous acts, and justice for everyone who's oppressed. 
He made known his ways to Moses, his deeds to the children of Israel. 
God is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness. 
He won't always accuse; neither will he stay angry for a lifetime. 
He hasn't dealt with us according to our mistakes, nor repaid us for our guilt. 
As the sky are high above the earth, so great is his loving kindness toward those who fear him. 
As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our rebellion from us. 
Like a father has compassion on his children, so God has compassion on those who fear him. 
He knows how we're made. He remembers that we're dust. 
As for man, his days are like grass. As a flower of the field, so he flourishes. 
The wind passes over it, and it's gone. Its place remembers it no more. 
But God's loving kindness is from perpetual to perpetual with those who fear him, his righteousness to children's children, 
to those who keep his covenant, to those who remember to obey his precepts. 
God has established his throne in the sky. His kingdom rules over all. 
Praise God, you messengers of his, who are mighty in strength, who fulfill his word, obeying the voice of his word. 
Praise God, all you armies of his, you servants of his, who do his pleasure. 
Praise God, all you works of his, in all places of his dominion. Praise God, my soul!
