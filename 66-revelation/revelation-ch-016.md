I heard a loud voice from inside the temple, saying to the seven messengers, "Go and pour out the seven bowls of God's heat on the land!" 
The first went and poured out his bowl on the land, and a painful, running sore broke out on the people who had the mark of the beast and who bowed down to its image. 
The second messenger poured out his bowl into the sea, and it turned into blood like that of a dead person. Every living thing in the sea died. 
The third poured out his bowl into the rivers and the springs of water, and they turned into blood. 
Then I heard the messenger of the waters saying, "You're right, you who are and who were, you who are set apart, because you have passed this verdict. 
They poured out the blood of the set-apart ones and of the spokesmen, and you have given them blood to drink. That's what they deserve." 
Then I heard the altar say, "Yes, Lord God, the one who holds sway over all, your verdicts are true and right." 
The fourth poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was allowed to scorch people with fire. 
People were scorched by terrible heat, and they slandered the name of God, who has power over these plagues. They didn't have a change of heart and give him weight. 
The fifth poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast, and its kingdom was plunged into darkness. People chewed their tongues in pain, 
and they slandered the God of the sky because of their pains and their sores. They still didn't have a change of heart about what they had done. 
The sixth poured out his bowl on the great river, the Euphrates. Its water dried up to make a road for the kings who come from where the sun rises. 
I saw three unclean spirits that looked like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false spokesman. 
They're unclean spirits that work signs. They go out to the kings of the whole world, to gather them together for the battle on the great day of God the one who holds sway over all. 
"Look, I'm coming like a thief. Flourishing is the one who stays awake and keeps their clothes on, so that they don't walk around naked and let people see their shame." 
They gathered them together at the place that in Hebrew is called Harmagedon. 
The seventh poured out his bowl into the air, and a loud voice came out of the temple in the sky, from the throne, saying, "It's done!" 
There were flashes of lightning, rumblings, and peals of thunder, and there was a great earthquake, such a huge and mighty earthquake as hadn't happened since people first came to be on the land. 
The great city split into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell. Babylon the great was remembered in God's sight, and he gave her the cup of the wine of his furious heat. 
Every island ran away, and the mountains couldn't be found. 
Huge hailstones, each weighing about a talent, came down out of the sky on people, and people slandered God because of the plague of hail, because that plague was terrible beyond measure.
