The word which came to Jeremiah from God, when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur the son of Malchijah, and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah, the priest, saying, 
"Please inquire of God for us; for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon makes war against us. Perhaps God will deal with us according to all his wondrous works, that he can withdraw from us." 
Then Jeremiah said to them, "Tell Zedekiah: 
'The God of Israel says, "Look, I'll turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, with which you fight against the king of Babylon, and against the Chaldeans who besiege you outside the walls; and I'll gather them into the middle of this city. 
I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, even in anger, in wrath, and in great indignation. 
I'll strike the inhabitants of this city, both man and animal. They'll die of a great pestilence. 
Afterward," says God, "I'll deliver Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, and the people, even those who are left in this city from the pestilence, from the sword, and from the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their life. He'll strike them with the edge of the sword. He won't spare them, have pity, or have mercy."' 
"You will say to this people, 'God says: "Look, I set before you the way of life and the way of death. 
He who remains in this city will die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, but he who goes out and passes over to the Chaldeans who besiege you, he'll live, and he'll escape with his life. 
I have set my face on this city for bad, and not for good," says God. "It'll be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he'll burn it with fire."' 
"Concerning the house of the king of Judah, hear God's word: 
House of David, God says, 'Execute justice in the morning, and deliver him who's robbed out of the hand of the oppressor, lest my wrath go out like fire, and burn so that no one can quench it, because of the bad of your doings. 
Look, I'm against you, O inhabitant of the valley, and of the rock of the plain,' says God. 'You that say, "Who would come down against us?" or, "Who would enter into our homes?" 
I'll punish you according to the fruit of your doings,' says God; 'and I'll kindle a fire in her forest, and it'll devour all that's around her.'"
