God says: "Maintain justice and do what's right, for my salvation is near and my righteousness will soon be revealed. 
Blessed is the man who does this, and the son of man who holds it fast; who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it and keeps his hand from doing any bad." 
Let no foreigner who has joined himself to God speak, saying, "God will surely separate me from his people." Don't let the eunuch say, "Look, I'm a dry tree." 
God says, "To the eunuchs who keep my Sabbaths, choose the things that please me, and hold fast to my covenant, 
I'll give them in my house and within my walls a memorial and a name better than of sons and of daughters. I'll give them an perpetual name that won't be cut off. 
Also the foreigners who join themselves to God to serve him, and to love God's name, to be his servants, everyone who keeps the Sabbath from profaning it, and holds fast my covenant, 
I'll bring these to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer. Their burnt offerings and their sacrifices will be accepted on my altar; for my house will be called a house of prayer for all peoples." 
The God, who gathers the outcasts of Israel, says, "I'll yet gather others to him, in addition to his own who are gathered." 
All you animals of the field, come to devour, all you animals in the forest. 
His watchmen are blind. They're all without knowledge. They're all mute dogs. They can't bark, dreaming, lying down, loving to slumber. 
Yes, the dogs are greedy. They can never have enough. They're shepherds who can't understand. They have all turned to their own way, each one to his gain, from every quarter. 
"Come," they say, "I'll get wine, and we'll fill ourselves with strong drink; and tomorrow will be as today, great beyond measure."
