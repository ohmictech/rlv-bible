Whoever loves correction loves knowledge, but he who hates reproof is stupid. 
A good man will obtain favor from God, but he'll condemn a man of guilty plans. 
A man won't be established by guilt, but the root of the righteous won't be moved. 
A worthy woman is the crown of her husband, but a disgraceful wife is as rottenness in his bones. 
The thoughts of the righteous are just, but the advice of the guilty is deceitful. 
The words of the guilty are about lying in wait for blood, but the speech of the upright rescues them. 
The guilty are overthrown, and are no more, but the house of the righteous will stand. 
A man will be commended according to his wisdom, but he who has a warped mind will be despised. 
Better is he who's little known, and has a servant, than he who honors himself and lacks bread. 
A righteous man respects the life of his animal, but the tender mercies of the guilty are cruel. 
He who tills his land will have plenty of bread, but he who chases fantasies is void of understanding. 
The guilty desires the plunder of bad men, but the root of the righteous flourishes. 
An bad man is trapped by sinfulness of lips, but the righteous will come out of trouble. 
A man will be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth. The work of a man's hands will be rewarded to him. 
The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but he who's wise listens to counsel. 
A fool shows his annoyance the same day, but one who overlooks an insult is prudent. 
He who's truthful testifies honestly, but a false witness lies. 
There's one who speaks rashly like the piercing of a sword, but the tongue of the wise heals. 
Truth's lips will be established for the eras, but a lying tongue is only momentary. 
Deceit is in the heart of those who plot bad, but joy comes to the promoters of peace. 
No mischief will happen to the righteous, but the guilty will be filled with bad. 
Lying lips are an abomination to God, but those who do the truth are his delight. 
A prudent man keeps his knowledge, but the hearts of fools proclaim foolishness. 
The hands of the diligent ones will rule, but laziness ends in slave labor. 
Anxiety in a man's heart weighs it down, but a kind word makes it glad. 
A righteous person is cautious in friendship, but the way of the guilty leads them astray. 
The slothful man doesn't roast his game, but the possessions of diligent men are prized. 
In the way of righteousness is life; in its path there's no death.
