"'You are not to make idols for yourselves, or set up a carved image or a sacred pillar, or place a carved stone in your land to bow down to it, because I am your God. 
"'Keep my Sabbaths and honor my Holy Place. I am God. 
"'If you live by my rules and keep my commands and do them, 
then I will give you rain at the right time, and the land will produce its crops, and the trees of the field will bear their fruit. 
Your threshing will go on until the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will go on until sowing time. You will eat your bread until you are full and live in your land in safety. 
"'I will give peace in the land, and you will lie down with no one to make you afraid. I will remove dangerous animals from the land, and no sword will pass through your land. 
You will chase your enemies, and they will fall by the sword in front of you. 
Five of you will chase a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase ten thousand, and your enemies will fall by the sword in front of you. 
"'I will look on you with favor, make you fruitful and multiply you, and I will keep my covenant with you. 
You will eat from last year's stores, and you will clear out the old to make room for the new. 
I will set my tent among you, and I will not turn away from you in disgust. 
I will walk among you and be your God, and you will be my people. 
I am your God, who brought you out of Egypt so you would not be their slaves. I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk with your heads up. 
"'But if you won't listen to me and won't do all these commands, 
and if you turn against my rules and are sickened by my decisions, so that you don't do all my commands and break my covenant, 
then this is what I will do to you: I will bring sudden terror on you, wasting disease and fever that drain your eyes and wear you down. You will sow your seed for nothing, because your enemies will eat it. 
I will set my face against you, and you will be beaten by your enemies. Those who hate you will rule over you, and you will run when no one is chasing you. 
"'If in spite of all this you still won't listen to me, I will punish you seven times over for your wrongs. 
I will break your proud strength, and I will make the sky over you like iron and the ground under you like bronze. 
Your strength will be spent for nothing, because your land won't produce its crops and the trees of the land won't bear fruit. 
"'If you still walk against me and won't listen to me, I will bring seven times more plagues on you for your wrongs. 
I will send wild animals among you, and they will take your children, destroy your livestock and cut your numbers down, and your roads will be left empty. 
"'If with all this you won't turn back to me, but keep walking against me, 
then I will walk against you too, and I will strike you seven times for your wrongs. 
I will bring a sword on you to carry out the covenant's revenge. When you gather inside your cities, I will send disease among you, and you will be handed over to the enemy. 
When I cut off your supply of bread, ten women will bake your bread in one oven, and they will hand out your bread by weight. You will eat and still be hungry. 
"'If in spite of this you won't listen to me, but keep walking against me, 
then I will walk against you in anger, and I will punish you seven times for your wrongs. 
You will eat the flesh of your sons and the flesh of your daughters. 
I will destroy your high places, cut down your incense altars and pile your dead bodies on your idols, and I will turn away from you in disgust. 
I will leave your cities in ruins and bring your Holy Places to nothing. I will take no pleasure in the soothing smell of your offerings. 
I will bring the land to ruin, and your enemies who live in it will be shocked by it. 
I will scatter you among the nations and chase you with the sword. Your land will be ruined and your cities left in ruins. 
Then the land will make up its Sabbaths all the time it lies ruined, while you are in your enemies' land. Then the land will rest and make up its Sabbaths. 
All the time it lies ruined it will rest, making up for the rest it did not have in your Sabbaths while you lived on it. 
"'As for those of you who are left, I will put fear in their hearts in the land of their enemies. The sound of a leaf blowing will send them running, and they will run as if from a sword, and fall when no one is chasing them. 
They will stumble over each other as if running from a sword, though no one is chasing them. You will have no strength to stand against your enemies. 
You will die out among the nations, and the land of your enemies will swallow you up. 
Those of you who are left will waste away in your guilt in your enemies' land, and because of the guilt of their fathers they will waste away with them. 
"'But if they confess their guilt and their fathers' guilt, the unfaithfulness they committed against me, and how they walked against me, 
so that I walked against them and brought them into their enemies' land, and then their stubborn hearts are humbled and they accept the punishment for their guilt, 
then I will remember my covenant with Jacob, my covenant with Isaac and my covenant with Abraham, and I will remember the land. 
The land will be left by them and will make up its Sabbaths while it lies ruined without them, and they will accept the punishment for their guilt, because they turned against my decisions and were sickened by my rules. 
Yet even then, while they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them or be sickened by them and destroy them completely and break my covenant with them, because I am their God. 
But for their sake I will remember the covenant with their ancestors, whom I brought out of Egypt in the sight of the nations, to be their God. I am God.'" 
These are the rules, the decisions and the laws that God made between himself and the Israelites on Mount Sinai through Moses.
