Let every life be subject to the higher authorities, for there's no authority except from God, and those who exist have been appointed by God. 
So he who resists the authority withstands the ordinance of God; and those who withstand will receive to themselves verdict. 
Rulers aren't a terror to the good deed, but to the bad. Do you desire to have no fear of the authority? Do that which is good, and you'll have praise from the authority, 
for he's a servant of God to you for good. But if you do that which is bad, be afraid, for he doesn't carry the sword for nothing; for he's a servant of God, one who brings heat on the one who does bad. 
So you need to be in subjection, not only because of the heat, but also for conscience' sake. 
That's why you also pay taxes, for they're servants of God's service, continually doing this very thing. 
So give everyone what you owe: if you owe taxes, pay taxes; if customs, then customs; if respect, then respect; if honor, then honor. 
Owe no one anything, except to love one another; for he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law. 
The commandments, "You won't cheat," "You won't murder," "You won't steal," "You won't covet," and whatever other commandments there are, are all summed up in this saying, namely, "You will love your neighbor as yourself." 
Love doesn't harm a neighbor. Love is the fulfillment of the law. 
Do this, knowing the time, that it's already time for you to awaken out of sleep, for rescue is now nearer to us than when we first trusted. 
The night is far gone, and the day is near. Let's throw off the deeds of darkness, and let's put on the armor of light. 
Let's walk properly, as in the day; not in wild parties and drunkenness, not in sleeping around and shameless acts, and not in strife and jealousy. 
But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, for its desires.
