Where do the fights and quarrels among you come from? Don't they come from your pleasures, which are at war inside you? 
You crave and don't have. You murder and you're jealous, and you can't get it. You fight and make war. You don't have, because you don't ask. 
You ask, and don't receive, because you ask with bad motives, so that you can spend it on your pleasures. 
You who cheat on God! Don't you know that loving the world means hating God? Anyone who chooses to be a friend of the world makes themselves an enemy of God. 
Or do you think the Scripture says for nothing, "The Spirit he made to live in us longs for us jealously"? 
But he gives more favor. That's why it says, "God stands against the proud, but gives favor to the humble." 
So submit to God. Stand against the Slanderer, and he'll run from you. 
Come near to God, and he'll come near to you. Wash your hands, you who keep drifting. Make your hearts pure, you who are of two minds. 
Be miserable, mourn, and weep. Let your laughter turn to mourning and your joy to gloom. 
Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he'll lift you up. 
Brothers and sisters, don't speak against one another. Anyone who speaks against a brother or sister, or passes a verdict on them, speaks against the law and passes a verdict on the law. And if you pass a verdict on the law, you aren't doing the law; you're setting yourself up as its judge. 
There's only one lawgiver, the one who's able to rescue and to destroy. But who are you to pass a verdict on your neighbor? 
Now listen, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we'll go to this city, spend a year there, do business, and make money." 
You don't even know what will happen tomorrow. What's your life? You're a mist that appears for a little time and then disappears. 
Instead, you ought to say, "If the Lord wills it, we'll live, and do this or that." 
But as it is, you brag and take pride in it. All such bragging is evil. 
So anyone who knows the good they ought to do, and doesn't do it, that's their wrongdoing.
