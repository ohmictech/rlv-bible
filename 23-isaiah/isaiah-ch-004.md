Seven women will take hold of one man in that day, saying, "We'll eat our own bread, and wear our own clothing. Just let's be called by your name. Take away our reproach." 
In that day, God's branch will be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the land will be the beauty and glory of the survivors of Israel. 
It'll happen that he who's left in Zion and he who remains in Jerusalem will be called holy, even everyone who's written among the living in Jerusalem, 
when the Lord will have washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion, and will have purged the blood of Jerusalem from within it, by the spirit of justice and by the spirit of burning. 
God will create over the whole habitation of Mount Zion and over her assemblies, a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night, for over all the glory will be a canopy. 
There will be a pavilion for a shade in the daytime from the heat, and for a refuge and for a shelter from storm and from rain.
