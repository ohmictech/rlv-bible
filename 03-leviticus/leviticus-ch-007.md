"'This is the law of the trespass offering: It's most holy. 
In the place where they kill the burnt offering, he will kill the trespass offering; and its blood he will sprinkle around on the altar. 
He will offer all of its fat: the fat tail, and the fat that covers the innards, 
and he will take away the two kidneys, and the fat that's on them, which is by the loins, and the cover on the liver, with the kidneys; 
and the priest will burn them on the altar for an offering made by fire to God: it's a trespass offering. 
Every male among the priests may eat of it. It will be eaten in a holy place. It's most holy. 
"'As is the offering for wrongdoing, so is the trespass offering; there's one law for them. The priest who makes things right with them will have it. 
The priest who offers any man's burnt offering will have for himself the skin of the burnt offering which he has offered. 
Every meal offering that's baked in the oven, and all that's prepared in the pan and on the griddle, will be the priest's who offers it. 
Every meal offering, mixed with oil or dry, belongs to all the sons of Aaron, one as well as another. 
"'This is the law of the sacrifice of offerings for wholeness, which one will offer to God: 
If he offers it for a thanksgiving, then he will offer with the sacrifice of thanksgiving unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and cakes mixed with oil. 
He will offer his offering with the sacrifice of his offerings for wholeness for thanksgiving with cakes of leavened bread. 
Of it he will offer one out of each offering for a heave offering to God. It will be the priest's who sprinkles the blood of the offerings for wholeness. 
The flesh of the sacrifice of his offerings for wholeness for thanksgiving will be eaten on the day of his offering. He won't leave any of it until the morning. 
"'But if the sacrifice of his offering is a vow, or a free will offering, it will be eaten on the day that he offers his sacrifice. On the next day what remains of it will be eaten, 
but what remains of the meat of the sacrifice on the third day will be burned with fire. 
If any of the meat of the sacrifice of his offerings for wholeness is eaten on the third day, it won't be accepted, and it won't be credited to him who offers it. It'll be an abomination, and the soul who eats any of it'll bear his guilt. 
"'The meat that touches any unclean thing won't be eaten. It will be burned with fire. As for the meat, everyone who's clean may eat it; 
but the soul who eats of the meat of the sacrifice of offerings for wholeness that belongs to God, having his uncleanness on him, that soul will be cut off from his people. 
When anyone touches any unclean thing, the uncleanness of man, or an unclean animal, or any unclean abomination, and eats some of the meat of the sacrifice of offerings for wholeness which belong to God, that soul will be cut off from his people.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'You will eat no fat, of bull, or sheep, or goat. 
The fat of that which dies of itself, and the fat of that which is torn of animals, may be used for any other service, but you will in no way eat of it. 
Whoever eats the fat of the animal which men offer as an offering made by fire to God, even the soul who eats it will be cut off from his people. 
Don't eat any blood, whether it's of bird or of animal, in any of your dwellings. 
Whoever it's who eats any blood, that soul will be cut off from his people.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'He who offers the sacrifice of his offerings for wholeness to God will bring his offering to God out of the sacrifice of his offerings for wholeness. 
With his own hands he will bring the offerings of God made by fire. He will bring the fat with the breast, that the breast may be waved for a wave offering before God. 
The priest will burn the fat on the altar, but the breast will be Aaron's and his sons'. 
The right thigh you will give to the priest for a heave offering out of the sacrifices of your offerings for wholeness. 
He among the sons of Aaron who offers the blood of the offerings for wholeness, and the fat, will have the right thigh for a portion. 
The waved breast and the heaved thigh I have taken from the children of Israel out of the sacrifices of their offerings for wholeness, and have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as their portion for the eras from the children of Israel.'" 
This is the consecrated portion of Aaron, and the consecrated portion of his sons, out of the offerings of God made by fire, in the day when he presented them to minister to God in the priest's office; 
which God commanded to be given them of the children of Israel, in the day that he anointed them. It's their portion for the eras throughout their generations. 
This is the law of the burnt offering, the meal offering, the offering for wrongdoing, the trespass offering, the consecration, and the sacrifice of offerings for wholeness 
which God commanded Moses in Mount Sinai in the day that he commanded the children of Israel to offer their offerings to God, in the wilderness of Sinai.
