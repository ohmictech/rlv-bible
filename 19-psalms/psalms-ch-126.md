A Song of Ascents. When God brought back those who returned to Zion, we were like those who dream. 
Then our mouth was filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing. Then they said among the nations, "God has done great things for them." 
God has done great things for us, and we're glad. 
Restore our fortunes again, God, like the streams in the Negev. 
Those who sow in tears will reap in joy. 
He who goes out weeping, carrying seed for sowing, will certainly come again with joy, carrying his sheaves.
