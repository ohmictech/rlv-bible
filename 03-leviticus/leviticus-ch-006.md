God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"If anyone drifts, and commits a trespass against God, and deals falsely with his neighbor in a matter of deposit, or of bargain, or of robbery, or has oppressed his neighbor, 
or has found that which was lost, and lied about it, and swearing to a lie, in any of these things that a a man drifts in his actions 
then it will be, if he has drifted, and is guilty, he will restore that which he took by robbery, or the thing which he has gotten by oppression, or the deposit which was committed to him, or the lost thing which he found, 
or any thing about which he has sworn falsely: he will restore it in full, and will add a fifth part more to it. He will return it to him to whom it belongs in the day of his being found guilty. 
He will bring his trespass offering to God: a ram without defect from the flock, according to your estimation, for a trespass offering, to the priest. 
The priest will make things right for him before God, and he'll be forgiven concerning whatever he does to become guilty." 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Command Aaron and his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the burnt offering: the burnt offering will be on the hearth on the altar all night until the morning; and the fire of the altar will be kept burning on it. 
The priest will put on his linen garment, and he will put on his linen trousers upon his body; and he will remove the ashes from where the fire has consumed the burnt offering on the altar, and he will put them beside the altar. 
He will take off his garments, and put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place. 
The fire on the altar will be kept burning on it, it won't go out; and the priest will burn wood on it every morning. He will lay the burnt offering in order upon it, and will burn on it the fat of the offerings for wholeness. 
Fire will be kept burning on the altar continually; it won't go out. 
"'This is the law of the meal offering: the sons of Aaron will offer it before God, before the altar. 
He will take from there his handful of the fine flour of the meal offering, and of its oil, and all the frankincense which is on the meal offering, and will burn it on the altar for a pleasant aroma, as its memorial portion, to God. 
That which is left of it Aaron and his sons will eat. It will be eaten without yeast in a holy place. They will eat it in the court of the Tent of Meeting. 
It won't be baked with yeast. I have given it as their portion of my offerings made by fire. It's most holy, as are the offering for wrongdoing and the trespass offering. 
Every male among the children of Aaron will eat of it, as their portion for the eras throughout your generations, from the offerings of God made by fire. Whoever touches them will be holy.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"This is the offering of Aaron and of his sons, which they will offer to God in the day when he's anointed: one tenth of an ephah of fine flour for a meal offering perpetually, half of it in the morning, and half of it in the evening. 
It will be made with oil in a griddle. When it's soaked, you will bring it in. You will offer the meal offering in baked pieces for a pleasant aroma to God. 
The anointed priest that will be in his place from among his sons will offer it. By a statute for the eras, it will be wholly burned to God. 
Every meal offering of a priest will be wholly burned. It won't be eaten." 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, 'This is the law of the offering for wrongdoing: in the place where the burnt offering is killed, the offering for wrongdoing will be killed before God. It's most holy. 
The priest who offers it as an offering for wrongdoing will eat it. It will be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the Tent of Meeting. 
Whatever will touch its flesh will be holy. When there's any of its blood sprinkled on a garment, you will wash that on which it was sprinkled in a holy place. 
But the earthen vessel in which it's boiled will be broken; and if it's boiled in a bronze vessel, it will be scoured, and rinsed in water. 
Every male among the priests will eat of it. It's most holy. 
No offering for wrongdoing, of which any of the blood is brought into the Tent of Meeting to make things right in the Holy Place, will be eaten. It will be burned with fire.
