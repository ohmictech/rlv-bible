44, 46, 48 | These are Isaiah's words, from the last verse of his book (Isaiah 66:24). He is describing the rubbish heap outside Jerusalem, where the bodies of executed criminals were thrown and the fires were kept burning. The maggots there never finish their work and the fire is never put out, which means the disposal is final and public rather than that the burning goes on without end. Read that way, the warning is about how much a person loses, not about how long they suffer.
