"To the messenger of the gathering in Sardis write: These are the words of the one who holds the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars. I know your deeds. You have a name for being alive, but you are dead. 
Wake up and strengthen what is left, which was about to be thrown away, because I have found nothing you have done that is complete in the sight of my God. 
Remember how you received and heard it. Hold on to it and have a change of heart. If you don't wake up, I will come like a thief, and you won't know what hour I come to you. 
But you have a few people in Sardis who haven't made their clothes unclean. They will walk with me in white, because they are worthy. 
The one who wins the victory will be dressed in white clothes, and I will in no way wipe their name out of the book of real life. I will openly declare their name in front of my Father and in front of his messengers. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings. 
"To the messenger of the gathering in Philadelphia write: These are the words of the one who is set apart, the one who is true, who holds the key of David, who opens and no one can shut, who shuts and no one can open: 
I know your deeds. Look, I have put an open door in front of you, which no one can shut. I know that you have little strength, and yet you have kept my message and haven't disowned my name. 
Look, I will make those of the gathering of the Adversary, who say they are Jewish and aren't, but are lying, I will make them come and bow down at your feet, and they will know that I have loved you. 
Because you have kept my command to hold on, I will also keep you safe in the hour of testing that is coming on the whole world, to test those who live on the land. 
I am coming soon. Hold on to what you have, so that no one takes your wreath. 
The one who wins the victory I will make a pillar in the temple of my God, and they will never leave it again. I will write on them the name of my God and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down out of the sky from my God, and my own new name. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings. 
"To the messenger of the gathering in Laodicea write: These are the words of the Amen, the trustworthy and true witness, the beginning of God's creation. 
I know your deeds: you are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were cold or hot. 
So because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I am about to spit you out of my mouth. 
You say, "I am rich. I have made a fortune and need nothing." But you don't know that you are the wretched one, pitiful, poor, blind, and naked. 
So I advise you to buy from me gold refined by fire, so that you can be rich, and white clothes to wear, so that your shameful nakedness isn't seen, and eye salve to put on your eyes, so that you can see. 
I correct and train everyone I love. So be earnest and have a change of heart. 
Look, I am standing at the door and knocking. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in and eat with them, and they with me. 
The one who wins the victory I will give the right to sit with me on my throne, just as I also won the victory and sat down with my Father on his throne. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings."
