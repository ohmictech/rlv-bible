Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, saying, "Keep all the commandment which I command you today. 
It will be on the day when you will pass over the Jordan to the land which your God gives you, that you will set yourself up great stones, and coat them with plaster. 
You will write on them all the words of this law, when you have passed over, that you may go in to the land which your God gives you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as the God of your fathers has promised you. 
It will be, when you have crossed over the Jordan, that you will set up these stones, which I command you today, on Mount Ebal, and you will coat them with plaster. 
There you will build an altar to your God, an altar of stones. You won't use any iron tool on them. 
You will build your God's altar of uncut stones. You will offer burnt offerings on it to your God. 
You will sacrifice offerings for wholeness, and will eat there. You will rejoice before your God. 
You will write on the stones all the words of this law very plainly." 
Moses and the Levitical priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Be silent and listen, Israel! Today you have become the people of your God. 
You will obey your God's voice, and do his commandments and his statutes, which I command you today." 
Moses commanded the people the same day, saying, 
"These will stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people, when you have crossed over the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin. 
These will stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali. 
With a loud voice, the Levites will say to all the men of Israel, 
'Cursed is the man who makes an engraved or molten image, an abomination to God, the work of the hands of the craftsman, and sets it up in secret.' Everyone will answer and say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who dishonors his father or his mother.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who removes his neighbor's landmark.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who leads the blind astray on the road.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who withholds justice from the foreigner, fatherless, and widow.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who lies with his father's wife, because he dishonors his father's bed.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who lies with any kind of animal.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who lies with his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who lies with his mother-in-law.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who secretly kills his neighbor.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who takes a bribe to kill an innocent person.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is he who doesn't uphold the words of this law by doing them.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.'"
