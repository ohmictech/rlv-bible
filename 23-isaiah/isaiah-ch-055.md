"Hey! Come, everyone who thirsts, to the waters! Come, he who has no money, buy, and eat! Yes, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price. 
Why do you spend money for that which isn't bread, and your labor for that which doesn't satisfy? Listen diligently to me, and eat that which is good, and let your soul delight itself in richness. 
Turn your ear, and come to me. Hear, and your soul will live. I'll make an perpetual covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David. 
Look, I have given him for a witness to the peoples, a leader and commander to the peoples. 
Look, you will call a nation that you don't know; and a nation that didn't know you will run to you, because of your God, and for the Holy One of Israel; for he has glorified you." 
Seek God while he can be found. Call on him while he's near. 
Let the guilty forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts. Let him return to God, and he'll have mercy on him, to our God, for he'll freely pardon. 
"My thoughts aren't your thoughts, and your ways aren't my ways," says God. 
"As the sky are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts. 
As the rain comes down and the snow from the sky, and doesn't return there, but waters the earth, and makes it grow and bud, and gives seed to the sower and bread to the eater; 
so is my word that goes out of my mouth: it won't return to me void, but it'll accomplish that which I please, and it'll prosper in the thing I sent it to do. 
You will go out with joy, and be led out with peace. The mountains and the hills will break out before you into driftging; and all the trees of the fields will clap their hands. 
Instead of the thorn the cypress tree will come up; and instead of the brier the myrtle tree will come up. It'll make a name for God, for an perpetual sign that won't be cut off."
