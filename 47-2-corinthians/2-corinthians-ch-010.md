Now I, Paul, beg you by the humility and gentleness of Christ, I who am humble among you when present, but bold toward you when away. 
I beg you that when I am present I won't have to be bold with the confidence I mean to use against some, who think we are walking by human standards. 
Though we walk in the flesh, we don't wage war according to the flesh; 
for the weapons we fight with are not human weapons, but are powerful before God for pulling down strongholds, 
pulling down arguments and every high thing raised against the knowledge of God, and taking every thought captive to obey Christ, 
and being ready to take payback on every disobedience, once your obedience is complete. 
Do you look at things only as they appear in front of your face? If anyone trusts in himself that he is Christ's, let him consider this again with himself, that even as he is Christ's, so we also are Christ's. 
Even if I take pride somewhat abundantly in our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for pulling you down, I will not be ashamed, 
that I may not seem as if I desire to terrify you by my letters. 
They say, "His letters are weighty and strong, but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech is looked down on." 
Let such a person consider this, that what we are in word by letters when we are absent, such are we also in deed when we are present. 
We are not bold to number or compare ourselves with some of those who commend themselves. But they themselves, measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves with themselves, are without understanding. 
But we will not take pride beyond proper limits, but within the boundaries with which God appointed to us, which reach even to you. 
We don't stretch ourselves too much, as though we didn't reach to you. We came even as far as to you with the Good News of Christ, 
not taking pride beyond our limits in other people's hard work, but hoping that as your trust grows, we will grow much larger through you within our own boundaries, 
so as to announce the Good News in the regions beyond you, not taking pride in work someone else has already done. 
But "he who takes pride, let him take pride in the Lord." 
It isn't he who commends himself who is approved, but whom the Lord commends.
