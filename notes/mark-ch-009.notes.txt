44, 46, 48 | These are Isaiah's words, from the last verse of his book (Isaiah 66:24). He is describing the rubbish heap outside Jerusalem, where the bodies of executed criminals were thrown and the fires were kept burning to consume them. Maggots finish what the fire has not reached. So the picture is refuse being consumed, and the point of saying the worm never dies and the fire never goes out is that nothing stops the work and nothing puts back what it took. What decays does not come back. The warning is about how much a person loses, not about how long they suffer.
