So since Christ suffered in the body, arm yourselves with the same way of thinking, because anyone who has suffered in the body is done with the drift, 
so that you no longer spend the rest of your life in the body chasing human cravings, but doing God's will. 
For you have spent enough of your past time doing what the nations want: shameless living, craving, heavy drinking, wild parties, drunken brawls, and hateful idol worship. 
They think it strange that you no longer plunge into the same flood of wild living with them, and they slander you. 
They will have to answer to the one who is ready to pass a verdict on the living and the dead. 
That is why the Good News was announced even to the dead, so that although they were judged as people in the body, they might live as God lives, in the Spirit. 
The end of everything is near. So be clear-headed and self-controlled, so that you can pray. 
Above all, love one another deeply, because love covers over a great many mistakes. 
Welcome each other into your homes without grumbling. 
Each of you has received a gift. Use it to serve one another, as good managers of God's gift in all its different forms. 
If anyone speaks, they should speak God's own words. If anyone serves, they should serve with the strength God provides, so that in everything God may be honored through Jesus Christ. To him belong the weight and the power for all the eras. Amen. 
My truly loved friends, don't be surprised at the fire that has come on you to test you, as though something strange were happening to you. 
Instead, celebrate, because you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may also celebrate and be glad when his weight is unveiled. 
If you are insulted because of the name of Christ, you are flourishing, because the Spirit of splendor, the Spirit of God, rests on you. As for them, he is slandered; as for you, he is honored. 
But none of you should suffer as a murderer, a thief, someone who does wrong, or someone who meddles in other people's business. 
But if you suffer for being a Christian, do not be ashamed. Give honor to God because you bear that name. 
For the time has come for the verdict to begin with God's own household. And if it begins with us, what will happen to those who refuse to obey God's Good News? 
"If it is hard for the upright to be rescued, what will become of those who disregard God and keep drifting?" 
So those who suffer because God wills it, while they do good, should hand their lives over to a trustworthy Creator.
