When the Sabbath was over, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices, so that they could come and anoint him. 
Very early on the first day of the week, they came to the tomb just after sunrise. 
They were saying among themselves, "Who will roll the stone away from the entrance of the tomb for us?" 
for it was very large. Looking up, they saw that the stone had been rolled away. 
Going into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, dressed in a white robe, and they were amazed. 
He said to them, "Don't be amazed. You are looking for Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He has been raised! He isn't here. See the place where they laid him! 
But go, tell his students and Peter, 'He is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see him, just as he told you.'" 
They went out and fled from the tomb, for trembling and astonishment had come over them. They said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid. 
Now when he had been raised early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom he had driven out seven unclean spirits. 
She went and told those who had been with him, as they mourned and cried. 
When they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, they didn't trust it. 
After these things he appeared in another form to two of them as they walked along on their way into the countryside. 
They went away and told it to the rest. They didn't trust them, either. 
Afterward he appeared to the eleven themselves as they sat at the table, and he rebuked them for their lack of trust and hardness of heart, because they didn't trust those who had seen him after he had been raised. 
He said to them, "Go into all the world and preach the Good News to all creation. 
Whoever trusts and is immersed will be rescued, but whoever doesn't trust will be sentenced. 
These signs will accompany those who trust: in my name they will drive out unclean spirits, they will speak in new languages, 
they will pick up snakes, and if they drink anything deadly, it will in no way harm them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will get well." 
So then the Lord, after he had spoken to them, was taken up into the sky and sat down at the right hand of God. 
They went out and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed. Amen.
