This is why I, Paul, am a prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you nations, 
if indeed you have heard of the household plan of God's gift that was given to me for you, 
that is, how the secret was made known to me by an unveiling, just as I wrote before in a few words, 
so that when you read it you can see how well I understand the secret of Christ. 
In other generations it wasn't made known to people as it has now been unveiled by the Spirit to his set-apart sent ones and spokesmen, 
that the nations are given the charge together with us, are parts of the same body, and share together in the promise in Christ Jesus through the Good News, 
of which I became a servant by God's gift, given to me through the working of his power. 
To me, the least of all the set-apart ones, this gift was given: to announce to the nations the riches of Christ that no one can trace out, 
and to make plain to everyone what the household plan of that secret is, the secret hidden for ages in God, who created everything through Jesus Christ, 
so that now, through the gathering, God's many-sided wisdom might be made known to the ruling powers and the authorities in the sky, 
in keeping with the purpose of the eras, which he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord. 
In him we can speak out in the open and come in with confidence, because we trust him. 
So I ask you not to be discouraged by my troubles for you, because they're your weight. 
This is why I get down on my knees before the Father, 
from whom every family in the sky and on the land takes its name, 
asking him to give you, out of the riches of his weight, to be made strong with power through his Spirit in your inner being, 
so that Christ can make his home in your hearts through trust, and so that you, rooted and founded in love, 
may have the strength to grasp, along with all the set-apart ones, how wide and long and high and deep it is, 
and to know the love of Christ that goes beyond all knowing, so that you can be filled with all the fullness of God. 
Now to the one who's able to do far beyond all that we ask or imagine, measured by the power at work in us, 
to him be the weight in the gathering and in Christ Jesus through all generations, through all the eras. Amen.
