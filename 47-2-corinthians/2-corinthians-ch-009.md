It's really not necessary for me to write to you about the service to the set-apart ones, 
for I know your readiness, of which I take pride in you to those of Macedonia, that Achaia has been prepared for the past year. Your passion has stirred up very many of them. 
But I have sent the brothers so that our pride in you may not come to nothing in this respect, that, just as I said, you may be prepared, 
lest by any means, if anyone from Macedonia comes there with me and finds you unprepared, we (to say nothing of you) would be disappointed in this confident pride. 
So I thought it necessary to beg the brothers to go on ahead to you and arrange in advance the generous gift you promised, so that it would be ready as a gift of generosity and not of greed. 
Remember this: he who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly. He who sows bountifully will also reap bountifully. 
Let each person give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under pressure, for God loves a cheerful giver. 
And God is able to make every gift overflow to you, so that you always have enough of everything and overflow into every good work. 
As it's written, "He has scattered abroad. He has given to the poor. His rightness remains for the eras." 
Now may he who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food, supply and multiply your seed for sowing, and increase the fruits of your rightness, 
you being made rich in everything for all generosity, which produces thanksgiving to God through us. 
This service you perform not only makes up what the set-apart ones lack, but also overflows in much thanksgiving to God, 
because through the proof of this service, they give God weight for your obedience in agreeing to the Good News of Christ and for the generosity of what you share with them and with everyone, 
while they also, praying for you, long for you because of the great favor of God in you. 
Thanks be to God for his gift beyond words!
