Observe the month of Abib and keep the Passover to your God, because in the month of Abib your God brought you out of Egypt at night. 
Sacrifice the Passover to your God from the flock and the herd, in the place God will choose for his name to be. 
Don't eat bread made with yeast with it. For seven days eat bread without yeast with it, the bread of hardship, because you came out of Egypt in a hurry, so that you remember the day you came out of Egypt all the days of your life. 
No yeast is to be seen anywhere in your borders for seven days, and none of the meat you sacrifice on the first evening may be left overnight until morning. 
You can't sacrifice the Passover in any of your towns your God is giving you. 
Sacrifice it only at the place your God will choose for his name to be, in the evening, at sunset, at the time you came out of Egypt. 
Roast it and eat it in the place your God chooses. Then in the morning go back to your tents. 
For six days eat bread without yeast, and on the seventh day hold a set apart gathering to your God. Do no work. 
Count off seven weeks for yourself. Start counting seven weeks from the time you first put the sickle to the standing grain. 
Then keep the Feast of Weeks to your God with a freewill offering of your hand, which you give according to how your God has blessed you. 
Celebrate before your God, you, your son, your daughter, your male and female servants, the Levite inside your gates, the foreigner, the orphans and the widows among you, in the place your God will choose for his name to be. 
Remember that you were a slave in Egypt. Be careful to do these rules. 
Keep the Feast of Shelters for seven days, after you have gathered in from your threshing floor and your winepress. 
Celebrate at your feast, you, your son, your daughter, your male and female servants, the Levite, the foreigner, the orphans and the widows inside your gates. 
Keep a feast to your God for seven days in the place God chooses, because your God will bless you in all your crops and in all the work of your hands, and you will be full of joy. 
Three times a year all your males must appear before your God in the place he chooses: at the Feast of Bread Without Yeast, at the Feast of Weeks and at the Feast of Shelters. They must not appear before God empty-handed. 
Each man gives what he can, according to the blessing your God has given you. 
Appoint judges and officials in every town your God is giving you, tribe by tribe, and they are to judge the people fairly. 
Don't bend justice. Don't show favoritism. Don't take a bribe, because a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and twists the words of those who are in the right. 
Follow only what is right, so that you may live and take over the land your God is giving you. 
Don't plant any kind of Asherah pole beside the altar of your God that you make for yourself, 
and don't set up a sacred stone, because your God hates that.
