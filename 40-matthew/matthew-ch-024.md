Jesus left the temple courts and was on his way, when his students came to him to show him the temple buildings. 
But he answered them, "Do you see all these things? Truly I tell you, there won't be left here one stone on another that won't be thrown down." 
As he sat on the Mount of Olives, the students came to him privately, saying, "Tell us, when will these things be? What's the sign of your arrival, and of the completion of the era?" 
Jesus answered them, "Watch out that no one leads you astray. 
Many will come in my name, saying, 'I'm the Christ,' and will lead many astray. 
You'll hear of wars and reports of wars. See that you aren't troubled, for all this must happen, but the end isn't yet. 
Nation will rise against nation, and reign against reign, and there will be famines, plagues, and earthquakes in various places. 
But all these things are the beginning of birth pains. 
"Then they'll put you under pressure and kill you. You'll be hated by all the nations because of my name. 
Then many will be trapped, and will hand one another over, and will hate one another. 
Many false spokesmen will arise and will lead many astray. 
Because living as if there were no law will multiply, the love of many will grow cold. 
But he who sticks it out to the end will be rescued. 
This Good News of the reign will be preached in the whole world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come. 
"So when you see the detestable thing that brings desolation, spoken of through Daniel the spokesman, standing in the holy place (let the reader take note), 
then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains. 
Let the one on the housetop not go down to take out the things that are in his house. 
Let the one in the field not turn back to get his clothes. 
But how terrible for those who are pregnant and for nursing mothers in those days! 
Pray that your escape won't be in the winter or on a Sabbath, 
for then there will be great pressure, such as hasn't happened from the beginning of the world until now, and never will again. 
Unless those days had been shortened, no flesh would have been rescued. But for the sake of the chosen ones, those days will be shortened. 
"Then if anyone tells you, 'Look, here's the Christ!' or, 'There!' don't trust it. 
False christs and false spokesmen will arise, and they'll show great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen ones. 
"Look, I have told you beforehand. 
"So if they tell you, 'Look, he's out in the empty land,' don't go out, or 'Look, he's in the inner rooms,' don't trust it. 
As the lightning flashes from the east and is seen even to the west, so will the arrival of the Human One be. 
Wherever the carcass is, that's where the vultures gather. 
"But immediately after the pressure of those days, the sun will be darkened, the moon won't give its light, the stars will fall from the sky, and the powers of the sky will be shaken, 
and then the sign of the Human One will appear in the sky. Then all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they'll see the Human One coming on the clouds of the sky with power and great weight. 
He'll send out his messengers with a great trumpet call, and they'll gather together his chosen ones from the four winds, from one end of the sky to the other. 
"Now learn this story from the fig tree: When its branch has become tender and puts out its leaves, you know that the summer is near. 
Even so you also, when you see all these things, know that he's near, right at the doors. 
Truly I tell you, this generation won't pass away until all these things are done. 
The sky and the land will pass away, but my words won't pass away. 
"But no one knows that day or hour, not even the messengers in the sky, but only my Father. 
As the days of Noah were, so will the arrival of the Human One be. 
As in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and being given in marriage, until the day that Noah entered the ship, 
and they didn't know until the flood came and swept them all away, so will the arrival of the Human One be. 
Then two men will be in the field: one will be taken and one will be left. 
Two women will be grinding at the mill: one will be taken and one will be left. 
So stay awake, for you don't know what hour your Lord is coming. 
But know this: if the master of the house had known what time of night the thief was coming, he'd have stayed awake, and wouldn't have let his house be broken into. 
So you also be ready, for the Human One will come at an hour you don't expect. 
"Who then is the faithful and sensible slave, whom his lord has set over his household, to give them their food at the right time? 
Flourishing is that slave whom his lord finds doing so when he comes. 
Truly I tell you that he'll set him over all that he has. 
But if that bad slave should say in his heart, 'My lord is delaying his arrival,' 
and begins to beat his fellow slaves, and eats and drinks with the drunkards, 
the lord of that slave will come on a day when he doesn't expect him and at an hour he doesn't know, 
and will cut him in two and assign his portion with the pretenders. That's where the crying and grinding of teeth will be.
