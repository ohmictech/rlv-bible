"When all these things have come on you, the blessing and the curse that I have set before you, and you call them to mind among all the nations where your God has driven you, 
and you return to your God and obey his voice in everything I am commanding you today, you and your children, with all your heart and all your life, 
then your God will bring you back from captivity, have compassion on you, and gather you again from all the peoples where your God has scattered you. 
Even if your scattered people are at the far edge of the sky, your God will gather you from there and bring you back. 
Your God will bring you into the land your fathers owned, and you will own it. He will do you good and make you more numerous than your fathers. 
Your God will cut away the hard covering of your heart and the heart of your descendants, so that you love your God with all your heart and all your life, and so live. 
Your God will put all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate you and chased you. 
Then you will return and obey the voice of God and do all his commands that I am giving you today. 
Your God will make everything you do succeed, in your children, your livestock and your crops, for good, because God will again take delight in you and do you good, as he delighted in your fathers, 
if you obey the voice of your God and keep his commands and his rules written in the scroll of this law, and turn to your God with all your heart and all your life. 
This command I am giving you today isn't too hard for you or out of reach. 
It isn't in the sky, so that you have to say, 'Who will go up to the sky for us and bring it down, so we can hear it and do it?' 
It isn't across the sea, so that you have to say, 'Who will cross the sea for us and bring it back, so we can hear it and do it?' 
No, the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so you can do it. 
Look, today I have set before you life and good, and death and evil. 
I am commanding you today to love your God, to walk in his ways and to keep his commands, his rules and his decisions, so that you may live and multiply and your God may bless you in the land you are going in to take. 
But if your heart turns away and you don't listen, and you are drawn off to bow down to other gods and serve them, 
then I tell you today that you will certainly be destroyed. You won't live long in the land you are crossing the Jordan to go in and take. 
I call the sky and the earth as witnesses against you today: I have set before you life and death, the blessing and the curse. So choose life, so that you and your children may live, 
by loving your God, obeying his voice and holding on to him, because he is your life and the length of your days, so that you may live in the land God swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, to give them."
