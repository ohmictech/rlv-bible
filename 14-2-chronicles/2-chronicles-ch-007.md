Now when Solomon had finished praying, fire came down from the sky and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices; and God's glory filled the house. 
The priests couldn't enter into God's house, because God's glory filled God's house. 
All the children of Israel looked on, when the fire came down, and God's glory was on the house. They bowed themselves with their faces to the ground on the pavement, worshiped, and gave thanks to God, saying, "For he's good, for his loving kindness endures for the eras!" 
Then the king and everyone offered sacrifices before God. 
King Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty-two thousand head of cattle and a hundred twenty thousand sheep. So the king and everyone dedicated God's house. 
The priests stood, according to their positions; the Levites also with instruments of music of God, which David the king had made to give thanks to God, when David praised by their ministry, saying "For his loving kindness endures for the eras." The priests sounded trumpets before them; and all Israel stood. 
Moreover Solomon made the middle of the court that was before God's house holy; for there he offered the burnt offerings and the fat of the offerings for wholeness, because the bronze altar which Solomon had made wasn't able to receive the burnt offering, the meal offering, and the fat. 
So Solomon held the feast at that time for seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great assembly, from the entrance of Hamath to the brook of Egypt. 
On the eighth day, they held a solemn assembly; for they kept the dedication of the altar seven days, and the feast seven days. 
On the twenty-third day of the seventh month, he sent the people away to their tents, joyful and glad of heart for the goodness that God had shown to David, to Solomon, and to Israel his people. 
Thus Solomon finished God's house and the king's house; and he successfully completed all that came into Solomon's heart to make in God's house and in his own house. 
Then God appeared to Solomon by night, and said to him, "I have heard your prayer, and have chosen this place for myself for a house of sacrifice. 
"If I shut up the sky so that there's no rain, or if I command the locust to devour the land, or if I send pestilence among my people, 
if my people who are called by my name will humble themselves, pray, seek my face, and turn from their guilty ways, then I'll hear from the sky, will forgive their wrongdoing, and will heal their land. 
Now my eyes will be open and my ears attentive to prayer that's made in this place. 
Now I have chosen and made this house holy, that my name may be there for the eras; and my eyes and my heart will be there perpetually. 
"As for you, if you'll walk before me as David your father walked, and do according to all that I have commanded you, and will keep my statutes and my ordinances, 
then I'll establish the throne of your kingdom, according as I covenanted with David your father, saying, 'There won't fail you a man to be ruler in Israel.' 
But if you turn away and forsake my statutes and my commandments which I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and worship them, 
then I'll pluck them up by the roots out of my land which I have given them; and this house, which I have made holy for my name, I'll cast out of my sight, and I'll make it a proverb and a byword among all peoples. 
This house, which is so high, everyone who passes by it'll be astonished and say, 'Why has God done this to this land and to this house?' 
They will answer, 'Because they abandoned the God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and took other gods, worshiped them, and served them. So he has brought all this bad on them.'"
