But in the latter days, it'll happen that the mountain of God's temple will be established on the top of the mountains, and it'll be exalted above the hills; and peoples will stream to it. 
Many nations will go and say, "Come! Let's go up to the mountain of God, and to the house of the God of Jacob; and he'll teach us of his ways, and we'll walk in his paths." The law will go out of Zion, and God's word from Jerusalem; 
and he'll judge between many peoples, and will decide concerning strong nations afar off. They'll beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruning hooks. Nation won't lift up sword against nation, neither will they learn war any more. 
But every man will sit under his vine and under his fig tree. No one will make them afraid, for the mouth of the God of Armies has spoken. 
Indeed all the nations may walk in the name of their gods, but we'll walk in the name of our God for all the eras. 
"In that day," says God, "I'll assemble that which is lame, and I'll gather that which is driven away, and that which I have afflicted; 
and I'll make that which was lame a remnant, and that which was cast far off a strong nation: and God will reign over them on Mount Zion from then on, even for the eras." 
You, tower of the flock, the hill of the daughter of Zion, to you it'll come. Yes, the former dominion will come, the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem. 
Now why do you cry out aloud? Is there no king in you? Has your counselor perished, that pains have taken hold of you as of a woman in travail? 
Be in pain, and labor to give birth, daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail; for now you'll go out of the city, and will dwell in the field, and will come even to Babylon. There you'll be rescued. There God will buy you back from the hand of your enemies. 
Now many nations have assembled against you, that say, "Let her be defiled, and let our eye gloat over Zion." 
But they don't know the thoughts of God, neither do they understand his counsel; for he has gathered them like the sheaves to the threshing floor. 
Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion, for I'll make your horn iron, and I'll make your hoofs bronze. You'll beat in pieces many peoples. I'll devote their gain to God, and their substance to the Lord of the whole earth.
