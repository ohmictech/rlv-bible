God spoke to Moses: 
"Tell Aaron and his sons to treat the set apart offerings the Israelites give me with care, so they don't dishonor my set apart name. I am God. 
"Tell them, 'If any of your descendants through all their generations comes near the set apart offerings the Israelites set apart for God while he is unclean, that person is to be cut off from before me. I am God. 
"'No descendant of Aaron who has a serious skin disease or a discharge may eat the set apart offerings until he is clean. Anyone who touches something made unclean by a dead body, or a man who has a seminal emission, 
or who touches any crawling thing that makes him unclean, or any person who makes him unclean, whatever his uncleanness, 
anyone who touches such a thing will be unclean until evening and may not eat the set apart offerings unless he washes his body in water. 
When the sun goes down he will be clean, and afterward he may eat the set apart offerings, because that is his food. 
He is not to eat an animal that died on its own or was torn by wild animals and make himself unclean with it. I am God. 
"'They are to follow my command, so they don't carry guilt for it and die because of it by dishonoring it. I am God, who sets them apart. 
"'No outsider may eat a set apart offering. A foreigner living with the priests, or a hired worker, may not eat it. 
But if a priest buys a slave with his own money, that slave may eat it, and those born in his house may eat his food. 
If a priest's daughter marries an outsider, she may not eat from the contribution of the set apart offerings. 
But if a priest's daughter is widowed or divorced, has no child, and returns to her father's house as in her youth, she may eat her father's food. But no outsider may eat it. 
"'If anyone eats a set apart offering by mistake, he is to add a fifth of its value to it and give the set apart offering to the priest. 
The priests must not dishonor the set apart offerings the Israelites lift up to God, 
and so make them carry guilt that brings punishment when they eat their set apart offerings. I am God, who sets them apart.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to Aaron, his sons and all the Israelites and tell them, 'If anyone from Israel, or any foreigner in Israel, brings an offering, whether for a vow or as a freewill offering, and offers it to God as a burnt offering, 
it is to be a male without defect, from the cattle, the sheep or the goats, for you to be accepted. 
You are not to offer anything with a defect, because it will not be accepted for you. 
Whoever brings a wholeness sacrifice to God to keep a vow or as a freewill offering, from the herd or the flock, must bring one without defect to be accepted. It must have no defect. 
Don't offer anything blind, injured, maimed, with a wart, a festering sore or a running sore to God, or put any such thing on the altar as an offering made by fire to God. 
An ox or a lamb that is deformed or stunted you may offer as a freewill offering, but it will not be accepted for a vow. 
You must not offer to God an animal whose testicles are bruised, crushed, torn or cut. You must not do this in your land, 
and you must not accept any such animal from a foreigner to offer as the bread of your God, because it is corrupted and defective. It will not be accepted for you.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"When an ox, a sheep or a goat is born, it is to stay with its mother for seven days. From the eighth day on it may be accepted as an offering made by fire to God. 
Whether it is a cow or a ewe, you are not to kill it and its young on the same day. 
"When you sacrifice a thanksgiving sacrifice to God, sacrifice it so that you will be accepted. 
It is to be eaten that same day, and you are to leave none of it until morning. I am God. 
"So keep my commands and do them. I am God. 
Don't dishonor my set apart name, but let me be treated as set apart among the Israelites. I am God, who sets you apart, 
who brought you out of Egypt to be your God. I am God."
