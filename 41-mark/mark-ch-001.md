The beginning of the Good News of Jesus Christ, the Son of God. 
As the spokesmen wrote: "Look, I send my messenger ahead of you, who will prepare your way before you: 
the voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Get the way of the Lord ready! Make his paths straight!'" 
John came, immersing in the wilderness and preaching an immersion that marked a change of heart for the release of mistakes. 
All the country of Judea and everyone in Jerusalem went out to him. They were immersed by him in the Jordan river, confessing their mistakes out loud. 
John was clothed with camel's hair and a leather belt around his waist. He ate locusts and wild honey. 
He preached, saying, "After me comes one mightier than I, whose sandal strap I'm not worthy to bend down and untie. 
I immersed you in water, but he will immerse you in the Holy Spirit." 
In those days, Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee and was immersed by John in the Jordan. 
Immediately, coming up out of the water, he saw the sky splitting open and the Spirit coming down on him like a dove. 
A voice came out of the sky, "You are my Son, whom I truly love, and I am delighted with you." 
Immediately the Spirit drove him out into the wilderness. 
He was in the wilderness forty days, being tested by the Slanderer. He was with the wild animals, and the messengers were serving him. 
Now after John was arrested, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the Good News of God's reign, 
and saying, "The time has come, and God's reign is near! Have a change of heart, and trust in the Good News." 
As he walked along by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, Simon's brother, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen. 
Jesus said to them, "Come after me, and I will make you fishers of people." 
Immediately they left their nets, and followed him. 
Going a little further from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee and his brother John, who were in the boat mending the nets. 
Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired hands, and went after him. 
They went into Capernaum, and immediately on the Sabbath he went into the gathering place and taught. 
They were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as one who had authority, and not like the Bible teachers. 
Immediately there was in their gathering place a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out, 
saying, "Ha! What do we have to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to kill us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!" 
Jesus rebuked him, saying, "Be quiet, and come out of him!" 
The unclean spirit, throwing him into convulsions and crying out with a loud voice, came out of him. 
They were all amazed, so that they questioned among themselves, saying, "What is this? A new teaching with authority! He even commands the unclean spirits, and they obey him!" 
The news about him went out immediately into all the region of Galilee and its surrounding area. 
Immediately, when they had come out of the gathering place, they went into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John. 
Now Simon's wife's mother lay sick with a fever, and immediately they told him about her. 
He came and took her by the hand and raised her up. The fever left her immediately, and she served them. 
At evening, when the sun had set, they brought to him everyone who was sick and those who were possessed by unclean spirits. 
All the city was gathered together at the door. 
He healed many who were sick with various diseases and drove out many unclean spirits. He didn't let the unclean spirits speak, because they knew him. 
Early in the morning, while it was still dark, he got up and went out, and left for an empty place, and prayed there. 
Simon and those who were with him searched for him. 
They found him and told him, "Everyone is looking for you." 
He said to them, "Let's go somewhere else, into the next towns, so that I may preach there too, because that is why I came out." 
He went into their gathering places throughout all Galilee, preaching and driving out unclean spirits. 
A man with leprosy came to him, begging him and kneeling down to him, saying, "If you want to, you can make me clean." 
Moved with deep compassion, he stretched out his hand, touched him, and said to him, "I want to. Be made clean." 
When he had said this, immediately the leprosy left him and he was made clean. 
He strictly warned him and immediately sent him out, 
and said to him, "See that you say nothing to anyone, but go show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, as a witness to them." 
But he went out and began to proclaim it widely and to spread the news, so that Jesus could no longer openly enter a city, but stayed outside in empty places. People came to him from everywhere.
