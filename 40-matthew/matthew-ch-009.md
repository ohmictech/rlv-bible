He entered into a boat and crossed over, and came into his own city. 
Suddenly, they brought to him a man who was paralyzed, lying on a bed. Jesus, seeing their trust, said to the paralyzed man, "Son, cheer up! Your mistakes are released." 
Suddenly, some of the Bible teachers said to themselves, "This man is slandering." 
Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said, "Why do you think malicious thoughts in your hearts? 
Which is easier, to say, 'Your mistakes are released,' or to say, 'Get up, and walk'? 
But so that you may know that the Human One has authority on earth to release mistakes," (then he said to the paralyzed man), "Get up, take up your mat, and go to your house." 
He got up and went to his house. 
But when the crowds saw it, they were amazed and made much of God, who had given such authority to people. 
As Jesus went on from there, he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax collection office. He said to him, "Follow me." He got up and followed him. 
As he sat in the house, many tax collectors and those who have drifted came and sat down with Jesus and his students. 
When the strict religious ones saw it, they said to his students, "Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and those who have drifted?" 
When Jesus heard it, he said to them, "Those who are healthy have no need for a doctor, but those who are sick do. 
But you go and learn what this means: 'I want mercy, and not sacrifice,' for I came not to call the ones who are in the right, but to call those who have drifted to a change of heart." 
Then John's students came to him, saying, "Why do we and the strict religious ones go without food often, but your students don't go without food?" 
Jesus said to them, "Can the friends of the bridegroom mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? But the days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will go without food. 
No one puts a piece of unshrunk cloth on an old garment, for the patch would tear away from the garment, and a worse hole is made. 
Neither do people put new wine into old wineskins, or else the skins would burst, the wine would spill, and the skins would be broken up. No, they put new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved." 
While he told these things to them, suddenly a ruler came and worshiped him, saying, "My daughter has just died, but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live." 
Jesus got up and followed him, and so did his students. 
Suddenly, a woman who had been bleeding for twelve years came behind him, and touched the fringe of his garment. 
for she said within herself, "If I just touch his garment, I will be healed and made whole." 
But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said, "Daughter, cheer up! Your trust has healed you and made you whole." And the woman was healed and made whole from that hour. 
When Jesus came into the ruler's house and saw the flute players and the crowd making a commotion, 
he said to them, "Make room, because the girl isn't dead, but sleeping." They laughed at him. 
But when the crowd was sent out, he went in, took her by the hand, and the girl got up. 
The report of this went out into all that land. 
As Jesus went on from there, two blind men followed him, calling out and saying, "Have mercy on us, Son of David!" 
When he had come into the house, the blind men came to him. Jesus said to them, "Do you trust that I am able to do this?" They told him, "Yes, Lord." 
Then he touched their eyes, saying, "According to your trust, let it be done to you." 
Then their eyes were opened. Jesus strictly commanded them, saying, "See that no one knows about this." 
But they went out and spread the news about him in all that land. 
As they went out, suddenly a man who could not speak, possessed by an unclean spirit, was brought to him. 
When the unclean spirit was driven out, the man who could not speak spoke. The crowds were amazed, saying, "Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel!" 
But the strict religious ones said, "By the ruler of the unclean spirits, he drives out unclean spirits." 
Jesus went about all the cities and the villages, teaching in their gathering places and preaching the good news of the reign, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people. 
But when he saw the crowds, he was moved with deep compassion for them, because they were harassed and scattered, like sheep without a shepherd. 
Then he said to his students, "The harvest indeed is plentiful, but the workers are few. 
So pray that the Lord of the harvest will send out workers into his harvest."
