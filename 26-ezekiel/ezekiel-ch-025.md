God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, set your face toward the children of Ammon, and prophesy against them. 
Tell the children of Ammon, 'Hear the word of God! The God says, "Because you said, 'Aha!' against my sanctuary when it was profaned, and against the land of Israel when it was made desolate, and against the house of Judah when they went into captivity, 
So, look, I'll deliver you to the children of the east for a possession. They'll set their encampments in you and make their dwellings in you. They'll eat your fruit and they'll drink your milk. 
I'll make Rabbah a stable for camels and the children of Ammon a resting place for flocks. Then you'll know that I'm God." 
God says: "Because you have clapped your hands, stamped with the feet, and rejoiced with all the contempt of your soul against the land of Israel, 
So, look, I have stretched out my hand on you, and will deliver you for a plunder to the nations. I'll cut you off from the peoples, and I'll cause you to perish out of the countries. I'll destroy you. Then you'll know that I'm God." 
"'The God says: "Because Moab and Seir say, 'Look, the house of Judah is like all the nations,' 
So, look, I'll open the side of Moab from the cities, from his cities which are on its frontiers, the glory of the country, Beth Jeshimoth, Baal Meon, and Kiriathaim, 
to the children of the east, to go against the children of Ammon; and I'll give them for a possession, that the children of Ammon can't be remembered among the nations. 
I'll execute judgments on Moab. Then they'll know that I'm God." 
"'The God says: "Because Edom has dealt against the house of Judah by taking vengeance, and has greatly offended, and taken revenge on them," 
So God says, "I'll stretch out my hand on Edom, and will cut off man and animal from it; and I'll make it desolate from Teman. They'll fall by the sword even to Dedan. 
I'll lay my vengeance on Edom by the hand of my people Israel. They'll do in Edom according to my anger and according to my wrath. Then they'll know my vengeance," says God. 
"'The God says: "Because the Philistines have taken revenge, and have taken vengeance with contempt of soul to destroy with perpetual hostility," 
So God says, "Look, I'll stretch out my hand on the Philistines, and I'll cut off the Cherethites, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast. 
I'll execute great vengeance on them with wrathful rebukes. Then they'll know that I'm God, when I lay my vengeance on them."'"
