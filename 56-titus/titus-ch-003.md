Remind them to be obedient to rulers and authorities, to do what they are told, and to be ready for every good deed, 
to speak slander of no one, to avoid fighting, to be gentle, and to show every kindness to everyone. 
There was a time when we too were foolish, refusing to be told what to do, and led astray. We were slaves to all kinds of cravings and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful and hating one another. 
But then the kindness of God our Restorer appeared, along with his love for people, 
and he rescued us, not because of any right deeds we had done, but out of his mercy, through the washing of being born all over again and the making new that comes from the Holy Spirit, 
whom he poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Restorer. 
He did this so that, made right by his favor, we might become heirs with the expectation of the real life of the era. 
This saying is trustworthy, and I want you to insist on these things, so that those who have put their trust in God make it their business to do good deeds. These things are good and useful to people. 
But have nothing to do with foolish arguments, endless family trees, quarrels, and fights about the law, because they are useless and empty. 
Warn a divider once, and warn him a second time; after that, have nothing to do with him. 
You know that a person like that has turned off the road and keeps on drifting, and he has passed sentence on himself. 
When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, do your best to come to me at Nicopolis, because I have decided to spend the winter there. 
Do everything you can to help Zenas the expert in the law and Apollos on their way, and see that they lack nothing. 
Our people must learn to do good deeds and meet whatever needs come up, so that they aren't fruitless. 
Everyone with me sends you greetings. Greet those who love us in the trust. Favor be with you all. Amen.
