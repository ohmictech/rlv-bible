Working together with him, we also beg you not to receive the gift of God for nothing. 
He says, "At the right time I listened to you, and on a day of rescue I helped you." Look, now is the right time. Look, now is the day of rescue. 
We put no cause for stumbling in anyone's way, so that our service isn't blamed, 
but in everything commending ourselves as God's servants: in great staying power, in hard times, in hardships, in tight spots, 
in beatings, in prisons, in riots, in toil, in nights without sleep, in days without food, 
in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love without pretending, 
in the word of truth, in the power of God, with the weapons of rightness in the right hand and the left, 
through weight and dishonor, through bad report and good report, treated as liars and yet true, 
as unknown and yet well known, as dying, and look, we live, as disciplined and not killed, 
as sorrowful yet always celebrating, as poor yet making many rich, as having nothing and yet possessing all things. 
Our mouth is open to you, Corinthians. Our heart is enlarged. 
You aren't restricted by us, but you are restricted by your own affections. 
Now in return (I speak as to my children) you also open your hearts. 
Don't be tied together with those who don't trust, for what partnership can rightness have with living as if there were no law? Or what does light share with darkness? 
What agreement does Christ have with Belial? Or what share does one who trusts have with one who doesn't? 
What agreement does God's temple have with idols? You are the temple of the living God. As God said, "I will live in them and walk among them. I will be their God, and they will be my people." 
So "'Come out from among them, and be separate,' says the Lord. 'Touch nothing unclean, and I will welcome you. 
I will be a Father to you, and you will be my sons and daughters,' says the Lord, the one who holds sway over all."
