Israel stayed at Shittim, and the people began sleeping around with the women of Moab, 
because they invited the people to the sacrifices for their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods. 
Israel joined itself to Baal Peor, and God's anger burned against Israel. 
God said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and hang them in the sun before God, so that my fierce anger turns away from Israel." 
Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Each of you kill the men in your charge who have joined themselves to Baal Peor." 
Then one of the Israelites came and brought a Midianite woman to his brothers in plain sight of Moses and of the whole community of Israel, while they were weeping at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 
When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he got up from the middle of the community and took a spear in his hand. 
He followed the Israelite man into the tent and drove the spear through both of them, the man and the woman, through her belly. So the plague stopped among the Israelites. 
Those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned my anger away from the Israelites, because he was as jealous for me as I am, so I didn't destroy the Israelites in my jealousy. 
So tell him, 'I am giving him my covenant of peace. 
It will be for him and his descendants after him, the covenant of a priesthood that lasts, because he was jealous for his God and made things right for the Israelites.'" 
The name of the Israelite man who was killed, the one killed with the Midianite woman, was Zimri son of Salu, a leader of a family among the Simeonites. 
The name of the Midianite woman who was killed was Cozbi, daughter of Zur. He was head of a family in Midian. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Treat the Midianites as enemies and strike them down, 
because they treated you as enemies with their tricks and deceived you in the matter of Peor and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a Midianite leader, their sister, who was killed on the day of the plague over Peor."
