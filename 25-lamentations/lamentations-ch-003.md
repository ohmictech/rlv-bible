I'm the man who has seen affliction by the rod of his wrath. 
He has led me and caused me to walk in darkness, and not in light. 
Surely he turns his hand against me again and again all day long. 
He has made my flesh and my skin old. He has broken my bones. 
He has built against me, and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship. 
He has made me dwell in dark places, as those who have been long dead. 
He has walled me about, so that I can't go out. He has made my chain heavy. 
Yes, when I cry, and call for help, he shuts out my prayer. 
He has walled up my ways with cut stone. He has made my paths crooked. 
He's to me as a bear lying in wait, as a lion in hiding. 
He has turned away my path, and pulled me in pieces. He has made me desolate. 
He has bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow. 
He has caused the shafts of his quiver to enter into my kidneys. 
I have become a derision to all my people, and their song all day long. 
He has filled me with bitterness. He has stuffed me with wormwood. 
He has also broken my teeth with gravel. He has covered me with ashes. 
You have removed my soul far away from peace. I forgot prosperity. 
I said, "My strength has perished, along with my expectation from God." 
Remember my affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the bitterness. 
My soul still remembers them, and is bowed down within me. 
This I recall to my mind; I have hope. 
It's because of God's loving kindnesses that we aren't consumed, because his mercies don't fail. 
They're new every morning. Great is your faithfulness. 
"God is my portion," says my soul. "So I'll hope in him." 
God is good to those who wait for him, to the soul who seeks him. 
It's good that a man should hope and quietly wait for the salvation of God. 
It's good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth. 
Let him sit alone and keep silence, because he has laid it on him. 
Let him put his mouth in the dust, if it's so that there can be hope. 
Let him give his cheek to him who strikes him. Let him be filled full of reproach. 
The Lord won't cast off for a lifetime. 
Though he causes grief, yet he'll have compassion according to the multitude of his loving kindnesses. 
He doesn't afflict willingly, nor grieve the children of men. 
To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth, 
to turn away the right of a man before the face of the Most High, 
to subvert a man in his cause, the Lord doesn't approve. 
Who's he who says, and it comes to pass, when the Lord doesn't command it? 
Doesn't bad and good come out of the mouth of the Most High? 
Why should a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his mistakes? 
Let's search and try our ways, and turn again to God. 
Let's lift up our heart with our hands to God in the sky. 
"We have transgressed and have rebelled. You haven't pardoned. 
"You have covered us with anger and pursued us. You have killed. You haven't pitied. 
You have covered yourself with a cloud, so that no prayer can pass through. 
You have made us an off-scouring and refuse in the middle of the peoples. 
"All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us. 
Terror and the pit have come on us, devastation and destruction." 
My eye runs down with streams of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people. 
My eye pours down and doesn't cease, without any intermission, 
until God looks down, and sees from the sky. 
My eye affects my soul, because of all the daughters of my city. 
They have chased me relentlessly like a bird, those who are my enemies without cause. 
They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and have cast a stone on me. 
Waters flowed over my head. I said, "I'm cut off." 
I called on your name, God, out of the lowest dungeon. 
You heard my voice: "Don't hide your ear from my sighing, and my cry." 
You came near in the day that I called on you. You said, "Don't be afraid." 
Lord, you have pleaded the causes of my soul. You have bought back my life. 
God, you have seen my wrong. Judge my cause. 
You have seen all their vengeance and all their plans against me. 
You have heard their reproach, God, and all their plans against me, 
the lips of those that rose up against me, and their plots against me all day long. 
You see their sitting down and their rising up. I'm their song. 
You'll pay them back, God, according to the work of their hands. 
You'll give them hardness of heart, your curse to them. 
You'll pursue them in anger, and destroy them from under the sky of God.
