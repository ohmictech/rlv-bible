God said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your family with you will carry the guilt that comes on the Holy Place, and you and your sons with you will carry the guilt that comes on your priesthood. 
Bring your brothers from the tribe of Levi, your father's tribe, to join you, and they will assist you and serve with you while you and your sons serve in front of the Tent of the Testimony. 
They are to do what you tell them and take care of the whole tent, but they are not to come near the utensils of the Holy Place or the altar, or they will die, and you with them. 
They are to join you and take responsibility for the Tent of Meeting, for all the work of the tent, and no outsider is to come near you. 
"You are to carry out the work of the Holy Place and the work of the altar, so that anger doesn't come on the Israelites again. 
I myself have taken your brothers the Levites from among the Israelites. They are given to you as a gift, set apart to God, to do the work of the Tent of Meeting. 
You and your sons with you are to carry out your priesthood for everything at the altar and for what is behind the curtain. That is your service. I give you the priesthood as a gift, and any outsider who comes near is to be put to death." 
God said to Aaron, "I have given you charge of the wave offerings brought to me, all the set apart gifts of the Israelites. I have given them to you and to your sons as your share for the eras, because you are anointed. 
This is yours from the most set apart things kept back from the fire: every offering of theirs, every gift offering, every offering for wrongdoing and every offering for guilt that they bring to me, will be most set apart for you and your sons. 
You are to eat it as something most set apart. Every male may eat it. It is set apart to you. 
"This is yours too: the wave offering of their gifts, all the wave offerings of the Israelites. I have given them to you, your sons and your daughters with you, as a share for the eras. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat it. 
"I have given you the best of the oil, the best of the new wine and of the grain, the firstfruits of what they give God. 
The first ripe fruit of everything in their land that they bring to God will be yours. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat it. 
"Everything given over to God in Israel will be yours. 
Everything that opens the womb, of any creature they bring to God, whether human or animal, will be yours. But you are to buy back the firstborn of humans and the firstborn of unclean animals. 
You are to have them bought back from a month old, at the price you set, five shekels of silver by the sanctuary standard, which is twenty gerahs. 
"But you are not to buy back the firstborn of a cow, a sheep or a goat. They are set apart. You are to splash their blood against the altar and burn their fat as an offering made by fire, a soothing aroma to God. 
Their meat will be yours, just as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh are yours. 
All the wave offerings of the set apart gifts the Israelites bring to God I have given to you, your sons and your daughters with you, as a share for the eras. It is a covenant of salt for the eras before God for you and your descendants with you." 
God said to Aaron, "You will have no land of your own among them and no share with them. I am your share and your land among the Israelites. 
"To the Levites I have given every tithe in Israel as their share, in return for the work they do, the work of the Tent of Meeting. 
From now on the Israelites are not to come near the Tent of Meeting, or they will carry guilt and die. 
The Levites are to do the work of the Tent of Meeting and carry that responsibility. This is to be a perpetual rule for all your generations. Among the Israelites they will have no share of land, 
because I have given the Levites the tithe the Israelites bring to God as a wave offering as their share. That is why I said of them, 'Among the Israelites they will have no share of land.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Levites and tell them, 'When you receive from the Israelites the tithe I have given you as your share, you are to lift up a wave offering from it to God, a tenth of the tithe. 
Your wave offering will count for you as though it were the grain from the threshing floor and the wine from the press. 
In this way you also are to lift up a wave offering to God from all the tithes you receive from the Israelites, and you are to give God's wave offering from it to Aaron the priest. 
From everything given to you, lift up every wave offering to God, the best part of it, the part that is set apart.' 
"Tell them, 'When you have lifted up the best part, it will count for the Levites as the produce of the threshing floor and the produce of the wine press. 
You and your households may eat it anywhere, because it is your pay for your work at the Tent of Meeting. 
You will not carry guilt because of it once you have lifted up the best part, and you will not make the set apart gifts of the Israelites unclean, so you won't die.'"
