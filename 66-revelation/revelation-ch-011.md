I was given a measuring rod like a staff, and someone said, "Get up and measure God's temple, and the altar, and those who bow down in it. 
Leave out the courtyard outside the temple and don't measure it, because it has been given to the nations. They will trample the holy city underfoot for forty-two months. 
I will give power to my two witnesses, and they will speak for God for one thousand two hundred sixty days, dressed in coarse cloth." 
These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands standing in front of the Lord of the land. 
If anyone wants to harm them, fire comes out of their mouths and burns up their enemies. If anyone wants to harm them, that is how they must be killed. 
These have the power to shut up the sky so that no rain falls during the days they speak for God. They also have power over the waters, to turn them into blood, and to strike the land with every kind of plague, as often as they want. 
When they have finished giving their witness, the beast that comes up out of the deep will make war on them, defeat them, and kill them. 
Their bodies will lie in the street of the great city, which in picture-language is called Sodom and Egypt, where their Lord was also crucified. 
For three and a half days, people from every tribe, language, and nation will stare at their bodies and will not allow them to be put in a tomb. 
Those who live on the land will celebrate over them and be glad. They will send each other gifts, because these two spokesmen had caused anguish to those who live on the land. 
But after the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered them, and they stood up on their feet. Great fear fell on everyone who saw them. 
Then I heard a loud voice from the sky saying to them, "Come up here!" And they went up into the sky in a cloud while their enemies watched. 
At that same hour there was a great earthquake, and a tenth of the city fell. Seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake, and the rest were terrified and gave weight to the God of the sky. 
The second how terrible has passed. Look, the third how terrible is coming soon. 
The seventh messenger sounded, and loud voices in the sky said, "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ, and he will reign through all the eras!" 
The twenty-four elders, who sit on their thrones in front of God's throne, fell on their faces and bowed down to God, 
saying, "We give you thanks, Lord God, the one who holds sway over all, the one who is and who was, because you have taken your great power and begun to reign. 
The nations were angry, and your heat came, and with it the time for the dead to be judged and for your slaves the spokesmen to be given their reward, along with the set-apart ones and all who honor your name, both small and great, and for those who destroy the land to be destroyed." 
Then God's temple in the sky was opened, and the chest of his binding arrangement was seen inside his temple. There were flashes of lightning, rumblings, peals of thunder, an earthquake, and heavy hail.
