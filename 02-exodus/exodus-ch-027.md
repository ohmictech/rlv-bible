"Make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide, a square, and three cubits high. 
Make horns at its four corners, all one piece with it, and cover it with bronze. 
Make its pots for the ashes, its shovels, its bowls, its meat forks and its fire pans, all of bronze. 
Make a grating of bronze netting for it, with four bronze rings at its four corners. 
Set it under the ledge of the altar, so the grating reaches halfway up the altar. 
Make poles of acacia wood for the altar and cover them with bronze. 
The poles go through the rings and rest on the two sides of the altar when it is carried. 
Make it hollow, out of boards. It is to be made just as it was shown you on the mountain. 
"Make the courtyard of the tent. For the south side there are to be curtains of fine twisted linen a hundred cubits long for one side, 
with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands on the posts. 
The same for the north side: curtains a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts, twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands on the posts. 
The west side of the courtyard is to be fifty cubits wide, with ten posts and ten bases. 
The east side is to be fifty cubits wide. 
On one side of the gate the curtains are to be fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 
On the other side the curtains are to be fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases. 
The gate of the courtyard is to be a screen twenty cubits long, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, embroidered work, with four posts and four bases. 
All the posts around the courtyard are to have silver bands, silver hooks and bronze bases. 
The courtyard is to be a hundred cubits long, fifty wide and five high, with curtains of fine twisted linen and bases of bronze. 
All the equipment of the tent used in its service, and all its pegs and the pegs of the courtyard, are to be of bronze. 
"Command the Israelites to bring you pure beaten olive oil for the light, so the lamp keeps burning. 
In the Tent of Meeting, outside the curtain that hangs in front of the covenant, Aaron and his sons are to keep the lamps burning from evening to morning before God. This is a rule for the eras, kept by the Israelites through all their generations.
