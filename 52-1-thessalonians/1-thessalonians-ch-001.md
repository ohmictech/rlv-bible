Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the gathering of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
We always give thanks to God for all of you, remembering you in our prayers, 
never forgetting, before our God and Father, your work of trust, your labor of love, and your staying power of expectation in our Lord Jesus Christ. 
We know, brothers and sisters truly loved by God, that you have been chosen, 
and that our Good News didn't come to you in words only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with deep conviction. You know what kind of people we proved to be among you, for your sake. 
You took us and the Lord as your example, receiving the word in the middle of much hard times, with joy from the Holy Spirit, 
so that you became a pattern for everyone in Macedonia and Achaia who trusts. 
From you the word of the Lord has rung out, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but everywhere your trust in God has become known, so that we don't need to say a thing. 
They themselves report what kind of welcome we had from you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve a living and true God, 
and to wait for his Son from the sky, whom he raised from the dead, Jesus, who rescues us from the heat that is coming.
