God says, "Where is the bill of your mother's divorce, with which I have put her away? Or to which of my creditors have I sold you? Look, you were sold for your guilt, and your mother was put away for your rebellion. 
Why, when I came, was there no one? When I called, why was there no one to answer? Is my hand shortened at all, that it can't buy back? Or have I no power to deliver? Look, at my rebuke I dry up the sea. I make the rivers a wilderness. Their fish stink because there's no water, and die of thirst. 
I clothe the sky with blackness. I make sackcloth their covering." 
The God has given me the tongue of those who are taught, that I may know how to sustain with words him who's weary. He awakens morning by morning, he awakens my ear to hear as those who are taught. 
The God has opened my ear. I wasn't rebellious. I haven't turned back. 
I gave my back to those who beat me, and my cheeks to those who plucked off the hair. I didn't hide my face from shame and spitting. 
God will help me. So I haven't been confounded. So I have set my face like a flint, and I know that I won't be disappointed. 
He who justifies me is near. Who will bring charges against me? Let's stand up together. Who's my adversary? Let him come near to me. 
Look, God will help me! Who's he who will condemn me? Look, they'll all grow old like a garment. The moths will eat them up. 
Who among you fears God and obeys the voice of his servant? He who walks in darkness and has no light, let him trust in God's name, and rely on his God. 
Look, all you who kindle a fire, who adorn yourselves with torches around yourselves, walk in the flame of your fire, and among the torches that you have kindled. You'll have this from my hand: you'll lie down in sorrow.
