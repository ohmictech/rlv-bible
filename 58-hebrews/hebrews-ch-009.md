The first arrangement had rules for worship and a place set apart here on earth. 
A tent was set up. In its first room were the lampstand, the table, and the bread set out before God, and this room is called the Holy Place. 
Behind the second curtain was the room called the Most Holy Place, 
which had a golden altar for incense and the chest of the arrangement, covered all over with gold. Inside it were a golden jar holding the manna, Aaron's rod that budded, and the stone tablets of the arrangement. 
Above it were the cherubim of weight, overshadowing the mercy seat. We cannot go into all of that now. 
With everything set up like this, the priests go into the first room regularly to do their service, 
but only the high priest goes into the second room, and only once a year, and never without blood, which he offers for his own mistakes and for the mistakes the people made in ignorance. 
The Holy Spirit was showing by this that the way into the Holy Place had not yet been opened up while the first tent was still standing. 
This is a picture for the present era. The gifts and sacrifices offered there cannot make the worshiper complete in their conscience, 
being only (with foods and drinks and various immersions) outward rules, put in place until the time when things would be set right. 
But Christ came as high priest of the good things that have come, and he went through the greater and more complete tent that is not made by hands, that is, not part of this creation. 
He did not enter by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood, and he entered the Holy Place once and for all, having won release of the era. 
If the blood of goats and bulls, and the ashes of a young cow sprinkled on those who have become unclean, set them apart so that their bodies are clean, 
how much more will the blood of Christ, who offered himself without any fault to God through the Spirit of the eras, make your conscience clean from dead deeds, so that you can serve the living God? 
That is why he is the mediator of a new binding arrangement: a death has taken place that brings release from the false steps committed under the first arrangement, so that those who are called may receive the promise of the charge handed down for the eras. 
Where there is a will, the one who made it must be shown to have died. 
A will only takes effect when someone has died; it never takes effect while the one who made it is still alive. 
That is why even the first arrangement was not put into effect without blood. 
When Moses had announced every command of the law to everyone, he took the blood of the calves and the goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, and sprinkled the scroll itself and everyone, 
saying, "This is the blood of the arrangement which God has commanded you to keep." 
In the same way he sprinkled the tent and all the vessels used in worship with the blood. 
Under the law, almost everything is made clean with blood, and without blood being poured out there is no release. 
So the copies of what is in the sky had to be made clean with these things, but the things in the sky themselves need better sacrifices than these. 
Christ did not enter a place set apart that was made by hands, which is only a picture of the real one. He entered the sky itself, and now he stands before God on our behalf. 
And he did not go in to offer himself over and over, the way the high priest enters the Holy Place year after year with blood that is not his own. 
If he had, he would have had to suffer many times since the world was founded. But as it is, he has appeared once, at the end of the eras, to do away with the drift by sacrificing himself. 
Just as people are appointed to die once, and after that comes the verdict, 
so Christ also, having been offered once to carry the mistakes of everyone, will appear a second time, not to deal with the drift, but to rescue those who are waiting for him with eager expectation.
