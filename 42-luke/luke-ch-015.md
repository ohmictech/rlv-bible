Now all the tax collectors and those who miss the mark were coming close to him to hear him. 
The Pharisees and the Bible teachers grumbled, saying, "This man welcomes those who miss the mark, and eats with them." 
He told them this story: 
"Which of you, if you had one hundred sheep and lost one of them, wouldn't leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness and go after the one that was lost, until he found it? 
When he has found it, he carries it on his shoulders, celebrating. 
When he comes home, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, 'Celebrate with me, for I have found my sheep which was lost!' 
I tell you that even so there will be more joy in the sky over one person who misses the mark and has a change of heart, than over ninety-nine upright people who need no change of heart. 
"Or what woman, if she had ten silver coins, if she lost one silver coin, wouldn't light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she found it? 
When she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, 'Celebrate with me, for I have found the silver coin which I had lost!' 
Even so, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the messengers of God over one person who misses the mark and has a change of heart." 
He said, "A certain man had two sons. 
The younger of them said to his father, 'Father, give me my share of your property.' So he divided his livelihood between them. 
Not many days after, the younger son gathered all of this together and traveled into a far country. There he wasted his property with wild living. 
When he had spent all of it, there arose a severe famine in that country, and he began to be in need. 
He went and joined himself to one of the citizens of that country, and he sent him into his fields to feed pigs. 
He wanted to fill his stomach with the pods the pigs were eating, but no one gave him any. 
But when he came to himself, he said, 'How many hired hands of my father's have bread enough to spare, and I'm dying with hunger! 
I will get up and go to my father, and will tell him, "Father, I have missed the mark against the sky and in your sight. 
I am no more worthy to be called your son. Make me as one of your hired hands."' 
"He arose and came to his father. But while he was still far off, his father saw him and was moved with deep compassion, and ran to him, threw his arms around him, and kissed him. 
The son said to him, 'Father, I have missed the mark against the sky and in your sight. I am no longer worthy to be called your son.' 
"But the father said to his slaves, 'Bring out the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet. 
Bring the fattened calf, kill it, and let's eat and celebrate; 
for this, my son, was dead and is alive again. He was lost and is found.' Then they began to celebrate. 
"Now his elder son was in the field. As he came near to the house, he heard music and dancing. 
He called one of the slaves to him and asked what was going on. 
He said to him, 'Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fattened calf, because he has received him back safe and healthy.' 
But he was angry and would not go in. Therefore his father came out and begged him. 
But he answered his father, 'Look, these many years I have served you, and I never disobeyed a commandment of yours, but you never gave me a goat, that I might celebrate with my friends. 
But when this your son came, who has devoured your living with prostitutes, you killed the fattened calf for him.' 
"He said to him, 'Son, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours. 
But it was appropriate to celebrate and be glad, for this, your brother, was dead, and is alive again. He was lost, and is found.'"
