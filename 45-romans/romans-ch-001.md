Paul, a slave of Jesus Christ, called to be a sent one, set apart for the Good News of God, 
which he promised before through his spokesmen in the set-apart Scriptures, 
concerning his Son, who was born of David's offspring according to the flesh, 
who was declared to be the Son of God with power according to the Holy Spirit, by the rising from the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord, 
who we received favor and were sent out through, for the obedience that comes from trust among all the nations for his name's sake; 
you're also called to belong to Jesus Christ as a part of them; 
to everyone who's in Rome, truly loved by God, called to be set-apart ones: Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, that your trust is announced throughout the whole world. 
God is my witness, who I serve in my spirit in the Good News of his Son, how I never stop mentioning you in my prayers, 
always asking that somehow, by the will of God, I can finally find my way to you. 
I long to see you, so that I can share with you some gift of the Spirit, to make you strong; 
that we can be encouraged together, you and I, by each other's trust. 
Now I don't want you to be unaware, brothers, that I often planned to come to you (and was prevented until now), so that I could have some fruit among you too, just as among the rest of the nations. 
I'm a debtor both to Greeks and to foreigners, both to the wise and to the foolish. 
So, for my part, I'm eager to announce the Good News to you also who are in Rome. 
I'm not ashamed of the Good News of Christ, because it's the power of God for rescue for everyone who trusts, for the Jewish people first, and also for the Greek. 
In it God's rightness is unveiled from trust to trust. As it's written, "But the one who's right will live by trust." 
The heat of God is unveiled from the sky against all disregard for God and all the wrong of people who hold the truth down by their wrong, 
because what can be known about God is plain to them, for God made it plain to them. 
His unseen nature has clearly been seen since the world was made, understood from the things he made, even his never-ending power and his divine nature, so that they have no excuse. 
Because, knowing God, they didn't give him weight as God or give thanks, but became empty in their thinking, and their heart, understanding nothing, grew dark. 
Claiming to be wise, they became fools, 
and traded the weight of the undecaying God for images made to look like a decaying human being, and like birds, four-footed animals, and crawling things. 
So God handed them over, in the cravings of their hearts, to uncleanness, so that their bodies were dishonored among themselves; 
They traded the truth of God for a lie, and worshiped and served the creation rather than its Creator, who's praised for all the eras. Amen. 
That's why God handed them over to dishonoring passions. Their women traded the natural function for what's against nature. 
In the same way the men also left the natural function of the woman and burned in their craving for one another. Men did shameful things with men, and received in themselves the payback their wrongdoing deserved. 
And just as they didn't think it worth holding God in their knowledge, God handed them over to a mind that fails the test, to do things that shouldn't be done; 
being filled with every kind of wrong, sleeping around, malice, greed, ill will; full of envy, murder, quarreling, deceit, and spite, 
slanderers, haters of God, insolent, arrogant, boastful, inventors of bad things, disobedient to parents, 
without understanding, breaking their word, without family love, unforgiving, without mercy; 
who, knowing God's right ruling that those who do such things deserve death, not only do them but also approve of those who do them.
