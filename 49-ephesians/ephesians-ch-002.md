You were made alive when you were dead in your false steps and mistakes, 
in which you once lived, following the way this world's era runs, following the ruler of the power of the air, the spirit that is at work now in those who refuse to listen. 
We all once lived among them, driven by the cravings of our flesh, doing what our flesh and our mind wanted, and by nature we were children of heat, just like everyone else. 
But God, rich in mercy, because of the great love he truly had for us, 
made us alive together with Christ even when we were dead in our false steps, you have been rescued by favor, 
and raised us up with him, and seated us with him in the sky in Christ Jesus, 
so that in the eras to come he could show how far beyond all measure the riches of his gift are, in his kindness toward us in Christ Jesus. 
For by favor you have been rescued through trust, and this is not from yourselves; it is God's gift, 
not by deeds, so that no one can boast. 
For we are what he made, created in Christ Jesus for good deeds, which God prepared ahead of time so that we would live in them. 
So remember that you, the nations by birth, the ones called "the uncircumcised" by people who call themselves "the circumcised," a cutting made by hands in the flesh, 
remember that you were at that time separate from Christ, outside the nation of Israel, and strangers to the binding arrangements of the promise, with no expectation and no God in the world. 
But now, in Christ Jesus, you who were once far away have been brought near by the blood of Christ. 
For he himself is our wholeness. He made the two into one and tore down the wall in the middle that separated us, 
putting the hostility out of work in his own flesh, the law of commands with its rules, so that he could make one new person out of the two, in himself, making wholeness, 
and so that he could make them both friends with God in one body through the cross, having killed the hostility by it. 
He came and announced wholeness to you who were far away and to those who were near. 
For through him we both have a way in to the Father by one Spirit. 
So then you are no longer strangers and outsiders; you are fellow citizens with the set-apart ones, and members of God's household, 
built on the foundation of the sent ones and the spokesmen, with Christ Jesus himself as the cornerstone; 
in whom the whole building, fitted together, grows into a set-apart temple in the Lord; 
in whom you also are being built together into a place where God lives by the Spirit.
