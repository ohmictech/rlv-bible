I want you to know how hard I'm struggling for you, and for those at Laodicea, and for everyone who has never seen me face to face. 
I want their hearts to be encouraged, knit together in love, and to reach all the riches of complete confidence in understanding, so that they come to know the secret of God, both the Father's and Christ's. 
In him are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowing. 
I'm saying this so that no one talks you around with slick arguments. 
Though I'm away from you in body, I'm with you in spirit, celebrating as I see your good order and the firmness of your trust in Christ. 
So then, just as you received Christ Jesus as Lord, keep living in him, 
rooted and built up in him, made firm in the trust just as you were taught, and overflowing with thanksgiving. 
Watch out that no one carries you off with philosophy and empty trickery, following what people have handed down, following the basic principles of this world, and not following Christ. 
because in him the whole fullness of God himself lives in a body. 
And in him you're complete, and he's the head over every ruling power and authority. 
In him you were also circumcised with a circumcision not made by hands, in the stripping off of the body of the flesh and its mistakes, in the circumcision of Christ, 
You were buried with him in immersion, and in it you were also raised with him through trust in the working of God, who raised him from the dead. 
You were dead in your false steps and in your uncircumcised flesh, and he made you alive together with him, having let's off all our false steps. 
He wiped out the record of charges with its rules that stood against us. He took it out of the way and nailed it to the cross. 
Having stripped the ruling powers and the authorities, he put them on public display and led them in a victory parade in it. 
So don't let anyone pass a verdict on you about food or drink, or about a festival or a new moon or a Sabbath day. 
These are only a shadow of what was coming, and the substance belongs to Christ. 
Don't let anyone cheat you out of your prize, taking his stand on false humility and the worship of messengers, going on about what he claims to have seen, puffed up with empty pride by his fleshly mind, 
and not holding on to the Head. From him the whole body, supplied and knit together through its joints and ligaments, grows the way God makes it grow. 
If you died with Christ and are done with the basic principles of this world, why do you live as though you still belonged to the world, and put yourselves under rules like 
"Don't handle this, don't taste that, don't touch the other" 
all of which are used up and gone, rules and teachings that come from people? 
These things look like wisdom, with their self-made religion, their false humility, and their harsh treatment of the body, but they're worth nothing against the flesh's cravings.
