Don't sacrifice to your God an ox or a sheep in which is a defect or anything bad; for that's an abomination to your God. 
If there's found among you, within any of your gates which your God gives you, a man or woman who does that which is bad in your God's sight in transgressing his covenant, 
and has gone and served other gods and worshiped them, or the sun, or the moon, or any of the stars of the sky, which I haven't commanded, 
and you're told, and you have heard of it, then you will inquire diligently. Look, if it's true, and the thing certain, that such abomination is done in Israel, 
then you will bring out that man or that woman who has done this bad thing to your gates, even that same man or woman; and you will stone them to death with stones. 
At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, he who's to die will be put to death. At the mouth of one witness he won't be put to death. 
The hands of the witnesses will be first on him to put him to death, and afterward the hands of everyone. So you will remove the bad from among you. 
If there arises a matter too hard for you in judgment, between blood and blood, between plea and plea, and between stroke and stroke, being matters of controversy within your gates, then you will arise, and go up to the place which your God chooses. 
You will come to the priests who are Levites and to the judge who will be in those days. You will inquire, and they will give you the verdict. 
You will do according to the decisions of the verdict which they will give you from that place which God chooses. You will observe to do according to all that they will teach you. 
According to the decisions of the law which they will teach you, and according to the judgment which they will tell you, you will do. You won't turn away from the sentence which they announce to you, to the right hand, nor to the left. 
The man who does presumptuously in not listening to the priest who stands to minister there before your God, or to the judge, even that man will die. You will put away the bad from Israel. 
Everyone will hear and fear, and do no more presumptuously. 
When you have come to the land which your God gives you, and possess it and dwell in it, and say, "I'll set a king over me, like all the nations that are around me," 
you will surely set him whom your God chooses as king over yourselves. You will set as king over you one from among your brothers. You may not put a foreigner over you, who isn't your brother. 
Only he won't multiply horses to himself, nor cause the people to return to Egypt, to the end that he may multiply horses; because God has said to you, "You won't go back that way again." 
He won't multiply wives to himself, that his heart not turn away. He won't greatly multiply to himself silver and gold. 
It will be, when he sits on the throne of his kingdom, that he will write himself a copy of this law in a book, out of that which is before the Levitical priests. 
It will be with him, and he will read from it all the days of his life, that he may learn to fear his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them; 
that his heart not be lifted up above his brothers, and that he not turn away from the commandment to the right hand, or to the left, to the end that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he and his children, in the middle of Israel.
