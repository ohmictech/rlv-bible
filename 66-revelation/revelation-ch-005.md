Then I saw, in the right hand of the one sitting on the throne, a scroll written on both sides and sealed shut with seven seals. 
And I saw a mighty messenger calling out in a loud voice, "Who's worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?" 
But no one in the sky above, or on the earth, or under the earth was able to open the scroll or even look inside it. 
I wept and wept, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look inside it. 
Then one of the elders said to me, "Don't weep. Look, the Lion from the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has won the victory, and he can open the scroll and its seven seals." 
Then I saw, standing between the throne and the four living creatures and among the elders, a Lamb that looked as though it had been killed. It had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent out into all the land. 
He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the one sitting on the throne. 
When he had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down in front of the Lamb. Each of them had a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the set-apart ones. 
And they sang a new song: "You're worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, because you were killed, and with your blood you bought people for God out of every tribe, language, people, and nation. 
You made us a kingdom and priests to serve our God, and we'll reign on the land." 
Then I looked and heard the voice of many messengers around the throne, and of the living creatures and the elders. There were ten thousands of ten thousands of them, and thousands of thousands, 
and they said in a loud voice, "The Lamb who was killed is worthy to receive power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, weight, and blessing!" 
And I heard every created thing in the sky, on the land, under the land, on the sea, and everything in them, saying, "To the one who sits on the throne and to the Lamb be blessing, honor, weight, and power for all the eras! Amen!" 
The four living creatures said, "Amen!" And the elders fell down and bowed low.
