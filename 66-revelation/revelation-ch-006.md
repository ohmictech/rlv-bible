I saw that the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying, in a voice like thunder, "Come!" 
Then a white horse appeared, and the one sitting on it had a bow. A wreath was given to him, and he rode out winning the victory, and to win it. 
When he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, "Come!" 
Another horse came out, a red one. The one sitting on it was given power to take wholeness from the land, so that people would kill one another, and a great sword was given to him. 
When he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, "Come!" And look, a black horse, and the one sitting on it had a pair of scales in his hand. 
I heard a voice in the middle of the four living creatures saying, "A quart of wheat for a silver coin, and three quarts of barley for a silver coin! And don't damage the oil and the wine!" 
When he opened the fourth seal, I heard the fourth living creature saying, "Come!" 
And look, a pale horse, and the name of the one sitting on it was Death, and Hades followed behind him. Authority over a fourth of the land was given to them, to kill with the sword, with famine, with death, and by the wild animals of the land. 
When he opened the fifth seal, I saw underneath the altar the lives of those who had been killed for God's message and for the witness they held to the Lamb. 
They cried out in a loud voice, saying, "How long, Master, you who are set apart and true, until you pass a verdict and bring payback for our blood on those who live on the land?" 
A long white robe was given to each of them, and they were told to rest a little longer, until the full number was reached of their fellow slaves and their brothers and sisters, who were going to be killed just as they had been. 
I watched as he opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake. The sun turned black like coarse cloth, and the whole moon became like blood. 
The stars of the sky fell to the land, like a fig tree dropping its unripe figs when it's shaken by a great wind. 
The sky was rolled up like a scroll. Every mountain and island was moved out of its place. 
The kings of the land, the great ones, the commanding officers, the rich, the strong, and every slave and every free person hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains. 
They said to the mountains and the rocks, "Fall on us and hide us from the face of the one who sits on the throne, and from the heat of the Lamb, 
because the great day of their heat has come, and who's able to stand?"
