God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Look, I have called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah. 
I have filled him with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all kinds of workmanship, 
to devise skillful works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in bronze, 
and in cutting of stones for setting, and in carving of wood, to work in all kinds of workmanship. 
Look, I myself have appointed with him Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and in the heart of everyone who's wise-hearted I have put wisdom, that they can make all that I have commanded you: 
the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the covenant, the mercy seat that's on it, all the furniture of the Tent, 
the table and its vessels, the pure lamp stand with all its vessels, the altar of incense, 
the altar of burnt offering with all its vessels, the bamistake and its base, 
the finely worked garments, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, the garments of his sons to minister in the priest's office 
the anointing oil, and the incense of sweet spices for the holy place: according to all that I have commanded you they will do." 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak also to the children of Israel, saying, 'Most certainly you will keep my Sabbaths; for it's a sign between me and you throughout your generations, that you can know that I'm God who sanctifies you. 
You will keep the Sabbath, for it's holy to you. Everyone who profanes it will surely be put to death; for whoever does any work therein, that soul will be cut off from among his people. 
Six days will work be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, holy to God. Whoever does any work on the Sabbath day will surely be put to death. 
So the children of Israel will keep the Sabbath, to observe the Sabbath throughout their generations, for a perpetual covenant. 
It's a sign between me and the children of Israel for the eras; for in six days God made the sky and the earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed.'" 
When he finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, he gave Moses the two tablets of the covenant, stone tablets, written with God's finger.
