In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from God, saying, 
God says to me: "Make bonds and bars, and put them on your neck. 
Then send them to the king of Edom, to the king of Moab, to the king of the children of Ammon, to the king of Tyre, and to the king of Sidon, by the hand of the messengers who come to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah. 
Give them a command to their masters, saying, 'The God of Armies, the God of Israel says, "You will tell your masters: 
'I have made the earth, the men, and the animals that are on the surface of the earth by my great power and by my outstretched arm. I give it to whom it seems right to me. 
Now I have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, my servant. I have also given the animals of the field to him to serve him. 
All the nations will serve him, his son, and his son's son, until the time of his own land comes. Then many nations and great kings will make him their bondservant. 
"'"'It'll happen that I'll punish the nation and the kingdom which won't serve the same Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and that won't put their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon,' says God, 'with the sword, with famine, and with pestilence, until I have consumed them by his hand. 
But as for you, don't listen to your spokesmen, to your diviners, to your dreams, to your soothsayers, or to your sorcerers, who speak to you, saying, "You won't serve the king of Babylon;" 
They prophesy a lie to you, to remove you far from your land, so that I'd drive you out, and you'd perish. 
But the nation that brings their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, that nation I'll let remain in their own land,' says God; 'and they'll till it and dwell in it.'"'" 
I spoke to Zedekiah king of Judah according to all these words, saying, "Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him and his people, and live. 
Why will you die, you and your people, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, as God has spoken concerning the nation that won't serve the king of Babylon? 
Don't listen to the words of the spokesmen who speak to you, saying, 'You won't serve the king of Babylon;' for they prophesy a lie to you. 
I haven't sent them," says God, "but they prophesy falsely in my name; that I can drive you out, and that you can perish, you, and the spokesmen who prophesy to you." 
Also I spoke to the priests and to all this people, saying, God says, "Don't listen to the words of your spokesmen who prophesy to you, saying, 'Look, the vessels of God's house will now shortly be brought again from Babylon;' for they prophesy a lie to you. 
Don't listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon, and live. Why should this city become a desolation? 
But if they're spokesmen, and if God's word is with them, let them now make intercession to the God of Armies, that the vessels which are left in God's house, in the house of the king of Judah, and at Jerusalem, don't go to Babylon. 
The God of Armies says concerning the pillars, concerning the sea, concerning the bases, and concerning the rest of the vessels that are left in this city, 
which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon didn't take when he carried away captive Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, from Jerusalem to Babylon, and all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem 
yes, the God of Armies, the God of Israel says concerning the vessels that are left in God's house, and in the house of the king of Judah, and at Jerusalem: 
'They'll be carried to Babylon, and there they'll be, until the day that I visit them,' says God; 'then I'll bring them up, and restore them to this place.'"
