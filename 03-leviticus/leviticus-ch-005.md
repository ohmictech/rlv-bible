"'If anyone drifts, in that he hears a public adjuration to testify, he being a witness, whether he has seen or known, if he doesn't report it, then he shall bear his guilt. 
"'Or if anyone touches any unclean thing, whether it's the carcass of an unclean animal, or the carcass of unclean livestock, or the carcass of unclean creeping things, and it's hidden from him, and he's unclean, then he shall be guilty. 
"'Or if he touches the uncleanness of man, whatever his uncleanness is with which he's unclean, and it's hidden from him; when he knows of it, then he shall be guilty. 
"'Or if anyone swears rashly with his lips to do bad things or to do good, whatever it's that a man might utter rashly with an oath, and it's hidden from him, when he knows of it, then he'll be guilty of one of these. 
It shall be, when he's guilty of one of these, he shall confess that in which he has drifted; 
and he shall bring his trespass offering to God for the wrong he has done: a female from the flock, a lamb or a goat, for an offering for wrongdoing; and the priest shall make things right for him concerning his wrongdoing. 
"'If he can't afford a lamb, then he shall bring his trespass offering for that in which he has drifted, two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, to God; one for an offering for wrongdoing, and the other for a burnt offering. 
He shall bring them to the priest, who shall first offer the one which is for the offering for wrongdoing. He shall wring off its head from its neck, but shall not sever it completely. 
He shall sprinkle some of the blood of the offering for wrongdoing on the side of the altar; and the rest of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar. It's an offering for wrongdoing. 
He shall offer the second for a burnt offering, according to the ordinance; and the priest shall make things right for him concerning the wrong he has done, and he shall be forgiven. 
"'But if he can't afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring as his offering for that in which he has drifted, one tenth of an ephah of fine flour for an offering for wrongdoing. He shall put no oil on it, and he shall not put any frankincense on it, for it's an offering for wrongdoing. 
He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it as the memorial portion, and burn it on the altar, on the offerings of God made by fire. It's an offering for wrongdoing. 
The priest shall make things right for him concerning the wrong he has done in any of these things, and he'll be forgiven; and the rest shall be the priest's, as the meal offering.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"If anyone commits a trespass, and drifts unwittingly regarding God's holy things, then he shall bring his trespass offering to God: a ram without defect from the flock, according to your estimation in silver by shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass offering. 
He shall make restitution for that which he has done wrong regarding the holy thing, and shall add a fifth part to it, and give it to the priest; and the priest shall make things right for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and he'll be forgiven. 
"If anyone drifts, doing any of the things which God has commanded not to be done, though he didn't know it, he's still guilty, and shall bear his guilt. 
He shall bring a ram without defect from of the flock, according to your estimation, for a trespass offering, to the priest; and the priest shall make things right for him concerning the thing in which he drifted and didn't know it, and he'll be forgiven. 
It's a trespass offering. He's certainly guilty before God."
