"These are the rulings you are to set before them: 
"If you buy a Hebrew slave, he is to work six years, and in the seventh year he goes free without paying anything. 
If he came in alone, he goes out alone. If he was married, his wife goes out with him. 
If his master gave him a wife and she bore him sons or daughters, the wife and her children belong to her master, and he goes out alone. 
But if the slave says plainly, 'I love my master, my wife and my children. I won't go free,' 
then his master is to bring him before God and take him to the door or the doorpost, and his master will pierce his ear with an awl, and he will serve him for the eras. 
"If a man sells his daughter as a female slave, she doesn't go free the way male slaves do. 
If she doesn't please the master who took her as his wife, he must let her be bought back. He has no right to sell her to foreigners, because he has broken faith with her. 
If he gives her to his son, he is to treat her as a daughter. 
If he takes another wife, he must not cut back her food, her clothing or her marital rights. 
If he doesn't do these three things for her, she goes free without paying any money. 
"Anyone who strikes a man so that he dies must be put to death, 
but if it wasn't deliberate and God let it happen, I will give you a place he can run to. 
But if a man plans it and kills his neighbor on purpose, take him even from my altar and put him to death. 
"Anyone who attacks his father or mother must be put to death. 
"Anyone who kidnaps a person, whether he sells them or they are found in his hand, must be put to death. 
"Anyone who curses his father or mother must be put to death. 
"If men quarrel and one hits the other with a stone or with his fist, and the man doesn't die but has to stay in bed, 
then if he gets up and walks around outside on his staff, the one who hit him is not guilty. He must still pay for the time the man lost and see that he is properly healed. 
"If a man beats his male or female slave with a stick and the slave dies on the spot, he must be punished. 
But if the slave gets up after a day or two, he won't be punished, because the slave is his property. 
"If men are fighting and hit a pregnant woman so that her child comes out, but nothing worse happens, the man responsible must pay whatever the woman's husband demands and the judges agree to. 
But if something worse happens, you must take life for life, 
eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot, 
burn for burn, wound for wound, bruise for bruise. 
"If a man hits his male or female slave in the eye and ruins it, he must let the slave go free to pay for the eye. 
If he knocks out a tooth of his male or female slave, he must let the slave go free to pay for the tooth. 
"If an ox gores a man or a woman to death, the ox must be stoned and its meat not eaten, but the owner of the ox is not held responsible. 
But if the ox had a habit of goring, and the owner was warned and did not keep it in, and it kills a man or a woman, the ox must be stoned and the owner must also be put to death. 
If a ransom is demanded of him, he may buy back his life by paying whatever is demanded. 
Whether it gored a son or a daughter, the same rule applies. 
If the ox gores a male or female slave, thirty shekels of silver must be paid to their master, and the ox must be stoned. 
"If a man opens a pit, or digs a pit and doesn't cover it, and an ox or a donkey falls into it, 
the owner of the pit must make it good. He must pay the owner of the animal, and the dead animal becomes his. 
"If one man's ox hurts another man's so that it dies, they are to sell the live ox and split the money, and split the dead animal too. 
But if it was known that the ox had gored before and the owner did not keep it in, he must pay ox for ox, and the dead animal becomes his own.
