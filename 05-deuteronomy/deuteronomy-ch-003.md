Then we turned and went up the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out against us with all his people to fight at Edrei. 
God said to me, "Don't be afraid of him, because I have handed him over to you with all his people and his land. Do to him what you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon." 
So our God also handed Og king of Bashan and all his people over to us, and we struck him down until no one was left. 
At that time we captured all his cities. There wasn't a town we didn't take from them, sixty cities, the whole region of Argob, Og's kingdom in Bashan. 
All of them were towns with high walls, gates and bars, as well as a great many unwalled villages. 
We completely destroyed them, just as we did to Sihon king of Heshbon, destroying every town with its women and children. 
But all the livestock and everything worth taking from the towns we kept for ourselves. 
At that time we took the land from the two Amorite kings across the Jordan, from the Arnon valley to Mount Hermon. 
(The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir.) 
We took all the towns of the plateau, all Gilead and all Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei, towns of Og's kingdom in Bashan. 
(Only Og king of Bashan was left of the last of the Rephaim. His bed was made of iron. Isn't it in Rabbah of the Ammonites? It is nine cubits long and four cubits wide, by the standard cubit.) 
Of that land we took at that time, I gave the Reubenites and the Gadites the part from Aroer by the Arnon valley, and half the hill country of Gilead with its towns. 
The rest of Gilead and all Bashan, Og's kingdom, I gave to the half tribe of Manasseh, the whole region of Argob, which is all Bashan and was called the land of the Rephaim. 
Jair son of Manasseh took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and named it after himself, Havvoth Jair, which is its name to this day. 
I gave Gilead to Machir. 
To the Reubenites and the Gadites I gave the land from Gilead to the Arnon valley, with the middle of the valley as the border, and on to the Jabbok river, which is the border of the Ammonites, 
along with the Arabah and the Jordan as its border, from Chinnereth down to the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, below the slopes of Pisgah on the east. 
At that time I gave you this order: "Your God has given you this land to take as your own. All your fighting men are to cross over armed ahead of your brothers, the Israelites. 
But your wives, your children and your livestock, since I know you have a lot of livestock, will stay in the towns I have given you, 
until God gives rest to your brothers as he has to you, and they also take the land your God is giving them across the Jordan. Then each of you may go back to the property I have given you." 
I gave Joshua this order at that time: "Your eyes have seen everything your God has done to these two kings. God will do the same to all the kingdoms you are going into. 
Don't be afraid of them, because your God himself fights for you." 
At that time I pleaded with God: 
"God, you have begun to show your servant your greatness and your strong hand. What god is there in the sky or on the earth who can do the works and mighty acts you do? 
Please let me cross over and see the good land beyond the Jordan, that fine hill country and Lebanon." 
But God was angry with me because of you and wouldn't listen. God said to me, "That's enough. Don't say another word to me about this. 
Go up to the top of Pisgah and look west, north, south and east, and see it with your own eyes, because you will not cross this Jordan. 
But commission Joshua, and encourage him and strengthen him, because he will cross over ahead of this people and will lead them into the land you are going to see." 
So we stayed in the valley near Beth Peor.
