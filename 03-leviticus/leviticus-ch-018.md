God said to Moses, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, 'I'm your God. 
Don't do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived. Don't do as they do in the land of Canaan, where I am bringing you. Don't follow their statutes. 
You will do my ordinances. You will keep my statutes and walk in them. I'm your God. 
You will keep my statutes and my ordinances, which if a man does, he will live in them. I'm God. 
"'None of you will approach any close relatives, to uncover their nakedness: I'm God. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your father, nor the nakedness of your mother: she's your mother. Don't uncover her nakedness. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your father's wife. It's your father's nakedness. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your sister, the daughter of your father, or the daughter of your mother, whether born at home or born abroad. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your son's daughter, or of your daughter's daughter, even their nakedness; for theirs is your own nakedness. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your father's wife's daughter, conceived by your father, since she's your sister. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your father's sister. She's your father's near kinswoman. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister, for she's your mother's near kinswoman. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your father's brother. Don't approach his wife. She's your aunt. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law. She's your son's wife. Don't uncover her nakedness. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife. It's your brother's nakedness. 
"'Don't uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter. Don't take her son's daughter, or her daughter's daughter, to uncover her nakedness. They're near kinswomen. It's guilt. 
"'Don't take a wife in addition to her sister, to be a rival, to uncover her nakedness, while her sister is still alive. 
"'Don't approach a woman to uncover her nakedness, as long as she's impure by her uncleanness. 
"'Don't lie carnally with your neighbor's wife, and defile yourself with her. 
"'Don't give any of your children as a sacrifice to Molech. Don't profane the name of your God. I'm God. 
"'Don't lie with a man as with a woman. That's detestable. 
"'Don't lie with any animal to defile yourself with it. No woman may give herself to an animal, to lie down with it: it's a perversion. 
"'Don't defile yourselves in any of these things; for in all these the nations which I'm casting out before you were defiled. 
The land was defiled. So I punished its guilt, and the land vomited out her inhabitants. 
You will keep my statutes and my ordinances, and won't do any of these abominations; neither the native-born, nor the stranger who lives as a foreigner among you 
(for the men of the land that were before you had done all these abominations, and the land became defiled), 
that the land not vomit you out also, when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you. 
"'Whoever will do any of these abominations, even the souls that do them will be cut off from among their people. 
So you will keep my requirements, that you don't practice any of these abominable customs which were practiced before you, and that you don't defile yourselves with them. I'm your God.'"
