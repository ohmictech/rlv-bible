"'When anyone brings a gift offering to God, his offering is to be of fine flour. He is to pour oil on it and put frankincense on it. 
He is to bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests, and one of them is to take a handful of the flour and the oil, with all the frankincense, and the priest is to burn the memorial portion on the altar, an offering made by fire, a soothing aroma to God. 
What is left of the gift offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of God's offerings made by fire. 
"'When you bring a gift offering baked in an oven, it is to be cakes of fine flour mixed with oil and made without yeast, or wafers made without yeast with oil poured over them. 
If your offering is a gift offering cooked on a griddle, it is to be fine flour mixed with oil and made without yeast. 
Break it into pieces and pour oil on it. It is a gift offering. 
If your offering is a gift offering cooked in a pan, it is to be made of fine flour with oil. 
Bring the gift offering made in any of these ways to God, give it to the priest, and he will bring it to the altar. 
The priest is to take the memorial portion from the gift offering and burn it on the altar, an offering made by fire, a soothing aroma to God. 
What is left of the gift offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of God's offerings made by fire. 
"'No gift offering you bring to God is to be made with yeast, because you are never to burn yeast or honey as an offering made by fire to God. 
You may bring them to God as an offering of firstfruits, but they are not to go up on the altar as a soothing aroma. 
Season every gift offering you bring with salt. Never leave the salt of your God's covenant out of your gift offering. Bring salt with every offering you make. 
"'If you bring a gift offering of firstfruits to God, bring new grain, roasted in the fire and crushed, as the gift offering of your firstfruits. 
Put oil on it and lay frankincense on it. It is a gift offering. 
The priest is to burn the memorial portion of the crushed grain and the oil, along with all the frankincense, as an offering made by fire to God.
