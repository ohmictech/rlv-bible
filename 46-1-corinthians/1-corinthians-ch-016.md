Now about the collection for the set-apart ones: do as I told the gatherings of Galatia. 
On the first day of every week, let each one of you set something aside as he's able, so that no collections are made when I come. 
When I arrive, I'll send the people you approve, with letters, to carry your gift to Jerusalem. 
If it's appropriate for me to go also, they'll go with me. 
I'll come to you when I have passed through Macedonia, for I'm passing through Macedonia. 
But I can stay with you, or even spend the winter with you, so that you can send me on my way wherever I go. 
I don't want to see you now just in passing, but I hope to stay a while with you, if the Lord permits. 
But I'll stay at Ephesus until Pentecost, 
for a great and effective door has opened to me, and there are many adversaries. 
Now if Timothy comes, see that he's with you without fear, for he does the work of the Lord, as I also do. 
So let no one look down on him. Send him on his way in wholeness, so that he can come to me; for I'm expecting him with the brothers. 
Now concerning Apollos the brother, I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but it wasn't at all his desire to come now; but he'll come when he has an opportunity. 
Stay awake! Stand firm in the trust! Be brave! Be strong! 
Let all that you do be done in love. 
Now I beg you, brothers, you know the household of Stephanas, that it's the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have set themselves to serve the set-apart ones, 
that you also be subject to people like that, and to everyone who helps in the work and works hard. 
I celebrate at the coming of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus; for that which was lacking on your part, they supplied. 
They refreshed my spirit and yours. So acknowledge those who are like that. 
The gatherings of Asia greet you. Aquila and Priscilla greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the gathering that meets in their house. 
All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a set-apart kiss. 
This greeting is by me, Paul, with my own hand. 
If anyone doesn't love the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be given over. Come, Lord! 
The favor of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you. 
My love to all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.
