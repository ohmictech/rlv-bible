My truly loved friends, do not trust every spirit. Test the spirits to see whether they are from God, because many false spokesmen have gone out into the world. 
This is how you recognize the Spirit of God: every spirit that openly declares that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God, 
and every spirit that does not declare that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is not from God. That is the spirit of the one against Christ. You heard that it was coming, and now it is in the world already. 
You are from God, dear children, and you have defeated them, because the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world. 
They belong to the world, so they speak the way the world speaks, and the world listens to them. 
We belong to God. Whoever knows God listens to us; whoever does not belong to God does not listen to us. This is how we recognize the spirit of truth and the spirit of error. 
My truly loved friends, let us love one another, because love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. 
Whoever does not love does not know God, because God is love. 
This is how God's love appeared among us: God sent his only Son into the world so that we might have real life through him. 
This is what love is: not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son as the sacrifice that makes things right for our mistakes. 
My truly loved friends, if God loved us like that, we should love one another too. 
No one has ever seen God, but if we love one another, God makes his home in us, and his love has been made complete in us. 
This is how we know that we make our home in him and he in us: he has given us his Spirit. 
We have seen and we bear witness that the Father has sent the Son to be the Restorer of the world. 
Whoever openly declares that Jesus is the Son of God, God makes his home in them, and they in God. 
We know the love God has for us, and we have put our trust in it. God is love, and whoever makes their home in love makes their home in God, and God makes his home in them. 
This is how love has been made complete among us, so that we may have boldness on the day of the verdict: in this world we are just as he is. 
There is no fear in love. Complete love drives out fear, because fear has to do with correction. Whoever is afraid has not been made complete in love. 
We love, because he loved us first. 
If anyone says, "I love God," and hates a brother or sister, they are a liar. Anyone who does not love the brother or sister they have seen cannot love God, whom they have not seen. 
And this is the command we have from him: whoever loves God must also love their brother or sister.
