In those days, John the Baptist came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea, saying, 
"Have a change of heart, because the reign of the sky is at hand!" 
He is the one the spokesman Isaiah spoke about when he said, "A voice crying in the wilderness: make the way of the Lord ready. Make his paths straight." 
Now John himself wore clothing made of camel's hair with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts and wild honey. 
Then people from Jerusalem, all of Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him. 
They were immersed in the Jordan by him, agreeing out loud about their mistakes. 
But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to be immersed, he said to them, "You offspring of vipers, who warned you to run away from the heat to come? 
So bear fruit that shows your change of heart is real! 
Don't think to yourselves, 'We have Abraham for our father,' for I tell you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham from these stones. 
The ax is already lying at the root of the trees. Every tree that doesn't produce good fruit will be cut down and thrown into the fire. 
I immerse you in water for a change of heart, but the one coming after me is mightier than I am, and I am not worthy to carry his sandals. He will immerse you in the Holy Spirit. 
His shovel is in his hand, and he will clean out his threshing floor. He will gather his wheat into the barn, but the husks he will burn with fire that never goes out." 
Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to John, to be immersed by him. 
But John tried to stop him, saying, "I need you to immerse me, and you come to me?" 
But Jesus, answering, said to him, "Let it be so now, because this is the fitting way for us to fill all rightness completely." Then he allowed him. 
As soon as Jesus was immersed, he came up out of the water. And suddenly the sky opened for him. He saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and coming to rest on him. 
Suddenly, a voice out of the sky said, "This is my Son, whom I truly love, and I am delighted with him."
