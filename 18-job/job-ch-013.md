"Look, my eye has seen all this. My ear has heard and understood it. 
What you know, I know also. I'm not inferior to you. 
"Surely I'd speak to the Almighty. I desire to reason with God. 
But you're forgers of lies. You're all physicians of no value. 
Oh that you'd be completely silent! Then you'd be wise. 
Hear now my reasoning. Listen to the pleadings of my lips. 
Will you speak unrighteously for God, and talk deceitfully for him? 
Will you show partiality to him? Will you contend for God? 
Is it good that he should search you out? Or as one deceives a man, will you deceive him? 
He'll surely reprove you if you secretly show partiality. 
Won't his majesty make you afraid and his dread fall on you? 
Your memorable sayings are proverbs of ashes. Your defenses are defenses of clay. 
"Be silent! Leave me alone, that I can speak. Let come on me what will. 
Why should I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in my hand? 
Look, he'll kill me. I have no hope. Nevertheless, I'll maintain my ways before him. 
This also will be my salvation, that a godless man won't come before him. 
Listen carefully to my speech. Let my declaration be in your ears. 
See now, I have set my cause in order. I know that I'm righteous. 
Who's he who will contend with me? Then I'd hold my peace and give up the spirit. 
"Only don't do two things to me, then I won't hide myself from your face: 
withdraw your hand far from me, and don't let your terror make me afraid. 
Then call, and I'll answer, or let me speak, and you answer me. 
How many are my wrongdoings and my drift? Make me know my disobedience and my wrongdoing. 
Why do you hide your face, and consider me your enemy? 
Will you harass a driven leaf? Will you pursue the dry stubble? 
You write bitter things against me, and make me inherit the guilt of my youth. 
You also put my feet in the stocks, and mark all my paths. You set a bound to the soles of my feet, 
though I'm decaying like a rotten thing, like a garment that's moth-eaten.
