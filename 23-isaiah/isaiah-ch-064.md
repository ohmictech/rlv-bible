Oh that you'd tear the sky, that you'd come down, that the mountains might quake at your presence 
as when fire kindles the brushwood, and the fire causes the water to boil. Make your name known to your adversaries, that the nations may tremble at your presence! 
When you did awesome things which we didn't look for, you came down, and the mountains quaked at your presence. 
From of old men haven't heard, nor perceived by the ear, nor has the eye seen a God besides you, who works for him who waits for him. 
You meet him who rejoices and does righteousness, those who remember you in your ways. Look, you were angry, and we drifted. We have been in wrongdoing for a long time. Shall we be saved? 
We have all become like one who's unclean, and all our righteousness is like a polluted garment. We all fade like a leaf; and our guilt, like the wind, take us away. 
There's no one who calls on your name, who stirs himself up to take hold of you; for you have hidden your face from us, and have consumed us by means of our guilt. 
But now, God, you're our Father. We're the clay and you our potter. We all are the work of your hand. 
Don't be furious, God. Don't remember guilt for the eras. Look and see, we beg you, we're all your people. 
Your holy cities have become a wilderness. Zion has become a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation. 
Our holy and our beautiful house where our fathers praised you is burned with fire. All our pleasant places are laid waste. 
Will you hold yourself back for these things, God? Will you keep silent and punish us very severely?
