After these things the word of God came to Abram in a vision: "Don't be afraid, Abram. I am your shield, your very great reward." 
Abram said, "God, what will you give me? I go on childless, and the one who will inherit my estate is Eliezer of Damascus." 
Abram said, "Look, you have given me no children, and a servant born in my house will be my heir." 
Then the word of God came to him: "This man will not be your heir, but a son who comes from your own body will be your heir." 
He brought him outside and said, "Look up at the sky and count the stars, if you can count them." Then he said to him, "So your offspring will be." 
He trusted God, and it was credited to him as rightness. 
He said to him, "I am God who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess." 
He said, "God, how will I know that I will possess it?" 
He said to him, "Bring me a heifer three years old, a female goat three years old, a ram three years old, a turtledove, and a young pigeon." 
He brought him all these, and divided them in the middle, and laid each half opposite the other; but he didn't divide the birds. 
The birds of prey came down on the carcasses, and Abram drove them away. 
When the sun was going down, Abram fell into a deep sleep, and a dreadful darkness came over him. 
He said to Abram, "Know for certain that your offspring will live as foreigners in a land that is not theirs, and will serve them. They will mistreat them for four hundred years. 
I will also judge that nation, whom they will serve. Afterward they will come out with great wealth; 
but you will go to your fathers in peace. You will be buried at a good old age. 
In the fourth generation they will come here again, for the guilt of the Amorite is not yet full." 
When the sun had gone down and it was dark, a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch appeared and passed between the pieces. 
In that day God made a covenant with Abram, saying, "I have given this land to your offspring, from the river of Egypt to the great river Euphrates: 
the land of the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, 
the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim, 
the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites."
