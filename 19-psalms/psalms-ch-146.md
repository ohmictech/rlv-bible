Praise God! Praise God, my soul. 
While I live, I'll praise God. I'll sing praises to my God as long as I exist. 
Don't put your trust in princes, in a son of man in whom there's no help. 
His spirit departs, and he returns to the earth. In that very day, his thoughts perish. 
Happy is he who has the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in God, his God, 
who made the sky and the earth, the sea, and all that's in them; who keeps truth for the eras; 
who executes justice for the oppressed; who gives food to the hungry. God frees the prisoners. 
God opens the eyes of the blind. God raises up those who are bowed down. God loves the righteous. 
God preserves the foreigners. He upholds the fatherless and widow, but he turns the way of the guilty upside down. 
God will reign for the eras; your God, O Zion, to all generations. Praise God!
