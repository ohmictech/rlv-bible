Then the daughters of Zelophehad came forward. He was the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the families of Manasseh son of Joseph. His daughters were named Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah. 
They stood in front of Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders and the whole community at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and said, 
"Our father died in the wilderness. He wasn't part of the group that gathered against God with Korah, but he died for his own wrongdoing, and he had no sons. 
Why should our father's name disappear from his family just because he had no son? Give us property among our father's brothers." 
Moses brought their case before God. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"The daughters of Zelophehad are right. Give them property as an inheritance among their father's brothers, and pass their father's inheritance on to them. 
Say to the Israelites, 'If a man dies and has no son, pass his inheritance on to his daughter. 
If he has no daughter, give his inheritance to his brothers. 
If he has no brothers, give his inheritance to his father's brothers. 
If his father has no brothers, give his inheritance to the nearest relative in his family, and that relative will own it. This is to be a rule and a decision for the Israelites, just as God commanded Moses.'" 
God said to Moses, "Go up this mountain in the Abarim range and look at the land I have given the Israelites. 
When you have seen it, you also will be gathered to your people, the way your brother Aaron was, 
because at Meribah, when the community quarreled, you rebelled against my word in the Zin wilderness and didn't treat me as set apart in front of them." These are the waters of Meribah at Kadesh in the Zin wilderness. 
Moses spoke to God: 
"Let God, the God of the spirits of every living person, appoint a man over the community, 
someone who can go out in front of them and come in before them, someone who can lead them out and bring them in, so the community of God isn't like sheep without a shepherd." 
God said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun, a man who has the Spirit in him, and lay your hand on him. 
Set him in front of Eleazar the priest and the whole community, and commission him while they watch. 
Give him some of your authority, so that the whole community of the Israelites will obey him. 
He is to stand in front of Eleazar the priest, who will ask God's decision for him using the Urim. At his word they will go out, and at his word they will come in, he and all the Israelites with him, the whole community." 
Moses did as God commanded. He took Joshua and set him in front of Eleazar the priest and the whole community, 
and he laid his hands on him and commissioned him, just as God said through Moses.
