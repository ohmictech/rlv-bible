God said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and tell them, 'A priest is not to make himself unclean for a dead person among his people, 
except for his closest relatives: his mother, his father, his son, his daughter, his brother, 
and his virgin sister who is close to him and has had no husband. For her he may make himself unclean. 
He is not to make himself unclean as a leader among his people and dishonor himself. 
"'They are not to shave their heads, cut off the edges of their beards or cut their bodies. 
They are to be set apart to their God and not dishonor the name of their God, because they offer God's offerings made by fire, the bread of their God. So they are to be set apart. 
"'They are not to marry a woman who is a prostitute or has been made unclean, and a priest is not to marry a woman divorced from her husband, because he is set apart to his God. 
You are to set him apart, because he offers the bread of your God. He is to be set apart to you, because I, God, who sets you apart, am set apart. 
"'If a priest's daughter makes herself unclean by becoming a prostitute, she dishonors her father, and she is to be burned with fire. 
"'The high priest among his brothers, on whose head the anointing oil was poured and who was set apart to wear the garments, is not to let his hair hang loose or tear his clothes. 
He must not go near any dead body or make himself unclean for his father or mother. 
He is not to leave the Holy Place or dishonor the Holy Place of his God, because the crown of the anointing oil of his God is on him. I am God. 
"'He is to marry a woman who is a virgin. 
He is not to marry a widow, a divorced woman, a woman who has been made unclean, or a prostitute. He is to marry a virgin from his own people, 
so he doesn't dishonor his children among his people, because I am God, who sets him apart.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Tell Aaron, 'None of your descendants through all their generations who has a defect may come near to offer the bread of his God. 
No one who has a defect may come near: a man who is blind or lame, who has a disfigured face or a deformed limb, 
or who has a broken foot or hand, 
or who is hunchbacked or a dwarf, or who has a defect in his eye, or a skin rash, or scabs, or damaged testicles. 
No descendant of Aaron the priest who has a defect may come near to offer God's offerings made by fire. Since he has a defect, he may not come near to offer the bread of his God. 
He may eat the bread of his God, both the most holy and the holy, 
but he may not come near the curtain or the altar, because he has a defect, so he doesn't dishonor my Holy Places, because I am God, who sets them apart.'" 
So Moses spoke to Aaron, his sons and all the Israelites.
