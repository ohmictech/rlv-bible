"If two men have a dispute and go to court, and the judges decide the case, they will declare the one who is in the right innocent and the one who is guilty guilty. 
If the guilty man deserves a beating, the judge will have him lie down and be beaten in front of him, with the number of strokes his crime deserves. 
He may give him forty strokes, but no more. If he beats him more than that, your brother will be shamed in your eyes. 
Don't muzzle the ox while it is treading out the grain. 
If brothers live together and one of them dies without a son, his widow must not marry an outsider. Her husband's brother must go to her and marry her and do his duty as her husband's brother. 
The first son she bears will carry on the name of the dead brother, so that his name isn't wiped out of Israel. 
If the man doesn't want to marry his brother's wife, she must go to the gate to the elders and say, 'My husband's brother refuses to keep his brother's name alive in Israel. He won't do his duty as my husband's brother.' 
Then the elders of his city must call him and talk to him. If he stands firm and says, 'I don't want to marry her,' 
then his brother's wife must come to him in front of the elders, pull his sandal off his foot and spit in his face, and say, 'This is what is done to the man who won't build up his brother's family.' 
His name in Israel will be called 'The family of the man whose sandal was pulled off.' 
If two men are fighting and the wife of one comes to rescue her husband from the man hitting him, and she reaches out and grabs him by the private parts, 
cut off her hand. Show her no pity. 
Don't keep two different weights in your bag, a heavy one and a light one. 
Don't keep two different measures in your house, a large one and a small one. 
Use only accurate and honest weights and accurate and honest measures, so that you may live long in the land your God is giving you. 
Everyone who does dishonest things like that is disgusting to your God. 
Remember what Amalek did to you on the road when you came out of Egypt, 
how he met you on the road and attacked the stragglers at the back, all the weak ones trailing behind, when you were tired and worn out, and he didn't fear God. 
So when your God has given you rest from all your enemies all around, in the land your God is giving you as an inheritance to take, you must wipe out the memory of Amalek from under the sky. Don't forget.
