Now, brothers and sisters, about the arrival of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to him, we ask you 
not to be shaken out of your right mind so quickly, or thrown into a panic, whether by some spirit or by a message or by a letter claiming to be from us, saying that the day of Christ has already come. 
Don't let anyone lead you astray, in any way. That day won't come unless the standing away comes first, and the man of wrongdoing is unveiled, the son of ruin. 
He sets himself against everything called God or worshiped, and lifts himself above it all, so that he takes his seat in God's temple and claims to be God himself. 
Don't you remember that I told you all this while I was still with you? 
And you know what's holding him back now, so that he'll be unveiled when his own time comes. 
The secret of living as if there were no law is already at work. Only the one holding it back is doing so for now, until he's out of the way. 
Then the one who lives as if there were no law will be unveiled, and the Lord will kill him with the breath of his mouth and put him out of work by the appearing of his arrival. 
His arrival is the work of the Adversary, with every kind of power and sign and lying wonder, 
and with every kind of wrong trickery aimed at those who are on their way to ruin, because they wouldn't welcome the love of the truth and be rescued. 
That's why God lets them be powerfully led astray, so that they go on trusting a lie, 
so that everyone may be sentenced, those who wouldn't trust the truth but took pleasure in wrong. 
But we have to keep giving thanks to God for you, brothers and sisters truly loved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning to be rescued, through the Spirit setting you apart and through your trusting the truth. 
He called you to this through our Good News, so that you may come to share the weight of our Lord Jesus Christ. 
So then, brothers and sisters, stand firm and hold on to what we handed down to you, whether by word or by letter. 
May our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father, who has truly loved us and given us comfort for the eras and a good expectation, through favor, 
encourage your hearts and make you strong in every good deed and word.
