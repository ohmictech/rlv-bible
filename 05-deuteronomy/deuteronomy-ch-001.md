These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel across the Jordan in the wilderness, in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth and Dizahab. 
It is eleven days from Horeb to Kadesh Barnea by way of Mount Seir. 
In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses spoke to the Israelites about everything God had commanded him to tell them, 
after he had defeated Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei. 
Across the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to explain this law, saying: 
"Our God spoke to us at Horeb: 'You have stayed at this mountain long enough. 
Turn and set out, and go into the hill country of the Amorites and to everyone living near there: in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the lowlands, in the Negev, along the coast, in the land of the Canaanites, and in Lebanon, as far as the great river, the Euphrates. 
Look, I have set the land in front of you. Go in and take it as your own, the land God swore to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, that he would give to them and to their descendants after them.'" 
At that time I said to you, "I can't carry you by myself. 
Your God has made you as many as the stars in the sky. 
May the God of your fathers make you a thousand times more numerous and bless you, just as he promised you. 
But how can I carry your problems, your burdens and your quarrels by myself? 
Pick out wise, understanding and respected men from each of your tribes, and I will put them in charge of you." 
You answered me, "What you say is good." 
So I took the respected and wise men from your tribes and put them in charge of you as commanders of thousands, of hundreds, of fifties and of tens, and as officers by your tribes. 
I gave your judges their orders at that time: "Listen to your brothers' cases and judge fairly between a man and his brother, or between a man and the foreigner living with him. 
Don't play favorites in court. Listen to the small and the great the same way. Don't be afraid of anyone, because the verdict is God's. Bring any case too hard for you to me, and I will hear it." 
I gave you all your instructions at that time. 
We set out from Horeb and went through all that great and terrible wilderness you saw, along the road to the Amorite hill country, just as our God commanded us, and we came to Kadesh Barnea. 
I said to you, "You have come to the Amorite hill country, which our God is giving us. 
Look, your God has set the land in front of you. Go up and take it, just as God, the God of your fathers, told you. Don't be afraid and don't be discouraged." 
Then all of you came to me and said, "Let's send men ahead of us to scout the land and bring back word about the road we should take and the cities we will come to." 
I thought that was a good idea, so I took twelve of your men, one from each tribe. 
They turned and went up into the hill country, came to the Eshcol valley and scouted it. 
They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us and reported, "It's a good land our God is giving us." 
But you wouldn't go up. You rebelled against the command of your God. 
You grumbled in your tents and said, "Because God hates us, he brought us out of Egypt to hand us over to the Amorites to destroy us. 
Where are we going? Our brothers have melted our hearts, saying, 'The people are bigger and taller than we are, and the cities are large and walled up to the sky. We even saw the descendants of Anak there.'" 
Then I said to you, "Don't be terrified and don't be afraid of them. 
Your God, who goes ahead of you, will fight for you, just as he did for you in Egypt, right in front of your eyes, 
and in the wilderness, where you saw how your God carried you the way a man carries his son, all along the road you traveled until you came to this place." 
But you still didn't trust your God, 
who went ahead of you on the road to find you places to camp, in fire at night to show you the way, and in the cloud by day. 
God heard what you said and was angry, and he swore, 
"Not one of these men of this bad generation will see the good land I swore to give your fathers, 
except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He will see it, and I will give him and his children the land he walked on, because he followed God completely." 
God was angry with me too because of you, and said, "You won't go in either. 
Joshua son of Nun, who stands in front of you, will go in. Encourage him, because he will lead Israel into it. 
And your children, the ones you said would be taken as plunder, who today don't know good from bad, will go in. I will give it to them, and they will take it. 
But as for you, turn around and set out into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea." 
Then you answered me, "We have done wrong against God. We will go up and fight, just as our God commanded us." Each of you put on his weapons and got ready to go up into the hill country. 
God said to me, "Tell them, 'Don't go up and don't fight, because I am not among you, or you will be beaten by your enemies.'" 
So I told you, and you didn't listen. You rebelled against God's command, and you were arrogant and went up into the hill country. 
The Amorites living in that hill country came out against you and chased you like bees and beat you down from Seir all the way to Hormah. 
You came back and wept before God, but God didn't listen to you or turn his ear to you. 
So you stayed at Kadesh for many days, as long as you stayed there.
