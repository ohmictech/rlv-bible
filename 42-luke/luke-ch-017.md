He said to his students, "It is impossible that no traps should come, but how terrible for him through whom they come! 
It would be better for him to have a millstone hung around his neck and be thrown into the sea than to trap one of these little ones. 
Be careful. If your brother wrongs you, rebuke him. If he has a change of heart, release him. 
If he wrongs you seven times in the day, and seven times returns, saying, 'I have a change of heart,' you must release him." 
The ones sent out said to the Lord, "Increase our trust." 
The Lord said, "If you had trust like a grain of mustard seed, you would tell this sycamore tree, 'Be uprooted and be planted in the sea,' and it would obey you. 
But who is there among you, having a slave plowing or keeping sheep, that will say when he comes in from the field, 'Come immediately and sit down at the table'? 
Wouldn't he rather tell him, 'Prepare my supper, clothe yourself properly, and serve me while I eat and drink. Afterward you will eat and drink'? 
Does he thank that slave because he did the things that were commanded? I don't think so. 
Even so you also, when you have done all the things that are commanded you, say, 'We are unworthy slaves. We have only done our duty.'" 
As he was on his way to Jerusalem, he was passing along the borders of Samaria and Galilee. 
As he entered into a certain village, ten men who had leprosy met him, who stood at a distance. 
They lifted up their voices, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!" 
When he saw them, he said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." As they went, they were made clean. 
One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, giving weight to God with a loud voice. 
He fell on his face at Jesus' feet, giving him thanks; and he was a Samaritan. 
Jesus answered, "Weren't the ten made clean? But where are the nine? 
Were there none found who returned to give weight to God, except this foreigner?" 
Then he said to him, "Get up, and go your way. Your trust has healed you and made you whole." 
Being asked by the strict religious ones when God's reign would come, he answered them, "God's reign doesn't come with visible signs; 
neither will they say, 'Look, here!' or, 'Look, there!' for look, God's reign is within you." 
He said to his students, "The days will come when you will desire to see one of the days of the Human One, and you will not see it. 
They will tell you, 'Look, here!' or 'Look, there!' Don't go away or follow after them, 
for as the lightning, when it flashes out of one part under the sky, shines to another part under the sky, so will the Human One be in his day. 
But first, he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation. 
As it was in the days of Noah, even so it will also be in the days of the Human One. 
They ate, they drank, they married, and they were given in marriage until the day that Noah entered into the ship, and the flood came and killed them all. 
Likewise, even as it was in the days of Lot: they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they built; 
but in the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from the sky and killed them all. 
It will be the same way in the day that the Human One is revealed. 
In that day, whoever is on the housetop with his goods in the house must not go down to take them away. Let him who is in the field likewise not turn back. 
Remember Lot's wife! 
Whoever seeks to rescue his life loses it, but whoever loses his life preserves it. 
I tell you, in that night there will be two people in one bed. One will be taken and the other will be left. 
There will be two grinding grain together. One will be taken and the other will be left." 
They asked him, "Where, Lord?" He said to them, "Where the body is, there the vultures will gather."
