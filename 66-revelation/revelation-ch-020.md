Then I saw a messenger coming down out of the sky, holding the key to the deep and a great chain in his hand. 
He seized the dragon, that ancient snake, the Slanderer and the Adversary, who deceives the whole world, and chained him up for a thousand years. 
He threw him into the deep, locked it, and sealed it over him, so that he could no longer deceive the nations until the thousand years were finished. After that, he must be set free for a short time. 
I saw thrones, and those who sat on them were given the right to pass a verdict. I saw the lives of those who had been beheaded because of the witness Jesus gave and because of God's message, and of those who hadn't bowed down to the beast or to its image and hadn't received the mark on their forehead or hand. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years. 
The rest of the dead didn't come to life until the thousand years were finished. This is the first rising again. 
Flourishing and set apart is anyone who has a part in the first rising again. The second death has no power over them, but they'll be priests of God and of Christ, and they'll reign with him for a thousand years. 
When the thousand years are over, the Adversary will be let out of his prison, 
and he'll go out to deceive the nations in the four corners of the land, Gog and Magog, and gather them for battle. Their number is like the sand of the seashore. 
They marched across the whole land and surrounded the camp of the set-apart ones and the city that's truly loved. But fire came down from the sky from God and burned them up. 
The Slanderer who had deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false spokesman already were, and they'll be in anguish day and night for all the eras. 
Then I saw a great white throne and the one sitting on it. The land and the sky ran away from his face, and no place was found for them. 
I saw the dead, great and small, standing in front of the throne, and books were opened. Then another book was opened, which is the book of real life. The dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to what they had done. 
The sea gave up the dead in it, and Death and Hades gave up the dead in them, and each person was judged according to what they had done. 
Then Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This lake of fire is the second death. 
And anyone whose name wasn't found written in the book of real life was thrown into the lake of fire.
