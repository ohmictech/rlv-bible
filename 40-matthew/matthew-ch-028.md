Now after the Sabbath, as it began to dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to look at the tomb. 
Look, there was a great earthquake, for a messenger of the Lord came down from the sky and rolled the stone away from the entrance and sat on it. 
His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing white as snow. 
Fear of him, the guards shook, and became like dead men. 
The messenger said to the women, "Don't be afraid, for I know that you're looking for Jesus, who was crucified. 
He isn't here, for he has been raised, just as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord was lying. 
Go quickly and tell his students, 'He has been raised from the dead, and look, he's going ahead of you into Galilee. There you'll see him.' Look, I have told you." 
They left the tomb quickly with fear and great joy, and ran to bring his students word. 
As they went to tell his students, look, Jesus met them, saying, "Celebrate!" They came and took hold of his feet, and worshiped him. 
Then Jesus said to them, "Don't be afraid. Go tell my brothers to go into Galilee, and there they'll see me." 
Now while they were going, look, some of the guards came into the city and told the chief priests everything that had happened. 
When they had gathered with the elders and plotted together, they gave a large amount of silver to the soldiers, 
saying, "Say that his students came at night and stole him away while we were asleep. 
If this reaches the governor's ears, we'll satisfy him and keep you out of trouble." 
So they took the money and did as they were told. This story spread among the Jews, and it's still told today. 
But the eleven students went into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had told them to go. 
When they saw him, they bowed down to him; but some doubted. 
Jesus came to them and spoke to them, saying, "All authority has been given to me in the sky and on the land. 
Go and make students of all the nations, immersing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, 
teaching them to keep everything that I commanded you. Look, I'm with you always, to the completion of the era." Amen.
