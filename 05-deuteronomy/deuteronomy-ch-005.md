Moses called all Israel together and said to them, "Listen, Israel, to the rules and decisions I am speaking to you today. Learn them and be careful to do them. 
Our God made a covenant with us at Horeb. 
God didn't make this covenant with our fathers, but with us, all of us who are alive here today. 
God spoke with you face to face on the mountain out of the middle of the fire. 
I stood between God and you at that time to tell you what God said, because you were afraid of the fire and didn't go up the mountain. He said: 
"I am your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. 
"Don't have any other gods before me. 
"Don't make yourself an idol, or an image of anything in the sky above, on the earth below, or in the water under the earth. 
Don't bow down to them or serve them, because I, your God, am a jealous God, and I bring the guilt of the fathers on their children, to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me, 
but I show loyal love to thousands who love me and keep my commands. 
"Don't misuse the name of your God, because God won't let anyone off who misuses his name. 
"Keep the Sabbath day and set it apart, as your God commanded you. 
You have six days to do all your work, 
but the seventh day is a Sabbath to your God. Don't do any work on it, neither you, nor your son or daughter, nor your male or female servant, nor your ox, your donkey or any of your livestock, nor the foreigner living in your towns, so that your male and female servants can rest the way you do. 
Remember that you were slaves in Egypt, and your God brought you out of there with a strong hand and an outstretched arm. That is why your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day. 
"Honor your father and your mother, as your God commanded you, so that you live long and things go well for you in the land God is giving you. 
"Don't murder. 
"Don't commit adultery. 
"Don't steal. 
"Don't give false testimony against your neighbor. 
"Don't covet your neighbor's wife. Don't long for your neighbor's house, his field, his male or female servant, his ox or donkey, or anything else that belongs to your neighbor." 
These are the words God spoke to your whole assembly on the mountain out of the middle of the fire, the cloud and the thick darkness, in a great voice, and he added nothing more. He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me. 
When you heard the voice out of the darkness while the mountain was burning with fire, all the heads of your tribes and your elders came to me 
and said, "Look, our God has shown us his splendor and his greatness, and we have heard his voice out of the middle of the fire. Today we have seen that God can speak with a person and that person still lives. 
But why should we die? This great fire will consume us. If we hear the voice of our God any more, we will die. 
Who of all living people has heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the middle of the fire, as we have, and lived? 
Go near and hear everything our God says, and tell us everything our God tells you, and we will listen and do it." 
God heard what you said when you spoke to me, and God said to me, "I have heard what this people said to you. Everything they said is right. 
If only they had a heart like that in them, to fear me and keep all my commands always, so that things would go well for them and their children for the eras. 
Go and tell them, 'Go back to your tents.' 
But you, stand here by me, and I will tell you all the commands, rules and decisions you are to teach them, so they can do them in the land I am giving them to take as their own." 
So be careful to do what your God has commanded you. Don't turn off to the right or the left. 
Walk in every way your God has commanded you, so that you may live and things may go well for you, and so that you may live long in the land you are going to take.
