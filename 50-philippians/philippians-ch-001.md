Paul and Timothy, slaves of Jesus Christ, to all the set-apart ones in Christ Jesus who are at Philippi, together with the overseers and the servants: 
Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
I thank my God every time I remember you, 
always, in every prayer of mine for all of you, praying with joy, 
because of your partnership in the Good News from the first day until now. 
I'm sure of this very thing: the one who began a good deed in you'll finish it by the day of Jesus Christ. 
It's right for me to feel this way about all of you, since I have you in my heart, and since you all share with me in the gift, both in my chains and in defending and confirming the Good News. 
God is my witness how I long for all of you with the deep compassion of Christ Jesus. 
And this is my prayer: that your love may overflow more and more, in knowing and in every kind of insight, 
so that you can pick out what really matters and be genuine and without stumbling on the day of Christ, 
filled with the fruit of rightness that comes through Jesus Christ, to the weight and praise of God. 
Now I want you to know, brothers and sisters, that what happened to me has actually worked out for the progress of the Good News, 
so that it has become clear throughout the whole governor's guard, and to everyone else, that my chains are because of Christ. 
And most of the brothers and sisters, gaining confidence from my chains, have grown much bolder about speaking God's word without fear. 
Some certainly announce Christ out of envy and quarreling, and some out of good will. 
The former announce Christ out of selfish ambition, with mixed motives, thinking they can add pressure to my chains. 
but the latter out of love, knowing that I'm put here to defend the Good News. 
What does it matter? The one thing that matters is that Christ is announced in every way, whether from false motives or true. And in that I celebrate, yes, and I'll keep celebrating. 
I know that this will turn out for my rescue, through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, 
It's my own eager expectation and hope that I'll never be disgraced, but that with all openness, now as always, Christ will be made great in my body, whether by life or by death. 
To me, truly living is Christ, and dying is gain. 
But if I go on living in the flesh, that means fruit from my work. Yet I don't know which I'd choose. 
I'm torn between the two: I long to leave and be with Christ, which is far better. 
But staying on in the flesh is more necessary for your sake. 
Convinced of this, I know I'll stay and go on with all of you, for your progress and joy in the trust, 
so that your pride in Christ Jesus can overflow because of me, when I'm with you again. 
Only live in a way that's worthy of the Good News of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or only hear about you, I'll know that you stand firm in one spirit, working side by side with one life for the trust of the Good News, 
and that nothing frightens you when people oppose you. Them that's a sign of ruin, and for you a sign of rescue, and that comes from God. 
Because you have been given this on Christ's behalf: not only to trust in him, but also to suffer for him, 
since you face the same struggle you saw in me and now hear that I still face.
