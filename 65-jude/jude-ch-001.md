Jude, a slave of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who have been called, set apart by God the Father, and kept safe for Jesus Christ: 
May mercy, wholeness, and love be yours in abundance. 
My truly loved friends, I was very eager to write to you about the rescue we share, but I found I had to write and urge you to fight hard for the trust that was handed down to the set-apart ones once and for all. 
Certain people have slipped in among you unnoticed. They were marked out long ago for this verdict: they disregard God, they turn the favor of our God into an excuse for shameless living, and they deny our only Master, God and Lord, Jesus Christ. 
I want to remind you, even though you already know it, that the Lord rescued a people out of the land of Egypt and afterward ruined those who didn't trust him. 
And the messengers who didn't keep their own place of authority, but left their proper home, he has kept in chains of the era under darkness for the verdict of the great day. 
In the same way, Sodom and Gomorrah and the towns around them gave themselves over to sleeping around and went after bodies that weren't their own. They stand as an example, suffering the penalty of fire for the eras. 
In the same way, these dreamers make the flesh unclean, despise authority, and slander glorious beings. 
But even Michael the archangel, when he was arguing with the Slanderer about the body of Moses, didn't dare to bring a slanderous verdict against him. Instead he said, "May the Lord correct you!" 
But these people slander whatever they don't understand, and what they do understand by instinct, like animals without reason, is what ruins them. 
How terrible for them! They have gone the way of Cain. They have rushed into Balaam's error for pay. They have been ruined in Korah's rebellion. 
These people are hidden reefs at your love feasts when they feast with you. They're shepherds who feed themselves without a qualm. They're clouds without water, carried along by winds; trees in late autumn with no fruit, twice dead, pulled up by the roots; 
wild waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame; wandering stars, and the deepest darkness has been kept for them for the eras. 
Enoch, the seventh in line from Adam, also spoke about these people when he said, "Look, the Lord has come with tens of thousands of his set-apart ones, 
to pass a verdict on everyone, and to show everyone who disregards God every way they have disregarded him, and every harsh word they have spoken against him." 
These people grumble and find fault, following their own cravings, and their mouths talk big, and they flatter people to get what they want. 
But you, my truly loved friends, remember what the sent ones of our Lord Jesus Christ said long ago. 
They told you, "In the last time there will be people who mock, following their own cravings that disregard God." 
These are the ones who make divisions. They're unspiritual and don't have the Spirit. 
But you, my truly loved friends, keep building yourselves up on your most set-apart trust, praying in the Holy Spirit. 
Keep yourselves in God's love, waiting for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ, which brings the real life of the era. 
Show mercy to some, who are wavering, 
and save others by snatching them out of the fire; and to others show mercy mixed with fear, hating even the clothing stained by the flesh. 
Now to the one who's able to keep you from tripping, and to bring you without fault into the presence of his weight with great joy, 
to God our Restorer, who alone is wise, be weight and majesty, authority and power, both now and for all the eras. Amen.
