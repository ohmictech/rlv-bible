Saul was consenting to his death. A great persecution arose against the gathering which was in Jerusalem in that day. They were all scattered abroad throughout the regions of Judea and Samaria, except for the sent ones. 
Devout men buried Stephen and lamented greatly over him. 
But Saul ravaged the gathering. Going from house to house, he dragged both men and women off to prison. 
So those who were scattered abroad went around telling the word. 
Philip went down to the city of Samaria and proclaimed to them the Christ. 
The crowds listened with one accord to the things that were spoken by Philip when they heard and saw the signs which he did. 
Unclean spirits came out of many of those who had them. They came out, crying with a loud voice. Many who had been paralyzed and lame were healed. 
There was great joy in that city. 
But there was a certain man, Simon by name, who used to practice sorcery in the city and amazed the people of Samaria, making himself out to be some great one, 
to whom they all listened, from the least to the greatest, saying, "This man is that great power of God." 
They listened to him because for a long time he had amazed them with his sorceries. 
But when they trusted Philip announcing good news concerning God's reign and the name of Jesus Christ, they were immersed, both men and women. 
Simon himself also trusted. Being immersed, he continued with Philip. Seeing signs and great powerful deeds occurring, he was amazed. 
Now when the sent ones who were at Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them, 
who, when they had come down, prayed for them, so that they would receive the Holy Spirit; 
for as yet he had fallen on none of them. They had only been immersed in the name of Christ Jesus. 
Then they laid their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit. 
Now when Simon saw that the Holy Spirit was given through the laying on of the sent ones' hands, he offered them money, 
saying, "Give me also this power, that whomever I lay my hands on may receive the Holy Spirit." 
But Peter said to him, "May your silver be destroyed with you, because you thought you could obtain the gift of God with money! 
You have neither part nor lot in this matter, for your heart isn't right before God. 
Have a change of heart about this ill will of yours, and ask God if perhaps the thought of your heart may be released to you. 
I see that you are in the poison of bitterness and bound by wrong." 
Simon answered, "Pray for me to the Lord, that none of the things which you have spoken happen to me." 
They, when they had testified and spoken the word of the Lord, returned to Jerusalem, and announced the Good News to many villages of the Samaritans. 
Then a messenger of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, "Get up and go toward the south to the way that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza. This is a desert." 
He arose and went; and look, there was a man of Ethiopia, a eunuch of great authority under Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasure, who had come to Jerusalem to worship. 
He was returning and sitting in his chariot, and was reading the spokesman Isaiah. 
The Spirit said to Philip, "Go near and join him in that chariot." 
Philip ran to him, and heard him reading Isaiah the spokesman, and said, "Do you understand what you are reading?" 
He said, "How can I, unless someone explains it to me?" He begged Philip to come up and sit with him. 
Now the passage of the Scripture which he was reading was this, "He was led as a sheep to the slaughter. As a lamb before his shearer is silent, so he doesn't open his mouth. 
In his humiliation, his verdict was taken away. Who will declare His generation? His life is taken from the earth." 
The eunuch answered Philip, "Who is the spokesman talking about? About himself, or about someone else?" 
Philip began with this Scripture and told him the good news about Jesus. 
As they went on the way, they came to some water; and the eunuch said, "Look, here is water. What is keeping me from being immersed?" 
He commanded the chariot to stand still, and they both went down into the water, both Philip and the eunuch, and he immersed him. 
When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught Philip away, and the eunuch didn't see him any more, for he went on his way celebrating. 
But Philip was found at Azotus. Passing through, he announced the Good News in all the cities until he came to Caesarea.
