God said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your fathers' house with you will bear the guilt of the sanctuary; and you and your sons with you will bear the guilt of your priesthood. 
Bring your brothers also, the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father, near with you, that they may be joined to you, and minister to you; but you and your sons with you will be before the Tent of the Testimony. 
They will keep your commands and the duty of the whole Tent; only they won't come near to the vessels of the sanctuary and to the altar, that they not die, neither they nor you. 
They will be joined to you and keep the responsibility of the Tent of Meeting, for all the service of the Tent. A stranger won't come near to you. 
"You will perform the duty of the sanctuary and the duty of the altar, that there be no more wrath on the children of Israel. 
Look, I myself have taken your brothers the Levites from among the children of Israel. They're a gift to you, dedicated to God, to do the service of the Tent of Meeting. 
You and your sons with you will keep your priesthood for everything of the altar, and for that within the veil. You will serve. I give you the service of the priesthood as a gift. The stranger who comes near will be put to death." 
God spoke to Aaron, "Look, I myself have given you the command of my wave offerings, even all the holy things of the children of Israel. I have given them to you by reason of the anointing, and to your sons, as a portion for the eras. 
This will be yours of the most holy things from the fire: every offering of theirs, even every meal offering of theirs, and every offering for wrongdoing of theirs, and every trespass offering of theirs, which they will give to me, will be most holy for you and for your sons. 
You will eat of it like the most holy things. Every male will eat of it. It will be holy to you. 
"This is yours, too: the wave offering of their gift, even all the wave offerings of the children of Israel. I have given them to you, and to your sons and to your daughters with you, as a portion for the eras. Everyone who's clean in your house will eat of it. 
"I have given to you all the best of the oil, all the best of the vintage, and of the grain, the first fruits of them which they give to God. 
The first-ripe fruits of all that's in their land, which they bring to God, will be yours. Everyone who's clean in your house will eat of it. 
"Everything devoted in Israel will be yours. 
Everything that opens the womb, of all flesh which they offer to God, both of man and animal, will be yours. Nevertheless, you will surely buy back the firstborn of man, and you will buy back the firstborn of unclean animals. 
You will buy back those who are to be bought back of them from a month old, according to your estimation, for five shekels of money, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, which weighs twenty gerahs. 
"But you won't buy back the firstborn of a cow, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat. They're holy. You will sprinkle their blood on the altar, and will burn their fat for an offering made by fire, for a pleasant aroma to God. 
Their meat will be yours, as the wave offering breast and as the right thigh, it will be yours. 
All the wave offerings of the holy things which the children of Israel offer to God, I have given you and your sons and your daughters with you, as a portion for the eras. It's a covenant of salt for the eras before God to you and to your offspring with you." 
God said to Aaron, "You will have no inheritance in their land, neither will you have any portion among them. I'm your portion and your inheritance among the children of Israel. 
"To the children of Levi, look, I have given all the tithe in Israel for an inheritance, in return for their service which they serve, even the service of the Tent of Meeting. 
Henceforth the children of Israel won't come near the Tent of Meeting, lest they incur guilt and die. 
But the Levites will do the service of the Tent of Meeting, and they will bear their guilt. It will be a statute for the eras throughout your generations. Among the children of Israel, they will have no inheritance. 
The tithe of the children of Israel, which they offer as a wave offering to God, I have given to the Levites for an inheritance. So I have said to them, 'Among the children of Israel they will have no inheritance.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Moreover you will speak to the Levites, and tell them, 'When you take of the children of Israel the tithe which I have given you from them for your inheritance, then you will offer up a wave offering of it for God, a tithe of the tithe. 
Your wave offering will be credited to you, as though it were the grain of the threshing floor, and as the fullness of the wine press. 
Thus you also will offer a wave offering to God of all your tithes, which you receive of the children of Israel; and of it you will give God's wave offering to Aaron the priest. 
Out of all your gifts, you will offer every wave offering to God, of all its best parts, even the holy part of it.' 
"Therefore you will tell them, 'When you heave its best from it, then it will be credited to the Levites as the increase of the threshing floor, and as the increase of the wine press. 
You may eat it anywhere, you and your households, for it's your reward in return for your service in the Tent of Meeting. 
You will bear no guilt by reason of it, when you have heaved from it its best. You won't profane the holy things of the children of Israel, that you not die.'"
