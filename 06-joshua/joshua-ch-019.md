The second lot came up for Simeon, for the tribe of Simeon, clan by clan. Their inheritance lay inside the inheritance of the people of Judah. 
Their share was Beersheba, or Sheba, Moladah, 
Hazar Shual, Balah, Ezem, 
Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah, 
Ziklag, Beth Marcaboth, Hazar Susah, 
Beth Lebaoth and Sharuhen. That is thirteen towns with their villages; 
Ain, Rimmon, Ether and Ashan. That is four towns with their villages; 
and all the villages around these towns as far as Baalath Beer, Ramah of the Negev. This was the inheritance of the tribe of Simeon, clan by clan. 
The inheritance of Simeon came out of Judah's share, because Judah's portion was too big for them. So the Simeonites received their inheritance inside Judah's. 
The third lot came up for the tribe of Zebulun, clan by clan. The border of their inheritance went as far as Sarid. 
Their border went up west to Maralah, reached Dabbesheth and reached the brook near Jokneam. 
From Sarid it turned east toward the sunrise to the border of Chisloth Tabor, went out to Daberath and up to Japhia. 
From there it went east to Gath Hepher and Ethkazin, and out at Rimmon, which stretches to Neah. 
The border turned north around it to Hannathon and ended at the valley of Iphtah El, 
with Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah and Bethlehem. That is twelve towns with their villages. 
This was the inheritance of the tribe of Zebulun, clan by clan, these towns with their villages. 
The fourth lot came up for Issachar, for the tribe of Issachar, clan by clan. 
Their territory included Jezreel, Chesulloth, Shunem, 
Hapharaim, Shion, Anaharath, 
Rabbith, Kishion, Ebez, 
Remeth, Engannim, En Haddah and Beth Pazzez. 
The border reached Tabor, Shahazumah and Beth Shemesh, and ended at the Jordan. That is sixteen towns with their villages. 
This was the inheritance of the tribe of Issachar, clan by clan, the towns with their villages. 
The fifth lot came up for the tribe of Asher, clan by clan. 
Their territory was Helkath, Hali, Beten, Achshaph, 
Allammelech, Amad and Mishal, and it reached Carmel on the west and Shihor Libnath. 
It turned east toward Beth Dagon and reached Zebulun and the valley of Iphtah El on the north, to Beth Emek and Neiel, and out to Cabul on the left, 
and Ebron, Rehob, Hammon and Kanah, as far as great Sidon. 
The border turned to Ramah, to the walled city of Tyre, then turned to Hosah and ended at the sea by the region of Achzib, 
with Ummah, Aphek and Rehob. That is twenty-two towns with their villages. 
This was the inheritance of the tribe of Asher, clan by clan, these towns with their villages. 
The sixth lot came up for Naphtali, for the tribe of Naphtali, clan by clan. 
Their border ran from Heleph, from the oak in Zaanannim, Adami Nekeb and Jabneel, to Lakkum, and ended at the Jordan. 
The border turned west to Aznoth Tabor and went out from there to Hukkok. It reached Zebulun on the south and Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan toward the sunrise. 
The walled cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Chinnereth, 
Adamah, Ramah, Hazor, 
Kedesh, Edrei, En Hazor, 
Iron, Migdal El, Horem, Beth Anath and Beth Shemesh. That is nineteen towns with their villages. 
This was the inheritance of the tribe of Naphtali, clan by clan, the towns with their villages. 
The seventh lot came up for the tribe of Dan, clan by clan. 
The territory of their inheritance was Zorah, Eshtaol, Irshemesh, 
Shaalabbin, Aijalon, Ithlah, 
Elon, Timnah, Ekron, 
Eltekeh, Gibbethon, Baalath, 
Jehud, Bene Berak, Gath Rimmon, 
Me Jarkon and Rakkon, with the border opposite Joppa. 
But the territory of the tribe of Dan was too small for them, so the Danites went up and attacked Leshem, took it, struck it with the sword and took it over, settled in it and renamed it Dan after Dan their ancestor. 
This was the inheritance of the tribe of Dan, clan by clan, these towns with their villages. 
When they had finished dividing the land by its borders, the Israelites gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them. 
As God had commanded, they gave him the town he asked for, Timnath Serah in the hill country of Ephraim, and he rebuilt the town and lived there. 
These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the families of the tribes of Israel divided by lot at Shiloh before God, at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting. So they finished dividing the land.
