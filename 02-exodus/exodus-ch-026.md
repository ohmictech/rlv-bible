"Make the tent out of ten curtains of fine twisted linen, with blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and cherubim worked into them by a skilled craftsman. 
Each curtain is to be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide, all the same size. 
Join five of the curtains to each other, and the other five to each other. 
Make blue loops along the edge of the last curtain in the first set, and do the same on the edge of the last curtain in the second set. 
Make fifty loops on one curtain and fifty on the other, with the loops facing each other. 
Make fifty gold clasps and use them to join the curtains, so the tent becomes one piece. 
"Make eleven curtains of goats' hair to cover the tent. 
Each curtain is to be thirty cubits long and four cubits wide, all eleven the same size. 
Join five of them into one set and six into another, and fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent. 
Make fifty loops on the edge of the last curtain in the first set, and fifty on the edge of the last curtain in the second set. 
Make fifty bronze clasps, put them in the loops and join the tent together so it is one piece. 
The extra half curtain that is left over will hang down the back of the tent. 
The extra half cubit on each side, from the length of the curtains, will hang over the two sides of the tent to cover it. 
Make a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and over that a covering of sea cow hide. 
"Make upright frames of acacia wood for the tent. 
Each frame is to be ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 
Each frame is to have two pegs joined by a crossbar, and you are to make all the frames of the tent this way. 
Make twenty frames for the south side of the tent, 
with forty silver bases under the twenty frames, two bases under each frame for its two pegs. 
For the north side make twenty frames, 
with their forty silver bases, two under each frame. 
For the back of the tent, on the west side, make six frames. 
Make two frames for the corners at the back of the tent. 
They are to be doubled at the bottom and joined at the top by a single ring. Do this for both corners. 
So there will be eight frames and sixteen silver bases, two under each frame. 
"Make crossbars of acacia wood, five for the frames on one side of the tent, 
five for the frames on the other side, and five for the frames at the back, on the west side. 
The middle crossbar is to run through the frames from end to end. 
Cover the frames with gold and make gold rings to hold the crossbars, and cover the crossbars with gold. 
Set up the tent according to the plan shown to you on the mountain. 
"Make a curtain of blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, with cherubim worked into it by a skilled craftsman. 
Hang it on four acacia posts covered with gold, with gold hooks, standing on four silver bases. 
Hang the curtain from the clasps and put the ark of the covenant behind it, inside the curtain. The curtain will separate the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place. 
Put the mercy seat on the ark of the covenant in the Most Holy Place. 
Set the table outside the curtain, and the lampstand across from it on the south side of the tent, with the table on the north side. 
"Make a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, embroidered work. 
Make five acacia posts for the screen and cover them with gold, with gold hooks, and cast five bronze bases for them.
