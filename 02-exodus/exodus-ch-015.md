Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to God: "I will sing to God, because he has won a great victory. He has thrown the horse and its rider into the sea. 
God is my strength and my song, and he has become my rescue. This is my God, and I will praise him, my father's God, and I will lift him high. 
God is a warrior. God is his name. 
He has thrown Pharaoh's chariots and his army into the sea. His best officers are drowned in the Red Sea. 
The deep waters cover them. They sank to the bottom like a stone. 
Your right hand, God, is magnificent in power. Your right hand, God, shatters the enemy. 
In your great majesty you throw down those who rise against you. You send out your anger, and it burns them up like straw. 
At the blast of your breath the waters piled up. The currents stood up in a heap, and the deep waters thickened in the heart of the sea. 
The enemy said, 'I'll chase them. I'll catch them. I'll divide the loot. I'll have what I want from them. I'll draw my sword, and my hand will destroy them.' 
You blew with your wind, and the sea covered them. They sank like lead in the mighty waters. 
Who is like you, God, among the gods? Who is like you, majestic in holiness, awe-inspiring in praise, doing wonders? 
You stretched out your right hand, and the earth swallowed them. 
In your loyal love you led the people you bought back. In your strength you guided them to your holy home. 
The peoples heard and trembled. Pain seized the people of Philistia. 
Then the chiefs of Edom were terrified. Trembling seized the warriors of Moab, and everyone in Canaan melted away. 
Terror and dread fall on them. By the greatness of your arm they are still as stone, until your people pass over, God, until the people you bought pass over. 
You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain that is your own, the place, God, you made for yourself to live in, the sanctuary, Lord, that your hands established. 
God will reign over all the eras." 
Pharaoh's horses went into the sea with his chariots and horsemen, and God brought the water back over them, but the Israelites walked through the middle of the sea on dry ground. 
Then Miriam the prophetess, Aaron's sister, took a tambourine, and all the women followed her with tambourines and dancing. 
Miriam sang back to them: "Sing to God, because he has won a great victory. He has thrown the horse and its rider into the sea." 
Moses led Israel on from the Red Sea into the wilderness of Shur, and they traveled three days in the wilderness without finding water. 
When they reached Marah they couldn't drink the water there, because it was bitter. That is why the place is called Marah. 
The people complained to Moses, "What are we going to drink?" 
So Moses cried out to God, and God showed him a piece of wood, and he threw it into the water, and the water turned sweet. There God made a rule and a law for them, and there he tested them. 
He said, "If you listen carefully to the voice of God and do what is right in his eyes, if you pay attention to his commands and keep all his rules, I will not put on you any of the diseases I put on the Egyptians, because I am God, the one who heals you." 
Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs and seventy palm trees, and they camped there beside the water.
