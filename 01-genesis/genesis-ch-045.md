Joseph couldn't hold it in any longer in front of everyone standing around him. He shouted, "Get everyone out of here!" So no one was left with him when he made himself known to his brothers. 
He wept so loudly that the Egyptians heard it, and Pharaoh's household heard it. 
He said to his brothers, "I'm Joseph. Is my father still alive?" They were too terrified to answer him. 
He said, "Come closer to me." They came closer, and he said, "I'm Joseph, your brother, the one you sold into Egypt. 
Don't be upset and don't blame yourselves for selling me here, because God sent me ahead of you to keep you alive. 
The famine has been in the land two years now, and there are five more coming with no plowing and no harvest. 
God sent me ahead of you to keep a remnant of you on the earth and to save your lives in a great rescue. 
So it wasn't you who sent me here, it was God, and he has made me a father to Pharaoh, master of his whole household, and ruler over all Egypt. 
Hurry home to my father and tell him, 'This is what your son Joseph says. God has made me master of all Egypt. Come down to me. Don't wait. 
You'll live in Goshen and be near me, you and your children and your grandchildren, your flocks and herds and everything you have. 
I'll take care of you there, because there are still five years of famine coming, and I don't want you to end up with nothing, you and your family and everything you have.' 
You can see for yourselves, and so can my brother Benjamin, that it's really me speaking to you. 
Tell my father about all the honor I have in Egypt, and everything you've seen. Hurry and bring my father down here." 
Then he threw his arms around his brother Benjamin and wept, and Benjamin wept on his shoulder. 
He kissed all his brothers and wept over them, and after that they talked with him. 
Word reached Pharaoh's household that Joseph's brothers had come, and Pharaoh and his servants were pleased. 
Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Tell your brothers to load up their animals and go back to Canaan. 
Then bring your father and your families to me. I'll give you the best of Egypt, and you'll live off the fat of the land. 
And this is what you're to do: take wagons from Egypt for your children and your wives, and bring your father and come. 
Don't worry about your belongings, because the best of all Egypt is yours." 
That's what the sons of Israel did. Joseph gave them wagons as Pharaoh had ordered, and supplies for the journey. 
He gave each of them a change of clothes, but Benjamin got three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of clothes. 
He sent his father ten donkeys loaded with the best of Egypt, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain and bread and supplies for the journey. 
Then he sent his brothers off, and as they left he said, "Don't fight on the way." 
They went up out of Egypt and came to their father Jacob in Canaan. 
They told him, "Joseph is still alive, and he's the ruler over all Egypt." His heart stood still, because he didn't believe them. 
But when they told him everything Joseph had said, and when he saw the wagons Joseph had sent to carry him, their father Jacob revived. 
Israel said, "That's enough. My son Joseph is still alive. I'll go and see him before I die."
