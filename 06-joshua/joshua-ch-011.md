When Jabin king of Hazor heard about it, he sent word to Jobab king of Madon, to the king of Shimron and to the king of Achshaph, 
and to the kings in the north, in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinneroth, in the lowlands and on the heights of Dor in the west, 
to the Canaanites east and west, the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites and Jebusites in the hill country, and the Hivites below Hermon in the land of Mizpah. 
They came out with all their armies, a great many people, as many as the sand on the seashore, with a great many horses and chariots. 
All these kings joined forces, came and camped together at the waters of Merom to fight Israel. 
God said to Joshua, "Don't be afraid of them, because by this time tomorrow I will hand all of them over dead to Israel. Cripple their horses and burn their chariots with fire." 
So Joshua and all his fighting men came on them suddenly at the waters of Merom and attacked them. 
God handed them over to Israel, and they struck them down and chased them as far as great Sidon, as far as Misrephoth Maim, and to the valley of Mizpah on the east. They struck them down until no one was left. 
Joshua did as God told him: he crippled their horses and burned their chariots with fire. 
Then Joshua turned back and took Hazor and struck its king with the sword, because Hazor had been the head of all those kingdoms. 
They struck down everyone in it with the sword, completely destroying them, until no one was left breathing, and he burned Hazor with fire. 
Joshua captured all the cities of those kings and their kings, and struck them with the sword, completely destroying them, as Moses the servant of God had commanded. 
But Israel burned none of the cities built on mounds, except Hazor, which Joshua burned. 
The Israelites took all the plunder of these cities and the livestock for themselves, but they struck down every person with the sword until they had destroyed them, leaving no one breathing. 
As God had commanded Moses his servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and Joshua did it. He left nothing undone of everything God had commanded Moses. 
So Joshua took all that land: the hill country, all the Negev, all the land of Goshen, the lowlands, the Arabah, the hill country of Israel and its lowlands, 
from Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir, all the way to Baal Gad in the valley of Lebanon below Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings, struck them down and put them to death. 
Joshua fought all those kings for a long time. 
There wasn't a city that made peace with the Israelites except the Hivites living in Gibeon. They took all the rest in battle. 
It was God who hardened their hearts so they would come against Israel in battle, so that he could destroy them without mercy and wipe them out, as God had commanded Moses. 
At that time Joshua came and cut off the Anakim from the hill country: from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah and of Israel. Joshua completely destroyed them and their cities. 
No Anakim were left in the land of the Israelites. Only in Gaza, in Gath and in Ashdod did some remain. 
So Joshua took the whole land, just as God had spoken to Moses, and Joshua gave it to Israel as an inheritance, divided among their tribes. Then the land had rest from war.
