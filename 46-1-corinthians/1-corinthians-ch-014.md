Pursue love, and earnestly desire the spiritual gifts, but especially that you may speak for God. 
The one who speaks in another language speaks not to people but to God, for no one understands, but in the Spirit he speaks secrets. 
But the one who speaks for God speaks to people for their building up, encouragement, and comfort. 
The one who speaks in another language builds himself up, but the one who speaks for God builds up the gathering. 
Now I want you all to speak with other languages, but even more that you would speak for God. The one who speaks for God is greater than the one who speaks with other languages, unless he interprets, so that the gathering may be built up. 
But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking with other languages, what good would I do you unless I speak to you either by way of unveiling, or of knowledge, or of speaking for God, or of teaching? 
Even lifeless things that make a sound, whether pipe or harp, if they didn't give a distinction in the sounds, how would it be known what is piped or harped? 
If the trumpet gave an uncertain sound, who would prepare himself for war? 
So also you, unless you uttered by the tongue words easy to understand, how would it be known what is spoken? You would be speaking into the air. 
There are, it may be, so many kinds of languages in the world, and none of them is without meaning. 
If then I don't know the meaning of the language, I would be to him who speaks a foreigner, and he who speaks would be a foreigner to me. 
So also you, since you are eager for spiritual gifts, seek to have them overflowing for the building up of the gathering. 
So let him who speaks in another language pray that he may interpret. 
If I pray in another language, my spirit prays, but my understanding is unfruitful. 
What should I do? I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray with the understanding also. I will sing with the spirit, and I will sing with the understanding also. 
Otherwise, if you bless with the spirit, how will he who fills the place of the unlearned say the "Amen" at your giving of thanks, seeing he doesn't know what you say? 
You most certainly give thanks well, but the other person is not built up. 
I thank my God, I speak with other languages more than you all. 
However, in the gathering I would rather speak five words with my understanding, so that I might also teach others, than ten thousand words in another language. 
Brothers, don't be children in your thinking. Be babies when it comes to ill will, but be grown up in your thinking. 
In the law it is written, "By men of strange languages and by the lips of strangers I will speak to this people. They won't even listen to me that way, says the Lord." 
So other languages are a sign, not for those who trust but for those who don't; but speaking for God is a sign, not for those who don't trust but for those who do. 
If the whole gathering comes together and all speak with other languages, and outsiders or people who don't trust come in, won't they say you are crazy? 
But if all speak for God, and someone who doesn't trust or an outsider comes in, he is shown his fault by all, and he is judged by all. 
And so the secrets of his heart are laid bare. Then he will fall on his face and bow down to God, declaring that God is really among you. 
What is it then, brothers? When you come together, each one of you has a song, has a teaching, has an unveiling, has another language, or has an interpretation. Let everything be done to build each other up. 
If any man speaks in another language, let there be two, or at the most three, and in turn; and let one interpret. 
But if there is no interpreter, let him keep quiet in the gathering, and speak to himself and to God. 
Let two or three spokesmen speak, and let the others weigh what is said. 
But if something is unveiled to another who is sitting by, let the first keep quiet. 
You can all speak for God one by one, so that all may learn and all may be encouraged. 
The spirits of the spokesmen are subject to the spokesmen, 
for God is not a God of confusion but of wholeness, as in all the gatherings of the set-apart ones. 
Let the wives keep quiet in the gatherings, for they are not permitted to talk, but are to be under authority, as the law also says, 
if they want to learn anything. "Let them ask their own husbands at home, for it is shameful for a wife to be talking in the gathering." 
What!? Was it from you that the word of God went out? Or did it come to you alone? 
If anyone thinks he is a spokesman or spiritual, let him recognize that the things I write to you are the Lord's command. 
But if anyone is ignorant, let him be ignorant. 
So, brothers, earnestly desire to speak for God, and don't forbid speaking with other languages. 
Let all things be done decently and in order.
