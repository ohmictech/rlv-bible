Stand firm, then, in the freedom Christ has set us free with, and don't get tangled up again in a yoke of slavery. 
Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you let yourselves be circumcised, Christ will do you no good. 
I testify again to every man who lets himself be circumcised that he owes it to do the whole law. 
You are cut off from Christ, you who want to be made right by the law. You have fallen away from the favor. 
For we, through the Spirit, by trust, wait for the expectation of rightness. 
For in Christ Jesus neither being circumcised nor not being circumcised counts for anything, but trust working through love. 
You were running well! Who interfered with you that you should not obey the truth? 
This persuasion is not from him who calls you. 
A little yeast grows through the whole lump. 
I have confidence toward you in the Lord that you will think no other way. But whoever is troubling you will carry his verdict, whoever he is. 
But I, brothers, if I still announce circumcision, why am I still hunted down? Then the trap of the cross would be gone. 
I wish that those who disturb you would cut themselves off. 
For you, brothers, were called for freedom. Only don't use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another. 
For the whole law is fulfilled in one word, in this: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself." 
But if you bite and devour one another, be careful that you don't consume one another. 
But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you won't satisfy what the flesh craves. 
For the flesh craves what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit what is against the flesh; and these are opposed to one another, so that you may not do the things that you want. 
But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law. 
Now the deeds of the flesh are plain: cheating, sleeping around, uncleanness, shamelessness, 
idol worship, drug-magic, hatred, quarreling, jealousy, flare, selfish ambition, divisions, factions, 
envy, murders, drunkenness, orgies, and things like these; of which I forewarn you, even as I also forewarned you, that those who do such things will not be given charge of God's reign. 
But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, wholeness, patience, kindness, goodness, trust, 
gentleness, and self-control. Against such things there is no law. 
Those who belong to Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and cravings. 
If we live by the Spirit, let's also walk by the Spirit. 
Let's not become conceited, provoking one another, and envying one another.
