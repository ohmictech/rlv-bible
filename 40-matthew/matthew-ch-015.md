Then some strict religious ones and Bible teachers came to Jesus from Jerusalem, saying, 
"Why do your students disobey the tradition of the elders? They don't wash their hands when they eat bread." 
He answered them, "Why do you also disobey the command of God because of your tradition? 
God commanded, 'Honor your father and your mother,' and, 'He who speaks maliciously of father or mother, let him be put to death.' 
But you say, 'Anyone who tells his father or his mother, "What I could have given you is a gift devoted to God," 
doesn't have to honor his father or mother.' You have set aside the command of God because of your tradition. 
You pretenders! Isaiah spoke well for God about you, saying, 
'These people draw near to me with their mouth, and honor me with their lips, but their heart is far from me. 
And they worship me for nothing, teaching as their teaching the rules made by people.'" 
He summoned the crowd, and said to them, "Hear, and understand. 
That which enters into the mouth doesn't make a person unclean, but that which comes out of the mouth, this makes a person unclean." 
Then the students came and said to him, "Do you know that the strict religious ones were trapped when they heard this saying?" 
But he answered, "Every plant which my Father in the sky didn't plant will be pulled up by the roots. 
Leave them alone. They're blind guides of the blind. If the blind guide the blind, both will fall into a pit." 
Peter answered him, "Explain the story to us." 
So Jesus said, "Do you also still not understand? 
Don't you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the belly and then out of the body? 
But the things which come out of the mouth come out of the heart, and they make a person unclean. 
Out of the heart come malicious thoughts, murders, cheating, sleeping around, thefts, false witness, and slander. 
These are the things which make a person unclean, but to eat with unwashed hands doesn't make a person unclean." 
Jesus went out from there and withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon. 
Suddenly, a Canaanite woman came out from those borders and cried, saying, "Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is badly possessed by an unclean spirit!" 
But he answered her not a word. His students came and begged him, saying, "Send her away, because she cries after us." 
But he answered, "I wasn't sent to anyone but the lost sheep of the house of Israel." 
But she came and worshiped him, saying, "Lord, help me." 
But he answered, "It isn't appropriate to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs." 
But she said, "Yes, Lord, but even the dogs eat the crumbs which fall from their masters' table." 
Then Jesus answered her, "Woman, your trust is great! Let it be done for you just as you want." And her daughter was healed from that hour. 
Jesus departed from there and came near to the sea of Galilee, and he went up on the mountain and sat there. 
Great crowds came to him, having with them the lame, blind, unable to speak, crippled, and many others, and they put them down at his feet. He healed them, 
so that the crowd was amazed when they saw those who couldn't speak speaking, the crippled healed, the lame walking, and the blind seeing, and they made much of the God of Israel. 
Jesus summoned his students and said, "I'm moved with deep compassion for the crowd, because they have stayed with me now three days and have nothing to eat. I don't want to send them away hungry, or they might faint on the way." 
The students said to him, "Where could we get so many loaves in an empty place as to satisfy so great a crowd?" 
Jesus said to them, "How many loaves do you have?" They said, "Seven, and a few small fish." 
He commanded the crowd to sit down on the ground. 
and he took the seven loaves and the fish. He gave thanks and broke them, and gave them to the students, and the students gave them to the crowds. 
They all ate and were filled. They took up seven baskets full of the broken pieces that were left over. 
Those who ate were four thousand men, in addition to women and children. 
Then he sent away the crowds, got into the boat, and came to the region of Magdala.
