Oh that my head were waters, and my eyes a spring of tears, that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people! 
Oh that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfaring men, that I might leave my people and go from them! They're all adulterers, an assembly of treacherous men. 
"They bend their tongue, as their bow, for falsehood. They have grown strong in the land, but not for truth; for they proceed from what's bad to bad, and they don't know me," says God. 
"Everyone beware of his neighbor, and don't trust in any brother; for every brother will utterly supplant, and every neighbor will go around like a slanderer. 
Friends deceive each other, and won't speak the truth. They have taught their tongue to speak lies. They weary themselves committing guilt. 
Your habitation is in the middle of deceit. Through deceit, they refuse to know me," says God. 
So the God of Armies says, "Look, I'll melt them and test them; for how should I deal with the daughter of my people? 
Their tongue is a deadly arrow. It speaks deceit. One speaks peaceably to his neighbor with his mouth, but in his heart, he waits to ambush him. 
Shouldn't I punish them for these things?" says God. "Shouldn't my soul be avenged on a nation such as this? 
I'll weep and wail for the mountains, and lament for the pastures of the wilderness, because they're burned up, so that no one passes through; Men can't hear the voice of the livestock. Both the birds of the sky and the animals have fled. They're gone. 
"I'll make Jerusalem heaps, a dwelling place of jackals. I'll make the cities of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant." 
Who's wise enough to understand this? Who's he to whom the mouth of God has spoken, that he may declare it? Why has the land perished and burned up like a wilderness, so that no one passes through? 
God says, "Because they have forsaken my law which I set before them, and haven't obeyed my voice or walked in my ways, 
but have walked after the stubbornness of their own heart and after the Baals, which their fathers taught them." 
So the God of Armies, the God of Israel says, "Look, I'll feed them, even this people, with wormwood and give them poisoned water to drink. 
I'll scatter them also among the nations, whom neither they nor their fathers have known. I'll send the sword after them, until I have consumed them." 
The God of Armies says, "Consider, and call for the mourning women, that they may come. Send for the skillful women, that they may come. 
Let them make haste and take up a wailing for us, that our eyes may run down with tears and our eyelids gush out with waters. 
A voice of wailing is heard out of Zion, 'How we're ruined! We're greatly confounded because we have forsaken the land, because they have cast down our dwellings.'" 
Yet hear God's word, you women. Let your ear receive the word of his mouth. Teach your daughters wailing. Everyone teach her neighbor a lamentation. 
Death has come up into our windows. It has entered into our palaces to cut off the children from outside, and the young men from the streets. 
Speak, "God says, "'The dead bodies of men will fall as dung on the open field, and as the handful after the harvester. No one will gather them.'" 
God says, "Don't let the wise man glory in his wisdom. Don't let the mighty man glory in his might. Don't let the rich man glory in his riches. 
But let him who glories glory in this, that he has understanding, and knows me, that I'm God who exercises loving kindness, justice, and righteousness on the earth, for I delight in these things," says God. 
"Look, the days come," says God, "that I'll punish everyone who's circumcised only in their flesh: 
Egypt, Judah, Edom, the children of Ammon, Moab, and everyone who has the corners of their hair cut off, who dwell in the wilderness, for all the nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel are uncircumcised in heart."
