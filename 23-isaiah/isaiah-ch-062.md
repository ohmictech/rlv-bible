Zion's sake I won't hold my peace, and for Jerusalem's sake I won't rest, until her righteousness shines out like the dawn, and her salvation like a burning lamp. 
The nations will see your righteousness, and all kings your glory. You'll be called by a new name, which God's mouth will name. 
You'll also be a crown of beauty in God's hand, and a royal diadem in your God's hand. 
You won't be called Forsaken any more, nor will your land be called Desolate any more; but you'll be called Hephzibah, and your land Beulah; for God delights in you, and your land will be married. 
As a young man marries a virgin, so your sons will marry you. As a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, so your God will rejoice over you. 
I have set watchmen on your walls, Jerusalem. They'll never be silent day nor night. You who call on God, take no rest, 
and give him no rest until he establishes, and until he makes Jerusalem a praise on the earth. 
God has sworn by his right hand, and by the arm of his strength, "Surely I'll no more give your grain to be food for your enemies, and foreigners won't drink your new wine, for which you have labored, 
but those who have harvested it'll eat it, and praise God. Those who have gathered it'll drink it in the courts of my sanctuary." 
Go through, go through the gates! Prepare the way of the people! Build up, build up the highway! Gather out the stones! Lift up a banner for the peoples. 
Look, God has proclaimed to the end of the earth: "Say to the daughter of Zion, 'Look, your salvation comes! Look, his reward is with him, and his recompense before him!'" 
They'll call them "The Holy People, God's Bought back". You'll be called "Sought Out, A City Not Forsaken".
