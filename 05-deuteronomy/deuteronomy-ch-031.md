Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel. 
He said to them, "I'm one hundred twenty years old today. I can no more go out and come in. God has said to me, 'You won't go over this Jordan.' 
Your God himself will go over before you. He'll destroy these nations from before you, and you will dispossess them. Joshua will go over before you, as God has spoken. 
God will do to them as he did to Sihon and to Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, when he destroyed them. 
God will deliver them up before you, and you will do to them according to all the commandment which I have commanded you. 
Be strong and courageous. Don't be afraid or scared of them, for your God himself is who goes with you. He won't fail you nor forsake you." 
Moses called to Joshua, and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and courageous, for you will go with this people into the land which God has sworn to their fathers to give them; and you will cause them to inherit it. 
God himself is who goes before you. He'll be with you. He won't fail you nor forsake you. Don't be afraid. Don't be discouraged." 
Moses wrote this law and delivered it to the priests the sons of Levi, who bore the ark of God's covenant, and to all the elders of Israel. 
Moses commanded them, saying, "At the end of every seven years, in the set time of the year of release, in the feast of booths, 
when all Israel has come to appear before your God in the place which he'll choose, you will read this law before all Israel in their hearing. 
Assemble the people, the men and the women and the little ones, and the foreigners who are within your gates, that they can hear, learn, fear your God, and observe to do all the words of this law, 
and that their children, who haven't known, may hear and learn to fear your God, as long as you live in the land where you go over the Jordan to possess it." 
God said to Moses, "Look, your days approach that you must die. Call Joshua, and present yourselves in the Tent of Meeting, that I can commission him." Moses and Joshua went, and presented themselves in the Tent of Meeting. 
God appeared in the Tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood over the Tent's door. 
God said to Moses, "Look, you will sleep with your fathers. This people will rise up and play the prostitute after the strange gods of the land where they go to be among them, and will forsake me and break my covenant which I have made with them. 
Then my anger will be kindled against them in that day, and I'll forsake them, and I'll hide my face from them, and they will be devoured, and many bad things and troubles will come on them; so that they'll say in that day, 'Haven't these bad things come on us because our God isn't among us?' 
I'll surely hide my face in that day for all the bad which they have done, in that they have turned to other gods. 
"Now write this song for yourselves, and teach it to the children of Israel. Put it in their mouths, that this song may be a witness for me against the children of Israel. 
When I have brought them into the land which I swore to their fathers, flowing with milk and honey, and they have eaten and filled themselves, and grown fat, then they'll turn to other gods, and serve them, and despise me, and break my covenant. 
It'll happen, when many bad things and troubles have come on them, that this song will testify before them as a witness; for it won't be forgotten out of the mouths of their descendants; for I know their ways and what they're doing today, before I have brought them into the land which I promised them." 
So Moses wrote this song the same day, and taught it to the children of Israel. 
He commissioned Joshua the son of Nun, and said, "Be strong and courageous; for you will bring the children of Israel into the land which I swore to them. I'll be with you." 
When Moses had finished writing the words of this law in a book, until they were finished, 
Moses commanded the Levites, who bore the ark of God's covenant, saying, 
"Take this book of the law, and put it by the side of the ark of your God's covenant, that it might be there for a witness against you. 
I know your rebellion and your stiff neck. Look, while I'm yet alive with you today, you have been rebellious against God. How much more after my death? 
Assemble to me all the elders of your tribes and your officers, that I can speak these words in their ears, and call the sky and earth to witness against them. 
I know that after my death you'll utterly corrupt yourselves, and turn away from the way which I have commanded you; and bad will happen to you in the latter days, because you'll do that which is bad in God's sight, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands." 
Moses spoke in the ears of all the assembly of Israel the words of this song, until they were finished.
