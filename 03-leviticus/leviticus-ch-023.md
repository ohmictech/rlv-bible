God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'These are God's appointed feasts, the set apart gatherings you are to announce. These are my feasts. 
"'You have six days to work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, a set apart gathering. You are to do no work at all. It is a Sabbath to God wherever you live. 
"'These are God's appointed feasts, the set apart gatherings you are to announce at their proper time. 
On the fourteenth day of the first month at twilight is God's Passover. 
On the fifteenth day of the same month is the Feast of Bread Without Yeast to God. For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast. 
On the first day you are to have a set apart gathering and do no regular work, 
but you are to present an offering made by fire to God for seven days. On the seventh day there is a set apart gathering and you are to do no regular work.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'When you come into the land I am giving you and harvest its crops, bring the first bundle of your harvest to the priest. 
He is to wave the bundle before God so it will be accepted for you. The priest is to wave it on the day after the Sabbath. 
On the day you wave the bundle, offer a male lamb a year old without defect as a burnt offering to God. 
With it offer a gift offering of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, an offering made by fire to God, a soothing aroma, and a drink offering of wine, a quarter of a hin. 
You must not eat bread, roasted grain or fresh grain until that same day, until you have brought the offering of your God. This is a perpetual rule for all your generations, wherever you live. 
"'Count off seven full weeks from the day after the Sabbath, from the day you bring the bundle of the wave offering. 
Count fifty days to the day after the seventh Sabbath, and then present a new gift offering to God. 
From wherever you live, bring two loaves of bread as a wave offering, made from two tenths of an ephah of fine flour and baked with yeast, as firstfruits to God. 
Present with the bread seven lambs a year old without defect, one young bull and two rams. They are to be a burnt offering to God, along with their gift offerings and drink offerings, an offering made by fire, a soothing aroma to God. 
You are to offer one male goat for an offering for wrongdoing and two male lambs a year old as a wholeness sacrifice. 
The priest is to wave them with the bread of the firstfruits as a wave offering before God, along with the two lambs. They are set apart to God for the priest. 
On that same day announce a set apart gathering for yourselves, and do no regular work. This is a perpetual rule wherever you live, for all your generations. 
"'When you harvest your land, don't reap all the way to the edges of your field or pick up what is left after the harvest. Leave it for the poor and the foreigner. I am your God.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'On the first day of the seventh month you are to have a day of complete rest, a memorial announced with trumpet blasts, a set apart gathering. 
Do no regular work, and present an offering made by fire to God.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"On the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Making Things Right. It is to be a set apart gathering for you, and you are to humble yourselves and present an offering made by fire to God. 
You are to do no work at all on that day, because it is a day of making things right, to make things right for you before your God. 
Anyone who does not humble himself on that day is to be cut off from his people. 
Anyone who does any work on that day, I will destroy from among his people. 
You are to do no work at all. This is a perpetual rule for all your generations, wherever you live. 
It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you are to humble yourselves. From the evening of the ninth day of the month until the next evening you are to keep your Sabbath." 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Shelters, to God, for seven days. 
On the first day there is to be a set apart gathering, and you are to do no regular work. 
For seven days you are to present an offering made by fire to God. On the eighth day you are to have a set apart gathering and present an offering made by fire to God. It is a closing assembly, and you are to do no regular work. 
"'These are God's appointed feasts, which you are to announce as set apart gatherings, to present an offering made by fire to God, a burnt offering, a gift offering, a sacrifice and drink offerings, each on its own day, 
in addition to God's Sabbaths, and in addition to your gifts, all your vows and all your freewill offerings that you give to God. 
"'So on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered the crops of the land, you are to keep the feast of God for seven days. The first day is a day of complete rest, and the eighth day is a day of complete rest. 
On the first day take the fruit of majestic trees, palm branches, branches of leafy trees and willows from the brook, and rejoice before your God for seven days. 
Keep it as a feast to God for seven days each year. This is a perpetual rule for all your generations. Keep it in the seventh month. 
You are to live in temporary shelters for seven days. Everyone born in Israel is to live in temporary shelters, 
so your generations will know that I made the Israelites live in temporary shelters when I brought them out of Egypt. I am your God.'" 
So Moses announced to the Israelites the appointed feasts of God.
