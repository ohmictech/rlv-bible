Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men from Shittim as spies, saying, "Go and look over the land, especially Jericho." So they went and came into the house of a prostitute named Rahab, and stayed there. 
The king of Jericho was told, "Look, some Israelites came in here tonight to spy out the land." 
The king of Jericho sent word to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to you and entered your house, because they have come to spy out the whole land." 
But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I didn't know where they were from. 
About the time the gate was being shut, when it got dark, the men went out. I don't know where they went. Chase them quickly. You can catch up with them." 
But she had taken them up to the roof and hidden them under the stalks of flax she had laid out on the roof. 
The men chased them along the road to the fords of the Jordan, and as soon as the pursuers had gone out, the gate was shut. 
Before the men lay down for the night, she came up to them on the roof 
and said, "I know that God has given you the land. Fear of you has fallen on us, and everyone in the land is melting away because of you. 
We heard how God dried up the water of the Red Sea ahead of you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites on the other side of the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you completely destroyed. 
When we heard it, our hearts melted, and there was no courage left in anyone because of you, because your God is God in the sky above and on the earth below. 
Now please swear to me by God that, since I have been kind to you, you will be kind to my father's family too, and give me a reliable sign 
that you will let my father, my mother, my brothers, my sisters and everyone they have live, and save our lives from death." 
The men said to her, "Our lives for yours, if you don't tell anyone about this business of ours. When God gives us the land, we will be kind and faithful to you." 
Then she let them down by a rope through the window, because her house was built into the city wall and she lived in the wall. 
She said to them, "Go into the hills, or the men chasing you will find you. Hide there for three days until they come back. After that, you can go on your way." 
The men said to her, "We won't be held to this oath you made us swear 
unless, when we come into the land, you tie this scarlet cord in the window you let us down through, and gather your father, your mother, your brothers and all your father's family into your house. 
If anyone goes out the door of your house into the street, his blood is on his own head and we are free of it. But if anyone with you in the house is harmed, his blood is on our head. 
And if you tell anyone about this business of ours, then we are free of the oath you made us swear." 
She said, "Let it be as you have said." Then she sent them away and they left, and she tied the scarlet cord in the window. 
They went into the hills and stayed there three days, until the men chasing them came back. Those men searched the whole road but didn't find them. 
Then the two men came back, came down from the hills, crossed the river and came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him everything that had happened to them. 
They said to Joshua, "God really has handed the whole land over to us. Everyone in the land is melting away because of us."
