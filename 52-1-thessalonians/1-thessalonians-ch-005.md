About times and seasons, brothers and sisters, you don't need anything written to you. 
You know perfectly well that the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night. 
When people are saying, "Wholeness and safety," sudden ruin will come on them, like birth pains on a pregnant woman, and there will be no escape. 
But you, brothers and sisters, are not in darkness, so that the day should surprise you like a thief. 
You are all children of light and children of the day. We don't belong to night or to darkness, 
So then let us not sleep like the others do, but let us stay awake and keep a clear head. 
Those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk, get drunk at night. 
But since we belong to the day, let us keep a clear head, putting on the breastplate of trust and love, and the helmet of the expectation of rescue. 
God did not appoint us to heat, but to receive rescue through our Lord Jesus Christ, 
He died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we might live together with him. 
So encourage one another and build each other up, just as you are already doing. 
We ask you, brothers and sisters, to recognize those who work hard among you, who lead you in the Lord and warn you. 
and to hold them in the highest regard and love them because of the work they do. And live in wholeness among yourselves. 
We urge you, brothers and sisters: warn those who are out of line, encourage the discouraged, help the weak, and be patient with everyone. 
Make sure no one pays anyone back bad for bad, but always go after what is good for one another and for everyone. 
Always celebrate. 
Pray without stopping. 
Give thanks in everything. This is what God wants for you in Christ Jesus. 
Don't put out the Spirit's fire. 
Don't treat speaking for God with contempt. 
Test everything, and hold on to what is good. 
Keep away from every kind of bad. 
May the God of wholeness himself set you apart completely. May your whole spirit, life, and body be kept without fault at the arrival of our Lord Jesus Christ. 
The one who calls you is trustworthy, and he will do it. 
Brothers and sisters, pray for us. 
Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss. 
I put you under oath before the Lord: have this letter read to all the set-apart brothers and sisters. 
The favor of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.
