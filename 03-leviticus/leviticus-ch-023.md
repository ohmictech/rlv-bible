God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, 'The set feasts of God, which you will proclaim to be holy convocations, even these are my set feasts. 
"'Six days will work be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, a holy convocation; you will do no kind of work. It's a Sabbath to God in all your dwellings. 
"'These are the set feasts of God, even holy convocations, which you will proclaim in their appointed season. 
In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month in the evening, is God's Passover. 
On the fifteenth day of the same month is the feast of unleavened bread to God. Seven days you will eat unleavened bread. 
In the first day you will have a holy convocation. You will do no regular work. 
But you will offer an offering made by fire to God seven days. In the seventh day is a holy convocation. You will do no regular work.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, 'When you have come into the land which I give to you, and will reap its harvest, then you will bring the sheaf of the first fruits of your harvest to the priest. 
He will wave the sheaf before God, to be accepted for you. On the next day after the Sabbath the priest will wave it. 
On the day when you wave the sheaf, you will offer a male lamb without defect a year old for a burnt offering to God. 
The meal offering with it will be two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, an offering made by fire to God for a pleasant aroma; and the drink offering with it will be of wine, the fourth part of a hin. 
You must not eat bread, or roasted grain, or fresh grain, until this same day, until you have brought the offering of your God. This is a statute for the eras throughout your generations in all your dwellings. 
"'You will count from the next day after the Sabbath, from the day that you brought the sheaf of the wave offering: seven Sabbaths will be completed. 
The next day after the seventh Sabbath you will count fifty days; and you will offer a new meal offering to God. 
You will bring out of your habitations two loaves of bread for a wave offering made of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour. They will be baked with yeast, for first fruits to God. 
You will present with the bread seven lambs without defect a year old, one young bull, and two rams. They will be a burnt offering to God, with their meal offering and their drink offerings, even an offering made by fire, of a sweet aroma to God. 
You will offer one male goat for an offering for wrongdoing, and two male lambs a year old for a sacrifice of offerings for wholeness. 
The priest will wave them with the bread of the first fruits for a wave offering before God, with the two lambs. They will be holy to God for the priest. 
You will make proclamation on the same day that there will be a holy convocation to you. You will do no regular work. This is a statute for the eras in all your dwellings throughout your generations. 
"'When you reap the harvest of your land, you must not wholly reap into the corners of your field. You must not gather the gleanings of your harvest. You must leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I'm your God.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, there will be a solemn rest for you, a memorial of blowing of trumpets, a holy convocation. 
You will do no regular work. You will offer an offering made by fire to God.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"However on the tenth day of this seventh month is the day of making right. It will be a holy convocation to you. You will afflict yourselves and you will offer an offering made by fire to God. 
You will do no kind of work in that same day, for it's a day of making right, to make things right for you before your God. 
Whoever it's who won't deny himself in that same day will be cut off from his people. 
Whoever does any kind of work in that same day, I'll destroy that person from among his people. 
You will do no kind of work: it's a statute for the eras throughout your generations in all your dwellings. 
It will be a Sabbath of solemn rest for you, and you will deny yourselves. In the ninth day of the month at evening, from evening to evening, you will keep your Sabbath." 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, and say, 'On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the feast of booths for seven days to God. 
On the first day will be a holy convocation. You will do no regular work. 
Seven days you will offer an offering made by fire to God. On the eighth day will be a holy convocation to you. You will offer an offering made by fire to God. It's a solemn assembly; you will do no regular work. 
"'These are the appointed feasts of God which you will proclaim to be holy convocations, to offer an offering made by fire to God, a burnt offering, a meal offering, a sacrifice, and drink offerings, each on its own day 
in addition to the Sabbaths of God, and in addition to your gifts, and in addition to all your vows, and in addition to all your free will offerings, which you give to God. 
"'So on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the fruits of the land, you will keep the feast of God seven days. On the first day will be a solemn rest, and on the eighth day will be a solemn rest. 
You will take on the first day the fruit of majestic trees, branches of palm trees, and boughs of thick trees, and willows of the brook; and you will rejoice before your God seven days. 
You will keep it as a feast to God seven days in the year. It's a statute for the eras throughout your generations. You will keep it in the seventh month. 
You will dwell in temporary shelters for seven days. Everyone who's native-born in Israel will dwell in temporary shelters, 
that your generations may know that I made the children of Israel to dwell in temporary shelters when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I'm your God.'" 
So Moses declared to the children of Israel the appointed feasts of God.
