I love God, because he listens to my voice, and my cries for mercy. 
Because he has turned his ear to me, I'll call on him as long as I live. 
The cords of death surrounded me, the pains of Hades got a hold of me. I found trouble and sorrow. 
Then I called on God's name: "God, I beg you, deliver my soul." 
God is gracious and righteous. Yes, our God is merciful. 
God preserves the simple. I was brought low, and he saved me. 
Return to your rest, my soul, for God has dealt bountifully with you. 
You have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from falling. 
I'll walk before God in the land of the living. 
I believed, I said, "I was greatly afflicted." 
I said in my haste, "All people are liars." 
What will I give to God for all his benefits toward me? 
I'll take the cup of salvation, and call on God's name. 
I'll pay my vows to God, yes, in the presence of all his people. 
Precious in God's sight is the death of his saints. 
God, truly I'm your servant. I'm your servant, the son of your servant girl. You have freed me from my chains. 
I'll offer to you the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call on God's name. 
I'll pay my vows to God, yes, in the presence of all his people, 
in the courts of God's house, in the middle of you, Jerusalem. Praise God!
