Now the serpent was craftier than any animal of the field that God had made. He said to the woman, "Did God really say, 'You must not eat from any tree in the garden'?" 
The woman said to the serpent, "We can eat fruit from the trees of the garden, 
but not the fruit of the tree in the middle of the garden. God said, 'You must not eat from it. You must not touch it, or you'll die.'" 
The serpent said to the woman, "You won't really die, 
for God knows that in the day you eat it, your eyes will be opened, and you'll be like God, knowing good and bad." 
The woman saw that the tree was good for food, pleasant to look at, and desirable for gaining wisdom, so she took some of its fruit and ate it. She also gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate it. 
Their eyes were opened, and they both knew that they were naked. They sewed fig leaves together, and made coverings for themselves. 
They heard God walking in the garden in the cool of the day, and the man and his wife hid from God among the trees of the garden. 
God called to the man, and said to him, "Where are you?" 
The man said, "I heard you in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked; so I hid myself." 
God said, "Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree that I commanded you not to eat from?" 
The man said, "The woman you gave to be with me, she gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate it." 
God said to the woman, "What have you done?" The woman said, "The serpent deceived me, and I ate." 
God said to the serpent, "Because you have done this, you're cursed above all livestock and above every animal of the field. You'll crawl on your belly and eat dust all the days of your life. 
I'll put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring. He'll crush your head, and you'll strike his heel." 
To the woman he said, "I'll greatly multiply your pain in childbirth. You'll bear children in pain. Your desire will be for your husband, and he'll rule over you." 
To Adam he said, "Because you listened to your wife and ate from the tree I commanded you about, saying, 'You must not eat from it,' the ground is cursed because of you. You'll eat from it by hard work all the days of your life. 
It'll grow thorns and thistles for you, and you'll eat the plants of the field. 
You'll eat food by the sweat of your brow until you return to the ground, for you were taken out of it. You're dust, and you'll return to dust." 
The man called his wife Eve because she would be the mother of all the living. 
God made clothes of animal skin for Adam and for his wife, and clothed them. 
God said, "Look, the man has become like one of us, knowing good and bad. He must not be allowed to reach out his hand and take from the tree of life and eat, and live for the eras." 
So God sent him out from the garden of Eden, to work the ground he was taken from. 
So he drove out the man; and he placed cherubim at the east of the garden of Eden, and a flaming sword flashing back and forth to guard the way to the tree of life.
