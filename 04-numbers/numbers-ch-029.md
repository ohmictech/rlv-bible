"'On the first day of the seventh month you are to have a set apart gathering and do no regular work. It is a day for you to sound the trumpets. 
Bring a burnt offering, a soothing aroma to God: one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the ram, 
and a tenth for each of the seven lambs, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, to make things right for you, 
in addition to the new moon burnt offering with its gift offering, and the regular burnt offering with its gift offering and their drink offerings, following the rule, a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to God. 
"'On the tenth day of this seventh month you are to have a set apart gathering, and you are to humble yourselves and do no work at all. 
Bring a burnt offering to God, a soothing aroma: one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old, all without defect, 
with their gift offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for the bull and two tenths for the ram, 
and a tenth for each of the seven lambs, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the offering for wrongdoing that makes things right, and the regular burnt offering with its gift offering and their drink offerings. 
"'On the fifteenth day of the seventh month you are to have a set apart gathering and do no regular work, and you are to keep a feast to God for seven days. 
Bring a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a soothing aroma to God: thirteen young bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, all without defect, 
with their gift offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each of the thirteen bulls, two tenths for each of the two rams, 
and a tenth for each of the fourteen lambs, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'On the second day bring twelve young bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its gift offering and their drink offerings. 
"'On the third day: eleven bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'On the fourth day: ten bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'On the fifth day: nine bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'On the sixth day: eight bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'On the seventh day: seven bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'On the eighth day you are to have a closing assembly and do no regular work, 
but bring a burnt offering, an offering made by fire, a soothing aroma to God: one bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old without defect, 
with their gift offering and drink offerings for the bull, the ram and the lambs, by their number, following the rule, 
and one male goat as an offering for wrongdoing, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its gift offering and its drink offering. 
"'Bring these to God at your appointed feasts, in addition to your vows and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, your gift offerings, your drink offerings and your wholeness offerings.'" 
Moses told the Israelites everything God commanded Moses.
