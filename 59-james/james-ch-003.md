Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers and sisters, because you know that we who teach will face a stricter verdict. 
We all trip up in many ways. Anyone who never trips up in what they say is complete, and able to control their whole body too. 
We put bits in horses' mouths to make them obey us, and we turn their whole body. 
Look at ships too: they are so big and are driven by fierce winds, yet a very small rudder turns them wherever the pilot wants. 
In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body, but it makes big claims. See how a tiny spark can set a whole forest on fire! 
And the tongue is a fire. Among the parts of the body, the tongue is a whole world of wrong, and it makes the whole body unclean. It sets the whole course of a life on fire, and it is set on fire by Gehenna. 
Every kind of animal, bird, reptile, and sea creature has been tamed, and is tamed, by people; 
but nobody can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison. 
With it we bless our God and Father, and with it we curse people who are made in God's image. 
Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers and sisters, it shouldn't be like this. 
Does a spring pour fresh water and bitter water from the same opening? 
My brothers and sisters, can a fig tree produce olives, or a vine produce figs? And a salt spring can't give fresh water either. 
Who among you is wise and understanding? Let them show it by living well, and by doing their deeds in the gentleness that comes from wisdom. 
But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, don't brag about it and don't lie against the truth. 
That kind of wisdom doesn't come down from above. It is of the earth, it is unspiritual, and it belongs to unclean spirits. 
Where there is jealousy and selfish ambition, there is confusion and every kind of bad deed. 
But the wisdom from above is first of all pure, then it makes for wholeness, it is gentle and willing to listen, it is full of mercy and good fruit, and it doesn't play favorites or pretend. 
And the fruit of rightness is sown in wholeness by those who make wholeness.
