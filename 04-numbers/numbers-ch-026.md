After the plague, God spoke to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest: 
"Take a census of the whole community of Israel, twenty years old and up, by family, everyone able to serve in the army in Israel." 
Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho: 
"Take a census of everyone twenty years old and up, as God commanded Moses." These are the ones who came out of Egypt. 
Reuben, Israel's firstborn. The sons of Reuben: from Hanoch, the Hanochite family; from Pallu, the Palluite family; 
from Hezron, the Hezronite family; from Carmi, the Carmite family. 
These are the Reubenite families, and those counted were forty-three thousand seven hundred thirty. 
The son of Pallu was Eliab. 
The sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan and Abiram. These are the Dathan and Abiram who were chosen by the community and who rebelled against Moses and Aaron in Korah's group when they rebelled against God. 
The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them with Korah when that group died, at the time the fire burned up two hundred fifty men, and they became a warning sign. 
But the sons of Korah didn't die. 
The sons of Simeon by family: from Nemuel, the Nemuelite family; from Jamin, the Jaminite family; from Jachin, the Jachinite family; 
from Zerah, the Zerahite family; from Shaul, the Shaulite family. 
These are the Simeonite families, twenty-two thousand two hundred. 
The sons of Gad by family: from Zephon, the Zephonite family; from Haggi, the Haggite family; from Shuni, the Shunite family; 
from Ozni, the Oznite family; from Eri, the Erite family; 
from Arod, the Arodite family; from Areli, the Arelite family. 
These are the families of Gad, and those counted were forty thousand five hundred. 
The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. 
The sons of Judah by family were: from Shelah, the Shelanite family; from Perez, the Perezite family; from Zerah, the Zerahite family. 
The sons of Perez were: from Hezron, the Hezronite family; from Hamul, the Hamulite family. 
These are the families of Judah, and those counted were seventy-six thousand five hundred. 
The sons of Issachar by family: from Tola, the Tolaites; from Puvah, the Punites; 
from Jashub, the Jashubites; from Shimron, the Shimronites. 
These are the families of Issachar, and those counted were sixty-four thousand three hundred. 
The sons of Zebulun by family: from Sered, the Seredites; from Elon, the Elonites; from Jahleel, the Jahleelites. 
These are the families of Zebulun, and those counted were sixty thousand five hundred. 
The sons of Joseph by family were Manasseh and Ephraim. 
The sons of Manasseh: from Machir, the Machirites, and Machir was the father of Gilead; from Gilead, the Gileadites. 
These are the sons of Gilead: from Iezer, the Iezerites; from Helek, the Helekites; 
from Asriel, the Asrielites; from Shechem, the Shechemites; 
from Shemida, the Shemidaites; from Hepher, the Hepherites. 
Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters, and the names of Zelophehad's daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah. 
These are the families of Manasseh, and those counted were fifty-two thousand seven hundred. 
These are the sons of Ephraim by family: from Shuthelah, the Shuthelahites; from Becher, the Becherites; from Tahan, the Tahanites. 
These are the sons of Shuthelah: from Eran, the Eranites. 
These are the families of the sons of Ephraim, and those counted were thirty-two thousand five hundred. These are the sons of Joseph by family. 
The sons of Benjamin by family: from Bela, the Belaites; from Ashbel, the Ashbelites; from Ahiram, the Ahiramites; 
from Shephupham, the Shuphamites; from Hupham, the Huphamites. 
The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: from Ard, the Ardites; from Naaman, the Naamites. 
These are the sons of Benjamin by family, and those counted were forty-five thousand six hundred. 
These are the sons of Dan by family: from Shuham, the Shuhamites. These are the families of Dan by family. 
All the families of the Shuhamites, those counted, were sixty-four thousand four hundred. 
The sons of Asher by family: from Imnah, the Imnites; from Ishvi, the Ishvites; from Beriah, the Berites. 
From the sons of Beriah: from Heber, the Heberites; from Malchiel, the Malchielites. 
The name of Asher's daughter was Serah. 
These are the families of Asher, and those counted were fifty-three thousand four hundred. 
The sons of Naphtali by family: from Jahzeel, the Jahzeelites; from Guni, the Gunites; 
from Jezer, the Jezerites; from Shillem, the Shillemites. 
These are the families of Naphtali, and those counted were forty-five thousand four hundred. 
These are the Israelites who were counted, six hundred one thousand seven hundred thirty. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"The land is to be divided among these as their inheritance, by the number of names. 
To the larger group give a larger inheritance, and to the smaller group a smaller one. Each is to receive its inheritance according to how many were counted. 
But the land is to be divided by lot. They are to inherit by the names of their ancestral tribes. 
The inheritance is to be divided by lot between the larger and the smaller." 
These are the Levites who were counted by family: from Gershon, the Gershonites; from Kohath, the Kohathites; from Merari, the Merarites. 
These are the families of Levi: the Libnites, the Hebronites, the Mahlites, the Mushites and the Korahites. Kohath was the father of Amram. 
Amram's wife was named Jochebed, a daughter of Levi, born to Levi in Egypt. She bore to Amram Aaron, Moses and their sister Miriam. 
To Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. 
Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire before God. 
Those counted were twenty-three thousand, every male a month old and up, because they weren't counted with the Israelites, since no inheritance was given to them among the Israelites. 
These are the ones counted by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who counted the Israelites on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho. 
But none of them were among those counted by Moses and Aaron the priest when they counted the Israelites in the Sinai wilderness, 
because God had said of them, "They will surely die in the wilderness." Not one of them was left, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
