Moses and the elders of Israel commanded the people, "Keep the whole command I am giving you today. 
On the day you cross the Jordan into the land your God is giving you, set up large stones and coat them with plaster. 
Write all the words of this law on them after you have crossed over, so that you may go in to the land your God is giving you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as the God of your fathers promised you. 
When you have crossed the Jordan, set up these stones, which I am commanding you today, on Mount Ebal, and coat them with plaster. 
There build an altar to your God, an altar of stones. Don't use any iron tool on them. 
Build the altar of your God out of uncut stones, and offer burnt offerings on it to your God. 
Sacrifice wholeness offerings and eat there, and celebrate before your God. 
Write all the words of this law on the stones very clearly." 
Moses and the Levite priests spoke to all Israel, saying, "Be quiet and listen, Israel! Today you have become the people of your God. 
Obey the voice of your God and do his commands and his rules that I am giving you today." 
Moses commanded the people that same day, 
"These are to stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people when you have crossed the Jordan: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph and Benjamin. 
And these are to stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan and Naphtali. 
Then the Levites will say in a loud voice to all the men of Israel, 
'Cursed is the man who makes a carved or metal image, disgusting to God, the work of a craftsman's hands, and sets it up in secret.' Everyone will answer and say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who dishonors his father or his mother.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who moves his neighbor's boundary stone.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who leads a blind person off the road.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who denies justice to a foreigner, an orphan or a widow.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who has sex with his father's wife, because that is uncovering what belongs to his father.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who has sex with an animal.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who has sex with his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who has sex with his mother-in-law.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who secretly kills his neighbor.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who takes a bribe to kill an innocent person.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.' 
'Cursed is anyone who doesn't hold to the words of this law by doing them.' Everyone will say, 'Amen.'"
