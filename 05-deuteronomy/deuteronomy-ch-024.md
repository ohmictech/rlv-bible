"If a man marries a woman and she doesn't please him because he finds something indecent about her, he may write her a certificate of divorce, put it in her hand and send her out of his house. 
After she leaves his house, she may go and marry another man. 
If the second husband turns against her and writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand and sends her out of his house, or if the second husband dies, 
then her first husband, who sent her away, may not take her back as his wife, because she has been made unclean. That would be disgusting to God. Don't bring guilt on the land your God is giving you as an inheritance. 
If a man has just married, he must not go out with the army, and no duty is to be laid on him. He is to be free at home for one year and make his wife happy. 
No one may take a mill or its upper stone as a pledge, because that would be taking someone's life as a pledge. 
If a man is caught kidnapping one of his brothers among the Israelites, and treats him as a slave or sells him, that kidnapper must die. So get rid of the evil among you. 
Be very careful about a serious skin disease, and do everything the Levite priests teach you. Do just as I commanded them. 
Remember what your God did to Miriam on the way as you came out of Egypt. 
When you lend your neighbor anything, don't go into his house to take his pledge. 
Stand outside, and the man you are lending to will bring the pledge out to you. 
If he is a poor man, don't keep his pledge overnight. 
Give his pledge back to him at sunset so he can sleep in his cloak and bless you. That will be counted as right before your God. 
Don't cheat a hired worker who is poor and needy, whether he is one of your brothers or a foreigner living in your land, inside your towns. 
Pay him his wages that same day, before the sun goes down, because he is poor and is counting on it. Otherwise he will cry out against you to God, and you will be held guilty. 
Fathers must not be put to death for their children, and children must not be put to death for their fathers. Each person is put to death for his own wrong. 
Don't deny justice to a foreigner or an orphan, and don't take a widow's clothing as a pledge. 
Remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and your God bought you back from there. That is why I command you to do this. 
When you harvest your field and leave a sheaf behind, don't go back to get it. Leave it for the foreigner, the orphan and the widow, so that your God may bless you in all the work of your hands. 
When you beat your olive trees, don't go over the branches again. Leave what's left for the foreigner, the orphan and the widow. 
When you harvest your vineyard, don't go over it a second time. Leave what's left for the foreigner, the orphan and the widow. 
Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. That is why I command you to do this.
