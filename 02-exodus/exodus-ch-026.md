"Moreover you will make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, with cherubim. You will make them with the work of a skillful workman. 
The length of each curtain will be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits: all the curtains will have one measure. 
Five curtains will be coupled together to one another, and the other five curtains will be coupled to one another. 
You will make loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain from the edge in the coupling, and you will do likewise on the edge of the curtain that's outermost in the second coupling. 
You will make fifty loops in the one curtain, and you will make fifty loops in the edge of the curtain that's in the second coupling. The loops will be opposite one another. 
You will make fifty clasps of gold, and couple the curtains to one another with the clasps. The tabernacle will be a unit. 
"You will make curtains of goats' hair for a covering over the tabernacle. You will make eleven curtains. 
The length of each curtain will be thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits: the eleven curtains will have one measure. 
You will couple five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves, and will double over the sixth curtain in the forefront of the tent. 
You will make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain that's outermost in the coupling, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain which is outermost in the second coupling. 
You will make fifty clasps of bronze, and put the clasps into the loops, and couple the tent together, that it might be one. 
The overhanging part that remains of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that remains, will hang over the back of the tabernacle. 
The cubit on the one side and the cubit on the other side, of that which remains in the length of the curtains of the tent, will hang over the sides of the tabernacle on this side and on that side, to cover it. 
You will make a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of sea cow hides above. 
"You will make the boards for the tabernacle of acacia wood, standing upright. 
Ten cubits will be the length of a board, and one and a half cubits the width of each board. 
There will be two tenons in each board, joined to one another: thus you will make for all the boards of the tabernacle. 
You will make twenty boards for the tabernacle, for the south side southward. 
You will make forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards; two sockets under one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under another board for its two tenons. 
The second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, twenty boards, 
and their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board. 
The far side of the tabernacle westward you will make six boards. 
You will make two boards for the corners of the tabernacle in the far side. 
They will be double beneath, and in the same way they will be whole to its top to one ring: thus will it be for them both; they will be for the two corners. 
There will be eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board. 
"You will make bars of acacia wood: five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle, 
and five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the side of the tabernacle, for the far side westward. 
The middle bar in the middle of the boards will pass through from end to end. 
You will overlay the boards with gold, and make their rings of gold for places for the bars. You will overlay the bars with gold. 
You will set up the tabernacle according to the way that it was shown to you on the mountain. 
"You will make a veil of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, with cherubim. It will be the work of a skillful workman. 
You will hang it on four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold; their hooks will be of gold, on four sockets of silver. 
You will hang up the veil under the clasps, and will bring the ark of the covenant in there within the veil. The veil will separate the holy place from the most holy for you. 
You will put the mercy seat on the ark of the covenant in the most holy place. 
You will set the table outside the veil, and the lamp stand opposite the table on the side of the tabernacle toward the south. You will put the table on the north side. 
"You will make a screen for the door of the Tent, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, the work of the embroiderer. 
You will make for the screen five pillars of acacia, and overlay them with gold. Their hooks will be of gold. You will cast five sockets of bronze for them.
