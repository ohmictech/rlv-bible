Then I looked, and there was the Lamb standing on Mount Zion, and with him one hundred forty-four thousand who had his name and his Father's name written on their foreheads. 
I heard a sound from the sky like the sound of rushing waters and like a loud peal of thunder. The sound I heard was like harpists playing their harps. 
They sing a new song in front of the throne, in front of the four living creatures, and in front of the elders. No one could learn the song except the one hundred forty-four thousand who had been bought back from the land. 
These are the ones who have not made themselves unclean with women, because they are virgins. They follow the Lamb wherever he goes. They were bought back by Jesus from among people, the firstfruits for God and for the Lamb. 
No lie was found in their mouths; they are without fault. 
Then I saw another messenger flying in mid-sky, with good news of the era to announce to those who live on the land, to every nation, tribe, language, and people. 
He said in a loud voice, "Fear the Lord and give him weight, because the hour of his verdict has come. Bow down to the one who made the sky, the land, the sea, and the springs of water!" 
A second messenger followed, saying, "Babylon the great has fallen! She made all the nations drink the wine of the heat of her sleeping around." 
A third messenger followed them, saying in a loud voice, "If anyone bows down to the beast and its image, and receives a mark on their forehead or on their hand, 
they too will drink the wine of God's heat, poured full strength into the cup of his anger. They will be put in anguish with fire and sulfur in front of the set-apart messengers and in front of the Lamb. 
The smoke of their anguish rises for all the eras, and those who bow down to the beast and its image, and anyone who receives the mark of its name, have no rest day or night. 
This is where the set-apart ones need staying power, those who keep God's commands and hold to the trust of Jesus." 
Then I heard a voice from the sky saying, "Write this down: 'Flourishing are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.'" "Yes," says the Spirit, "they will rest from their hard work, because their deeds follow them." 
Then I looked, and there was a white cloud, and seated on the cloud was someone like a human being, with a gold wreath on his head and a sharp sickle in his hand. 
Another messenger came out of the temple, calling in a loud voice to the one sitting on the cloud, "Swing your sickle and reap, because the hour to reap has come and the harvest of the land is ripe!" 
The one sitting on the cloud swung his sickle over the land, and the land was harvested. 
Another messenger came out of the temple in the sky, and he too had a sharp sickle. 
Then another messenger came out from the altar, the one who has power over fire, and he called in a loud voice to the one with the sharp sickle, "Swing your sharp sickle and gather the clusters from the vine of the land, because its grapes are ripe!" 
The messenger swung his sickle over the land, gathered the grapes of the land, and threw them into the great winepress of God's heat. 
The winepress was trampled outside the city, and blood flowed out of the winepress as high as the horses' bridles, for one thousand six hundred stadia.
