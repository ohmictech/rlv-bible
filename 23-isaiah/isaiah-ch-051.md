"Listen to me, you who follow after righteousness, you who seek God. Look to the rock you were cut from, and to the quarry you were dug from. 
Look to Abraham your father, and to Sarah who bore you; for when he was but one I called him, I blessed him, and made him many. 
God has comforted Zion. He has comforted all her waste places, and has made her wilderness like Eden, and her desert like the garden of God. Joy and gladness will be found in them, thanksgiving, and the voice of melody. 
"Listen to me, my people; and hear me, my nation, for a law will go out from me, and I'll establish my justice for a light to the peoples. 
My righteousness is near. My salvation has gone out, and my arms will judge the peoples. The islands will wait for me, and they'll trust my arm. 
Lift up your eyes to the sky, and look at the earth beneath; for the sky will vanish away like smoke, and the earth will wear out like a garment. Its inhabitants will die in the same way, but my salvation will be for the eras, and my righteousness won't be abolished. 
"Listen to me, you who know righteousness, the people in whose heart is my law. Don't fear the reproach of men, and don't be dismayed at their insults. 
The moth will eat them up like a garment, and the worm will eat them like wool; but my righteousness will be for the eras, and my salvation to all generations." 
Awake, awake, put on strength, arm of God! Awake, as in the days of old, the generations of ancient times. Isn't it you who cut Rahab in pieces, who pierced the monster? 
Isn't it you who dried up the sea, the waters of the great deep; who made the depths of the sea a way for the bought back to pass over? 
Those ransomed by God will return, and come with singing to Zion. Perpetual joy shall be on their heads. They'll obtain gladness and joy. Sorrow and sighing shall flee away. 
"I, even I, am he who comforts you. Who are you, that you're afraid of man who shall die, and of the son of man who will be made as grass? 
Have you forgotten God your Maker, who stretched out the sky, and laid the foundations of the earth? Do you live in fear continually all day because of the fury of the oppressor, when he prepares to destroy? Where is the fury of the oppressor? 
The captive exile will speedily be freed. He won't die and go down into the pit. His bread won't fail. 
I'm your God, who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar. the God of Armies is his name. 
I have put my words in your mouth and have covered you in the shadow of my hand, that I may plant the sky, and lay the foundations of the earth, and tell Zion, 'You're my people.'" 
Awake, awake! Stand up, Jerusalem, you who have drunk from God's hand the cup of his wrath. You have drunken the bowl of the cup of staggering, and drained it. 
There's no one to guide her among all the sons to whom she has given birth; and there's no one who takes her by the hand among all the sons whom she has brought up. 
These two things have happened to you, who will grieve with you?, desolation and destruction, and famine and the sword. How can I comfort you? 
Your sons have fainted. They lie at the head of all the streets, like an antelope in a net. They're full of God's wrath, the rebuke of your God. 
So now hear this, you afflicted, and drunken, but not with wine: 
Your God, your God who pleads the cause of his people, says, "Look, I have taken out of your hand the cup of staggering, even the bowl of the cup of my wrath. You won't drink it any more. 
I'll put it into the hand of those who afflict you, who have said to your soul, 'Bow down, that we may walk over you;' and you have laid your back as the ground, like a street to those who walk over."
