God spoke all these words, saying, 
"I'm your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. 
"Don't have any other gods before me. 
"Don't make for yourselves an idol, nor any image of anything that's in the sky above, or that's on the earth beneath, or that's in the water under the earth: 
you won't bow yourself down to them, nor serve them, for I, your God, am a jealous God, visiting the guilt of the fathers on the children, on the third and on the fourth generation of those who hate me, 
and showing loving kindness to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments. 
"Don't misuse the name of your God, for God won't hold him guiltless who misuses his name. 
"Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. 
You will labor six days, and do all your work, 
but the seventh day is a Sabbath to your God. Don't do any work in it, you, nor your son, nor your daughter, your male servant, nor your female servant, nor your livestock, nor your stranger who's within your gates; 
for in six days God made the sky and the earth, the sea, and all that's in them, and rested the seventh day; God blessed the Sabbath day, and made it holy. 
"Honor your father and your mother, that your days may be long in the land which your God gives you. 
"Don't murder. 
"Don't commit adultery. 
"Don't steal. 
"Don't give false testimony against your neighbor. 
"Don't covet your neighbor's house. Don't covet your neighbor's wife, nor his male servant, nor his female servant, nor his ox, nor his donkey, nor anything that's your neighbor's." 
Everyone perceived the thunderings, the lightnings, the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking. When the people saw it, they trembled, and stayed at a distance. 
They said to Moses, "Speak with us yourself, and we'll listen; but don't let God speak with us, lest we die." 
Moses said to the people, "Don't be afraid, for God has come to test you, and that his fear may be before you, that you won't drift." 
The people stayed at a distance, and Moses came near to the thick darkness where God was. 
God said to Moses, "This is what you will tell the children of Israel: 'You yourselves have seen that I have talked with you from the sky. 
You will most certainly not make gods of silver or gods of gold for yourselves to be alongside me. 
You will make an altar of earth for me, and will sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your offerings for wholeness, your sheep and your cattle. In every place where I record my name I'll come to you and I'll bless you. 
If you make me an altar of stone, you won't build it of cut stones; for if you lift up your tool on it, you have polluted it. 
Don't go up by steps to my altar, that your nakedness can't be exposed to it.'
