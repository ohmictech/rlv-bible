Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon, who's truly loved and who works alongside us, 
to Apphia, who's truly loved, to Archippus who fights alongside us, and to the gathering in your house: 
Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
I always give thanks to my God when I mention you in my prayers, 
because I hear of your love and of the trust you have toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the set-apart ones, 
I pray that the sharing that comes from your trust may become active as you know every good thing that's in us, in Christ Jesus. 
Your love has given me much joy and comfort, because the hearts of the set-apart ones have been given relief through you, brother. 
So although I have plenty of boldness in Christ to tell you what you should do, 
still, for love's sake I'd rather ask you (I, Paul, an old man, and now a prisoner of Jesus Christ as well). 
I'm asking you on behalf of my child Onesimus, whose father I became while I was in chains. 
Once he was useless to you, but now he's useful to you and to me. 
I'm sending him back to you (him, that's, my own heart). 
I'd have liked to keep him with me, so that he could serve me in your place while I'm in chains for the Good News. 
But I didn't want to do anything without your consent, so that your kindness wouldn't be a matter of necessity, but of your own free choice. 
Maybe this is why he was separated from you for a while, so that you could have him back for good, 
no longer as a slave, but as more than a slave: a brother who's truly loved, especially to me, but how much more to you, both as a person and in the Lord. 
So if you count me as a partner, welcome him the way you'd welcome me. 
If he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to me. 
I, Paul, am writing this with my own hand: I'll pay it back, and I won't mention that you owe me your own self as well. 
Yes, brother, let me get some good out of you in the Lord. Give my heart relief in the Lord. 
I write to you trusting that you'll do as I ask, and I know that you'll do even more than I say. 
At the same time, prepare a guest room for me, because I hope that through your prayers I'll be given back to you. 
Epaphras, who's a prisoner with me in Christ Jesus, sends you greetings, 
and so do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, who work alongside me. 
May the favor of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.
