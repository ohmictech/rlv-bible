God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, set your face toward Gog, of the land of Magog, the prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal, and prophesy against him, 
and say, 'The God says: "Look, I'm against you, Gog, prince of Rosh, Meshech, and Tubal. 
I'll turn you around, and put hooks into your jaws, and I'll bring you out, with all your army, horses and horsemen, everyone clothed in full armor, a great company with buckler and shield, everyone handling swords; 
Persia, Cush, and Put with them, everyone with shield and helmet; 
Gomer, and all his hordes; the house of Togarmah in the uttermost parts of the north, and all his hordes, even many peoples with you. 
"'"Be prepared, yes, prepare yourself, you, and all your companies who are assembled to you, and be a guard to them. 
After many days you'll be visited. In the latter years you'll come into the land that's brought back from the sword, that's gathered out of many peoples, on the mountains of Israel, which have been a continual waste; but it's brought out of the peoples and they'll dwell securely, everyone. 
You'll ascend. You'll come like a storm. You'll be like a cloud to cover the land, you and all your hordes, and many peoples with you." 
"'The God says: "It'll happen in that day that things will come into your mind, and you'll devise an bad plan. 
You'll say, 'I'll go up to the land of unwalled villages. I'll go to those who are at rest, who dwell securely, everyone dwelling without walls, and having neither bars nor gates, 
to take the plunder and to take prey; to turn your hand against the waste places that are inhabited, and against the people who are gathered out of the nations, who have gotten livestock and goods, who dwell in the middle of the earth.' 
Sheba, Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish, with all its young lions, will ask you, 'Have you come to take the plunder? Have you assembled your company to take the prey, to carry away silver and gold, to take away livestock and goods, to take great plunder?'"' 
"Therefore, son of man, prophesy, and tell Gog, 'The God says: "In that day when my people Israel dwells securely, will you not know it? 
You'll come from your place out of the uttermost parts of the north, you, and many peoples with you, everyone riding on horses, a great company and a mighty army. 
You'll come up against my people Israel as a cloud to cover the land. It'll happen in the latter days that I'll bring you against my land, that the nations may know me when I'm sanctified in you, Gog, before their eyes." 
"'The God says: "Are you he of whom I spoke in old time by my servants the spokesmen of Israel, who prophesied in those days for years that I'd bring you against them? 
It'll happen in that day, when Gog comes against the land of Israel," says God, "that my wrath will come up into my nostrils. 
In my jealousy and in the fire of my wrath I have spoken. Surely in that day there will be a great shaking in the land of Israel, 
so that the fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the animals of the field, all creeping things who creep on the earth, and all the men who are on the surface of the earth will shake at my presence. Then the mountains will be thrown down, the steep places will fall, and every wall will fall to the ground. 
I'll call for a sword against him to all my mountains," says God. "Every man's sword will be against his brother. 
I'll enter into judgment with him with pestilence and with blood. I'll rain on him, on his hordes, and on the many peoples who are with him, torrential rains with great hailstones, fire, and sulfur. 
I'll magnify myself and sanctify myself, and I'll make myself known in the eyes of many nations. Then they'll know that I'm God."'
