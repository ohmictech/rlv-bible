When he entered again into Capernaum after some days, it was heard that he was at home. 
Immediately many gathered together, so that there was no more room, not even around the door, and he spoke the word to them. 
Four people came, carrying a paralyzed man to him. 
When they couldn't get near him because of the crowd, they made an opening in the roof above him. When they had broken through, they lowered the mat the paralyzed man was lying on. 
Jesus, seeing their trust, said to the paralyzed man, "Son, your mistakes are released." 
But some of the Bible teachers were sitting there, reasoning in their hearts, 
"Why does this man speak like that? He is slandering! Who can release mistakes but God alone?" 
Immediately Jesus, knowing in his spirit that they were reasoning this way among themselves, said to them, "Why are you reasoning like this in your hearts? 
Which is easier, to tell the paralyzed man, 'Your mistakes are released,' or to say, 'Get up, take your mat, and walk'? 
But so that you may know that the Human One has authority on earth to release mistakes" (he said to the paralyzed man), 
"I tell you, get up, take your mat, and go to your house." 
He got up, and immediately took the mat and went out in front of them all, so that they were all amazed and gave weight to God, saying, "We never saw anything like this!" 
He went out again by the seashore. All the crowd came to him, and he taught them. 
As he passed by, he saw Levi the son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax collection office. He said to him, "Follow me." And he got up and followed him. 
He was at the table in his house, and many tax collectors and those who have drifted sat down with Jesus and his students, for there were many, and they followed him. 
The Bible teachers and the strict religious ones, when they saw that he was eating with those who have drifted and tax collectors, said to his students, "Why does he eat and drink with tax collectors and those who have drifted?" 
When Jesus heard it, he said to them, "Those who are healthy have no need for a doctor, but those who are sick do. I came not to call the ones in the right, but to call those who have drifted to a change of heart." 
John's students and the strict religious ones were going without food, and they came and asked him, "Why do John's students and the students of the strict religious ones go without food, but your students don't?" 
Jesus said to them, "Can the groomsmen go without food while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they can't go without food. 
But the days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then, on that day, they will go without food. 
No one sews a piece of unshrunk cloth onto an old garment, or the patch shrinks and the new tears away from the old, and a worse hole is made. 
No one puts new wine into old wineskins, or the new wine will burst the skins, and the wine pours out and the skins are ruined. New wine goes into fresh wineskins." 
He was going through the grain fields on the Sabbath, and as they went his students began to pluck heads of grain. 
The strict religious ones said to him, "Look, why are they doing what isn't allowed on the Sabbath?" 
He said to them, "Have you never read what David did when he was in need and hungry (he and those who were with him)? 
How he went into the house of God when Abiathar was high priest, and ate the bread set out before God, which isn't allowed for anyone to eat except the priests, and he gave some to those who were with him?" 
He said to them, "The Sabbath was made for people, not people for the Sabbath. 
So the Human One is lord even of the Sabbath."
