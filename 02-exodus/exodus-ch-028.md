"Bring your brother Aaron and his sons with him close to you from among the Israelites, so he can serve me as priest: Aaron, and Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's sons. 
Make set apart garments for your brother Aaron, for honor and beauty. 
Speak to all the skilled craftsmen I have filled with the spirit of wisdom, and have them make Aaron's garments to set him apart, so he can serve me as priest. 
These are the garments they are to make: a breastplate, an ephod, a robe, a woven tunic, a turban and a sash. They are to make set apart garments for your brother Aaron and his sons, so they can serve me as priest. 
They are to use the gold, the blue, the purple, the scarlet and the fine linen. 
"They are to make the ephod of gold, blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of a skilled craftsman. 
It is to have two shoulder straps joined to its two ends, so it holds together. 
The woven band on it is to be of the same work and one piece with it, of gold, blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen. 
Take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel, 
six names on one stone and the other six on the other, in order of their birth. 
Engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel the way a seal is engraved, and set them in gold settings. 
Put the two stones on the shoulder straps of the ephod as memorial stones for the Israelites. Aaron will carry their names before God on his two shoulders as a memorial. 
Make gold settings, 
and two chains of pure gold, braided like cords, and fasten the braided chains to the settings. 
"Make a breastplate of judgment, the work of a skilled craftsman. Make it like the ephod, of gold, blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen. 
It is to be square and folded double, a span long and a span wide. 
Set four rows of stones in it. The first row is ruby, topaz and beryl; 
the second row turquoise, sapphire and emerald; 
the third row jacinth, agate and amethyst; 
and the fourth row chrysolite, onyx and jasper. They are to be set in gold settings. 
The stones are to be twelve, one for each of the names of the sons of Israel, each engraved like a seal with the name of one of the twelve tribes. 
Make chains of pure gold on the breastplate, braided like cords. 
Make two gold rings for the breastplate and put them on its two ends. 
Put the two braided gold chains in the two rings at the ends of the breastplate. 
Fasten the other two ends of the chains to the two settings, and attach them to the shoulder straps of the ephod at its front. 
Make two gold rings and put them on the two ends of the breastplate, on its inner edge, toward the ephod. 
Make two more gold rings and put them on the two shoulder straps of the ephod underneath, at its front, near where it joins, above the woven band of the ephod. 
Tie the breastplate by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, so it sits above the woven band of the ephod and the breastplate can't come loose from the ephod. 
Aaron will carry the names of the sons of Israel over his heart on the breastplate of judgment when he goes into the Holy Place, as a memorial before God at all times. 
Put the Urim and the Thummim in the breastplate of judgment, and they will be over Aaron's heart when he goes in before God. Aaron will carry the judgment of the Israelites over his heart before God at all times. 
"Make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue. 
It is to have an opening for the head in the middle of it, with a woven binding around the opening, like the opening of a coat of armor, so it doesn't tear. 
On its hem make pomegranates of blue, purple and scarlet yarn all around, with gold bells between them, 
a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe. 
Aaron is to wear it when he serves, and its sound will be heard when he goes into the Holy Place before God and when he comes out, so he doesn't die. 
"Make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it, the way a seal is engraved, 'SET APART FOR GOD.' 
Fasten it to a blue cord, and it is to be on the turban, on the front of it. 
It is to be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron will carry the guilt of the holy things the Israelites set apart in all their holy gifts, and it is to be on his forehead at all times, so they are accepted before God. 
Weave the tunic of fine linen, and make the turban of fine linen, and make the sash of embroidered work. 
"Make tunics for Aaron's sons, and sashes, and headbands, for honor and beauty. 
Put them on your brother Aaron and on his sons with him, and anoint them, ordain them and set them apart, so they can serve me as priest. 
Make linen undergarments to cover their naked flesh, reaching from the waist to the thighs. 
They are to be worn by Aaron and his sons whenever they go into the Tent of Meeting or come near the altar to serve in the Holy Place, so they don't carry guilt and die. This is a rule for the eras for him and for his descendants after him.
