Blessed is the man who doesn't walk in the counsel of the guilty, nor stand on the path of those who have drifted, nor sit in the seat of scoffers; 
but his delight is in God's law. On his law he meditates day and night. 
He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water, that produces its fruit in its season, whose leaf also doesn't wither. Whatever he does shall prosper. 
The guilty aren't so, but are like the chaff which the wind drives away. 
So the guilty shall not stand in the judgment, nor those who have drifted in the congregation of the righteous. 
God knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the guilty shall perish.
