Then I saw another great and wonderful sign in the sky: seven messengers with the seven last plagues, because in them God's heat comes to an end. 
And I saw something like a sea of glass mixed with fire, and those who had won the victory over the beast, its image, and the number of its name, standing beside the sea of glass, holding harps given by God. 
They sang the song of Moses, the slave of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying, "Great and wonderful are your deeds, Lord God, the one who holds sway over all! Right and true are your ways, King of the nations. 
Who wouldn't fear you, Lord, and give honor to your name? For you only are set apart. For all the nations will come and bow down in front of you. For your right acts have been shown openly." 
After this I looked, and the temple in the sky was opened, the tent of the witness. 
The seven messengers who had the seven plagues came out of it, dressed in clean, bright linen, with golden sashes around their chests. 
One of the four living creatures gave the seven messengers seven golden bowls full of the heat of God, who lives through all the eras. 
The temple was filled with smoke from the weight of God and from his power, and no one was able to go into the temple until the seven plagues of the seven messengers were finished.
