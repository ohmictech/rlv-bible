I saw another mighty messenger coming down out of the sky, wrapped in a cloud, with a rainbow over his head. His face was like the sun, and his legs were like pillars of fire. 
In his hand he held a little scroll, opened. He set his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land. 
He called out in a loud voice, the way a lion roars. When he called out, the seven thunders answered with their voices. 
When the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write it down, but I heard a voice from the sky saying, "Seal up what the seven thunders said, and don't write it down." 
Then the messenger I had seen standing on the sea and on the land raised his right hand to the sky 
and swore by the one who lives through all the eras, who created the sky and everything in it, the land and everything in it, and the sea and everything in it, that there would be no more delay, 
but in the days when the seventh messenger is about to sound his trumpet, God's secret will be finished, just as he announced to his slaves the spokesmen. 
Then the voice I had heard from the sky spoke to me again and said, "Go, take the scroll that's open in the hand of the messenger standing on the sea and on the land." 
I went to the messenger and asked him to give me the little scroll. He said to me, "Take it and eat it. It'll turn your stomach bitter, but in your mouth it'll be as sweet as honey." 
I took the little scroll out of the messenger's hand and ate it. It was as sweet as honey in my mouth, but when I had eaten it, my stomach turned bitter. 
They told me, "You must speak for God again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings."
