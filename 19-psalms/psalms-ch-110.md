A Psalm by David. God says to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand, until I make your enemies your footstool for your feet." 
God will send out the rod of your strength out of Zion. Rule among your enemies. 
Your people offer themselves willingly in the day of your power, in holy array. Out of the womb of the morning, you have the dew of your youth. 
God has sworn, and won't change his mind: "You're a priest for the eras in the order of Melchizedek." 
The Lord is at your right hand. He'll crush kings in the day of his wrath. 
He'll judge among the nations. He'll heap up dead bodies. He'll crush the ruler of the whole earth. 
He'll drink of the brook on the way; he'll lift up his head.
