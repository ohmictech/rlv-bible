A revelation about Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite. 
God is a jealous God and avenges. God avenges and is full of wrath. God takes vengeance on his adversaries, and he maintains wrath against his enemies. 
God is slow to anger, and great in power, and will by no means leave the guilty unpunished. God has his way in the whirlwind and in the storm, and the clouds are the dust of his feet. 
He rebukes the sea and makes it dry, and dries up all the rivers. Bashan and Carmel languish. The flower of Lebanon languishes. 
The mountains quake before him, and the hills melt away. The earth trembles at his presence, yes, the world, and everyone who dwells in it. 
Who can stand before his indignation? Who can endure the fierceness of his anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are broken apart by him. 
God is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble; and he knows those who take refuge in him. 
But with an overflowing flood, he'll make a full end of her place, and will pursue his enemies into darkness. 
What do you plot against God? He'll make a full end. Affliction won't rise up the second time. 
Entangled like thorns, and drunken as with their drink, they're consumed utterly like dry stubble. 
One has gone out of you who devises bad against God, who counsels guilt. 
God says: "Though they're in full strength and likewise many, even so they'll be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I'll afflict you no more. 
Now I'll break his yoke from off you, and will burst your bonds apart." 
God has commanded concerning you: "No more descendants will bear your name. Out of the house of your gods, I'll cut off the engraved image and the molten image. I'll make your grave, for you're vile." 
Look, on the mountains the feet of him who brings good news, who publishes peace! Keep your feasts, Judah! Perform your vows, for the guilty one will no more pass through you. He's utterly cut off.
