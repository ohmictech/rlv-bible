God said to Moses, "Now you will see what I'm going to do to Pharaoh. A strong hand will force him to let them go, and a strong hand will drive them out of his land." 
God spoke to Moses and said, "I am God. 
I appeared to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob as God Almighty, but they did not know me by my own name. 
I also made my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land where they lived as foreigners. 
And I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, who are held as slaves by the Egyptians, and I have remembered my covenant. 
So tell the Israelites, 'I am God. I will bring you out from under the burdens the Egyptians put on you, free you from their slavery, and buy you back with an outstretched arm and with great acts of judgment. 
I will take you as my own people and be your God, and you will know that I am your God, the one who brings you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians. 
I will bring you into the land I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and I will give it to you as your own. I am God.'" 
Moses told the Israelites all this, but they wouldn't listen to him, because they were crushed in spirit and worn down by cruel slavery. 
Then God said to Moses, 
"Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites leave his land." 
But Moses said to God, "The Israelites won't even listen to me. Why would Pharaoh listen to me? I have never been a good speaker." 
God spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them orders for the Israelites and for Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the Israelites out of Egypt. 
These were the heads of their families. The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi. These are the families of Reuben. 
The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar and Shaul, whose mother was a Canaanite woman. These are the families of Simeon. 
These are the names of the sons of Levi by their generations: Gershon, Kohath and Merari. Levi lived a hundred and thirty-seven years. 
The sons of Gershon by their families: Libni and Shimei. 
The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel. Kohath lived a hundred and thirty-three years. 
The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites by their generations. 
Amram married his father's sister Jochebed, and she bore him Aaron and Moses. Amram lived a hundred and thirty-seven years. 
The sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg and Zichri. 
The sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan and Sithri. 
Aaron married Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. 
The sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah and Abiasaph. These are the Korahite families. 
Eleazar son of Aaron married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the Levite families, family by family. 
This is the Aaron and Moses to whom God said, "Bring the Israelites out of Egypt, tribe by tribe." 
They are the ones who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt about bringing the Israelites out of Egypt. This is that Moses and Aaron.
