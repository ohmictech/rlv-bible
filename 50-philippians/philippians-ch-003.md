Finally, my brothers and sisters, celebrate in the Lord! Writing the same things to you is no trouble for me, and it keeps you safe. 
Watch out for the dogs. Watch out for the bad workers. Watch out for those who mutilate the flesh. 
It is we who are the circumcision, we who serve God by his Spirit, who celebrate in Christ Jesus, and who put no confidence in the flesh, 
though I myself could have confidence in the flesh too. If anyone else thinks they have reason to trust in the flesh, I have more: 
circumcised on the eighth day, from the people of Israel, from the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born of Hebrews; as to the law, a strict religious man; 
as to passion, hunting down the gathering; as to the rightness that the law demands, blameless. 
But whatever used to be my gain, I have counted as loss for the sake of Christ. 
Yes, and more than that: I count everything as loss because of the greater worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. His sake I have thrown everything away, and I count it all as garbage, so that I may gain Christ 
and be found in him, not with a rightness of my own that comes from the law, but with the rightness that comes through trusting Christ, the rightness from God that comes by trust, 
so that I may know him and the power of his rising again, and share in his sufferings, becoming like him in his death, 
if somehow I may reach the rising again from the dead. 
Not that I have already got there, or have already been made complete. But I press on to take hold of it, because Christ Jesus has taken hold of me. 
Brothers and sisters, I don't count myself as having taken hold of it yet. But this one thing I do: forgetting what is behind and reaching for what is ahead, 
I press on toward the goal for the prize that comes with God's call upward, in Christ Jesus. 
So all of us who are mature should think this way. And if you think differently about anything, God will make that clear to you too. 
Only let us keep in step with what we have already reached, and stay of one mind. 
Brothers and sisters, all of you follow my example together, and keep your eyes on those who live the way you have us as your example. 
There are many who live as enemies of the cross of Christ. I have told you about them often, and now I tell you even with tears. 
Their end is ruin; their god is their belly; their weight is in their shame; their minds are set on earthly things. 
But our citizenship is in the sky, and from there we eagerly wait for the Restorer, the Lord Jesus Christ, 
who will change our lowly bodies to be like his weight-filled body, by the same power that lets him bring everything under himself.
