When a man takes a wife and marries her, then it will be, if she finds no favor in his eyes because he has found some unseemly thing in her, that he will write her a certificate of divorce, put it in her hand, and send her out of his house. 
When she has departed out of his house, she can go and be another man's wife. 
If the latter husband hates her, and writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her out of his house; or if the latter husband dies, who took her to be his wife; 
her former husband, who sent her away, can't take her again to be his wife after she's defiled; for that would be an abomination to God. You won't cause the land to drift, which your God gives you for an inheritance. 
When a man takes a new wife, he won't go out in the army, neither will he be assigned any business. He will be free at home one year, and will cheer his wife whom he has taken. 
No man will take the mill or the upper millstone as a pledge, for he takes a life in pledge. 
If a man is found stealing any of his brothers of the children of Israel, and he deals with him as a slave, or sells him, then that thief will die. So you will remove the bad from among you. 
Be careful in the plague of leprosy, that you observe diligently and do according to all that the Levitical priests teach you. As I commanded them, so you will observe to do. 
Remember what your God did to Miriam, by the way as you came out of Egypt. 
When you lend your neighbor any kind of loan, you won't go into his house to get his pledge. 
You will stand outside, and the man to whom you lend will bring the pledge outside to you. 
If he's a poor man, you won't sleep with his pledge. 
You will surely restore to him the pledge when the sun goes down, that he can sleep in his garment and bless you. It will be righteousness to you before your God. 
Don't oppress a hired servant who's poor and needy, whether he's one of your brothers or one of the foreigners who are in your land within your gates. 
In his day you will give him his wages, neither will the sun go down on it, for he's poor and sets his heart on it, lest he cry against you to God, and it be guilt on you. 
The fathers won't be put to death for the children, neither will the children be put to death for the fathers. Every man will be put to death for his own wrongdoing. 
Don't deprive the foreigner or the fatherless of justice, nor take a widow's clothing in pledge; 
but you will remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and your God bought back you there. So I command you to do this thing. 
When you reap your harvest in your field, and have forgotten a sheaf in the field, you won't go again to get it. It will be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow, that your God may bless you in all the work of your hands. 
When you beat your olive tree, you won't go over the boughs again. It will be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow. 
When you harvest your vineyard, you won't glean it after yourselves. It will be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow. 
You will remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. So I command you to do this thing.
