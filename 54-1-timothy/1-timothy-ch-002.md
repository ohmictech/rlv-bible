First of all, then, I urge that requests, prayers, pleadings, and thanksgivings be made for everyone, 
for kings and for everyone in high office, so that we may live calm, quiet lives in all devotion to God and seriousness. 
This is good, and it pleases God our Restorer, 
who wants everyone to be rescued and to come to know the truth fully. 
There is one God, and one mediator between God and people, a human being, Christ Jesus, 
who gave himself as a ransom for everyone, the witness at the right time. 
That is what I was appointed to announce and to be a sent one for, I am telling the truth in Christ, I'm not lying, a teacher of the nations in trust and truth. 
So I want the men everywhere to pray, lifting up devoted hands, without temper and without arguing. 
In the same way, I want women to dress themselves in decent clothing, with modesty and good judgment, not with braided hair or gold or pearls or expensive clothes, 
but with good deeds, which is what suits women who say they are devoted to God. 
A woman should learn quietly, and put herself fully under instruction. 
I don't allow a woman to teach or to take authority over a man; she is to keep quiet. 
Adam was formed first, then Eve. 
And Adam wasn't the one tricked; it was the woman who was tricked and stepped over the line. 
But she will be rescued through bearing children, if they continue in trust and love and set-apartness, with good judgment.
