That same year, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month, Hananiah the son of Azzur, the spokesman, who was of Gibeon, spoke to me in God's house, in the presence of the priests and of everyone, saying, 
"The God of Armies, the God of Israel says, 'I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon. 
Within two full years I will bring again into this place all the vessels of God's house that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took away from this place and carried to Babylon. 
I will bring again to this place Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, with all the captives of Judah, who went to Babylon,' says God; 'for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.'" 
Then the spokesman Jeremiah said to the spokesman Hananiah in the presence of the priests, and in the presence of everyone who stood in God's house, 
even the spokesman Jeremiah said, "Amen! May God do so. May God perform your words which you have prophesied, to bring again the vessels of God's house, and everyone who is captives, from Babylon to this place. 
Nevertheless listen now to this word that I speak in your ears, and in the ears of everyone: 
The spokesmen who have been before me and before you of old prophesied against many countries, and against great kingdoms, of war, of bad, and of pestilence. 
As for the spokesman who prophesies of peace, when the word of the spokesman happens, then the spokesman will be known, that God has truly sent him." 
Then Hananiah the spokesman took the bar from off the spokesman Jeremiah's neck, and broke it. 
Hananiah spoke in the presence of everyone, saying, "God says: 'Even so I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from off the neck of all the nations within two full years.'" Then the spokesman Jeremiah went his way. 
Then God's word came to Jeremiah, after Hananiah the spokesman had broken the bar from off the neck of the spokesman Jeremiah, saying, 
"Go, and tell Hananiah, saying, 'God says, "You have broken the bars of wood, but you have made in their place bars of iron." 
The God of Armies, the God of Israel says, "I have put a yoke of iron on the neck of all these nations, that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon; and they will serve him. I have also given him the animals of the field."'" 
Then the spokesman Jeremiah said to Hananiah the spokesman, "Listen, Hananiah! God has not sent you, but you make this people trust in a lie. 
So God says, 'Look, I will send you away from off the surface of the earth. This year you will die, because you have spoken rebellion against God.'" 
So Hananiah the spokesman died the same year in the seventh month.
