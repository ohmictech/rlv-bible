"I am the Alpha and the Omega," says the Lord God, "the one who is and who was and who is coming, the one who holds sway over all." 
I, John, your brother and your partner in the hard times, in the reign, and in staying power in Christ Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of God's message and the witness Jesus gave. 
On the Lord's day I was in the Spirit, and I heard behind me a loud voice, like a trumpet, 
saying, "Write in a scroll what you see, and send it to the seven gatherings: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea." 
I turned around to see who was speaking to me, and when I turned I saw seven golden lampstands. 
Among the lampstands stood someone like a human being, wearing a robe that reached down to his feet and a golden sash around his chest. 
His head and his hair were white like wool, white as snow, and his eyes were like a flame of fire. 
His feet were like polished bronze glowing in a furnace, and his voice was like the sound of rushing waters. 
In his right hand he was holding seven stars. Out of his mouth came a sharp double-edged sword, and his face was like the sun shining at full strength. 
When I saw him, I fell at his feet as though I were dead. He laid his right hand on me and said, "Don't be afraid. I am the first and the last, 
and the living one. I was dead, and look, I am alive through all the eras. Amen. And I hold the keys of Death and of Hades. 
So write down what you have seen, what is now, and what will happen after this. 
Here is the secret of the seven stars you saw in my right hand, and of the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the messengers of the seven gatherings, and the seven lampstands are the seven gatherings.
