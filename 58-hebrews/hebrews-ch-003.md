So then, set-apart brothers and sisters, who share in a calling from the sky, think about Jesus, the sent one and high priest of what we declare. 
He was trustworthy to the one who appointed him, just as Moses was trustworthy in all God's house. 
He has been given more weight than Moses, just as the one who builds a house has more honor than the house itself. 
Every house is built by someone, but the one who built everything is God. 
Moses was trustworthy in all God's house as a servant, to bear witness to what would be spoken later, 
but Christ is trustworthy as a Son over God's house. We're his house, if we hold tightly to our boldness and to the pride we take in our expectation, firm to the end. 
So, just as the Holy Spirit says, "Today, if you hear his voice, 
don't harden your hearts as they did in the rebellion, on the day they tested me in the wilderness, 
where your fathers tested me and tried me, and saw what I did for forty years. 
That's why I was angry with that generation, and I said, 'They always go astray in their hearts, and they haven't known my ways.' 
So I swore in my heat, 'They'll never enter my rest.'" 
Watch out, brothers and sisters, that none of you has a bad heart that refuses to trust, and turns away from the living God. 
Instead, encourage one another every day, as long as it's still called "today", so that none of you is hardened by the way the drift deceives. 
We share in Christ, if we hold tightly to the boldness we had at the start, firm to the end, 
as long as it's still said, "Today, if you hear his voice, don't harden your hearts as they did in the rebellion." 
Who were the ones who heard and rebelled? Was it not all those Moses led out of Egypt? 
And with whom was he angry for forty years? Was it not with those who drifted, whose bodies fell in the wilderness? 
And to whom did he swear that they'd never enter his rest, if not to those who refused to obey? 
So we see that they weren't able to enter because they refused to trust.
