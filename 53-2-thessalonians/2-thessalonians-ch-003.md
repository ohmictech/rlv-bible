Finally, brothers and sisters, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may run fast and be honored, just as it has with you, 
and that we may be rescued from twisted and bad people, because not everyone has trust. 
But the Lord is trustworthy. He will make you strong and guard you from the Slanderer. 
We are confident in the Lord about you, that you are doing and will go on doing what we tell you. 
May the Lord guide your hearts into God's love and into the staying power of Christ. 
In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, brothers and sisters, we tell you to keep away from any brother or sister who lives out of line and not according to what we handed down. 
You know yourselves how you ought to follow our example. We were not out of line when we were with you, 
and we did not eat anyone's bread for free. Instead we worked night and day, wearing ourselves out, so that we would not be a burden to any of you. 
Not because we have no right to it, but to give you an example to follow. 
Even when we were with you, we gave you this rule: "If anyone will not work, he doesn't eat." 
We hear that some among you are living out of line, not working at all, but sticking their noses into everything. 
People like that we tell, and urge in the Lord Jesus Christ, to settle down, get to work, and eat their own bread. 
And you, brothers and sisters, don't get tired of doing what is right. 
If anyone won't listen to what we say in this letter, take note of him and don't keep company with him, so that he may feel ashamed. 
Don't treat him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother or sister. 
May the Lord of wholeness himself give you wholeness at all times and in every way. The Lord be with you all. 
I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. That is the sign in every letter of mine; this is how I write. 
The favor of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.
