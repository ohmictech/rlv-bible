The Chief Musician, with the flutes. A Psalm by David. Give ear to my words, God. Consider my meditation. 
Listen to the voice of my cry, my King and my God, for I pray to you. 
God, in the morning you'll hear my voice. In the morning I'll lay my requests before you, and will watch expectantly. 
You aren't a God who has pleasure in guilt. Bad can't live with you. 
The arrogant won't stand in your sight. You hate all workers of guilt. 
You'll destroy those who speak lies. God abhors the bloodthirsty and deceitful man. 
But as for me, in the abundance of your loving kindness I'll come into your house. I'll bow toward your holy temple in reverence of you. 
Lead me, God, in your righteousness because of my enemies. Make your way straight before my face. 
There's no faithfulness in their mouth. Their heart is destruction. Their throat is an open tomb. They flatter with their tongue. 
Hold them guilty, God. Let them fall by their own counsels. Thrust them out in the multitude of their rebellion, for they have rebelled against you. 
But let everyone who takes refuge in you rejoice. Let them always shout for joy, because you defend them. Let them also who love your name be joyful in you. 
You'll bless the righteous. God, you'll surround him with favor as with a shield.
