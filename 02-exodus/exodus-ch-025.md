God spoke to Moses: 
"Tell the Israelites to bring me an offering. Take my offering from everyone whose heart moves them to give. 
This is the offering you are to take from them: gold, silver, bronze, 
blue, purple and scarlet yarn, fine linen, goats' hair, 
rams' skins dyed red, sea cow hide, acacia wood, 
olive oil for the lamps, spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense, 
onyx stones, and stones to be set in the ephod and in the breastplate. 
Have them make me a sanctuary, so I can live among them. 
Make it exactly like the plan I am showing you, both the tent and all its furnishings. 
"They are to make an ark of acacia wood. It is to be two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high. 
Cover it with pure gold, inside and out, and put a gold trim all around it. 
Cast four gold rings for it and put them on its four feet, two rings on one side and two on the other. 
Make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold. 
Put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark so it can be carried. 
The poles stay in the rings. They must not be taken out. 
Put into the ark the covenant I am giving you. 
Make a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide. 
Make two cherubim of hammered gold at the two ends of the mercy seat. 
Make one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other, all of one piece with the mercy seat. 
The cherubim are to spread their wings upward, covering the mercy seat, with their faces toward each other, looking at the mercy seat. 
Put the mercy seat on top of the ark, and put the covenant I am giving you inside the ark. 
There I will meet with you, and from above the mercy seat, between the two cherubim on the ark of the covenant, I will tell you everything I command you for the Israelites. 
"Make a table of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide and a cubit and a half high. 
Cover it with pure gold and put a gold trim all around it. 
Make a rim a hand's width around it, and a gold trim around the rim. 
Make four gold rings and put them at the four corners, on its four legs. 
The rings are to be close to the rim, to hold the poles for carrying the table. 
Make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold, so the table can be carried with them. 
Make its plates, its pans, its pitchers and its bowls for pouring out the drink offerings, all of pure gold. 
Set the bread out on the table before me at all times. 
"Make a lampstand of pure gold, hammered work. Its base and its shaft, its cups, its buds and its flowers are all to be one piece with it. 
Six branches are to come out of its sides, three on one side and three on the other. 
Three cups shaped like almond blossoms on one branch, with a bud and a flower, and three on the other branch, with a bud and a flower, and so for the six branches coming out of the lampstand. 
On the lampstand itself, four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with their buds and flowers, 
and a bud under the first pair of branches, a bud under the second pair and a bud under the third pair, for the six branches coming out of the lampstand. 
Their buds and their branches are all to be one piece with it, one hammered work of pure gold. 
Make its seven lamps, and set them up to light the space in front of it. 
Its wick trimmers and its trays are to be of pure gold. 
Make it, and all these accessories, out of a talent of pure gold. 
See that you make everything according to the plan shown to you on the mountain.
