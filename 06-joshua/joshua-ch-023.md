After many days, when God had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them, and Joshua was old and well on in years, 
Joshua called all Israel together, their elders, their heads, their judges and their officials, and said to them, "I am old and well on in years. 
You have seen everything your God has done to all these nations for your sake, because it is your God who fought for you. 
Look, I have divided among your tribes the land of these nations that remain, as well as all the nations I have wiped out, from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west. 
Your God will drive them out ahead of you and push them out of your sight, and you will take their land, as your God has told you. 
So be very brave and careful to do everything written in the scroll of the law of Moses, not turning from it to the right or to the left. 
Don't mix with these nations that remain among you. Don't call on the names of their gods, don't swear by them, don't serve them and don't bow down to them. 
Instead, hold on to your God, as you have done to this day. 
God has driven out great and strong nations ahead of you, and no one has been able to stand against you to this day. 
One of you will chase a thousand, because it is your God who fights for you, as he told you. 
So watch yourselves carefully, and love your God. 
But if you do turn away and hold on to what is left of these nations that remain among you, and marry into them, and mix with them, and they with you, 
then you can be certain that your God will no longer drive these nations out ahead of you. They will become a trap and a snare to you, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you are wiped off this good land your God has given you. 
Look, today I am going the way of all the earth. You know with all your heart and all your life that not one thing has failed of all the good things your God said about you. Everything has happened to you, and not one thing has failed. 
And just as all the good things your God promised you have come to you, so God will bring on you all the bad things, until he has wiped you off this good land your God has given you, 
if you break the covenant of your God, which he commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them. Then God's anger will burn against you, and you will quickly be wiped off the good land he has given you."
