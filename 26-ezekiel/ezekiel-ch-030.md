God's word came again to me, saying, 
"Son of man, prophesy, and say, 'The God says: "Wail, 'Alas for the day!' 
The day is near, even God's day is near. It'll be a day of clouds, a time of the nations. 
A sword will come on Egypt, and anguish will be in Ethiopia, when the slain fall in Egypt. They take away her multitude, and her foundations are broken down. 
"'"Ethiopia, Put, Lud, all the mixed people, Cub, and the children of the land that's allied with them, will fall with them by the sword." 
"'God says: "They also who uphold Egypt will fall. The pride of her power will come down. They'll fall by the sword in it from the tower of Seveneh," says God. 
"They'll be desolate in the middle of the countries that are desolate. Her cities will be among the cities that are wasted. 
They'll know that I'm God when I have set a fire in Egypt, and all her helpers are destroyed. 
"'"In that day messengers will go out from before me in ships to make the careless Ethiopians afraid. There will be anguish on them, as in the day of Egypt; for, look, it comes." 
"'The God says: "I'll also make the multitude of Egypt to cease, by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. 
He and his people with him, the terrible of the nations, will be brought in to destroy the land. They'll draw their swords against Egypt, and fill the land with the slain. 
I'll make the rivers dry, and will sell the land into the hand of bad men. I'll make the land desolate, and all that's therein, by the hand of foreigners. I, God, have spoken it." 
"'The God says: "I'll also destroy the idols, and I'll cause the images to cease from Memphis. There will be no more a prince from the land of Egypt. I'll put a fear in the land of Egypt. 
I'll make Pathros desolate, and will set a fire in Zoan, and will execute judgments on No. 
I'll pour my wrath on Sin, the stronghold of Egypt. I'll cut off the multitude of No. 
I'll set a fire in Egypt, Sin will be in great anguish. No will be broken up. Memphis will have adversaries in the daytime. 
The young men of Aven and of Pibeseth will fall by the sword. They'll go into captivity. 
At Tehaphnehes also the day will withdraw itself, when I break the yokes of Egypt there. The pride of her power will cease in her. As for her, a cloud will cover her, and her daughters will go into captivity. 
Thus I'll execute judgments on Egypt. Then they'll know that I'm God."'" 
In the eleventh year, in the first month, in the seventh day of the month, God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt. Look, it hasn't been bound up, to apply medicines, to put a bandage to bind it, that it might become strong to hold the sword. 
So God says: 'Look, I'm against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and will break his arms, the strong arm, and that which was broken. I'll cause the sword to fall out of his hand. 
I'll scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries. 
I'll strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and put my sword in his hand; but I'll break the arms of Pharaoh, and he'll groan before the king of Babylon with the groaning of a mortally wounded man. 
I'll hold up the arms of the king of Babylon, but the arms of Pharaoh will fall down. Then they'll know that I'm God when I put my sword into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he stretches it out on the land of Egypt. 
I'll scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the countries. Then they'll know that I'm God.'"
