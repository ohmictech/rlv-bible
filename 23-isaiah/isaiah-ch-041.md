"Keep silent before me, islands, and let the peoples renew their strength. Let them come near, then let them speak. Let's meet together for judgment. 
Who has raised up one from the east? Who called him to his feet in righteousness? He hands over nations to him and makes him rule over kings. He gives them like the dust to his sword, like the driven stubble to his bow. 
He pursues them and passes by safely, even by a way that he hadn't gone with his feet. 
Who has worked and done it, calling the generations from the beginning? I, God, the first, and with the last, I'm he." 
The islands have seen, and fear. The ends of the earth tremble. They approach, and come. 
Everyone helps his neighbor. They say to their brothers, "Be strong!" 
So the carpenter encourages the goldsmith. He who smooths with the hammer encourages him who strikes the anvil, saying of the soldering, "It's good;" and he fastens it with nails, that it might not totter. 
"But you, Israel, my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the offspring of Abraham my friend, 
you whom I have taken hold of from the ends of the earth, and called from its corners, and said to you, 'You're my servant. I have chosen you and haven't cast you away.' 
Don't you be afraid, for I'm with you. Don't be dismayed, for I'm your God. I'll strengthen you. Yes, I'll help you. Yes, I'll uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness. 
Look, everyone who's incensed against you'll be disappointed and confounded. Those who strive with you'll be like nothing, and will perish. 
You'll seek them, and won't find them, even those who contend with you. Those who war against you'll be as nothing, as a nonexistent thing. 
I, your God, will hold your right hand, saying to you, 'Don't be afraid. I'll help you.' 
Don't be afraid, you worm Jacob, and you men of Israel. I'll help you," says God. "Ythe one who buys us back is the Holy One of Israel. 
Look, I have made you into a new sharp threshing instrument with teeth. You'll thresh the mountains, and beat them small, and will make the hills like chaff. 
You'll winnow them, and the wind will carry them away, and the whirlwind will scatter them. You'll rejoice in God. You'll glory in the Holy One of Israel. 
The poor and needy seek water, and there's none. Their tongue fails for thirst. I, God, will answer them. I, the God of Israel won't forsake them. 
I'll open rivers on the bare heights, and springs in the middle of the valleys. I'll make the wilderness a pool of water, and the dry land springs of water. 
I'll put cedar, acacia, myrtle, and oil trees in the wilderness. I'll set cypress trees, pine, and box trees together in the desert; 
that they can see, know, consider, and understand together, that God's hand has done this, and the Holy One of Israel has created it. 
Produce your cause," says God. "Bring out your strong reasons!" says the King of Jacob. 
"Let them announce and declare to us what will happen! Declare the former things, what they are, that we can consider them, and know the latter end of them; or show us things to come. 
Declare the things that are to come hereafter, that we can know that you're gods. Yes, do good, or do bad things, that we can be dismayed, and see it together. 
Look, you're nothing, and your work is nothing. He who chooses you is an abomination. 
"I have raised up one from the north, and he has come, from the rising of the sun, one who calls on my name, and he will come on rulers as on mortar, and as the potter treads clay. 
Who has declared it from the beginning, that we can know? and before, that we can say, 'He's right'? Surely, there's no one who declares. Surely, there's no one who shows. Surely, there's no one who hears your words. 
I'm the first to say to Zion, 'Look, look at them;' and I'll give one who brings good news to Jerusalem. 
When I look, there's no man, even among them there's no counselor who, when I ask, can answer a word. 
Look, all of their deeds are vanity and nothing. Their molten images are wind and confusion.
