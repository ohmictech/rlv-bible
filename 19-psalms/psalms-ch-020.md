The Chief Musician. A Psalm by David. May God answer you in the day of trouble. May the name of the God of Jacob set you up on high, 
send you help from the sanctuary, grant you support from Zion, 
remember all your offerings, and accept your burned sacrifice. Selah. 
May he grant you your heart's desire, and fulfill all your counsel. 
We'll triumph in your salvation. In the name of our God, we'll set up our banners. May God grant all your requests. 
Now I know that God saves his anointed. He'll answer him from his holy sky, with the saving strength of his right hand. 
Some trust in chariots, and some in horses, but we trust in the name of our God. 
They're bowed down and fallen, but we rise up, and stand upright. 
Save, God! Let the King answer us when we call!
