When he opened the seventh seal, there was silence in the sky for about half an hour. 
I saw the seven messengers who stand in front of God, and seven trumpets were given to them. 
Another messenger came and stood at the altar, holding a golden incense burner. He was given a great deal of incense to add to the prayers of all the set-apart ones on the golden altar in front of the throne. 
The smoke of the incense, together with the prayers of the set-apart ones, went up in front of God from the messenger's hand. 
Then the messenger took the incense burner, filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it on the land. There were peals of thunder, rumblings, flashes of lightning, and an earthquake. 
The seven messengers who had the seven trumpets got ready to sound them. 
The first sounded, and hail and fire mixed with blood followed, and it was thrown on the land. A third of the land was burned up, a third of the trees were burned up, and all the green grass was burned up. 
The second messenger sounded, and something like a great mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea. A third of the sea turned into blood, 
and a third of the living creatures in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed. 
The third messenger sounded, and a great star fell from the sky, burning like a torch, and it fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water. 
The name of the star is Wormwood. A third of the waters turned into wormwood, and many people died from the waters, because they had been made bitter. 
The fourth messenger sounded, and a third of the sun was struck, and a third of the moon, and a third of the stars, so that a third of them was darkened. The day lost a third of its light, and the night in the same way. 
I looked, and I heard an eagle flying in mid-sky, saying in a loud voice, "How terrible! How terrible! How terrible for those who live on the land, because of the trumpet blasts that the three messengers are still going to sound!"
