Thus God showed me: look, he formed locusts in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and look, it was the latter growth after the king's harvest. 
When they finished eating the grass of the land, then I said, "God, forgive, I beg you! How could Jacob stand? He's small." 
God relented concerning this. "It won't be," says God. 
Thus God showed me: look, God called for judgment by fire; and it dried up the great deep, and would have devoured the land. 
Then I said, "God, stop, I beg you! How could Jacob stand? He's small." 
God relented concerning this. "This also won't be," says God. 
Thus he showed me: look, the Lord stood beside a wall made by a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand. 
God said to me, "Amos, what do you see?" I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "Look, I'll set a plumb line in the middle of my people Israel. I won't again pass by them any more. 
The high places of Isaac will be desolate, the sanctuaries of Israel will be laid waste; and I'll rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword." 
Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, "Amos has conspired against you in the middle of the house of Israel. The land isn't able to bear all his words. 
Amos says, 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will surely be led away captive out of his land.'" 
Amaziah also said to Amos, "You seer, go, flee away into the land of Judah, and there eat bread, and prophesy there, 
but don't prophesy again any more at Bethel; for it's the king's sanctuary, and it's a royal house!" 
Then Amos answered Amaziah, "I was no spokesman, neither was I a spokesman's son, but I was a herdsman, and a farmer of sycamore figs; 
and God took me from following the flock, and God said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people Israel.' 
Now listen to God's word: 'You say, Don't prophesy against Israel, and don't preach against the house of Isaac.' 
Therefore God says: 'Your wife will be a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword, and your land will be divided by line; and you yourself will die in a land that's unclean, and Israel will surely be led away captive out of his land.'"
