The word that came to Jeremiah concerning everyone in Judah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah (this was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon), 
which Jeremiah the spokesman spoke to everyone in Judah, and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem: 
From the thirteenth year of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, even to this day, these twenty-three years, God's word has come to me, and I have spoken to you, rising up early and speaking; but you haven't listened. 
God has sent to you all his servants the spokesmen, rising up early and sending them (but you haven't listened or inclined your ear to hear), 
saying, "Return now everyone from his bad way, and from the bad of your doings, and dwell in the land that God has given to you and to your fathers, from of old and even for the eras more. 
Don't go after other gods to serve them or worship them, and don't provoke me to anger with the work of your hands; then I'll do you no harm." 
"Yet you haven't listened to me," says God, "that you can provoke me to anger with the work of your hands to your own hurt." 
So the God of Armies says: "Because you haven't heard my words, 
Look, I'll send and take all the families of the north," says God, "and I'll send to Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, my servant, and will bring them against this land, and against its inhabitants, and against all these nations around. I'll utterly destroy them, and make them an astonishment, and a hissing, and perpetual desolations. 
Moreover I'll take from them the voice of mirth and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the sound of the millstones, and the light of the lamp. 
This whole land will be a desolation, and an astonishment; and these nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years. 
"It'll happen, when seventy years are accomplished, that I'll punish the king of Babylon and that nation," says God, "for their guilt. I'll make the land of the Chaldeans desolate for the eras. 
I'll bring on that land all my words which I have pronounced against it, even all that's written in this book, which Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations. 
Many nations and great kings will make bondservants of them, even of them. I'll recompense them according to their deeds, and according to the work of their hands." 
The God of Israel says to me: "Take this cup of the wine of wrath from my hand, and cause all the nations to whom I send you to drink it. 
They'll drink, and reel back and forth, and be insane, because of the sword that I'll send among them." 
Then I took the cup at God's hand, and made all the nations to drink, to whom God had sent me: 
Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, with its kings and its princes, to make them a desolation, an astonishment, a hissing, and a curse, as it's today; 
Pharaoh king of Egypt, with his servants, his princes, and all his people; 
and all the mixed people, and all the kings of the land of Uz, all the kings of the Philistines, Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod; 
Edom, Moab, and the children of Ammon; 
and all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the isle which is beyond the sea; 
Dedan, Tema, Buz, and everyone who has the corners of their beard cut off; 
and all the kings of Arabia, all the kings of the mixed people who dwell in the wilderness; 
and all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Medes; 
and all the kings of the north, far and near, one with another; and all the kingdoms of the world, which are on the surface of the earth. The king of Sheshach will drink after them. 
"You will tell them, 'The God of Armies, the God of Israel says: "Drink, and be drunk, vomit, fall, and rise no more, because of the sword which I'll send among you."' 
It will be, if they refuse to take the cup at your hand to drink, then you will tell them, 'The God of Armies says: "You will surely drink. 
For, look, I begin to work bad at the city which is called by my name; and should you be utterly unpunished? You won't be unpunished; for I'll call for a sword on all the inhabitants of the earth, says the God of Armies."' 
"Therefore prophesy against them all these words, and tell them, "'God will roar from on high, and utter his voice from his holy habitation. He'll mightily roar against his fold. He'll give a shout, as those who tread grapes, against all the inhabitants of the earth. 
A noise will come even to the end of the earth; for God has a controversy with the nations. He'll enter into judgment with all flesh. As for the guilty, he'll give them to the sword,"' says God." 
The God of Armies says, "Look, bad will go out from nation to nation, and a great storm will be raised up from the uttermost parts of the earth." 
The slain of God will be at that day from one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth. They won't be lamented. They won't be gathered or buried. They'll be dung on the surface of the ground. 
Wail, you shepherds, and cry. Wallow in dust, you leader of the flock; for the days of your slaughter and of your dispersions have fully come, and you'll fall like fine pottery. 
The shepherds will have no way to flee. The leader of the flock will have no escape. 
A voice of the cry of the shepherds, and the wailing of the leader of the flock, for God destroys their pasture. 
The peaceful folds are brought to silence because of the fierce anger of God. 
He has left his covert, as the lion; for their land has become an astonishment because of the fierceness of the oppression, and because of his fierce anger.
