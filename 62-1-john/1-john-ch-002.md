My dear children, I am writing these things so that you won't drift. But if anyone does, we have someone who comes alongside us, with the Father, Jesus Christ, the upright one. 
He is the sacrifice that makes things right for our mistakes, and not only for ours, but for the whole world. 
This is how we know that we know him: if we keep his commands. 
Anyone who says, "I know him," but doesn't keep his commands is a liar, and the truth isn't in them. 
But whoever keeps his word has truly had God's love made complete in them. This is how we know that we are in him: 
whoever says they make their home in him should live the way he lived. 
Brothers and sisters, I'm not writing you a new command, but an old one you have had from the beginning. This old command is the message you heard. 
Yet I am writing you a new command, and it is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light is already shining. 
Anyone who says they are in the light and hates a brother or sister is still in the darkness. 
Anyone who loves their brother or sister makes their home in the light, and there is nothing in them to make others trip. 
But anyone who hates a brother or sister is in the darkness, and walks in the darkness, and doesn't know where they are going, because the darkness has blinded their eyes. 
I am writing to you, dear children, because your mistakes have been released for his name's sake. 
I am writing to you, fathers, because you know the one who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have defeated the Slanderer. I am writing to you, dear children, because you know the Father. 
I have written to you, fathers, because you know the one who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God makes its home in you, and you have defeated the Slanderer. 
Don't love the world or the things that are in the world. If anyone loves the world, the Father's love isn't in them. 
Everything that is in the world, the craving of the flesh, the craving of the eyes, and the pride that comes from what you have, isn't from the Father but from the world. 
The world is passing away along with its cravings, but whoever does God's will remains for the eras. 
Dear children, this is the last hour. You heard that the one against Christ is coming, and already many who are against Christ have appeared. That is how we know it is the last hour. 
They left us, but they never really belonged to us. If they had belonged to us, they would have stayed with us. They left so that it would be shown that none of them belonged to us. 
You have an anointing from the Set-apart One, and all of you have knowledge. 
I haven't written to you because you don't know the truth, but because you do know it, and because no lie comes from the truth. 
Who is the liar? The one who denies that Jesus is the Christ. That is the one against Christ, the one who denies the Father and the Son. 
Whoever denies the Son doesn't have the Father either. Whoever openly declares the Son has the Father too. 
As for you, let what you heard from the beginning make its home in you. If what you heard from the beginning makes its home in you, you also will make your home in the Son and in the Father. 
And this is what he promised us: the real life of the era. 
I have written these things to you about those who are trying to lead you astray. 
As for you, the anointing you received from him makes its home in you, and you don't need anyone to teach you. His anointing teaches you about everything, and it is true and not a lie. So make your home in him, just as it taught you. 
And now, dear children, make your home in him, so that when he appears we may have boldness and not be ashamed in front of him at his arrival. 
If you know that he is upright, you know that everyone who does what is right has been born of him.
