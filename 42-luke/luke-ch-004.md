Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness 
for forty days, being tested by the Slanderer. He ate nothing in those days. Afterward, when the days were completed, he was hungry. 
The Slanderer said to him, "If you are the Son of God, command this stone to become bread." 
Jesus answered him, saying, "It is written, 'People do not live on bread alone, but on every word of God.'" 
The Slanderer, leading him up on a high mountain, showed him all the realms of the world in a moment of time. 
The Slanderer said to him, "I will give you all this authority and their weight, for it has been handed over to me, and I give it to whomever I want. 
If you therefore will worship before me, it will all be yours." 
Jesus answered him, "Get behind me, Adversary! For it is written, 'You shall worship the Lord your God, and you shall serve him only.'" 
He led him to Jerusalem and set him on the highest point of the temple, and said to him, "If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down from here, 
for it is written, 'He will put his messengers in charge of you, to guard you;' 
and, 'On their hands they will bear you up, lest perhaps you dash your foot against a stone.'" 
Jesus answered him, "It has been said, 'You shall not test the Lord your God.'" 
When the Slanderer had completed every testing, he departed from him until another time. 
Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee, and news about him spread through all the surrounding region. 
He taught in their gathering places, being given honor by all. 
He came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up. As was his custom, he entered into the gathering place on the Sabbath day, and stood up to read. 
The book of the spokesman Isaiah was handed to him. He opened the book, and found the place where it was written, 
"The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to preach good news to the poor. He has sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim release to the captives, and recovery of sight to the blind, to set free those who are crushed, 
and to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord." 
He closed the book, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of all in the gathering place were fastened on him. 
He began to tell them, "Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing." 
All spoke well of him and were amazed at the gracious words which came out of his mouth; and they said, "Isn't this Joseph's son?" 
He said to them, "Doubtless you will tell me this proverb, 'Doctor, heal yourself! Whatever we have heard done at Capernaum, do also here in your hometown.'" 
He said, "Truly I tell you, no spokesman is acceptable in his hometown. 
But truly I tell you, there were many widows in Israel in the days of Elijah, when the sky was shut up three years and six months, when a great famine came over all the land. 
Elijah was sent to none of them, except to Zarephath, in the land of Sidon, to a woman who was a widow. 
There were many men with leprosy in Israel in the time of Elisha the spokesman, yet not one of them was made clean, except Naaman, the Syrian." 
Everyone in the gathering place flared up as they heard these things. 
They rose up, threw him out of the city, and led him to the brow of the hill that their city was built on, so that they could throw him off the cliff. 
But he walked right through the middle of them and went on his way. 
He came down to Capernaum, a city of Galilee. He was teaching them on the Sabbath day, 
and they were astonished at his teaching, for his word was with authority. 
In the gathering place there was a man who had an unclean spirit; and he cried out with a loud voice, 
saying, "Ah! what have we to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to kill us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!" 
Jesus rebuked him, saying, "Be quiet and come out of him!" When the unclean spirit had thrown him down in the middle of them, it came out of him without harming him. 
Amazement came on everyone, and they said to one another, "What is this message? For with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and they come out!" 
News about him went out into every place of the surrounding region. 
He rose up from the gathering place and entered into Simon's house. Simon's mother-in-law was suffering from a high fever, and they begged him to help her. 
He stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. Immediately she rose up and served them. 
When the sun was setting, everyone who had any sick with various diseases brought them to him; and he laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them. 
Unclean spirits also came out of many, crying out and saying, "You are the Christ, the Son of God!" He rebuked them and didn't let them speak, because they knew he was the Christ. 
When it was day, he departed and went into an uninhabited place and the crowds looked for him, and came to him, and held on to him, so that he wouldn't go away from them. 
But he said to them, "I must preach the good news of God's reign to the other cities also, because that is why I was sent." 
He was preaching in the gathering places of Galilee.
