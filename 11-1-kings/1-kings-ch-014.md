At that time Abijah the son of Jeroboam became sick. 
Jeroboam said to his wife, "Please get up and disguise yourself, so that you won't be recognized as Jeroboam's wife. Go to Shiloh. Look, Ahijah the spokesman is there, who said that I'd be king over this people. 
Take with you ten loaves of bread, some cakes, and a jar of honey, and go to him. He'll tell you what will become of the child." 
Jeroboam's wife did so, and arose and went to Shiloh, and came to Ahijah's house. Now Ahijah couldn't see, for his eyes were set by reason of his age. 
God said to Ahijah, "Look, Jeroboam's wife is coming to inquire of you concerning her son, for he's sick. Tell her such and such; for it'll be, when she comes in, that she'll pretend to be another woman." 
So when Ahijah heard the sound of her feet as she came in at the door, he said, "Come in, Jeroboam's wife! Why do you pretend to be another? I'm sent to you with heavy news. 
Go, tell Jeroboam, 'The God of Israel says: "Because I exalted you from among the people, and made you prince over my people Israel, 
and tore the kingdom away from David's house, and gave it to you; and yet you haven't been as my servant David, who kept my commandments, and who followed me with all his heart, to do that only which was right in my eyes, 
but have done bad above everyone who was before you, and have gone and made for yourself other gods, molten images, to provoke me to anger, and have cast me behind your back, 
So, look, I'll bring bad on the house of Jeroboam, and will cut off from Jeroboam everyone who urinates on a wall, he who's shut up and he who's left at large in Israel, and will utterly sweep away the house of Jeroboam, as a man sweeps away dung until it's all gone. 
The dogs will eat whoever belongs to Jeroboam who dies in the city; and the birds of the sky will eat whoever dies in the field, for God has spoken it."' 
Arise, and go to your house. When your feet enter into the city, the child will die. 
All Israel will mourn for him and bury him; for he only of Jeroboam will come to the grave, because in him there's found some good thing toward the God of Israel, in the house of Jeroboam. 
Moreover God will raise up a king for himself over Israel who will cut off the house of Jeroboam. This is the day! What? Even now. 
God will strike Israel, as a reed is shaken in the water; and he'll root up Israel out of this good land which he gave to their fathers, and will scatter them beyond the River, because they have made their Asherah poles, provoking God to anger. 
He'll give Israel up because of the drifts of Jeroboam, which he has drifted, and with which he has made Israel to drift." 
Jeroboam's wife arose and departed, and came to Tirzah. As she came to the threshold of the house, the child died. 
All Israel buried him and mourned for him, according to God's word, which he spoke by his servant Ahijah the spokesman. 
The rest of the acts of Jeroboam, how he fought and how he reigned, look, they're written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. 
The days which Jeroboam reigned were twenty two years; then he slept with his fathers, and Nadab his son reigned in his place. 
Rehoboam the son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city which God had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, to put his name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonitess. 
Judah did that which was bad in God's sight, and they provoked him to jealousy with their mistakes which they committed, above all that their fathers had done. 
They also built for themselves high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree. 
There were also sodomites in the land. They did according to all the abominations of the nations which God drove out before the children of Israel. 
In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem; 
and he took away the treasures of God's house and the treasures of the king's house. He even took away all of it, including all the gold shields which Solomon had made. 
King Rehoboam made shields of bronze in their place, and committed them to the hands of the captains of the guard, who guarded the door of the king's house. 
It was so, that as often as the king went into God's house, the guard bore them, and brought them back into the guard room. 
Now the rest of the acts of Rehoboam, and all that he did, aren't they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? 
There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually. 
Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in David's city. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonitess. Abijam his son reigned in his place.
