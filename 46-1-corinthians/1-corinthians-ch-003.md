Brothers, I couldn't speak to you as to spiritual people, but as to fleshly people, as to babies in Christ. 
I fed you with milk, not with solid food, for you weren't yet ready. Indeed, you aren't ready even now, 
for you're still fleshly. As long as there's jealousy, quarreling, and factions among you, aren't you fleshly, and aren't you walking the way people do? 
When one says, "I follow Paul," and another, "I follow Apollos," aren't you fleshly? 
Who then is Apollos, and who's Paul? Servants through whom you trusted, and each as the Lord gave to him. 
I planted. Apollos watered. But God gave the increase. 
So then neither he who plants is anything, nor he who waters, but God who gives the increase. 
Now the one who plants and the one who waters are one, but each will receive his own earned result according to his own toil. 
We're God's fellow workers. You're God's field, God's building. 
According to the gift of God given to me, as a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another builds on it. But let each person be careful how he builds on it. 
No one can lay any other foundation than that which has been laid, which is Jesus Christ. 
But if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, costly stones, wood, hay, or straw, 
each person's work will be revealed. The Day will make it clear, because it's unveiled in fire; and the fire itself will test what sort of work each person's work is. 
If anyone's work remains, the work he built on it, he'll receive his earned result. 
If anyone's work is burned up, he'll suffer loss, but he himself will be made whole, but as through fire. 
Don't you know that you're God's temple and that God's Spirit lives in you? 
If anyone spoils God's temple, God will spoil him; for God's temple is set apart, and that's what you are. 
Let no one lead himself astray. If anyone thinks he's wise among you in this world, let him become a fool, so that he can become wise. 
The wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. It's written, "He has taken the wise in their craftiness." 
And again, "The Lord knows the reasoning of the wise, that it's worthless." 
So let no one boast in men. All things are yours, 
whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come. All are yours, 
and you're Christ's, and Christ is God's.
