Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help, and rely on horses, and trust in chariots because they're many, and in horsemen because they're very strong, but they don't look to the Holy One of Israel, and they don't seek God! 
Yet he also is wise, and will bring disaster, and won't call back his words, but will arise against the house of the those who do bad, and against the help of those who work guilt. 
Now the Egyptians are men, and not God; and their horses flesh, and not spirit. When God stretches out his hand, both he who helps will stumble, and he who's helped will fall, and they all will be consumed together. 
God says to me, "As the lion and the young lion growling over his prey, if a multitude of shepherds is called together against him, won't be dismayed at their voice, nor abase himself for their noise, so the God of Armies will come down to fight on Mount Zion and on its heights. 
As birds hovering, so the God of Armies will protect Jerusalem. He'll protect and deliver it. He'll pass over and preserve it." 
Return to him from whom you have deeply revolted, children of Israel. 
In that day everyone will cast away his idols of silver and his idols of gold, wrongdoing which your own hands have made for you. 
"The Assyrian will fall by the sword, not of man; and the sword, not of mankind, will devour him. He'll flee from the sword, and his young men will become subject to forced labor. 
His rock will pass away by reason of terror, and his princes will be afraid of the banner," says God, whose fire is in Zion, and his furnace in Jerusalem.
