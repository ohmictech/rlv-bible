Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, each took his fire pan, put fire in it and laid incense on it, and offered unauthorized fire before God, which he had not commanded them to do. 
Fire came out from before God and burned them up, and they died before God. 
Then Moses said to Aaron, "This is what God meant when he said, 'I will show myself set apart among those who come near me, and I will be honored in front of all the people.'" Aaron said nothing. 
Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel, Aaron's uncle, and said to them, "Come and carry your cousins from in front of the Holy Place out of the camp." 
So they came and carried them out of the camp in their tunics, as Moses had said. 
Moses said to Aaron and to Eleazar and Ithamar, his sons, "Don't let your hair hang loose and don't tear your clothes, or you will die and he will be angry with the whole community. But your relatives, the whole house of Israel, may mourn the burning that God has kindled. 
Don't leave the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, or you will die, because the anointing oil of God is on you." So they did what Moses said. 
Then God said to Aaron, 
"You and your sons are not to drink wine or strong drink when you go into the Tent of Meeting, or you will die. This is a perpetual rule for all your generations. 
You must tell the difference between what is set apart and what is ordinary, and between unclean and clean, 
and you must teach the Israelites all the rules God has spoken to them through Moses." 
Moses said to Aaron and to Eleazar and Ithamar, his remaining sons, "Take the gift offering that is left over from God's offerings made by fire and eat it without yeast beside the altar, because it is most holy. 
Eat it in a holy place, because it is your share and your sons' share of God's offerings made by fire, as I was commanded. 
But you, your sons and your daughters with you may eat the breast that is waved and the thigh that is contributed in a clean place, because they are given to you and your sons as your share out of the sacrifices of the Israelites' wholeness offerings. 
They are to bring the thigh that is contributed and the breast that is waved with the offerings made by fire of the fat, to wave them as a wave offering before God. They will be yours and your sons' with you as a share for the eras, as God has commanded." 
Moses searched carefully for the goat of the offering for wrongdoing and found it had been burned. He was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's remaining sons, and said, 
"Why didn't you eat the offering for wrongdoing in the area of the Holy Place, since it is most holy and he gave it to you to carry the guilt of the community, to make things right for them before God? 
Its blood wasn't brought inside the Holy Place. You should certainly have eaten it in the Holy Place, as I commanded." 
Aaron said to Moses, "Today they brought their offering for wrongdoing and their burnt offering before God, and these things have happened to me. If I had eaten the offering for wrongdoing today, would God have been pleased?" 
When Moses heard that, he was satisfied.
