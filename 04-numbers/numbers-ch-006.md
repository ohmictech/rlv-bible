God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'When a man or a woman makes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite, to set himself apart to God, 
he is to keep away from wine and strong drink. He is not to drink vinegar made from wine or strong drink, or grape juice, or eat fresh grapes or raisins. 
All the days of his separation he is to eat nothing that comes from the grapevine, not even the seeds or the skins. 
"'All the days of his vow of separation no razor is to touch his head. Until the days of his separation to God are complete, he is to be set apart and let the hair of his head grow long. 
"'All the days he is set apart to God he is not to go near a dead body. 
He is not to make himself unclean for his father, mother, brother or sister when they die, because his separation to God is on his head. 
All the days of his separation he is set apart to God. 
"'If someone dies suddenly beside him and he makes the head of his separation unclean, he is to shave his head on the day of his cleansing. On the seventh day he is to shave it. 
On the eighth day he is to bring two doves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 
The priest is to offer one as an offering for wrongdoing and the other as a burnt offering, and make things right for him, because he drifted by being near the dead, and he is to set his head apart again that same day. 
He is to set apart to God the days of his separation again and bring a male lamb a year old as an offering for guilt. The earlier days don't count, because his separation was made unclean. 
"'This is the law of the Nazirite. When the days of his separation are complete, he is to be brought to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, 
and bring his offering to God: one male lamb a year old without defect for a burnt offering, one female lamb a year old without defect for an offering for wrongdoing, one ram without defect for a wholeness offering, 
a basket of bread made without yeast, cakes of fine flour mixed with oil, and wafers made without yeast with oil poured over them, along with their gift offering and drink offerings. 
The priest is to present them before God and offer his offering for wrongdoing and his burnt offering. 
He is to offer the ram as a wholeness sacrifice to God, with the basket of bread made without yeast, and the priest is to offer its gift offering and drink offering. 
The Nazirite is to shave the head of his separation at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, take the hair from his head and put it on the fire under the wholeness sacrifice. 
The priest is to take the boiled shoulder of the ram, one cake made without yeast from the basket and one wafer made without yeast, and put them in the Nazirite's hands after he has shaved his head. 
The priest is to wave them as a wave offering before God. They are set apart for the priest, along with the breast that is waved and the thigh that is contributed. After that the Nazirite may drink wine. 
"'This is the law of the Nazirite who makes a vow, and of his offering to God for his separation, in addition to whatever else he can afford. He must do exactly what he vowed, following the law of his separation.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Tell Aaron and his sons, 'This is how you are to bless the Israelites. Say to them, 
"May God bless you and keep you. 
May God make his face shine on you and be gracious to you. 
May God look on you with favor and give you peace." 
So they will put my name on the Israelites, and I will bless them."
