Brothers, even if a man is caught in some false step, you who are spiritual should set them right in a spirit of gentleness, watching yourself so that you aren't tested too. 
Carry one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ. 
If a man thinks himself to be something when he is nothing, he leads himself astray. 
But let each man examine his own work, and then he will have something to take real pride in, and not about someone else. 
Each person will carry his own burden. 
But let him who is taught in the word share all good things with him who teaches. 
Don't be led astray. God isn't mocked, for whatever a person sows, that is what he will reap. 
The one who sows to his own flesh will reap decay from the flesh. But the one who sows to the Spirit will reap real life for the eras from the Spirit. 
Let's not be weary in doing good, for we will reap in due season if we don't give up. 
So then, as we have opportunity, let's do good to everyone, and especially to those of the household of trust. 
See with what large letters I write to you with my own hand. 
As many as desire to make a good impression in the flesh compel you to be circumcised, only so that they won't be hunted down for the cross of Christ. 
Even they who receive circumcision don't keep the law themselves, but they desire to have you circumcised, so that they may boast in your flesh. 
But far be it from me to take pride except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. 
In Christ Jesus neither being circumcised nor not being circumcised is anything, but a new creation is. 
As many as walk by this rule, wholeness and mercy be on them, and on God's Israel. 
From now on, let no one cause me any trouble, for I bear the marks of the Lord Jesus branded on my body. 
The favor of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
