You are the children of your God. Don't cut yourselves or shave the hair between your eyes for the dead. 
You are a people set apart to your God, and God has chosen you to be his own people out of all the peoples on the face of the earth. 
Don't eat anything disgusting. 
These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep, the goat, 
the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope and the chamois. 
You may eat any animal that has a split hoof, divided into two parts, and that chews the cud. 
But you must not eat these from among those that chew the cud or have a split hoof: the camel, the hare and the rabbit. They chew the cud but don't have a split hoof, so they are unclean to you. 
The pig has a split hoof but doesn't chew the cud, so it is unclean to you. Don't eat its meat and don't touch its dead body. 
From everything in the water you may eat whatever has fins and scales. 
Anything that doesn't have fins and scales you must not eat. It is unclean to you. 
You may eat any clean bird. 
But these are the ones you must not eat: the eagle, the vulture, the osprey, 
the red kite, the falcon, any kind of kite, 
any kind of raven, 
the ostrich, the owl, the seagull, any kind of hawk, 
the little owl, the great owl, the horned owl, 
the pelican, the vulture, the cormorant, 
the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe and the bat. 
All winged insects are unclean to you. They must not be eaten. 
You may eat any clean bird. 
Don't eat anything that died on its own. Give it to the foreigner living in your towns and he may eat it, or sell it to a foreigner, because you are a people set apart to your God. Don't boil a young goat in its mother's milk. 
Be sure to set aside a tenth of all your crops that come out of the field each year. 
Eat before your God, in the place he chooses for his name to be, the tenth of your grain, your new wine and your oil, and the firstborn of your herd and flock, so that you learn to fear your God always. 
If the road is too long for you, so that you can't carry it because the place your God chooses for his name is too far from you, when your God blesses you, 
then turn it into money, tie the money up in your hand and go to the place your God will choose. 
Trade the money for whatever you want: cattle, sheep, wine, strong drink, or anything else you set your heart on. Then eat there before your God, and celebrate, you and your household. 
Don't neglect the Levite inside your gates, because he has no share or inheritance with you. 
At the end of every three years, bring out the whole tenth of that year's crops and store it inside your gates. 
Then the Levite, who has no share or inheritance with you, and the foreigner living among you, and the orphans and widows inside your gates will come and eat and be satisfied, so that your God may bless you in all the work you do.
