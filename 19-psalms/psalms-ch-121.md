A Song of Ascents. I will lift up my eyes to the hills. Where does my help come from? 
My help comes from God, who made the sky and the earth. 
He won't allow your foot to be moved. He who keeps you won't slumber. 
Look, he who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep. 
God is your keeper. God is your shade on your right hand. 
The sun won't harm you by day, nor the moon by night. 
God will keep you from all bad. He will keep your soul. 
God will keep your going out and your coming in, from this time forward, and for the eras more.
