So you're without excuse, O person, whoever judges. In the same way you judge another, you sentence yourself. You who judge practice the same things. 
We know that the verdict of God is according to truth against those who practice such things. 
Do you think this, O person who judges those who practice such things, and do the same, that you'll escape the verdict of God? 
Or do you despise the riches of his goodness, forbearance, and patience, not knowing that the goodness of God leads you to a change of heart? 
But according to your hardness and your unwillingness to have a change of heart, you're treasuring up for yourself heat in its day, with the unveiling and the right verdict of God, 
who "will pay back to everyone according to their deeds:" 
to those who by perseverance in well-doing seek for weight, honor, and incorruptibility, the real life of the era; 
but to those who are self-seeking and don't obey the truth, but obey wrong, will be heat and flare, 
pressure, and anguish on every person who does bad, to the Jewish person first, and also to the Greek. 
But weight, honor, and wholeness go to every person who does good, to the Jewish person first, and also to the Greek. 
There's no partiality with God. 
Anyone who drifted without the law will also be broken up without the law. Anyone who drifted under the law will be judged by the law. 
It's not the hearers of the law who are right before God, but the doers of the law who will be made right 
(for when the nations who don't have the law naturally do the things of the law, they, not having the law, are their own law, 
showing that it's written in their hearts, their conscience witnessing with them, and their thoughts accusing or excusing them) 
in the day when God will judge the secrets of people, according to my Good News, by Jesus Christ. 
Truly you have the name of a Jew, rest on the law, boast in God, 
know his will, and approve the things that are excellent, being instructed out of the law, 
and are confident that you yourself are a guide of the blind, a light to those who are in darkness, 
a corrector of the foolish, a teacher of babies, having in the law the form of knowledge and of the truth. 
You who teach another, don't you teach yourself? You who tell others not to steal, do you steal? 
You who say a man shouldn't sleep around, do you sleep around? You who hate idols, do you rob temples? 
You who boast in the law, do you dishonor God by disobeying the law? 
"the name of God is slandered among the nations because of you," just as it's written. 
Circumcision does have benefit, if you're a doer of the law, but if you break the law, you might as well not be circumcised. 
If those who aren't circumcised keep what the law rightly requires, won't their not being circumcised be counted as circumcision? 
Won't those who are physically uncircumcised, but fulfill the law, judge you, who with the letter and circumcision break the law? 
The person isn't a Jew who's one outwardly, and it's not about circumcision which is physical; 
but the person is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is of the heart, in the spirit, not in the letter; whose praise isn't from men, but from God.
