God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and collect staffs from them, one for each family, from all their leaders by family, twelve staffs. Write each man's name on his staff. 
Write Aaron's name on Levi's staff, since there is to be one staff for each head of a family. 
Lay them in the Tent of Meeting in front of the covenant, where I meet with you. 
The staff of the man I choose will sprout, and I will put an end to the grumbling of the Israelites that they keep grumbling against you." 
Moses spoke to the Israelites, and all their leaders gave him staffs, one for each leader by family, twelve staffs, and Aaron's staff was among them. 
Moses laid the staffs before God in the Tent of the Testimony. 
The next day Moses went into the Tent of the Testimony, and there was Aaron's staff for the house of Levi, sprouted, budding, blossoming and bearing ripe almonds. 
Moses brought all the staffs out from before God to all the Israelites, and they looked, and each man took his own staff. 
God said to Moses, "Put Aaron's staff back in front of the covenant, to be kept as a sign against those who rebel, so you can put a stop to their grumbling against me, and they don't die." 
Moses did this, just as God commanded him. 
The Israelites said to Moses, "Look, we're dying! We're finished! We're all finished! 
Anyone who comes near God's tent dies. Are we all going to die?"
