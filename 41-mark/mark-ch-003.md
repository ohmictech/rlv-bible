He entered the gathering place again, and a man was there whose hand was withered. 
They were watching him to see whether he would heal him on the Sabbath, so that they could accuse him. 
He said to the man whose hand was withered, "Stand up." 
He said to them, "Is it allowed on the Sabbath to do good or to do harm, to rescue a life or to kill?" But they were silent. 
He looked around at them in anger, grieved at how hard their hearts were, and said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He stretched it out, and his hand was restored, as healthy as the other. 
The strict religious ones went out, and immediately conspired with the Herodians against him, how they could kill him. 
Jesus withdrew to the sea with his students, and a great crowd followed him from Galilee, from Judea, 
from Jerusalem, from Idumaea, from beyond the Jordan, and from around Tyre and Sidon. Hearing what great things he was doing, a great crowd came to him. 
He told his students to keep a little boat ready for him because of the crowd, so that they wouldn't crush him. 
He had healed many, so that everyone who had diseases pressed in on him to touch him. 
The unclean spirits, whenever they saw him, fell down before him and cried, "You are the Son of God!" 
He sternly warned them that they shouldn't make him known. 
He went up into the mountain and called to himself those whom he wanted, and they went to him. 
He appointed twelve to be with him, and to send them out to preach 
and to have authority to heal sicknesses and to drive out unclean spirits: 
Simon (to whom he gave the name Peter); 
James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, whom he called Boanerges, which means Sons of Thunder; 
Andrew; Philip; Bartholomew; Matthew; Thomas; James, the son of Alphaeus; Thaddaeus; Simon the Zealot; 
and Judas Iscariot, who also handed him over. Then he came into a house. 
The crowd came together again, so that they couldn't even eat bread. 
When his family heard it, they went out to seize him, for they said, "He has lost his mind." 
The Bible teachers who came down from Jerusalem said, "He has Beelzebul," and, "He drives out unclean spirits by the ruler of the unclean spirits." 
He called them to him and said to them in stories, "How can the Adversary drive out the Adversary? 
If a reign is divided against itself, that reign can't stand. 
If a house is divided against itself, that house can't stand. 
If the Adversary has risen up against himself and is divided, he can't stand, but his end has come. 
But no one can enter a strong man's house and plunder it unless he first ties up the strong man. Then he can plunder his house. 
"Truly I tell you, all the mistakes of the children of people will be released, including their slanders, however they may slander, 
but whoever slanders the Holy Spirit doesn't have release for the eras, but is subject to the verdict of the era." 
Because they said, "He has an unclean spirit." 
His mother and his brothers came and stood outside and sent someone in to call him. 
A crowd was sitting around him, and they told him, "Look, your mother, your brothers, and your sisters are outside looking for you." 
He answered them, "Who are my mother and my brothers?" 
Looking around at those seated in a circle around him, he said, "Look, my mother and my brothers! 
Whoever does what God wants is my brother, my sister, and mother."
