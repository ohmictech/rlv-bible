The word which came to Jeremiah from God, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, with all his army, all the kingdoms of the earth that were under his dominion, and everyones, were fighting against Jerusalem and against all its cities, saying: 
"The God of Israel says, 'Go, and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and tell him, God says, "Look, I'll give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon and he'll burn it with fire. 
You won't escape out of his hand, but will surely be taken and delivered into his hand. Your eyes will see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he'll speak with you mouth to mouth. You'll go to Babylon."' 
"Yet hear God's word, O Zedekiah king of Judah. God says concerning you, 'You won't die by the sword. 
You'll die in peace; and with the burnings of your fathers, the former kings who were before you, so they'll make a burning for you. They'll lament you, saying, "Ah Lord!" for I have spoken the word,' says God." 
Then Jeremiah the spokesman spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem, 
when the king of Babylon's army was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish and against Azekah; for these alone remained of the cities of Judah as fortified cities. 
The word came to Jeremiah from God, after King Zedekiah had made a covenant with everyone who was at Jerusalem, to proclaim liberty to them, 
that every man should let his male servant, and every man his female servant, who's a Hebrew or a Hebrewess, go free, that no one should make bondservants of them, of a Jew his brother. 
All the princes and everyone obeyed who had entered into the covenant, that everyone should let his male servant and everyone his female servant go free, that no one should make bondservants of them any more. They obeyed and let them go, 
but afterwards they turned, and caused the servants and the handmaids whom they had let go free to return, and brought them into subjection for servants and for handmaids. 
So God's word came to Jeremiah from God, saying, 
"The God of Israel says: 'I made a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, saying: 
At the end of seven years, every man of you will release his brother who's a Hebrew, who has been sold to you, and has served you six years. You will let him go free from you. But your fathers didn't listen to me, and didn't incline their ear. 
You had now turned, and had done that which is right in my eyes, in every man proclaiming liberty to his neighbor. You had made a covenant before me in the house which is called by my name; 
but you turned and profaned my name, and every man caused his servant and every man his handmaid, whom you had let go free at their pleasure, to return. You brought them into subjection, to be to you for servants and for handmaids.'" 
So God says: "You haven't listened to me, to proclaim liberty, every man to his brother, and every man to his neighbor. Look, I proclaim to you a liberty," says God, "to the sword, to the pestilence, and to the famine. I'll make you be tossed back and forth among all the kingdoms of the earth. 
I'll give the men who have transgressed my covenant, who haven't performed the words of the covenant which they made before me when they cut the calf in two and passed between its parts: 
the princes of Judah, the princes of Jerusalem, the eunuchs, the priests, and everyone in the land, who passed between the parts of the calf. 
I'll even give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their life. Their dead bodies will be food for the birds of the sky and for the animals of the earth. 
"I'll give Zedekiah king of Judah and his princes into the hands of their enemies, into the hands of those who seek their life and into the hands of the king of Babylon's army, who has gone away from you. 
Look, I'll command," says God, "and cause them to return to this city. They'll fight against it, take it, and burn it with fire. I'll make the cities of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant."
