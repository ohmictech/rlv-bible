God said to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'I am your God. 
Don't do what they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived, and don't do what they do in the land of Canaan, where I am bringing you. Don't follow their customs. 
You are to do what I decide and keep my rules and live by them. I am your God. 
Keep my rules and my decisions, because the person who does them will live by them. I am God. 
"'None of you is to have sex with a close relative. I am God. 
"'Don't have sex with your father or your mother. She is your mother, so don't have sex with her. 
"'Don't have sex with your father's wife. That would be your father's own body. 
"'Don't have sex with your sister, whether she is your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, whether she was born at home or elsewhere. 
"'Don't have sex with your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter. That would be your own body. 
"'Don't have sex with your father's wife's daughter, fathered by your father, since she is your sister. 
"'Don't have sex with your father's sister. She is your father's close relative. 
"'Don't have sex with your mother's sister, because she is your mother's close relative. 
"'Don't have sex with your father's brother's wife. Don't go near her. She is your aunt. 
"'Don't have sex with your daughter-in-law. She is your son's wife, so don't have sex with her. 
"'Don't have sex with your brother's wife. That would be your brother's own body. 
"'Don't have sex with a woman and her daughter. Don't take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter for that. They are close relatives, and it would be a disgrace. 
"'Don't take your wife's sister as a second wife to be her rival by having sex with her while your wife is still alive. 
"'Don't go near a woman to have sex with her while she is unclean from her period. 
"'Don't have sex with your neighbor's wife and make yourself unclean with her. 
"'Don't give any of your children to be sacrificed to Molech, and don't make the name of your God unclean. I am God. 
"'Don't have sex with a man the way you would with a woman. That is detestable. 
"'Don't have sex with any animal and make yourself unclean with it. No woman is to give herself to an animal to mate with it. That is a perversion. 
"'Don't make yourselves unclean in any of these ways, because the nations I am driving out ahead of you made themselves unclean in all of them. 
The land became unclean, so I punished it for its guilt, and the land threw out its inhabitants. 
You are to keep my rules and my decisions and not do any of these detestable things, neither you native born nor the foreigner living among you, 
because the people who were in the land before you did all these detestable things and the land became unclean, 
so that the land won't throw you out too when you make it unclean, the way it threw out the nation before you. 
"'Anyone who does any of these detestable things is to be cut off from among his people. 
Keep my requirements and don't follow any of these disgusting customs that were practiced before you, and don't make yourselves unclean with them. I am your God.'"
