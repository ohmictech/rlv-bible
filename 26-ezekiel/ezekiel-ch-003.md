He said to me, "Son of man, eat what you find. Eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of Israel." 
So I opened my mouth, and he caused me to eat the scroll. 
He said to me, "Son of man, eat this scroll that I give you and fill your belly and your bowels with it." Then I ate it. It was as sweet as honey in my mouth. 
He said to me, "Son of man, go to the house of Israel, and speak my words to them. 
You aren't sent to a people of a strange speech and of a hard language, but to the house of Israel 
not to many peoples of a strange speech and of a hard language, whose words you can't understand. Surely, if I sent you to them, they'd listen to you. 
But the house of Israel won't listen to you, for they won't listen to me; for all the house of Israel are obstinate and hard-hearted. 
Look, I have made your face hard against their faces, and your forehead hard against their foreheads. 
I have made your forehead as a diamond, harder than flint. Don't be afraid of them, neither be dismayed at their looks, though they're a rebellious house." 
Moreover he said to me, "Son of man, receive in your heart and hear with your ears all my words that I speak to you. 
Go to them of the captivity, to the children of your people, and speak to them, and tell them, 'This is what God says,' whether they'll hear, or whether they'll refuse." 
Then the Spirit lifted me up, and I heard behind me the voice of a great rushing, saying, "Blessed be God's glory from his place." 
I heard the noise of the wings of the living creatures as they touched one another, and the noise of the wheels beside them, even the noise of a great rushing. 
So the Spirit lifted me up, and took me away; and I went in bitterness, in the heat of my spirit; and God's hand was strong on me. 
Then I came to them of the captivity at Tel Aviv who lived by the river Chebar, and to where they lived; and I sat there overwhelmed among them seven days. 
At the end of seven days, God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, I have made you a watchman to the house of Israel. So hear the word from my mouth, and warn them from me. 
When I tell the guilty, 'You'll surely die;' and you give him no warning, nor speak to warn the guilty from his guilty way, to save his life, that guilty man will die in his guilt; but I'll require his blood at your hand. 
Yet if you warn the guilty, and he doesn't turn from his guilt, nor from his guilty way, he'll die in his guilt; but you have delivered your soul." 
"Again, when a righteous man turns from his righteousness and commits guilt, and I lay a stumbling block before him, he'll die. Because you haven't given him warning, he'll die in his wrongdoing, and his righteous deeds which he has done won't be remembered; but I'll require his blood at your hand. 
Nevertheless if you warn the righteous man, that the righteous person doesn't drift, and he doesn't drift, he'll surely live, because he took warning; and you have delivered your soul." 
God's hand was there on me; and he said to me, "Arise, go out into the plain, and I'll talk with you there." 
Then I arose, and went out into the plain, and look, God's glory stood there, like the glory which I saw by the river Chebar. Then I fell on my face. 
Then the Spirit entered into me and set me on my feet. He spoke with me, and said to me, "Go, shut yourself inside your house. 
But you, son of man, look, they'll put ropes on you, and will bind you with them, and you won't go out among them. 
I'll make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth so that you'll be mute and won't be able to correct them, for they're a rebellious house. 
But when I speak with you, I'll open your mouth, and you will tell them, 'This is what God says.' He who hears, let him hear; and he who refuses, let him refuse; for they're a rebellious house."
