Seeing the large crowds, he went up onto the mountain. When he had sat down, his students came to him. 
He began to teach them, saying, 
"Flourishing are the ones who know they have nothing to offer God, for theirs is the reign of the sky. 
Flourishing are those who mourn, for they'll be comforted. 
Flourishing are the gentle, for they'll be given charge of the earth. 
Flourishing are those who hunger and thirst for rightness, for they'll be filled. 
Flourishing are the merciful, for they'll obtain mercy. 
Flourishing are the pure in heart, for they'll see God. 
Flourishing are the peacemakers, for they'll be called children of God. 
Flourishing are those who have been hunted down for the sake of rightness, for theirs is the reign of the sky. 
"Flourishing are you when people insult you, hunt you down, and tell all kinds of malicious lies about you because of me. 
Celebrate, and be very glad, for your earned result in the sky is great. That's how they hunted the spokesmen who were before you. 
"You're the salt of the earth, but if the salt goes flat, what will make it salty again? It's good for nothing anymore except to be thrown out and trampled on. 
"You're the light of the world. A city on a hill can't be hidden. 
People don't light a lamp and put it under a basket. They put it on a stand, and it shines for everyone in the house. 
Even so, let your light shine in front of people, that they may see your good deeds and honor your Father who's in the sky. 
"Don't think that I came to break up the law or the prophets. I didn't come to break up, but to fill completely. 
Truly, I tell you, until the sky and earth pass away, not even one smallest letter or one tiny pen stroke will in any way pass away from the law, until all things are accomplished. 
So whoever breaks one of the least of these commands and teaches others to do the same will be called least in the reign of the sky. But whoever does them and teaches them will be called great in the reign of the sky. 
I tell you the truth, unless your rightness runs deeper than the Bible teachers and strict religious ones, there's no way you'll enter into the reign of the sky. 
"You have heard that it was said to the ancient ones, 'You shouldn't murder,' and 'Whoever murders will be liable to the verdict.' 
But I tell you that everyone who's angry with his brother without a cause will be liable to the verdict. Whoever says to his brother, 'Empty head!' will be in danger of the council. Whoever says, 'Moron!' will be in danger of the fire of Gehenna. 
"So if you're offering your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you, 
leave your gift there before the altar, and go your way. First be made right with your brother, and then come and offer your gift. 
Agree with your adversary quickly while you're with him on the way, because the prosecutor might hand you over to the judge, and the judge hand you over to the officer, and you be thrown into prison. 
Truly I tell you, you'll by no means get out of there until you have paid the last small copper coin. 
"You have heard that it was said, 'You shouldn't cheat,' 
but I tell you that everyone who looks at a woman and craves her has already cheated with her in his heart. 
If your right eye traps you, tear it out and throw it away from you. It's better for you that one part of your body be broken up than that your whole body be broken up into Gehenna. 
If your right hand traps you, cut it off, and throw it away from you. It's better for you that one part of your body be broken up, than that your whole body be broken up into Gehenna. 
"It was also said: 'Whoever wants to split from his wife should give her a certificate of divorce.' 
But I tell you: everyone who splits from his wife, except for the matter of sleeping around, causes her to be unfaithful, and whoever marries a woman who has been split from cheats." 
"Again you have heard that it was said to the ones long ago, 'You shouldn't make false vows, but follow through with your vows to the Lord' 
but I tell you, don't make a vow at all: neither by the sky, for it's the throne of God, 
nor by the earth, for it's the footstool of his feet, nor by Jerusalem, for it's the city of the great King. 
Neither will you swear by your head, for you can't make one hair white or black. 
But let your 'Yes' be 'Yes' and your 'No' be 'No.' Anything more than this comes from the Slanderer. 
"You have heard that it was said, 'An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.' 
But I tell you, don't resist someone who's malicious. Instead, if anyone strikes you on your right cheek, turn the other cheek to him too. 
If anyone sues you to take away your coat, let him have your shirt also. 
Whoever compels you to go one mile, go with him two. 
Give to him who asks you, and don't turn away someone who seeks to borrow from you. 
"You have heard that it was said, 'You should love your neighbor and hate your enemy.' 
But I tell you, love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who mistreat you and hunt you down, 
that you may be children of your Father who's in the sky. He makes his sun rise on the malicious and the good, and sends rain on the right and the unright. 
If you love those who love you, what pay do you have? Don't even the tax collectors do the same? 
If you only greet your friends, what more do you do than others? Don't even the outsiders do the same? 
So be complete, just as your Father in the sky is complete.
