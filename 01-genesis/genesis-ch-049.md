Jacob called his sons and said, "Gather around, and I'll tell you what will happen to you in the days to come. 
Come together and listen, sons of Jacob. Listen to Israel your father. 
"Reuben, you're my firstborn, my strength and the first of my vigor, first in rank and first in power. 
Uncontrollable as water, you won't come out on top, because you climbed into your father's bed and defiled it. You went up onto my couch. 
"Simeon and Levi are brothers. Their swords are tools of violence. 
Let me never enter their council. Let my honor stay out of their assembly, because in their anger they killed men, and in their recklessness they hamstrung oxen. 
May their anger be cursed for its fierceness, and their rage for its cruelty. I'll scatter them in Jacob and spread them out in Israel. 
"Judah, your brothers will praise you. Your hand will be on the neck of your enemies. Your father's sons will bow down to you. 
Judah is a young lion. You have come up from the kill, my son. He crouches and lies down like a lion, like a lioness. Who would dare to wake him? 
The scepter won't leave Judah, nor the ruler's staff from between his feet, until the one comes who owns them, and the peoples obey him. 
He ties his donkey to the vine, his colt to the best vine. He washes his clothes in wine, his robes in the blood of grapes. 
His eyes will be dark with wine, his teeth white with milk. 
"Zebulun will live by the seashore and be a harbor for ships, with his border reaching to Sidon. 
"Issachar is a sturdy donkey lying down between the saddlebags. 
He saw that rest was good and the land was pleasant, so he bent his shoulder to the load and became a servant doing forced labor. 
"Dan will judge his people as one of the tribes of Israel. 
Dan will be a snake beside the road, a viper in the path, that bites the horse's heels so the rider falls backward. 
I wait for you to rescue me, God. 
"Raiders will press in on Gad, but he'll press on their heels. 
"Asher's food will be rich. He'll produce delicacies fit for kings. 
"Naphtali is a deer set free that bears beautiful fawns. 
"Joseph is a fruitful vine, a fruitful vine by a spring, with branches climbing over the wall. 
Archers attacked him bitterly and shot at him and hunted him down, 
but his bow stayed steady and his arms grew strong from the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, from there, the Shepherd, the Rock of Israel, 
by the God of your father, who helps you, by the Almighty, who blesses you with blessings of the sky above, blessings of the deep below, blessings of breast and womb. 
The blessings of your father are greater than the blessings of the ancient mountains, than the bounty of the everlasting hills. May they rest on Joseph's head, on the crown of the one set apart from his brothers. 
"Benjamin is a ravenous wolf. In the morning he devours the prey, and in the evening he divides the plunder." 
All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father said to them as he blessed them, each one with the blessing that fit him. 
Then he told them, "I'm about to be gathered to my people. Bury me with my fathers in the cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite, 
the cave in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre in Canaan, the field Abraham bought from Ephron the Hittite as a burial place. 
There they buried Abraham and his wife Sarah, there they buried Isaac and his wife Rebekah, and there I buried Leah, 
along with the field and the cave in it, bought from the sons of Heth." 
When Jacob had finished giving his sons these instructions, he drew his feet up into the bed, breathed his last, and was gathered to his people.
