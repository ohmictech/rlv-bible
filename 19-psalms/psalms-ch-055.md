The Chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A contemplation by David. Listen to my prayer, God. Don't hide yourself from my supplication. 
Attend to me, and answer me. I'm restless in my complaint, and moan 
because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the guilty. They bring suffering on me. In anger they hold a grudge against me. 
My heart is severely pained within me. The terrors of death have fallen on me. 
Fearfulness and trembling have come on me. Horror has overwhelmed me. 
I said, "Oh that I had wings like a dove! Then I'd fly away, and be at rest. 
Look, then I'd wander far off. I'd lodge in the wilderness." Selah. 
"I'd hurry to a shelter from the stormy wind and storm." 
Confuse them, Lord, and confound their language, for I have seen violence and strife in the city. 
Day and night they prowl around on its walls. Malice and abuse are also within her. 
Destructive forces are within her. Threats and lies don't depart from her streets. 
It wasn't an enemy who insulted me, then I could have endured it. Neither was it he who hated me who raised himself up against me, then I'd have hidden myself from him. 
But it was you, a man like me, my companion, and my familiar friend. 
We took sweet fellowship together. We walked in God's house with company. 
Let death come suddenly on them. Let them go down alive into Hades. Guilt is among them, in their dwelling. 
As for me, I'll call on God. God will save me. 
Evening, morning, and at noon, I'll cry out in distress. He'll hear my voice. 
He has bought back my soul in peace from the battle that was against me, although there are many who oppose me. 
God, who's enthroned for the eras, will hear and answer them. Selah. They never change and don't fear God. 
He raises his hands against his friends. He has violated his covenant. 
His mouth was smooth as butter, but his heart was war. His words were softer than oil, yet they were drawn swords. 
Cast your burden on God and he'll sustain you. He'll never allow the righteous to be moved. 
But you, God, will bring them down into the pit of destruction. Bloodthirsty and deceitful men won't live out half their days, but I'll trust in you.
