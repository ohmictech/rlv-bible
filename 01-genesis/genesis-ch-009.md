God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, "Be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth. 
Every animal of the earth and every bird of the sky will be afraid of you and dread you. Everything that moves along the ground and all the fish of the sea are given into your hand. 
Every moving thing that lives will be food for you. As I gave you the green plants, I now give you everything. 
But you must not eat flesh with its life in it, that is, its blood. 
I'll demand an accounting for your lifeblood. I'll demand it from every animal. And from every human being, each one for the life of his brother, I'll demand the life of a human being. 
Whoever sheds human blood, his own blood will be shed by human beings, because God made human beings in his own image. 
Be fruitful and multiply. Spread out over the earth and increase on it." 
God spoke to Noah and to his sons with him, saying, 
"As for me, look, I'm making my covenant with you and with your offspring after you, 
and with every living creature that's with you: the birds, the livestock, and every animal of the earth with you, of all that go out of the ship, even every animal of the earth. 
I'm making my covenant with you: never again will all flesh be cut off by the waters of a flood. There will never again be a flood to destroy the earth." 
God said, "This is the sign of the covenant I'm making between me and you and every living creature with you, for all generations to come: 
I have set my rainbow in the cloud, and it'll be a sign of a covenant between me and the earth. 
When I bring a cloud over the earth, that the rainbow will be seen in the cloud, 
I'll remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh, and the waters will never again become a flood to destroy all flesh. 
The rainbow will be in the cloud. I'll look at it, that I can remember the perpetual covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that's on the earth." 
God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant I have made between me and all flesh that's on the earth." 
The sons of Noah who went out from the ship were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham is the father of Canaan. 
These three were the sons of Noah, and from these the people of the whole earth spread out. 
Noah, a farmer, began to plant a vineyard. 
He drank some of the wine and got drunk, and he lay uncovered inside his tent. 
Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brothers outside. 
Shem and Japheth took a garment, laid it across both their shoulders, and walked in backwards to cover their father's nakedness. Their faces were turned away, so they didn't see their father's nakedness. 
Noah woke from his wine and knew what his youngest son had done to him. 
He said, "Canaan is cursed. He'll be the lowest slave to his brothers." 
He said, "Blessed be God, the God of Shem. Let Canaan be his slave. 
May God make room for Japheth. Let him live in the tents of Shem. Let Canaan be his slave." 
Noah lived three hundred fifty years after the flood. 
Noah lived nine hundred fifty years in all, and then he died.
