God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Command the children of Israel that they put out of the camp every leper, everyone who has a discharge, and whoever is unclean by a corpse. 
You will put both male and female outside of the camp so that they don't defile their camp, in the midst of which I dwell." 
The children of Israel did so, and put them outside of the camp; as God spoke to Moses, so the children of Israel did. 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel: 'When a man or woman commits any wrong that people do, so as to trespass against God, and that soul is guilty, 
then he will confess his wrongdoing which he has done; and he will make restitution for his guilt in full, add to it the fifth part of it, and give it to him in respect of whom he has been guilty. 
But if the man has no kinsman to whom restitution may be made for the guilt, the restitution for guilt which is made to God will be the priest's, in addition to the ram of the making right, by which things will be made right for him. 
Every heave offering of all the holy things of the children of Israel, which they present to the priest, will be his. 
Every man's holy things will be his; whatever any man gives the priest, it will be his.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them: 'If any man's wife goes astray and is unfaithful to him, 
and a man lies with her carnally, and it's hidden from the eyes of her husband and this is kept concealed, and she's defiled, there's no witness against her, and she isn't taken in the act; 
and the spirit of jealousy comes on him, and he's jealous of his wife and she's defiled; or if the spirit of jealousy comes on him, and he's jealous of his wife and she isn't defiled; 
then the man will bring his wife to the priest, and will bring her offering for her: one tenth of an ephah of barley meal. He will pour no oil on it, nor put frankincense on it, for it's a meal offering of jealousy, a meal offering of memorial, bringing guilt to memory. 
The priest will bring her near, and set her before God. 
The priest will take holy water in an earthen vessel; and the priest will take some of the dust that's on the floor of the tabernacle and put it into the water. 
The priest will set the woman before God, and let the hair of the woman's head go loose, and put the meal offering of memorial in her hands, which is the meal offering of jealousy. The priest will have in his hand the water of bitterness that brings a curse. 
The priest will cause her to take an oath and will tell the woman, "If no man has lain with you, and if you haven't gone aside to uncleanness, being under your husband's authority, be free from this water of bitterness that brings a curse. 
But if you have gone astray, being under your husband's authority, and if you're defiled, and some man has lain with you besides your husband, " 
then the priest will cause the woman to swear with the oath of cursing, and the priest will tell the woman, "May God make you a curse and an oath among your people, when God allows your thigh to fall away, and your body to swell; 
and this water that brings a curse will go into your bowels, and make your body swell, and your thigh fall away." The woman will say, "Amen, Amen." 
"'The priest will write these curses in a book, and he will wipe them into the water of bitterness. 
He will make the woman drink the water of bitterness that causes the curse; and the water that causes the curse will enter into her and become bitter. 
The priest will take the meal offering of jealousy out of the woman's hand, and will wave the meal offering before God, and bring it to the altar. 
The priest will take a handful of the meal offering, as its memorial portion, and burn it on the altar, and afterward will make the woman drink the water. 
When he has made her drink the water, then it will happen, if she's defiled and has committed a trespass against her husband, that the water that causes the curse will enter into her and become bitter, and her body will swell, and her thigh will fall away; and the woman will be a curse among her people. 
If the woman isn't defiled, but is clean; then she will be free, and will conceive offspring. 
"'This is the law of jealousy, when a wife, being under her husband, goes astray, and is defiled, 
or when the spirit of jealousy comes on a man, and he's jealous of his wife; then he will set the woman before God, and the priest will execute on her all this law. 
The man will be free from guilt, and that woman will bear her guilt.'"
