On the eighth day, Moses called Aaron and his sons and the elders of Israel, 
and he said to Aaron, "Take a calf from the herd for an offering for wrongdoing and a ram for a burnt offering, both without defect, and offer them before God. 
Then speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'Take a male goat for an offering for wrongdoing, and a calf and a lamb, both a year old and without defect, for a burnt offering, 
and a bull and a ram for the wholeness offerings, to sacrifice before God, and a gift offering mixed with oil, because today God is going to appear to you.'" 
They brought what Moses commanded to the front of the Tent of Meeting, and the whole community came near and stood before God. 
Moses said, "This is what God has commanded you to do, so that God's splendor will appear to you." 
Moses said to Aaron, "Come to the altar and offer your offering for wrongdoing and your burnt offering, and make things right for yourself and for the people. Then offer the people's offering and make things right for them, just as God commanded." 
So Aaron came to the altar and killed the calf of the offering for wrongdoing, which was for himself. 
Aaron's sons brought him the blood, and he dipped his finger in it and put it on the horns of the altar, and poured out the blood at the base of the altar, 
but the fat, the kidneys and the covering of the liver from the offering for wrongdoing he burned on the altar, just as God commanded Moses. 
The meat and the skin he burned with fire outside the camp. 
He killed the burnt offering, and Aaron's sons handed him the blood, and he splashed it against all sides of the altar. 
They handed him the burnt offering piece by piece, along with the head, and he burned them on the altar. 
He washed the inner organs and the legs and burned them on top of the burnt offering on the altar. 
He brought the people's offering, took the goat of the offering for wrongdoing that was for the people, and killed it and offered it as an offering for wrongdoing, the same way as the first. 
He brought the burnt offering and offered it following the rule. 
He brought the gift offering, took a handful from it and burned it on the altar, in addition to the morning burnt offering. 
He also killed the bull and the ram, the sacrifice of the wholeness offerings for the people. Aaron's sons handed him the blood, and he splashed it against all sides of the altar, 
along with the fat of the bull and the ram: the fat tail, the fat covering the inner organs, the kidneys and the covering of the liver. 
They put the fat on the breasts, and he burned the fat on the altar. 
Aaron waved the breasts and the right thigh as a wave offering before God, just as Moses commanded. 
Aaron lifted his hands toward the people and blessed them, and he came down from offering the offering for wrongdoing, the burnt offering and the wholeness offerings. 
Moses and Aaron went into the Tent of Meeting, and when they came out they blessed the people, and God's splendor appeared to everyone. 
Fire came out from before God and burned up the burnt offering and the fat on the altar. When the people saw it, they shouted and fell face down.
