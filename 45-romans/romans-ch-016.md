I commend to you Phoebe, our sister, who is a servant of the gathering that is at Cenchreae, 
that you receive her in the Lord in a way worthy of the set-apart ones, and that you assist her in whatever matter she may need from you, for she herself also has been a helper of many, and of my own self. 
Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus, 
who risked their own necks for my life, to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the gatherings of the nations. 
Greet the gathering that is in their house. Greet Epaenetus, whom I truly love, who is the firstfruits of Achaia to Christ. 
Greet Mary, who labored much for us. 
Greet Andronicus and Junia, my relatives and my fellow prisoners, who are notable among the sent ones, who were also in Christ before me. 
Greet Amplias, whom I truly love in the Lord. 
Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, whom I truly love. 
Greet Apelles, the approved in Christ. Greet those who are of the household of Aristobulus. 
Greet Herodion, my kinsman. Greet them of the household of Narcissus, who are in the Lord. 
Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Greet Persis, who is truly loved, who labored much in the Lord. 
Greet Rufus, the chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine. 
Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers who are with them. 
Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the set-apart ones who are with them. 
Greet one another with a set-apart kiss. The gatherings of Christ greet you. 
Now I beg you, brothers, look out for those who are causing the divisions and the traps, contrary to the teaching which you learned, and turn away from them. 
For those who are such don't serve our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by their smooth and flattering speech they deceive the hearts of the innocent. 
For your obedience has become known to all. I celebrate therefore over you. But I desire to have you wise in that which is good, but innocent in what is bad. 
And the God of wholeness will quickly crush the Adversary under your feet. The favor of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. 
Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you, as do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my relatives. 
I, Tertius, who write the letter, greet you in the Lord. 
Gaius, my host and host of the whole gathering, greets you. Erastus, the treasurer of the city, greets you, as does Quartus, the brother. 
The favor of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all! Amen.
