Now I don't want you to be unaware, brothers, that our ancestors were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea; 
and were all immersed into Moses in the cloud and in the sea; 
and all ate the same spiritual food; 
and all drank the same spiritual drink. They drank of a spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ. 
However, God wasn't delighted with most of them, for they were laid low in the empty land. 
Now these things became examples for us, so that we wouldn't crave bad things as they craved them. 
Don't become idol worshipers, as some of them were. As it's written, "The people sat down to eat and drink, and got up to play." 
Let's not sleep around, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day. 
Let's not test Christ, as some of them tested, and were killed by the snakes. 
Don't grumble, as some of them grumbled, and were killed by the destroyer. 
Now all these things happened to them by way of example, and they were written down to warn us, on whom the ends of the eras have come. 
So let him who thinks he stands be careful that he doesn't fall. 
No test has taken you except what's common to people. God is trustworthy, and he won't let you be tested beyond what you can bear, but with the test he'll also make a way out, so that you can stay with it. 
So, my truly loved, run from idol worship. 
I speak as to sensible people. Pass a verdict on what I say. 
The cup of blessing which we bless, isn't it a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, isn't it a sharing in the body of Christ? 
Because there's one loaf of bread, we, who are many, are one body; for we all partake of the one loaf of bread. 
Consider Israel according to the flesh. Don't those who eat the sacrifices participate in the altar? 
What am I saying then? That a thing sacrificed to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything? 
But I say that the things the nations sacrifice, they sacrifice to unclean spirits and not to God, and I don't want you to have a share with unclean spirits. 
You can't drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of unclean spirits. You can't share the table of the Lord and the table of unclean spirits. 
Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he? 
"All things are permitted for me," but not all things are helpful. "All things are permitted for me," but not all things build up. 
Let no one seek his own, but each one his neighbor's good. 
Whatever is sold in the butcher shop, eat, asking no question for the sake of conscience, 
for "the earth is the Lord's, and its fullness." 
But if one of those who don't trust invites you to a meal, and you're inclined to go, eat whatever is set before you, asking no questions for the sake of conscience. 
But if anyone says to you, "This was offered to idols," don't eat it for the sake of the one who told you, and for the sake of conscience. "the earth is the Lord's, with all its fullness." 
Conscience, I say, not your own, but the other's conscience. Why should my freedom be judged by someone else's conscience? 
If I partake with thanks, why am I denounced for something I give thanks for? 
Whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do it all to the weight of God. 
Give no cause for stumbling, whether to Jewish people, to Greeks, or to the gathering of God; 
just as I also please everyone in all things, not seeking my own good, but the good of everyone, so that they may be made whole.
