I must boast, though it does no good. I will come to visions and unveilings from the Lord. 
I know a man in Christ who was caught up to the third sky fourteen years ago, whether in the body, I don't know, or out of the body, I don't know; God knows. 
I know such a man (whether in the body, or outside of the body, I don't know; God knows), 
how he was caught up into paradise and heard words that can't be put into speech, which no one is allowed to say. 
On behalf of such a person I will take pride, but on my own behalf I will not take pride, except in my weaknesses. 
If I wanted to take pride, I would not be foolish, because I would be speaking the truth. But I refrain, so that no man may think more of me than that which he sees in me or hears from me. 
Because of the greatness of the unveilings, so that I wouldn't be lifted up too high, a thorn was given me in the flesh: a messenger of the Adversary to test me, so that I wouldn't be lifted up too high. 
Concerning this thing, I begged the Lord three times that it might depart from me. 
He said to me, "My gift is enough for you, for my power is made complete in weakness." So I will most gladly take pride all the more in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest on me. 
So I take pleasure in weaknesses, in insults, in hardships, in being hunted down, and in tight spots, for Christ's sake. When I am weak, then I am strong. 
I have become foolish in boasting. You forced me to it, for I ought to have been commended by you. I am in no way behind those super-sent ones, though I am nothing. 
The signs of a sent one were worked among you with all staying power, in signs and wonders and powerful works. 
How were you worse off than the other gatherings, except that I myself was not a burden to you? Release me from this wrong! 
Look, this is the third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not be a burden to you, for I don't want what you have, I want you. Children shouldn't save up for their parents, but parents for their children. 
I will most gladly spend and be spent for your lives. If I love you more, am I loved less? 
Even so, I myself didn't burden you. But you might say that being crafty, I caught you with deception. 
Did I take advantage of you by anyone of those whom I have sent to you? 
I exhorted Titus, and I sent the brother with him. Did Titus take any advantage of you? Didn't we walk in the same spirit? Didn't we walk in the same steps? 
Again, do you think that we are excusing ourselves to you? In the sight of God we speak in Christ. And all things, truly loved, are for building you up. 
I am afraid that perhaps when I come, I might find you not the way I want to, and you might find me not the way you want: perhaps quarreling, jealousy, flare, factions, slander, whispering, pride, and disorder. 
that again when I come my God would humble me before you, and I would grieve for many of those who drifted before now, and have not had a change of heart about the uncleanness, sleeping around, and shamelessness which they practiced.
