The land allotted to the descendants of Joseph began at the Jordan near Jericho, at the waters of Jericho on the east, then went into the wilderness, up from Jericho through the hill country to Bethel. 
It went out from Bethel to Luz and on to the border of the Archites at Ataroth, 
then down west to the border of the Japhletites, to the lower Beth Horon, and on to Gezer, ending at the sea. 
So the descendants of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their inheritance. 
This was the territory of the Ephraimites, clan by clan. The border of their inheritance on the east was Ataroth Addar as far as upper Beth Horon. 
The border went out west at Michmethath on the north, then turned east to Taanath Shiloh and passed east of Janoah. 
It went down from Janoah to Ataroth and Naarah, reached Jericho and came out at the Jordan. 
From Tappuah the border went west to the brook of Kanah and ended at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of Ephraim, clan by clan, 
together with the towns set apart for the Ephraimites inside the inheritance of the Manassites, all those towns with their villages. 
They didn't drive out the Canaanites living in Gezer, so the Canaanites still live among the Ephraimites to this day and are forced to work for them.
