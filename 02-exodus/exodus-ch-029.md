"This is the thing that you will do to them to make them holy, to minister to me in the priest's office: take one young bull and two rams without defect, 
unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil. You will make them of fine wheat flour. 
You will put them into one basket, and bring them in the basket, with the bull and the two rams. 
You will bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the Tent of Meeting, and will wash them with water. 
You will take the garments, and put on Aaron the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastplate, and clothe him with the skillfully woven band of the ephod. 
You will set the turban on his head, and put the holy crown on the turban. 
Then you will take the anointing oil, and pour it on his head, and anoint him. 
You will bring his sons, and put tunics on them. 
You will clothe them with belts, Aaron and his sons, and bind headbands on them. They will have the priesthood by a perpetual statute. You will consecrate Aaron and his sons. 
"You will bring the bull before the Tent of Meeting; and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the bull. 
You will kill the bull before God at the door of the Tent of Meeting. 
You will take of the blood of the bull, and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger; and you will pour out all the blood at the base of the altar. 
You will take all the fat that covers the innards, the cover of the liver, the two kidneys, and the fat that's on them, and burn them on the altar. 
But the meat of the bull, and its skin, and its dung, you will burn with fire outside of the camp. It's an offering for wrongdoing. 
"You will also take the one ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram. 
You will kill the ram, and you will take its blood, and sprinkle it around on the altar. 
You will cut the ram into its pieces, and wash its innards, and its legs, and put them with its pieces, and with its head. 
You will burn the whole ram on the altar: it's a burnt offering to God; it's a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to God. 
"You will take the other ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram. 
Then you will kill the ram, and take some of its blood, and put it on the tip of the right ear of Aaron, and on the tip of the right ear of his sons, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot; and sprinkle the blood around on the altar. 
You will take of the blood that's on the altar, and of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it on Aaron, and on his garments, and on his sons, and on the garments of his sons with him: and he will be made holy, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons' garments with him. 
Also you will take some of the ram's fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the innards, the cover of the liver, the two kidneys, the fat that's on them, and the right thigh (for it's a ram of consecration), 
and one loaf of bread, one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer out of the basket of unleavened bread that's before God. 
You will put all of this in Aaron's hands, and in his sons' hands, and will wave them for a wave offering before God. 
You will take them from their hands, and burn them on the altar on the burnt offering, for a pleasant aroma before God: it's an offering made by fire to God. 
"You will take the breast of Aaron's ram of consecration, and wave it for a wave offering before God. It will be your portion. 
You will sanctify the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the wave offering, which is waved, and which is raised up, of the ram of consecration, even of that which is for Aaron, and of that which is for his sons. 
It will be for Aaron and his sons as their portion for the eras from the children of Israel; for it's a wave offering. It will be a wave offering from the children of Israel of the sacrifices of their offerings for wholeness, even their wave offering to God. 
"The holy garments of Aaron will be for his sons after him, to be anointed in them, and to be consecrated in them. 
Seven days will the son who's priest in his place put them on, when he comes into the Tent of Meeting to minister in the holy place. 
"You will take the ram of consecration and boil its meat in a holy place. 
Aaron and his sons will eat the meat of the ram, and the bread that's in the basket, at the door of the Tent of Meeting. 
They will eat those things with which things were made right, to consecrate and sanctify them; but a stranger won't eat of it, because they're holy. 
If anything of the meat of the consecration, or of the bread, remains to the morning, then you will burn the remainder with fire. It won't be eaten, because it's holy. 
"You will do so to Aaron and to his sons, according to all that I have commanded you. You will consecrate them seven days. 
Every day you will offer the bull of offering for wrongdoing for making things right. You will cleanse the altar when you make things right for it. You will anoint it, to sanctify it. 
Seven days you will make things right for the altar, and sanctify it; and the altar will be most holy. Whatever touches the altar will be holy. 
"Now this is that which you will offer on the altar: two lambs a year old day by day continually. 
The one lamb you will offer in the morning; and the other lamb you will offer at evening; 
and with the one lamb a tenth part of an ephah of fine flour mixed with the fourth part of a hin of beaten oil, and the fourth part of a hin of wine for a drink offering. 
The other lamb you will offer at evening, and will do to it according to the meal offering of the morning and according to its drink offering, for a pleasant aroma, an offering made by fire to God. 
It will be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the door of the Tent of Meeting before God, where I'll meet with you, to speak there to you. 
There I'll meet with the children of Israel; and the place will be sanctified by my glory. 
I'll sanctify the Tent of Meeting and the altar. I'll also sanctify Aaron and his sons to minister to me in the priest's office. 
I'll dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God. 
They will know that I'm their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, that I might dwell among them: I'm their God.
