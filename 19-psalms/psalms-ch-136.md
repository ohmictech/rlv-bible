Give thanks to God, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures for the eras. 
Give thanks to the God of gods, for his loving kindness endures for the eras. 
Give thanks to the Lord of lords, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who alone does great wonders, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who by understanding made the heavens, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who spread out the earth above the waters, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who made the great lights, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
the sun to rule by day, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
the moon and stars to rule by night, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who struck down the Egyptian firstborn, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
and brought out Israel from among them, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
with a strong hand, and with an outstretched arm, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who divided the Red Sea apart, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
and made Israel to pass through the middle of it, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
but overthrew Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who led his people through the wilderness, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
to him who struck great kings, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
and killed mighty kings, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
Sihon king of the Amorites, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
Og king of Bashan, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
and gave their land as an inheritance, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
even a heritage to Israel his servant, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
who remembered us in our low estate, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
and has delivered us from our adversaries, for his loving kindness endures for the eras; 
who gives food to every creature, for his loving kindness endures for the eras. 
Oh give thanks to the God of heaven, for his loving kindness endures for the eras.
