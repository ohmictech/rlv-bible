In the same way, wives, submit to your own husbands, so that even if some of them refuse to obey the message, they may be won over without a word by the way their wives live, 
as they see the pure way you live, with respect. 
Your beauty should not come from the outside, from braided hair, gold jewelry, or fine clothes, 
It should come from the inner person, from the heart, from a gentle and quiet spirit that never decays. That is what God counts precious. 
This is how the set-apart women of the past who put their expectation in God made themselves beautiful: they submitted to their own husbands. 
Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him her master. You are her daughters if you do good and do not give in to fear. 
Husbands, in the same way, live with your wives with understanding. Give them honor as the weaker partner, and remember that they are heirs with you of the gift of life, so that nothing blocks your prayers. 
Finally, all of you should be of one mind. Sympathize with each other. Love each other as brothers and sisters. Be tenderhearted and humble. 
Do not pay back evil with evil or insult with insult. Instead, bless, because you were called to that, so that you may receive a blessing. 
For, "Whoever wants to love life and see good days must keep their tongue from evil and their lips from deceit. 
They must turn away from evil and do good. They must chase wholeness and go after it. 
For the eyes of the Lord are on the upright, and his ears are open to their prayer; but the face of the Lord is against those who do evil." 
And who is going to harm you if you are eager to do good? 
But even if you do suffer for doing what is right, you are flourishing. "Do not be afraid of what they fear, and do not be shaken." 
But set apart the Lord, the Christ, as holy in your hearts. Always be ready to give an answer to anyone who asks you why you have the expectation you have, but do it gently and with respect, 
and keep a clean conscience. Then those who slander your good way of life in Christ may be put to shame for the way they speak against you. 
For it is better, if God wills it, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil. 
For Christ also suffered for mistakes once and for all, the upright one for the wrongdoers, to bring you to God. He was put to death in the body, but made alive in the Spirit, 
in whom he also went and announced the message to the spirits in prison, 
to those who long ago refused to obey, while God waited patiently in the days of Noah, while the boat was being built. In it, only a few people, eight in all, were rescued through water. 
That water is a picture of immersion, which now rescues you, not by washing dirt off the body, but by the pledge of a clean conscience toward God, through the rising again of Jesus Christ, 
who has gone into the sky and is at God's right hand, with messengers, authorities, and powers made subject to him.
