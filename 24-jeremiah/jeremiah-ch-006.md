"Flee for safety, you children of Benjamin, out of the middle of Jerusalem! Blow the trumpet in Tekoa and raise up a signal on Beth Haccherem, for bad looks out from the north with a great destruction. 
I'll cut off the beautiful and delicate one, the daughter of Zion. 
Shepherds with their flocks will come to her. They'll pitch their tents against her all around. They'll feed everyone in his place." 
"Prepare war against her! Arise! Let's go up at noon. Woe to us! The day declines, for the shadows of the evening are stretched out. 
Arise! Let's go up by night, and let's destroy her palaces." 
The God of Armies said, "Cut down trees, and cast up a mound against Jerusalem. This is the city to be visited. She's filled with oppression within herself. 
As a well produces its waters, so she produces her guilt. Violence and destruction is heard in her. Sickness and wounds are continually before me. 
Be instructed, Jerusalem, lest my soul be alienated from you, lest I make you a desolation, an uninhabited land." 
The God of Armies says, "They'll thoroughly glean the remnant of Israel like a vine. Turn again your hand as a grape gatherer into the baskets." 
To whom should I speak and testify, that they may hear? Look, their ear is uncircumcised, and they can't listen. Look, God's word has become a reproach to them. They have no delight in it. 
So I'm full of God's wrath. I'm weary with holding it in. "Pour it out on the children in the street, and on the assembly of young men together; for even the husband with the wife will be taken, the aged with him who's full of days. 
Their houses will be turned to others, their fields and their wives together; for I'll stretch out my hand on the inhabitants of the land, says God." 
"From their least even to their greatest, everyone is given to covetousness. From the spokesman even to the priest, everyone deals falsely. 
They have healed also the hurt of my people superficially, saying, 'Peace, peace!' when there's no peace. 
Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? No, they weren't at all ashamed, neither could they blush. So they'll fall among those who fall. When I visit them, they'll be cast down," says God. 
God says, "Stand in the ways and see, and ask for the old paths, 'Where is the good way?' and walk in it, and you'll find rest for your souls. But they said, 'We won't walk in it.' 
I set watchmen over you, saying, 'Listen to the sound of the trumpet!' But they said, 'We won't listen!' 
Therefore hear, you nations, and know, congregation, what's among them. 
Hear, earth! Look, I'll bring bad on this people, even the fruit of their thoughts, because they haven't listened to my words; and as for my law, they have rejected it. 
To what purpose does frankincense from Sheba come to me, and the sweet cane from a far country? Your burnt offerings aren't acceptable, and your sacrifices aren't pleasing to me." 
So God says, "Look, I'll lay stumbling blocks before this people. The fathers and the sons together will stumble against them. The neighbor and his friend will perish." 
God says, "Look, a people comes from the north country. A great nation will be stirred up from the uttermost parts of the earth. 
They take hold of bow and spear. They're cruel, and have no mercy. Their voice roars like the sea, and they ride on horses, everyone set in array, as a man to the battle, against you, daughter of Zion." 
We have heard its report. Our hands become feeble. Anguish has taken hold of us, and pains as of a woman in labor. 
Don't go out into the field or walk by the way; for the sword of the enemy and terror are on every side. 
Daughter of my people, clothe yourself with sackcloth, and wallow in ashes! Mourn, as for an only son, most bitter lamentation, for the destroyer will suddenly come on us. 
"I have made you a tester of metals and a fortress among my people, that you may know and try their way. 
They're all grievous rebels, going around to slander. They're bronze and iron. All of them deal corruptly. 
The bellows blow fiercely. The lead is consumed in the fire. In vain they go on refining, for the guilty aren't plucked away. 
Men will call them rejected silver, because God has rejected them."
