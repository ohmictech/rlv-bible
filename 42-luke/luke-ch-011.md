When he finished praying in a certain place, one of his students said to him, "Lord, teach us to pray, just as John also taught his students." 
He said to them, "When you pray, say: 'Our Father in the sky, let your name be held in honor. Let your reign come. Let your will be done on land as it is in the sky. 
Give us today our bread for the coming day. 
Release us from what we owe, as we also release those who owe us. And do not bring us into the test, but rescue us from the Slanderer. For yours is the reign and the power and the honor, into the eras. Amen.'" 
He said to them, "Which of you, if you go to a friend at midnight and tell him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves of bread, 
for a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him,' 
and he from within will answer and say, 'Don't bother me. The door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed. I can't get up and give it to you'? 
I tell you, although he will not rise and give it to him because he is his friend, yet because of his persistence, he will get up and give him as many as he needs. 
"I tell you, keep asking, and it will be given you. Keep seeking, and you will find. Keep knocking, and it will be opened to you. 
For everyone who asks receives. He who seeks finds. To him who knocks it will be opened. 
"Which of you fathers, if your son asks for bread, will give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, he won't give him a snake instead of a fish, will he? 
Or if he asks for an egg, he won't give him a scorpion, will he? 
If you then, being malicious, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in the sky give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?" 
He was casting out an unclean spirit, and it kept the man from speaking. When the unclean spirit had gone out, the man who had been unable to speak spoke; and the crowds were amazed. 
But some of them said, "He drives out unclean spirits by Beelzebul, the ruler of the unclean spirits." 
Others, testing him, sought from him a sign from the sky. 
But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, "Every reign divided against itself is laid waste. A house divided against itself falls. 
If the Adversary also is divided against himself, how will his reign stand? For you say that I drive out unclean spirits by Beelzebul. 
But if I drive out unclean spirits by Beelzebul, by whom do your children drive them out? Therefore they will be your judges. 
But if I drive out unclean spirits by God's finger, then God's reign has come to you. 
"When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own dwelling, his goods are safe. 
But when someone stronger attacks him and overcomes him, he takes from him his whole armor in which he trusted, and divides his plunder. 
"He who is not with me is against me. He who doesn't gather with me scatters. 
The unclean spirit, when he has gone out of the man, passes through dry places, seeking rest; and finding none, he says, 'I will turn back to my house from which I came out.' 
When he returns, he finds it swept and put in order. 
Then he goes and takes seven other spirits more malicious than himself, and they enter in and dwell there. The last state of that man becomes worse than the first." 
It came to pass, as he said these things, a certain woman out of the crowd lifted up her voice and said to him, "Flourishing is the womb that bore you, and the breasts which nursed you!" 
But he said, "On the contrary, flourishing are those who hear the word of God, and keep it." 
When the crowds were gathering together to him, he began to say, "This is a malicious generation. It seeks after a sign. No sign will be given to it but the sign of Jonah the spokesman. 
For even as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so the Human One will also be to this generation. 
The Queen of the South will rise up at the verdict with the men of this generation and will sentence them, for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and look, one greater than Solomon is here. 
The men of Nineveh will stand up at the verdict with this generation, and will sentence it, for they had a change of heart at the preaching of Jonah; and look, one greater than Jonah is here. 
"No one who lights a lamp puts it in a cellar or under a basket, but on a stand, so that those who come in may see the light. 
The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore when your eye is good, your whole body is also full of light; but when it is malicious, your body also is full of darkness. 
So see to it that the light in you isn't darkness. 
If therefore your whole body is full of light, having no part dark, it will be wholly full of light, as when the lamp with its bright shining gives you light." 
Now as he spoke, a certain Pharisee asked him to dine with him. He went in and sat at the table. 
When the Pharisee saw it, he was amazed that he had not first immersed himself before dinner. 
The Lord said to him, "Now you Pharisees make clean the outside of the cup and of the dish, but your inside is full of robbery and malice. 
You foolish ones, didn't he who made the outside make the inside also? 
But give what is inside as a gift to the needy, and look, all things will be clean to you. 
But how terrible for you Pharisees! For you tithe mint and rue and every herb, but you bypass right judgment and God's love. You should have done these without leaving the others undone. 
How terrible for you Pharisees! For you love the best seats in the gathering places and the greetings in the marketplaces. 
How terrible for you, Bible teachers and Pharisees, pretenders! For you are like unmarked tombs, and the people who walk over them don't know it." 
One of the experts in the law answered him, "Teacher, in saying this you insult us also." 
He said, "How terrible for you experts in the law also! For you load men with heavy burdens, and you yourselves won't even lift one finger to help carry them. 
How terrible for you! For you build the tombs of the spokesmen, and your fathers killed them. 
So you testify and consent to the deeds of your ancestors. For they killed them, and you build their tombs. 
Therefore also the wisdom of God said, 'I will send to them spokesmen and those sent out; and some of them they will kill and hunt down, 
that the blood of all the spokesmen, which was shed from the founding of the world, may be required of this generation, 
from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zachariah, who was killed between the altar and the temple.' Yes, I tell you, it will be required of this generation. 
How terrible for you experts in the law! For you took away the key of knowledge. You didn't enter in yourselves, and those who were entering in, you hindered." 
As he said these things to them, the Bible teachers and the Pharisees began to be terribly angry, and to press him with many questions, 
lying in wait for him, and seeking to catch him in something he might say, that they might accuse him.
