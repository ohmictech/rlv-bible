"Say to your brothers, 'My people!' and to your sisters, 'My loved one!' 
Contend with your mother! Contend, for she isn't my wife, neither am I her husband; and let her put away her prostitution from her face, and her adulteries from between her breasts; 
lest I strip her naked, and make her bare as in the day that she was born, and make her like a wilderness, and set her like a dry land, and kill her with thirst. 
Indeed, on her children I'll have no mercy, for they're children of unfaithfulness. 
Their mother has played the prostitute. She who conceived them has done shamefully; for she said, 'I'll go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink.' 
Therefore look, I'll hedge up your way with thorns, and I'll build a wall against her, that she can't find her way. 
She'll follow after her lovers, but she won't overtake them; and she'll seek them, but won't find them. Then she'll say, 'I'll go and return to my first husband, for then it was better with me than now.' 
For she didn't know that I gave her the grain, the new wine, and the oil, and multiplied to her silver and gold, which they used for Baal. 
So I'll take back my grain in its time, and my new wine in its season, and will pluck away my wool and my flax which should have covered her nakedness. 
Now I'll uncover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and no one will deliver her out of my hand. 
I'll also cause all her celebrations to cease: her feasts, her new moons, her Sabbaths, and all her solemn assemblies. 
I'll lay waste her vines and her fig trees, about which she has said, 'These are my wages that my lovers have given me,' and I'll make them a forest, and the animals of the field will eat them. 
I'll visit on her the days of the Baals, to which she burned incense when she decked herself with her earrings and her jewels, and went after her lovers and forgot me," says God. 
"Look, I'll allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak tenderly to her. 
I'll give her vineyards from there, and the valley of Achor for a door of hope; and she'll respond there as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt. 
It'll be in that day," says God, "that you'll call me 'my husband,' and no longer call me 'my master.' 
For I'll take away the names of the Baals out of her mouth, and they'll no longer be mentioned by name. 
In that day I'll make a covenant for them with the animals of the field, and with the birds of the sky, and with the creeping things of the ground. I'll break the bow, the sword, and the battle out of the land, and will make them lie down safely. 
I'll betroth you to me for the eras. Yes, I'll betroth you to me in righteousness, in justice, in loving kindness, and in compassion. 
I'll even betroth you to me in faithfulness; and you will know God. 
It'll happen in that day, that I'll respond," says God. "I'll respond to the sky, and they'll respond to the earth; 
and the earth will respond to the grain, and the new wine, and the oil; and they'll respond to Jezreel. 
I'll sow her to me in the earth; and I'll have mercy on her who hadn't obtained mercy; and I'll tell those who weren't my people, 'You're my people;' and they'll say, 'You're My God!'"
