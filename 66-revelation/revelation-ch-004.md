After this I looked and saw a door standing open in the sky, and the voice I had first heard speaking to me like a trumpet said, "Come up here, and I'll show you what must happen after this." 
At once I was in the Spirit. There in the sky stood a throne, with someone sitting on it, 
and the one sitting there looked like jasper and ruby. A rainbow that looked like an emerald surrounded the throne. 
Around the throne were twenty-four other thrones, and on them sat twenty-four elders, dressed in white clothes with gold wreaths on their heads. 
From the throne came flashes of lightning, rumblings, and peals of thunder. In front of the throne seven lamps of fire were burning, and they're the seven Spirits of God. 
In front of the throne there was something like a sea of glass, clear as crystal. In the middle, around the throne, were four living creatures covered with eyes in front and behind. 
The first living creature was like a lion, the second was like a calf, the third had a face like a human being, and the fourth was like an eagle in flight. 
Each of the four living creatures had six wings and was covered with eyes all around and underneath. Day and night they never stop saying, "Set apart, set apart, set apart is the Lord God, the one who holds sway over all, who was and who's and who's coming!" 
Whenever the living creatures give weight, honor, and thanks to the one who sits on the throne and lives through all the eras, 
the twenty-four elders fall down before the one who sits on the throne and bow down to the one who lives through all the eras. They lay their wreaths in front of the throne and say, 
"You're worthy, our Lord and God, to receive the weight, the honor, and the power, because you created everything, and by your will it came to be and was created!"
