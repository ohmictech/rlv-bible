Follow my example, just as I also follow Christ's. 
Now I praise you, brothers, that you remember me in all things, and hold firmly to what was handed down, just as I handed it to you. 
But I would have you know that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of the woman is man, and the head of Christ is God. 
Every man who prays or speaks for God with his head covered dishonors his head. 
But every woman who prays or speaks for God with her head uncovered dishonors her head. It is the same as if she were shaved. 
For if a woman is not covered, let her hair also be cut off. But if it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut off or be shaved, let her be covered. 
For a man indeed ought not to have his head covered, because he is the image and weight of God, but the woman is the weight of the man. 
For man is not from woman, but woman from man; 
for man wasn't created for the woman, but woman for the man. 
For this cause the woman ought to have authority over her own head, because of the messengers. 
Nevertheless, neither is the woman independent of the man, nor the man independent of the woman, in the Lord. 
For as woman came from man, so a man also comes through a woman; but all things are from God. 
Pass a verdict among yourselves. Is it appropriate for a woman to pray to God unveiled? 
Doesn't even nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a dishonor to him? 
but if a woman has long hair, it is a weight to her, for her hair is given to her as a covering. 
But if any man seems to be contentious, we have no such custom, and neither do God's gatherings. 
But in giving you this command I don't praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse. 
For first of all, when you come together as a gathering, I hear that divisions exist among you, and I partly accept it. 
For there must also be factions among you, so that those who pass the test may become visible among you. 
When you come together then, it is not the Lord's meal that you eat. 
For in your eating each one takes his own dinner first. One is hungry, and another is drunk. 
What? Don't you have houses to eat and drink in? Or do you look down on God's gathering and shame those who have nothing? What should I tell you? Should I praise you? In this I don't praise you. 
For I received from the Lord that which also I delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus on the night he was handed over, took bread. 
When he had given thanks, he broke it and said, "Take, eat. This is my body, which is broken for you. Do this in memory of me." 
In the same way he also took the cup after dinner, saying, "This cup is the new binding arrangement in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink, in memory of me." 
For as often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you announce the Lord's death until he comes. 
Therefore whoever eats this bread or drinks the Lord's cup in a way unworthy of the Lord will be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord. 
But let a person examine himself, and then eat of the bread and drink of the cup. 
For the one who eats and drinks in an unworthy way eats and drinks a verdict against himself if he doesn't recognize the Lord's body. 
For this cause many among you are weak and sickly, and not a few sleep. 
For if we passed a verdict on ourselves, we wouldn't be judged. 
But when we are judged, we are disciplined by the Lord, so that we may not be sentenced with the world. 
Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another. 
But if anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that your coming together doesn't bring a verdict. The rest I will set in order when I come.
