When Jesus had spoken these words, he went out with his students over the brook Kidron, where there was a garden, into which he and his students entered. 
Now Judas, who handed him over, also knew the place, for Jesus often met there with his students. 
Judas then, having taken a detachment of soldiers and temple guards from the chief priests and the strict religious ones, came there with lanterns, torches, and weapons. 
Jesus therefore, knowing all the things that were happening to him, went out and said to them, "Who are you looking for?" 
They answered him, "Jesus of Nazareth." Jesus said to them, "I am the one." Judas also, who handed him over, was standing with them. 
When therefore he said to them, "I am the one," they went backward and fell to the ground. 
Again therefore he asked them, "Who are you looking for?" They said, "Jesus of Nazareth." 
Jesus answered, "I told you that I am the one. If therefore you seek me, let these go their way," 
so that the word would be fulfilled which he spoke, "Of those whom you have given me, I have lost none." 
Simon Peter therefore, having a sword, drew it, struck the high priest's slave, and cut off his right ear. The slave's name was Malchus. 
Jesus therefore said to Peter, "Put the sword into its sheath. Am I not going to drink the cup which the Father has given me?" 
So the detachment, the commanding officer, and the temple guards of the Jewish leaders seized Jesus and bound him, 
and led him to Annas first, for he was father-in-law to Caiaphas, who was high priest that year. 
Now it was Caiaphas who advised the Jewish leaders that it was better that one man should die for the people. 
Simon Peter followed Jesus, as did another student. Now that student was known to the high priest, and entered in with Jesus into the courtyard of the high priest; 
but Peter was standing at the door outside. So the other student, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to her who kept the door, and brought in Peter. 
Then the servant girl who kept the door said to Peter, "Are you also one of this man's students?" He said, "I am not." 
Now the slaves and the temple guards were standing there, having made a fire of coals, for it was cold. They were warming themselves. Peter was with them, standing and warming himself. 
The high priest therefore asked Jesus about his students and about his teaching. 
Jesus answered him, "I spoke openly to the world. I always taught in gathering places and in the temple courts, where the Jewish people always meet. I said nothing in secret. 
Why do you ask me? Ask those who have heard me what I said to them. Look, they know the things which I said." 
When he had said this, one of the temple guards standing by slapped Jesus with his hand, saying, "Do you answer the high priest like that?" 
Jesus answered him, "If I have spoken badly, testify to the bad thing; but if well, why do you hit me?" 
Annas sent him bound to Caiaphas, the high priest. 
Now Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. They said therefore to him, "You aren't also one of his students, are you?" He denied it and said, "I am not." 
One of the slaves of the high priest, being a relative of him whose ear Peter had cut off, said, "Didn't I see you in the garden with him?" 
Peter therefore denied it again, and immediately the rooster crowed. 
They led Jesus therefore from Caiaphas into the governor's headquarters. It was early, and they themselves didn't enter into the governor's headquarters, so that they would not be made unclean, but could eat the Passover. 
Pilate therefore went out to them and said, "What accusation do you bring against this man?" 
They answered him, "If this man weren't a criminal, we wouldn't have handed him over to you." 
Pilate therefore said to them, "Take him yourselves, and judge him according to your law." So the Jewish leaders said to him, "It is illegal for us to put anyone to death," 
so that the word of Jesus would be fulfilled, which he spoke, signifying by what kind of death he should die. 
Pilate therefore entered again into the governor's headquarters, called Jesus, and said to him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" 
Jesus answered him, "Do you say this by yourself, or did others tell you about me?" 
Pilate answered, "I'm not a Jewish man, am I? Your own nation and the chief priests handed you over to me. What have you done?" 
Jesus answered, "My reign is not of this world. If my reign were of this world, then my servants would fight, that I wouldn't be handed over to the Jewish leaders. But now my reign is not from here." 
Pilate therefore said to him, "Are you a king then?" Jesus answered, "You say that I am a king. That is why I have been born, and for this reason I have come into the world, that I should testify to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth listens to my voice." 
Pilate said to him, "What is truth?" When he had said this, he went out again to the Jewish leaders, and said to them, "I find no basis for a charge against him. 
But you have a custom that I should release someone to you at the Passover. So, do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?" 
Then they all shouted again, saying, "Not this man, but Barabbas!" Now Barabbas was a robber.
