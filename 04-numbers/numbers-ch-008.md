God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to Aaron and tell him, 'When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps are to give light in front of the lampstand.'" 
Aaron did this. He set up its lamps to give light in front of the lampstand, just as God commanded Moses. 
The lampstand was made of hammered gold, from its base to its flower shapes, all of it hammered work. He made the lampstand following the pattern God had shown Moses. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Take the Levites from among the Israelites and make them clean. 
This is what you are to do to make them clean: sprinkle the water of cleansing on them, have them shave their whole bodies, wash their clothes, and make themselves clean. 
Then have them take a young bull with its gift offering of fine flour mixed with oil, and take another young bull for an offering for wrongdoing. 
Bring the Levites in front of the Tent of Meeting and gather the whole community of Israel. 
Bring the Levites before God, and the Israelites are to lay their hands on the Levites. 
Aaron is to present the Levites before God as a wave offering on behalf of the Israelites, so they are set aside for the service of God. 
"The Levites are to lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you are to offer one as an offering for wrongdoing and the other as a burnt offering to God, to make things right for the Levites. 
Set the Levites before Aaron and his sons and present them as a wave offering to God. 
In this way you are to separate the Levites from among the Israelites, and the Levites will be mine. 
"After that the Levites may go in to do the work of the Tent of Meeting. Make them clean and present them as a wave offering, 
because they are given to me entirely from among the Israelites. In place of everyone who opens the womb, the firstborn of all the Israelites, I have taken them for myself. 
All the firstborn among the Israelites are mine, both people and animals. On the day I struck down all the firstborn in Egypt, I set them apart for myself. 
I have taken the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites. 
I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the Israelites, to do the work of the Israelites at the Tent of Meeting and to make things right for the Israelites, so that no plague will come on the Israelites when they come near the Holy Place." 
Moses, Aaron and the whole community of Israel did this for the Levites. The Israelites did everything God commanded Moses about the Levites. 
The Levites made themselves clean from wrongdoing and washed their clothes, and Aaron presented them as a wave offering before God, and Aaron made things right for them to make them clean. 
After that the Levites went in to do their work at the Tent of Meeting in front of Aaron and his sons, just as God commanded Moses about the Levites, and that is what they did. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"This is what applies to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and up they are to come in and take part in the work of the Tent of Meeting. 
At fifty years old they are to retire from the work and serve no longer, 
but they may help their brothers with their duties at the Tent of Meeting, though they are not to do the work themselves. This is how you are to have the Levites carry out their duties."
