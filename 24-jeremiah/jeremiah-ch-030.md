The word that came to Jeremiah from God, saying, 
"The God of Israel says, 'Write all the words that I have spoken to you in a book. 
For, look, the days come,' says God, 'that I'll reverse the captivity of my people Israel and Judah,' says God. 'I'll cause them to return to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they'll possess it.'" 
These are the words that God spoke concerning Israel and concerning Judah. 
God says: "We have heard a voice of trembling; a voice of fear, and not of peace. 
Ask now, and see whether a man travails with child. Why do I see every man with his hands on his waist, as a woman in travail, and all faces are turned pale? 
Alas, for that day is great, so that none is like it! It's even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he'll be saved out of it. 
It'll come to pass in that day, says the God of Armies, that I'll break his yoke from off your neck, and will burst your bonds. Strangers will no more make them their bondservants; 
but they'll serve their God, and David their king, whom I'll raise up to them. 
So don't be afraid, O Jacob my servant, says God. Don't be dismayed, Israel. For, look, I'll save you from afar, and save your offspring from the land of their captivity. Jacob will return, and will be quiet and at ease. No one will make him afraid. 
I'm with you, says God, to save you; for I'll make a full end of all the nations where I have scattered you, but I won't make a full end of you; but I'll correct you in measure, and will in no way leave you unpunished." 
God says, "Your hurt is incurable. Your wound is grievous. 
There's no one to plead your cause, that you may be bound up. You have no healing medicines. 
All your lovers have forgotten you. They don't seek you. I have wounded you with the wound of an enemy, with the chastisement of a cruel one, for the greatness of your guilt, because your mistakes were increased. 
Why do you cry over your injury? Your pain is incurable. The greatness of your guilt, because your mistakes have increased, I have done these things to you. 
So everyone who devours you'll be devoured. All your adversaries, everyone of them, will go into captivity. Those who plunder you'll be plunder. I'll make everyone who preys on you become prey. 
I'll restore health to you, and I'll heal you of your wounds," says God, "because they have called you an outcast, saying, 'It's Zion, whom no man seeks after.'" 
God says: "Look, I'll reverse the captivity of Jacob's tents, and have compassion on his dwelling places. The city will be built on its own hill, and the palace will be inhabited in its own place. 
Thanksgiving will proceed out of them with the voice of those who make merry. I'll multiply them, and they won't be few; I'll also glorify them, and they won't be small. 
Their children also will be as before, and their congregation will be established before me. I'll punish everyone who oppresses them. 
Their prince will be one of them, and their ruler will proceed from among them. I'll cause him to draw near, and he'll approach me; for who's he who has had boldness to approach me?" says God. 
"You will be my people, and I'll be your God. 
Look, God's storm, his wrath, has gone out, a sweeping storm; it'll burst on the head of the guilty. 
The fierce anger of God won't return until he has accomplished, and until he has performed the intentions of his heart. In the latter days you'll understand it."
