Being made right by trust, we have wholeness with God through our Lord Jesus Christ; 
through whom we also have a way in by trust into this favor, in which we stand. And we take pride in the expectation of the weight of God. 
Not only this, but we also take pride in our hard times, knowing that hard times produce endurance; 
and endurance, proven character; and proven character, expectation; 
and expectation doesn't disappoint us, because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit who was given to us. 
While we were still weak, at the right time Christ died for those with no regard for God. 
One will hardly die for a right person. Yet perhaps for a good person someone would even dare to die. 
But God demonstrates his own love toward us, like this: while we were still those who have drifted, Christ died for us. 
Much more then, being now made right by his blood, we'll be rescued from God's heat through him. 
If while we were enemies, we were brought back to God through the death of his Son, much more, being brought back, we'll be rescued by his life. 
Not only so, but we also take pride in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the bringing back together. 
So, as the drift entered into the world through one man, and death through the drift, so death passed to everyone because all drifted. 
Until the law came, the drift was in the world; but the drift isn't charged when there's no law. 
Nevertheless death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those whose drifting wasn't like Adam's disobedience, who's a foreshadowing of him who was to come. 
But the free gift isn't like the false step. If by the false step of the one everyone died, much more did the gift of God and the free gift through the one man, Jesus Christ, overflow to everyone. 
The gift isn't as through one who drifted; for the verdict came by one to a sentence, but the free gift followed many false steps to being made right. 
If by the false step of the one, death reigned through the one; so much more will those who receive the overflow of favor and of the gift of rightness reign in real life through the one, Jesus Christ. 
So then as through one false step, everyone was sentenced; even so through one act of rightness, everyone was made right to life. 
As through the one man's disobedience everyone was made drift-prone, even so through the obedience of the one, everyone will be made right. 
The law came in so that the false step would increase; but where wrongdoing overflowed, favor overflowed far more, 
that as the drift reigned in death, even so favor would reign through rightness to the real life of the era through Jesus Christ our Lord.
