Then I stood on the sand of the seashore. I saw a beast coming up out of the sea, with ten horns and seven heads. On its horns were ten crowns, and on its heads were names of slander. 
The beast I saw was like a leopard, with feet like a bear's and a mouth like a lion's mouth. The dragon gave it his power, his throne, and great authority. 
One of its heads looked as if it had been fatally wounded, but the deadly wound was healed, and the whole world was amazed and followed the beast. 
They bowed down to the dragon because he had given his authority to the beast, and they bowed down to the beast too, saying, "Who is like the beast? Who can fight against it?" 
It was given a mouth to speak great things and slander, and it was given authority to act for forty-two months. 
It opened its mouth to slander God, to slander his name, his dwelling, and those who live in the sky. 
It was allowed to make war on the set-apart ones and to defeat them, and it was given authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation. 
Everyone who lives on the land will bow down to it, everyone whose name hasn't been written, from the founding of the world, in the book of real life that belongs to the Lamb who was killed. 
If anyone has an ear, let him hear. 
If anyone is to be taken captive, into captivity they go. If anyone is to be killed with the sword, with the sword they will be killed. This is where the set-apart ones need staying power and trust. 
Then I saw another beast coming up out of the land. It had two horns like a lamb, but it spoke like a dragon. 
It exercises all the authority of the first beast on its behalf, and it makes the land and everyone living on it bow down to the first beast, whose deadly wound had been healed. 
It performs great signs, even making fire come down from the sky to the land while people watch. 
It deceives those who live on the land by the signs it was allowed to do in front of the beast, telling them to make an image in honor of the beast that had the sword wound and yet lived. 
It was allowed to give breath to the beast's image, so that the image could speak and could have everyone killed who refused to bow down to it. 
It made everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, receive a mark on their right hand or on their forehead, 
and no one could buy or sell unless they had the mark, the name of the beast or the number of its name. 
This is where wisdom comes in. Anyone with understanding should work out the number of the beast, because it is the number of a person. Its number is six hundred sixty-six.
