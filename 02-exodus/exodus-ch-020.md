God spoke all these words: 
"I am your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. 
"Don't have any other gods before me. 
"Don't make yourself an idol, or an image of anything in the sky above, on the earth below, or in the water under the earth. 
Don't bow down to them or serve them, because I, your God, am a jealous God, and I bring the guilt of the fathers on their children, to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me, 
but I show loyal love to thousands who love me and keep my commands. 
"Don't misuse the name of your God, because God won't let anyone off who misuses his name. 
"Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy. 
You have six days to do all your work, 
but the seventh day is a Sabbath to your God. Don't do any work on it, neither you, nor your son or daughter, nor your male or female servant, nor your livestock, nor the foreigner living in your towns. 
In six days God made the sky and the earth, the sea and everything in them, and on the seventh day he rested. That is why God blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy. 
"Honor your father and your mother, so that you live long in the land God is giving you. 
"Don't murder. 
"Don't commit adultery. 
"Don't steal. 
"Don't give false testimony against your neighbor. 
"Don't covet your neighbor's house. Don't covet your neighbor's wife, or his male or female servant, his ox or donkey, or anything else that belongs to your neighbor." 
All the people saw the thunder and lightning, heard the trumpet and saw the mountain smoking, and they trembled and stayed at a distance. 
They said to Moses, "You speak to us and we'll listen. Don't let God speak to us, or we'll die." 
Moses said to the people, "Don't be afraid. God has come to test you, so that the fear of him will be in front of you and you won't drift." 
The people stayed at a distance while Moses went near the thick darkness where God was. 
God said to Moses, "This is what you are to tell the Israelites: 'You have seen for yourselves that I spoke to you from the sky. 
Don't make any gods of silver or gold to be alongside me. 
Make me an altar of earth and sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your offerings for wholeness, your sheep and your cattle. Everywhere I cause my name to be remembered, I will come to you and bless you. 
If you make me an altar of stone, don't build it out of cut stones, because if you use a tool on it, you have made it unclean. 
And don't go up to my altar by steps, so your nakedness isn't exposed on it.'
