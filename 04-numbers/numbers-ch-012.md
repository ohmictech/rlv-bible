Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman he had married, since he had married a Cushite woman. 
They said, "Has God spoken only through Moses? Hasn't he spoken through us too?" And God heard it. 
Now Moses was a very humble man, more humble than anyone else on the face of the earth. 
God spoke suddenly to Moses, Aaron and Miriam: "You three, come out to the Tent of Meeting." The three of them came out. 
God came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the Tent, and called Aaron and Miriam, and they both came forward. 
He said, "Listen to my words. If there is a spokesman among you, I, God, make myself known to him in a vision and speak with him in a dream. 
My servant Moses isn't like that. He is faithful in all my house. 
With him I speak face to face, plainly and not in riddles, and he sees the form of God. Why weren't you afraid to speak against my servant Moses?" 
God's anger burned against them, and he left. 
The cloud lifted from over the Tent, and there was Miriam, white as snow with a serious skin disease. Aaron turned toward Miriam, and she was covered in it. 
Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, don't hold this wrong against us, this foolish thing we did, this drifting. 
Don't let her be like a baby that comes out of its mother dead, with half its flesh eaten away." 
Moses cried out to God, "God, please heal her!" 
God said to Moses, "If her father had spit in her face, wouldn't she be ashamed for seven days? Shut her outside the camp for seven days, and after that she can be brought back in." 
Miriam was shut outside the camp for seven days, and the people didn't travel until Miriam was brought back in. 
After that the people traveled from Hazeroth and camped in the Paran wilderness.
