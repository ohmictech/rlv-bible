Jesus answered and spoke to them again in stories, saying, 
"The reign of the sky is like a certain king, who made a wedding feast for his son, 
and sent out his slaves to call those who were invited to the wedding feast, but they wouldn't come. 
Again he sent out other slaves, saying, 'Tell those who are invited, "Look, I have prepared my dinner. My cattle and my fattened calves are killed, and all things are ready. Come to the wedding feast!"' 
But they paid no attention and went their ways, one to his own farm, another to his business. 
And the rest grabbed his slaves, treated them badly, and killed them. 
When the king heard that, he was angry, and sent his armies, ruined those murderers, and burned their city. 
"Then he said to his slaves, 'The wedding is ready, but those who were invited weren't worthy. 
So go to the main roads and invite everyone you find to the wedding feast.' 
Those slaves went out into the main roads and gathered together as many as they found, both bad and good. The wedding was filled with guests. 
"But when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man who didn't have on wedding clothing, 
and he said to him, 'Friend, how did you come in here not wearing wedding clothing?' He was speechless. 
Then the king said to his slaves, 'Tie him hand and foot, take him away, and throw him into the outer darkness. That's where the crying and grinding of teeth will be.' 
Many are called, but few chosen." 
Then the strict religious ones went and made plans how they could trap him in his words. 
They sent their students to him, along with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know that you're truthful, and teach the way of God in truth, no matter whom you teach, for you don't play favorites. 
So tell us, what do you think? Is it allowed to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?" 
But Jesus perceived their malice, and said, "Why do you test me, you pretenders? 
Show me the tax money." They brought to him a silver coin. 
He asked them, "Whose is this image and inscription?" 
They said to him, "Caesar's." Then he said to them, "So give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's." 
When they heard it, they were amazed, and left him and went away. 
On that day some of the religious elite (those who say that there's no rising again) came to him. They asked him, 
saying, "Teacher, Moses said, 'If a man dies, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.' 
Now there were with us seven brothers. The first married and died, and having no offspring left his wife to his brother. 
In the same way, the second also, and the third, to the seventh. 
After them all, the woman died. 
In the rising again, then, whose wife will she be of the seven? They all had her." 
But Jesus answered them, "You're wrong, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God. 
In the rising again they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like God's messengers in the sky. 
But concerning the rising again of the dead, haven't you read that which was spoken to you by God, saying, 
'I'm the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? God isn't the God of dead bodies, but of the living." 
When the crowds heard it, they were astonished at his teaching. 
But the strict religious ones, when they heard that he had silenced the religious elite, gathered themselves together. 
One of them, an expert in the law, asked him a question, testing him. 
"Teacher, which is the greatest command in the law?" 
Jesus said to him, "'You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your life, and with all your mind.' 
This is the first and great command. 
A second likewise is this, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.' 
The whole law and the prophets depend on these two commands." 
Now while the strict religious ones were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question, 
saying, "What do you think of the Christ? Whose son is he?" They said to him, "David's." 
He said to them, "How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying, 
'The Lord said to my Lord, sit on my right hand, until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet'? 
"If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?" 
No one was able to answer him a word, nor did anyone dare ask him any more questions from that day forward.
