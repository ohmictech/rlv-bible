In the eleventh year, in the first of the month, God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, because Tyre has said against Jerusalem, 'Aha! She's broken! She who was the gateway of the peoples has been returned to me. I'll be replenished, now that she's laid waste;' 
So God says, 'Look, I'm against you, Tyre, and will cause many nations to come up against you, as the sea causes its waves to come up. 
They'll destroy the walls of Tyre, and break down her towers. I'll also scrape her dust from her, and make her a bare rock. 
She'll be a place for the spreading of nets in the middle of the sea; for I have spoken it,' says God. 'She'll become plunder for the nations. 
Her daughters who are in the field will be slain with the sword. Then they'll know that I'm God.' 
"For God says: 'Look, I'll bring on Tyre Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, king of kings, from the north, with horses, with chariots, with horsemen, and an army with many people. 
He'll kill your daughters in the field with the sword. He'll make forts against you, cast up a mound against you, and raise up the buckler against you. 
He'll set his battering engines against your walls, and with his axes he'll break down your towers. 
By reason of the abundance of his horses, their dust will cover you. Your walls will shake at the noise of the horsemen, of the wagons, and of the chariots, when he enters into your gates, as men enter into a city which is broken open. 
He'll tread down all your streets with the hoofs of his horses. He'll kill your people with the sword. The pillars of your strength will go down to the ground. 
They'll make a plunder of your riches and make a prey of your merchandise. They'll break down your walls and destroy your pleasant houses. They'll lay your stones, your timber, and your dust in the middle of the waters. 
I'll cause the noise of your songs to cease. The sound of your harps won't be heard any more. 
I'll make you a bare rock. You'll be a place for the spreading of nets. You'll be built no more; for I God have spoken it,' says God. 
"The God says to Tyre: 'Won't the islands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when the slaughter is made within you? 
Then all the princes of the sea will come down from their thrones, and lay aside their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments. They'll clothe themselves with trembling. They'll sit on the ground, and will tremble every moment, and be astonished at you. 
They'll take up a lamentation over you, and tell you, "How you're destroyed, who were inhabited by seafaring men, the renowned city, who was strong in the sea, she and her inhabitants, who caused their terror to be on everyone who lived there!" 
Now the islands will tremble in the day of your fall. Yes, the islands that are in the sea will be dismayed at your departure.' 
"For God says: 'When I make you a desolate city, like the cities that aren't inhabited, when I bring up the deep on you, and the great waters cover you, 
then I'll bring you down with those who descend into the pit, to the people of old time, and will make you dwell in the lower parts of the earth, in the places that are desolate of old, with those who go down to the pit, that you be not inhabited; and I'll set glory in the land of the living. 
I'll make you a terror, and you'll no more have any being. Though you're sought for, yet you'll never be found again,' says God."
