Paul, a sent one of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the set-apart ones who are at Ephesus, and to those who trust in Christ Jesus: 
Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
Praised be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the sky, in Christ, 
even as he picked us out in him before the world was founded, so that we'd be set apart and without defect before him in love, 
having marked us out ahead of time to be placed as his own children through Jesus Christ, because that was his delight and his will, 
to the praise of the weight of his gift, which he gave us freely in the one he truly loves. 
In him we're released through his blood, and our false steps are let go, measured by the riches of his gift, 
which he let overflow on us, along with all wisdom and good sense, 
making known to us the secret of his will, according to the delight he had planned in him, 
to put his household plan into effect when the times are full, to gather up all things in Christ, things in the sky and things on the land, in him. 
In him we were also given our share, having been marked out ahead of time according to the purpose of the one who works everything out as his will decides, 
so that we'd be to the praise of his weight, we who had already set our expectation on Christ. 
In him you also, when you heard the word of truth, the Good News of your healing and wholeness, and when you trusted, were stamped with the Holy Spirit he had promised, 
who's the down payment on the charge handed down to us, right up to the release of the people God has made his own, to the praise of his weight. 
That's why I too, since I heard about the trust in the Lord Jesus that's among you, and the love you have for all the set-apart ones, 
haven't stopped giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers, 
I ask that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of weight, may give you a spirit of wisdom and unveiling as you come to know him, 
with the eyes of your heart opened, so that you may know what his calling makes you expect, and how rich and heavy the charge he has handed down among the set-apart ones is, 
and how far beyond all measure his power is toward us who trust, measured by that working of his powerful might, 
which he worked in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the sky, 
far above every rule and authority and power and lordship, and every name that can be named, not only in this era but also in the one to come. 
He put everything under his feet, and gave him as head over everything for the gathering, 
which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills everything in every way.
