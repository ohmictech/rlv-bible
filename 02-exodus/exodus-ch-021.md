"Now these are the ordinances which you will set before them: 
"If you buy a Hebrew servant, he will serve six years, and in the seventh he will go out free without paying anything. 
If he comes in by himself, he will go out by himself. If he's married, then his wife will go out with him. 
If his master gives him a wife and she bears him sons or daughters, the wife and her children will be her master's, and he will go out by himself. 
But if the servant will plainly say, 'I love my master, my wife, and my children. I won't go out free;' 
then his master will bring him to God, and will bring him to the door or to the doorpost, and his master will bore his ear through with an awl, and he will serve him for the eras. 
"If a man sells his daughter to be a female servant, she won't go out as the male servants do. 
If she doesn't please her master, who has married her to himself, then he will let her be bought back. He will have no right to sell her to a foreign people, since he has dealt deceitfully with her. 
If he marries her to his son, he will deal with her as a daughter. 
If he takes another wife to himself, he won't diminish her food, her clothing, and her marital rights. 
If he doesn't do these three things for her, she may go free without paying any money. 
"One who strikes a man so that he dies will surely be put to death, 
but not if it's unintentional, but God allows it to happen; then I'll appoint you a place where he will flee. 
If a man schemes and comes presumptuously on his neighbor to kill him, you will take him from my altar, that he may die. 
"Anyone who attacks his father or his mother will be surely put to death. 
"Anyone who kidnaps someone and sells him, or if he's found in his hand, he will surely be put to death. 
"Anyone who curses his father or his mother will surely be put to death. 
"If men quarrel and one strikes the other with a stone, or with his fist, and he doesn't die, but is confined to bed; 
if he rises again and walks around with his staff, then he who struck him will be cleared; only he will pay for the loss of his time, and will provide for his healing until he's thoroughly healed. 
"If a man strikes his servant or his maid with a rod, and he dies under his hand, the man will surely be punished. 
Notwithstanding, if his servant gets up after a day or two, he won't be punished, for the servant is his property. 
"If men fight and hurt a pregnant woman so that she gives birth prematurely, and yet no harm follows, he will be surely fined as much as the woman's husband demands and the judges allow. 
But if any harm follows, then you must take life for life, 
eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot, 
burning for burning, wound for wound, and bruise for bruise. 
"If a man strikes his servant's eye, or his maid's eye, and destroys it, he will let him go free for his eye's sake. 
If he strikes out his male servant's tooth, or his female servant's tooth, he will let the servant go free for his tooth's sake. 
"If a bull gores a man or a woman to death, the bull will surely be stoned, and its meat won't be eaten; but the owner of the bull won't be held responsible. 
But if the bull had a habit of goring in the past, and this has been testified to its owner, and he hasn't kept it in, but it has killed a man or a woman, the bull will be stoned, and its owner will also be put to death. 
If a ransom is imposed on him, then he will give for the buying back of his life whatever is imposed. 
Whether it has gored a son or has gored a daughter, according to this judgment it will be done to him. 
If the bull gores a male servant or a female servant, thirty shekels of silver will be given to their master, and the ox will be stoned. 
"If a man opens a pit, or if a man digs a pit and doesn't cover it, and a bull or a donkey falls into it, 
the owner of the pit will make it good. He will give money to its owner, and the dead animal will be his. 
"If one man's bull injures another's, so that it dies, then they will sell the live bull, and divide its price; and they will also divide the dead animal. 
Or if it's known that the bull was in the habit of goring in the past, and its owner hasn't kept it in, he will surely pay bull for bull, and the dead animal will be his own.
