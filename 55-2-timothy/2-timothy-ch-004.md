Before God and Christ Jesus, who will pass a verdict on the living and the dead when he appears and brings his reign, I command you: 
announce the word. Be ready whether the time is right or not. Show people their fault, correct them, and encourage them, with all patience and teaching. 
The time is coming when people won't put up with healthy teaching. Instead, because they itch to hear something new, they'll pile up teachers for themselves to suit their own cravings, 
and they'll turn their ears away from the truth and wander off into old stories. 
But you must keep a clear head in all things, put up with hardship, do the work of an announcer of good news, and finish your service. 
I'm already being poured out like a drink offering, and the time has come for me to leave. 
I have fought the good fight. I have finished the race. I have kept the trust. 
From now on the wreath of rightness is stored up for me, and the Lord, the right judge, will give it to me on that day, and not only to me, but to everyone who has truly loved his appearing. 
Do your best to come to me soon, 
because Demas has left me (he loved this present world) and gone to Thessalonica. Crescens has gone to Galatia, and Titus to Dalmatia. 
Only Luke is with me. Bring Mark with you when you come, because he's useful to me for the work. 
I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus. 
When you come, bring the coat I left with Carpus at Troas, and the books, especially the parchments. 
Alexander the metalworker did me a great deal of harm. The Lord will pay him back for what he has done. 
You should watch out for him too, because he fought hard against everything we said. 
At my first defense no one stood with me; everyone left me. May it not be held against them. 
But the Lord stood by me and gave me strength, so that through me the message might be announced in full and all the nations might hear it. And I was rescued out of the lion's mouth. 
The Lord will rescue me from every bad deed and bring me safely into his reign in the sky. To him be the weight through all the eras. Amen. 
Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus. 
Erastus stayed at Corinth, and I left Trophimus at Miletus sick. 
Do your best to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers and sisters send their greetings. 
The Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Favor be with you. Amen.
