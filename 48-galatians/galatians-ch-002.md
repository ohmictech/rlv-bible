Then after a period of fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus also with me. 
I went up because of an unveiling, and I laid before them the Good News I announce among the nations, but privately before those who were respected, for fear that I was running, or had run, for nothing. 
But not even Titus, who was with me, being a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised. 
This was because of the false brothers smuggled in, who slipped in to spy on the freedom we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might make us slaves, 
to whom we gave no place in the way of subjection, not for an hour, that the truth of the Good News might continue with you. 
But from those who were thought to be important (whatever they were makes no difference to me; God doesn't play favorites) those who were respected added nothing to me, 
but to the contrary, when they saw that I had been trusted with the Good News for those who are not circumcised, just as Peter was for those who are circumcised, 
for the one who worked through Peter as a sent one to the circumcised also worked through me to the nations, 
and when they saw the gift given to me, James and Cephas and John, those who were thought to be pillars, gave Barnabas and me the right hand of partnership, that we should go to the nations and they to the circumcision. 
They only asked us to remember the poor, which very thing I was also eager to do. 
But when Peter came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he was clearly in the wrong. 
For before some people came from James, he ate with the nations. But when they came, he drew back and separated himself, afraid of those of the circumcision. 
And the rest of the Jewish people joined him in his pretending, so that even Barnabas was carried away by their pretending. 
But when I saw that they weren't walking straight according to the truth of the Good News, I said to Peter in front of them all, "If you, a Jewish person, live the way the nations do and not the way Jewish people do, why do you force the nations to live the way Jewish people do? 
"We are Jewish by birth and not outsiders who drift, 
yet knowing that no one is made right by deeds of the law but through trust in Jesus Christ, we too trusted in Christ Jesus, so that we might be made right by trust in Christ and not by deeds of the law, because no flesh will be made right by deeds of the law. 
But if, while we were seeking to be made right in Christ, we ourselves were found to be people who drift, is Christ then a servant of the drift? Certainly not! 
For if I build again what I tore down, I show myself to be a breaker of the law. 
For I through the law died to the law, that I might live to God. 
I have been crucified with Christ, and it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the flesh, I live by trust in the Son of God, who truly loved me and gave himself up for me. 
I don't set aside the favor of God. For if rightness comes through the law, then Christ died for nothing!"
