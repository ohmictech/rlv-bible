Since we have these promises, truly loved, let's make ourselves clean from everything that makes flesh and spirit unclean, completing our set-apartness in the fear of God. 
Open your hearts to us. We wronged no one. We corrupted no one. We took advantage of no one. 
I don't say this to sentence you, for I have said before that you're in our hearts, to die together and live together. 
I speak to you with great openness. I take great pride in you. I'm full of comfort. I overflow with joy in all our hard times. 
Even when we came into Macedonia, our flesh had no relief, but we were in hard times on every side: fights outside, fear inside. 
Nevertheless, he who comforts the lowly, God, comforted us by the coming of Titus, 
and not by his coming only, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted in you while he told us of your longing, your mourning, and your passion for me, so that I celebrated still more. 
Though I grieved you with my letter, I don't regret it, though I did regret it. I see that my letter made you grieve, though just for a while. 
Now I celebrate, not that you were grieved, but that your grief led to a change of heart. You were grieved in a way that pleases God, so that you suffered no loss from us. 
Grief that pleases God produces a change of heart that leads to rescue, and brings no regret. But the grief of the world produces death. 
Look at what this very thing, your grief in a way that pleases God, worked in you: what earnestness, what defense, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what passion, what setting things right! In everything you showed yourselves to be clean in this matter. 
So although I wrote to you, I wrote not for his cause that did the wrong, nor for his cause that suffered the wrong, but that your earnest care for us might be revealed in you in the sight of God. 
So we have been comforted. In our comfort we celebrated the more exceedingly for the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by you all. 
If in anything I took pride in you to him, I wasn't disappointed. But as we spoke all things to you in truth, so the pride I took in you before Titus turned out to be true. 
His affection for you overflows all the more as he remembers how you all obeyed, and how you received him with fear and trembling. 
I celebrate that in everything I'm confident concerning you.
