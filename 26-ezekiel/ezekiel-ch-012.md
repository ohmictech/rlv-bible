God's word also came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, you dwell in the middle of the rebellious house, who have eyes to see, and don't see, who have ears to hear, and don't hear; for they're a rebellious house. 
"So, you son of man, prepare your baggage for moving, and move by day in their sight. You will move from your place to another place in their sight. It may be they'll consider, though they're a rebellious house. 
You will bring out your baggage by day in their sight, as baggage for moving. You will go out yourself at evening in their sight, as when men go out into exile. 
Dig through the wall in their sight, and carry your baggage out that way. 
In their sight you will bear it on your shoulder, and carry it out in the dark. You will cover your face, so that you don't see the land, for I have set you for a sign to the house of Israel." 
I did so as I was commanded. I brought out my baggage by day, as baggage for moving, and in the evening I dug through the wall with my hand. I brought it out in the dark, and bore it on my shoulder in their sight. 
In the morning, God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, hasn't the house of Israel, the rebellious house, said to you, 'What are you doing?' 
"Say to them, 'The God says: "This burden concerns the prince in Jerusalem, and all the house of Israel among whom they're."' 
"Say, 'I'm your sign. As I have done, so will it be done to them. They'll go into exile, into captivity. 
"'The prince who's among them will bear his baggage on his shoulder in the dark, and will go out. They'll dig through the wall to carry things out that way. He'll cover his face, because he won't see the land with his eyes. 
I'll also spread my net on him, and he'll be taken in my snare. I'll bring him to Babylon to the land of the Chaldeans; yet he won't see it, though he'll die there. 
I'll scatter toward every wind everyone who's around him to help him, and all his bands. I'll draw out the sword after them. 
"'They'll know that I'm God when I disperse them among the nations and scatter them through the countries. 
But I'll leave a few men of them from the sword, from the famine, and from the pestilence, that they can declare all their abominations among the nations where they come. Then they'll know that I'm God.'" 
Moreover God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, eat your bread with quaking, and drink your water with trembling and with fearfulness. 
Tell the people of the land, 'The God says concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem and the land of Israel: "They'll eat their bread with fearfulness and drink their water in dismay, that her land may be desolate, and all that's therein, because of the violence of everyone who dwells therein. 
The cities that are inhabited will be laid waste, and the land will be a desolation. Then you'll know that I'm God."'" 
God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, what's this proverb that you have in the land of Israel, saying, 'The days are prolonged, and every vision fails'? 
Tell them, 'The God says: "I'll make this proverb to cease, and they'll no more use it as a proverb in Israel;"' but tell them, '"The days are at hand, and the fulfillment of every vision. 
There will be no more any false vision nor flattering divination within the house of Israel. 
I'm God. I'll speak, and the word that I speak will be performed. It'll be no more deferred; for in your days, rebellious house, I'll speak the word and will perform it," says God.'" 
Again God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, look, they of the house of Israel say, 'The vision that he sees is for many days to come, and he prophesies of times that are far off.' 
"So tell them, 'The God says: "None of my words will be deferred any more, but the word which I speak will be performed," says God.'"
