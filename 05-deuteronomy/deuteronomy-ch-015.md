"At the end of every seven years you must cancel debts. 
This is how it works: every creditor must cancel what he has lent to his neighbor. He must not demand payment from his neighbor or his brother, because God's cancellation has been announced. 
You may demand it from a foreigner, but whatever your brother owes you, you must cancel. 
There should be no poor among you, because God will certainly bless you in the land your God is giving you to take as your inheritance, 
if only you carefully listen to the voice of your God and are careful to do all this command I am giving you today. 
Your God will bless you as he promised you. You will lend to many nations but you won't borrow. You will rule over many nations but they won't rule over you. 
If there is a poor man among your brothers, in any of your towns in your land your God is giving you, don't harden your heart or shut your hand against your poor brother. 
Open your hand wide to him and lend him enough to cover whatever he needs. 
Be careful that no mean thought comes into your heart, saying, 'The seventh year, the year of cancellation, is almost here,' and you look coldly at your poor brother and give him nothing, and he cries out to God against you, and you are held guilty. 
Give to him freely, and don't be grudging about it, because it is for this that your God will bless you in all your work and everything you put your hand to. 
The poor will never stop being in the land. That is why I command you to open your hand wide to your brother, to your needy and to your poor in your land. 
If your brother, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, is sold to you and serves you six years, then in the seventh year you must let him go free. 
When you let him go free, don't send him away empty-handed. 
Give him generously from your flock, from your threshing floor and from your winepress. Give to him as your God has blessed you. 
Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt and your God bought you back. That is why I am commanding you this today. 
But if he says to you, 'I won't leave you,' because he loves you and your household and is happy with you, 
then take an awl and push it through his ear into the door, and he will be your servant for the eras. Do the same with your female servant. 
Don't think it hard when you let him go free, because he has served you six years at double the value of a hired hand. Your God will bless you in everything you do. 
Set apart to your God every firstborn male from your herd and flock. Don't put the firstborn of your herd to work and don't shear the firstborn of your flock. 
Eat it before your God each year in the place God will choose, you and your household. 
If it has a defect, if it is lame or blind or has any other defect, don't sacrifice it to your God. 
Eat it inside your gates. Anyone unclean or clean may eat it, just as they eat gazelle or deer. 
Only don't eat its blood. Pour it out on the ground like water.
