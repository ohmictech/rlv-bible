But the Israelites broke faith about the things given over to destruction. Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from the tribe of Judah, took some of what had been given over to destruction, and God's anger burned against the Israelites. 
Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is near Beth Aven, east of Bethel, and told them, "Go up and scout out the land." So the men went up and scouted Ai. 
They came back to Joshua and said, "Don't send everybody up. Send about two or three thousand men to attack Ai. Don't make all the people work for that, because there are only a few of them." 
So about three thousand men went up, and they ran from the men of Ai. 
The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them. They chased them from the gate as far as Shebarim and struck them on the slope down, and the hearts of the people melted and turned to water. 
Joshua tore his clothes and fell face down on the ground in front of the ark of God until evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they put dust on their heads. 
Joshua said, "Oh no, Lord God, why did you bring this people across the Jordan at all, only to hand us over to the Amorites and destroy us? I wish we had been content to stay on the other side of the Jordan. 
Lord, what can I say now that Israel has run from its enemies? 
The Canaanites and everyone else in the land will hear about it, and they will surround us and wipe out our name from the earth. Then what will you do for your great name?" 
God said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you lying on your face like that? 
Israel has drifted. They have broken my covenant that I commanded them. They have taken some of what was given over to destruction, and they have stolen it, and lied about it, and put it among their own things. 
That is why the Israelites can't stand against their enemies. They turn and run because they have become something given over to destruction themselves. I won't be with you any longer unless you destroy what was given over to destruction among you. 
Get up! Set the people apart and say, 'Set yourselves apart for tomorrow, because the God of Israel says, "There is something given over to destruction among you, Israel. You can't stand against your enemies until you remove it from among you. 
In the morning you will be brought forward tribe by tribe. The tribe God picks will come forward by clans, the clan God picks will come forward by families, and the family God picks will come forward man by man. 
Whoever is caught with what was given over to destruction will be burned with fire, he and everything he has, because he broke the covenant of God and did a disgraceful thing in Israel."'" 
So Joshua got up early in the morning and brought Israel forward tribe by tribe, and the tribe of Judah was picked. 
He brought the clans of Judah forward, and the Zerahite clan was picked. He brought the Zerahite clan forward man by man, and Zabdi was picked. 
He brought Zabdi's family forward man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, from the tribe of Judah, was picked. 
Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give honor to the God of Israel and confess to him. Tell me now what you have done. Don't hide it from me." 
Achan answered Joshua, "It's true. I have wronged the God of Israel, and this is what I did. 
When I saw among the plunder a beautiful Babylonian robe, two hundred shekels of silver and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels, I wanted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground in the middle of my tent, with the silver underneath." 
So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent, and there it was, hidden in his tent with the silver underneath. 
They took it out of the tent and brought it to Joshua and all the Israelites, and spread it out in front of God. 
Then Joshua and all Israel with him took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the robe, the bar of gold, his sons and daughters, his cattle, donkeys and sheep, his tent and everything he had, and brought them up to the valley of Achor. 
Joshua said, "Why have you brought trouble on us? God will bring trouble on you today." And all Israel stoned him, and they burned them with fire and stoned them. 
They piled up a great heap of stones over him that is still there today. Then God turned from his fierce anger, and that is why the place has been called the valley of Achor to this day.
