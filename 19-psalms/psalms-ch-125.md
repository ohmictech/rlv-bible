A Song of Ascents. Those who trust in God are as Mount Zion, which can't be moved, but remains for the eras. 
As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so God surrounds his people from this time forward and for the eras more. 
For the scepter of guilt won't remain over the allotment of the righteous, so that the righteous won't use their hands to do bad things. 
Do good, God, to those who are good, to those who are upright in their hearts. 
But as for those who turn away to their crooked ways, God will lead them away with the workers of guilt. Peace be on Israel.
