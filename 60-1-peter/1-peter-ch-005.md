So I urge the elders among you, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and as one who will share in the weight that is going to be unveiled: 
shepherd God's flock that is in your care. Watch over it, not because you have to, but because you want to, the way God wants you to; not for shameful gain, but because you are eager to serve; 
do not boss around those in your charge, but make yourselves examples to the flock. 
And when the chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the wreath of weight that never fades. 
In the same way, you who are younger, submit to the elders. And all of you, wrap yourselves in humility as you serve one another, because "God stands against the proud, but gives favor to the humble." 
So humble yourselves under God's mighty hand, so that he may lift you up at the right time, 
throwing all your worries on him, because he cares about you. 
Stay clear-headed and stay awake. The one who is against you, the Slanderer, prowls around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to swallow up. 
Stand against him, firm in your trust, knowing that your brothers and sisters all over the world are going through the same kinds of suffering. 
And after you have suffered for a little while, may the God of all favor, who called you to his weight of the eras in Christ Jesus, make you complete, make you firm, make you strong, and set you on a solid foundation. 
To him be the weight and the power for all the eras. Amen. 
I have written to you briefly through Silvanus, whom I consider a trustworthy brother, to encourage you and to bear witness that this is the true favor of God. Stand firm in it. 
The gathering in Babylon, chosen along with you, sends you greetings. So does Mark, my son. 
Greet one another with a kiss of love. Wholeness to all of you who are in Christ. Amen.
