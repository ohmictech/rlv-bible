That is why we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, so that we do not drift away from it. 
For if the message spoken through messengers proved firm, and every wrong step and every refusal to obey received the penalty it deserved, 
how will we escape if we ignore so great a rescue? It was first spoken by the Lord, and it was confirmed to us by those who heard him, 
while God also testified with them by signs and wonders, by many kinds of powerful acts, and by gifts of the Holy Spirit, given out as he chose? 
For he did not put the world to come, the one we are talking about, under the authority of messengers. 
But someone has testified somewhere, saying, "What are people, that you think about them? Or a human being, that you care for him? 
You made him a little lower than the messengers. You crowned him with weight and honor. 
"You put everything under his feet." In putting everything under him, he left nothing that is not under him. But as it is, we do not yet see everything under him. 
But we do see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the messengers for a while, now crowned with weight and honor because he suffered death, so that by God's favor he should taste death for everyone. 
For it was fitting that God, for whom everything exists and through whom everything exists, in bringing many children to weight, should make the one who leads them to rescue complete through suffering. 
For the one who sets apart and those who are set apart all come from one Father. That is why he is not ashamed to call them brothers and sisters, 
when he says, "I will tell my brothers and sisters your name. In the middle of the gathering I will sing your praise." 
And again, "I will put my trust in him." And again, "Here I am, with the children God has given me." 
Since the children share in flesh and blood, he also shared in the same things himself, so that through his death he might put out of work the one who holds the power of death, that is, the Slanderer, 
and set free all those who were held in slavery their whole lives by the fear of death. 
For surely he does not come to the aid of messengers, but he comes to the aid of the offspring of Abraham. 
That is why he had to become like his brothers and sisters in every way, so that he could be a merciful and trustworthy high priest in God's service, to make things right for the mistakes of the people. 
Because he himself suffered when he was tested, he is able to help those who are being tested.
