God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to the children of Israel, saying, 'If a woman conceives, and bears a male child, then she will be unclean seven days; as in the days of her monthly period she will be unclean. 
In the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin will be circumcised. 
She will continue in the blood of purification thirty-three days. She won't touch any holy thing, nor come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purifying are completed. 
But if she bears a female child, then she will be unclean two weeks, as in her period; and she will continue in the blood of purification sixty-six days. 
"'When the days of her purification are completed for a son or for a daughter, she will bring to the priest at the door of the Tent of Meeting, a year old lamb for a burnt offering, and a young pigeon or a turtledove, for an offering for wrongdoing. 
He will offer it before God, and make things right for her; then she will be cleansed from the fountain of her blood. "'This is the law for her who bears, whether a male or a female. 
If she can't afford a lamb, then she will take two turtledoves or two young pigeons: the one for a burnt offering, and the other for an offering for wrongdoing. The priest will make things right for her, and she will be clean.'"
