God spoke to Moses: 
"This is the law for the person with a serious skin disease on the day he is made clean. He is to be brought to the priest, 
and the priest is to go outside the camp and examine him. If the skin disease has healed in him, 
the priest is to order that two live clean birds be brought for the one to be made clean, along with cedar wood, scarlet yarn and hyssop. 
The priest is to order one of the birds killed in a clay bowl over running water. 
Then he is to take the living bird, the cedar wood, the scarlet yarn and the hyssop, and dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird killed over the running water. 
He is to sprinkle it on the one to be made clean from the skin disease seven times, declare him clean, and let the living bird go free in the open field. 
"The one to be made clean is to wash his clothes, shave off all his hair and wash himself in water, and he will be clean. After that he may come into the camp, but he is to stay outside his tent for seven days. 
On the seventh day he is to shave off all his hair: his head, his beard and his eyebrows. He is to shave off all his hair, wash his clothes and wash his body in water, and then he will be clean. 
"On the eighth day he is to take two male lambs without defect, one female lamb a year old without defect, three tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a gift offering, and one log of oil. 
The priest who makes him clean is to bring the man and these things before God at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 
"The priest is to take one of the male lambs and offer it as an offering for guilt, along with the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before God. 
He is to kill the male lamb in the place where the offering for wrongdoing and the burnt offering are killed, in the area of the Holy Place, because the offering for guilt belongs to the priest, like the offering for wrongdoing. It is most holy. 
The priest is to take some of the blood of the offering for guilt and put it on the tip of the right ear of the one to be made clean, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot. 
The priest is to take some of the log of oil and pour it into the palm of his own left hand. 
The priest is to dip his right finger in the oil in his left hand and sprinkle some of the oil with his finger seven times before God. 
The priest is to put some of the rest of the oil in his hand on the tip of the right ear of the one to be made clean, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot, on top of the blood of the offering for guilt. 
The rest of the oil in the priest's hand he is to put on the head of the one to be made clean, and the priest will make things right for him before God. 
"The priest is to offer the offering for wrongdoing and make things right for the one to be made clean from his uncleanness. After that he is to kill the burnt offering, 
and the priest is to offer the burnt offering and the gift offering on the altar. The priest will make things right for him, and he will be clean. 
"If he is poor and can't afford that much, he is to take one male lamb as an offering for guilt to be waved, to make things right for him, and a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil for a gift offering, and a log of oil, 
and two doves or two young pigeons, whichever he can afford, one as an offering for wrongdoing and the other as a burnt offering. 
"On the eighth day he is to bring them for his cleansing to the priest, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, before God. 
The priest is to take the lamb of the offering for guilt and the log of oil and wave them as a wave offering before God. 
He is to kill the lamb of the offering for guilt, and the priest is to take some of its blood and put it on the tip of the right ear of the one to be made clean, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot. 
The priest is to pour some of the oil into the palm of his own left hand, 
and with his right finger sprinkle some of the oil in his left hand seven times before God. 
Then the priest is to put some of the oil in his hand on the tip of the right ear of the one to be made clean, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot, on the place where the blood of the offering for guilt was put. 
The rest of the oil in the priest's hand he is to put on the head of the one to be made clean, to make things right for him before God. 
He is to offer one of the doves or young pigeons, whichever he can afford, 
what he can afford, one as an offering for wrongdoing and the other as a burnt offering, along with the gift offering. The priest will make things right for him before God." 
This is the law for a person who has a serious skin disease and can't afford the usual sacrifice for his cleansing. 
God spoke to Moses and Aaron: 
"When you come into the land of Canaan, which I am giving you as a possession, and I put a spreading mildew in a house in your land, 
the owner of the house is to come and tell the priest, 'Something like a mildew spot has appeared in my house.' 
The priest is to order the house emptied before he goes in to examine the spot, so that nothing in the house becomes unclean. Then the priest is to go in and inspect the house. 
He is to examine the spot, and if it is in the walls of the house with hollow streaks, greenish or reddish, that look deeper than the wall, 
the priest is to go out to the door of the house and shut the house up for seven days. 
The priest is to come back on the seventh day and look, and if the spot has spread in the walls of the house, 
he is to order the stones with the spot in them taken out and thrown into an unclean place outside the city. 
He is to have the inside of the house scraped all over, and the scraped mortar is to be poured out in an unclean place outside the city. 
Then other stones are to be put in place of those stones, and other mortar is to be used to plaster the house. 
"If the spot comes back and breaks out in the house after the stones were taken out and the house was scraped and plastered, 
the priest is to come in and look, and if the spot has spread in the house, it is a destructive mildew in the house and it is unclean. 
He is to have the house torn down, its stones, its timber and all its mortar, and carried out of the city to an unclean place. 
"Anyone who goes into the house while it is shut up will be unclean until evening. 
Whoever lies down in the house is to wash his clothes, and whoever eats in the house is to wash his clothes. 
"If the priest comes in and examines it and the spot hasn't spread in the house after it was plastered, he is to declare the house clean, because the spot is healed. 
To make the house clean he is to take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn and hyssop. 
He is to kill one of the birds in a clay bowl over running water. 
He is to take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the scarlet yarn and the living bird, dip them in the blood of the killed bird and in the running water, and sprinkle the house seven times. 
So he is to make the house clean with the blood of the bird, the running water, the living bird, the cedar wood, the hyssop and the scarlet yarn, 
and then let the living bird go out of the city into the open field. In this way he will make things right for the house, and it will be clean." 
This is the law for any serious skin disease and for an itch, 
for destructive mildew in a garment and in a house, 
for a swelling, a scab and a bright spot, 
to teach when something is unclean and when it is clean. This is the law about serious skin disease.
