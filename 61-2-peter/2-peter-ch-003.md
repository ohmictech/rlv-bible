My truly loved friends, this is now the second letter I have written to you, and in both of them I have tried to stir up your honest minds by reminding you 
to remember the words spoken long ago by the set-apart spokesmen, and the command of our Lord and Restorer that was given to you by us, his sent ones. 
Above all, you must understand that in the last days people will come who mock, following their own cravings 
and saying, "Where is this arrival he promised? Ever since the fathers died, everything has gone on just as it has since the beginning of creation." 
They deliberately forget that the skies existed long ago, and that the land was formed out of water and by water through God's message; 
and by those waters the world of that time was flooded and destroyed. 
But the skies and the land that exist now are kept for fire by that same message, held for the day of the verdict, when people who disregard God will be destroyed. 
But do not forget this one thing, my truly loved friends: with the Lord a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day. 
The Lord is not slow about his promise, the way some people think of slowness. He is patient with you, because he does not want anyone to be ruined. He wants everyone to come to a change of heart. 
But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. On that day the skies will disappear with a roar, the elements will be dissolved by fire, and the land and what is in it will be burned up. 
Since everything is going to be destroyed like this, what kind of people should you be? You should live set-apart lives, devoted to God, 
as you look forward to the day of God and hurry it along. On that day the burning skies will be dissolved, and the elements will melt in the heat. 
But we are looking forward to new skies and a new land, as he promised, where rightness lives. 
So, my truly loved friends, while you are looking forward to this, make every effort to be found at wholeness with him, with no spot and nothing to blame. 
Think of our Lord's patience as rescue. That is what our truly loved brother Paul also wrote to you about, with the wisdom God gave him, 
and he says the same in all his letters. Some things in them are hard to understand, and people who do not know any better and are not steady twist them, as they do the rest of the Scriptures, to their own ruin. 
So, my truly loved friends, since you know this ahead of time, be on your guard so that you are not carried away by the error of those who live as if there were no law, and lose your own firm footing. 
Instead, grow in the favor and the knowing of our Lord and Restorer Jesus Christ. To him be the weight both now and for all the eras. Amen.
