God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'When a woman becomes pregnant and has a boy, she will be unclean for seven days, the same as during her monthly period. 
On the eighth day the boy's foreskin is to be cut off. 
Then she is to wait thirty-three days while her blood is purified. She is not to touch anything set apart or come into the Holy Place until the days of her purification are over. 
If she has a girl, she will be unclean for two weeks, the same as during her period, and then wait sixty-six days while her blood is purified. 
"'When the days of her purification are over, whether for a son or a daughter, she is to bring a lamb a year old to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting for a burnt offering, and a young pigeon or a dove for an offering for wrongdoing. 
He is to offer it before God and make things right for her, and she will be clean from the flow of her blood. This is the law for the woman who has a child, whether a boy or a girl. 
If she can't afford a lamb, she is to take two doves or two young pigeons, one for a burnt offering and the other for an offering for wrongdoing, and the priest will make things right for her, and she will be clean.'"
