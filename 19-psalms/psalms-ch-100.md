A Psalm of thanksgiving. Shout for joy to God, all you lands! 
Serve God with gladness. Come before his presence with singing. 
Know that he is God. It is he who has made us, and we are his. We are his people, and the sheep of his pasture. 
Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise. Give thanks to him, and bless his name. 
God is good. His loving kindness endures for the eras, his faithfulness to all generations.
