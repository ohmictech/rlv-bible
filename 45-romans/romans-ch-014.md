Now accept one who is weak in trust, but not for disputes over opinions. 
One person has trust to eat all things, but the one who is weak eats only vegetables. 
Don't let the one who eats despise the one who doesn't eat. Don't let the one who doesn't eat judge the one who eats, for God has accepted them. 
Who are you who judge another's servant? To his own lord he stands or falls. Yes, he will be made to stand, for God has power to make him stand. 
One person esteems one day as more important. Another esteems every day alike. Let each person be fully assured in their own mind. 
He who observes the day, observes it to the Lord; and he who does not observe the day, to the Lord he does not observe it. He who eats, eats to the Lord, for he gives God thanks. He who doesn't eat, to the Lord he doesn't eat, and gives God thanks. 
None of us lives to himself, and none dies to himself. 
If we live, we live to the Lord. Or if we die, we die to the Lord. If we live or die, we are the Lord's. 
To this end Christ died, rose, and lived again, so that he would be Lord of both the dead and the living. 
But you, why do you judge your brother? Or you again, why do you despise your brother? We will all stand before the judge's seat of Christ. 
It is written, "'As I live,' says the Lord, 'to me every knee will bow. Every tongue will confess to God.'" 
So then each one of us will give account of himself to God. 
So let's not judge one another any more, but decide this rather, that no one put a stumbling block in a brother's way, or a trap. 
I know and am persuaded in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean of itself; except that to the one who considers anything to be unclean, to them it is unclean. 
Yet if because of food your brother is grieved, you walk no longer in love. Don't ruin with your food the one for whom Christ died. 
Then don't let your good be slandered, 
for God's reign is not eating and drinking, but rightness, wholeness, and joy in the Holy Spirit. 
He who serves Christ in these things is acceptable to God and approved by people. 
So then, let's pursue what makes for wholeness, and what builds one another up. 
Don't overthrow God's work for food's sake. All things indeed are clean, however it is bad for that person who creates a stumbling block by eating. 
It is good to not eat meat, drink wine, nor do anything by which your brother stumbles, is trapped, or is made weak. 
Do you have trust? Have it to yourself before God. Flourishing is the one who doesn't judge themselves in that which they approve. 
But the one who doubts is sentenced if they eat, because it isn't of trust; and whatever is not of trust is the drift. 
Now to him who is able to establish you according to my Good News and the announcing of Jesus Christ, according to the unveiling of the secret which has been kept secret through long ages, 
but now is revealed, and by the Scriptures of the spokesmen, according to the commandment of the God of the eras, is made known for obedience of trust to all the nations; 
to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom be the weight for all the eras! Amen.
