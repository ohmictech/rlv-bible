The Chief Musician. To the tune of "Do Not Destroy." A Psalm by Asaph. A song. We give thanks to you, God. We give thanks, for your Name is near. Men tell about your wondrous works. 
When I choose the appointed time, I'll judge blamelessly. 
The earth and all its inhabitants quake. I firmly hold its pillars. Selah. 
I said to the arrogant, "Don't boast!" I said to the guilty, "Don't lift up the horn. 
Don't lift up your horn on high. Don't speak with a stiff neck." 
Neither from the east, nor from the west, nor yet from the south, comes exaltation. 
But God is the judge. He puts down one, and lifts up another. 
In God's hand there's a cup, full of foaming wine mixed with spices. He pours it out. Indeed the guilty of the earth drink and drink it to its very dregs. 
But I'll declare this for the eras: I'll sing praises to the God of Jacob. 
I'll cut off all the horns of the guilty, but the horns of the righteous will be lifted up.
