"Don't sacrifice to your God an ox or a sheep that has a defect or anything wrong with it, because that is disgusting to your God. 
If there is found among you, in any of your towns your God is giving you, a man or woman who does what is bad in the sight of your God by breaking his covenant, 
and has gone and served other gods and bowed down to them, or to the sun or the moon or any of the stars in the sky, which I have not commanded, 
and you are told about it and you hear of it, then you must investigate carefully. And if it is true and certain that this disgusting thing has been done in Israel, 
then bring that man or that woman who has done this bad thing out to your gates, and stone them to death. 
No one is to be put to death on the word of one witness. A person is put to death on the testimony of two or three witnesses. 
The hands of the witnesses must be the first on him to put him to death, and afterwards the hands of everyone else. So get rid of the evil among you. 
If a case comes to you that is too hard to judge, whether it is about blood or about a claim or about an injury, matters of dispute in your towns, then get up and go to the place your God chooses. 
Go to the priests, who are Levites, and to the judge in office at that time. Ask them, and they will give you the verdict. 
You must act according to the decision they give you from that place God chooses. Be careful to do everything they teach you. 
Act according to the law they teach you and the judgment they give you. Don't turn aside from the verdict they announce to you, either to the right or to the left. 
Anyone who acts arrogantly and refuses to listen to the priest who stands there serving your God, or to the judge, that person must die. So get rid of the evil from Israel. 
Then everyone will hear and be afraid and will stop acting so arrogantly. 
When you come into the land your God is giving you and you take it and live in it, and you say, 'I will set a king over me, like all the nations around me,' 
you must be sure to set as king over you the one your God chooses. Set a king over you from among your brothers. You can't put a foreigner over you who isn't your brother. 
But he must not build up a large number of horses for himself, and he must not send the people back to Egypt to get more horses, because God has said to you, 'You must never go back that way again.' 
He must not take many wives, or his heart will turn away. He must not pile up great amounts of silver and gold for himself. 
When he sits on the throne of his kingdom, he is to write out a copy of this law for himself in a scroll, from the one kept by the Levite priests. 
He is to keep it with him and read from it all the days of his life, so that he learns to fear his God and is careful to keep all the words of this law and these rules and do them. 
Then his heart won't become proud above his brothers, and he won't turn aside from the command, either to the right or to the left, so that he and his children may rule for a long time in his kingdom in the middle of Israel.
