Now about food sacrificed to idols: we know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up. 
But if anyone thinks that he knows anything, he doesn't yet know as he ought to know. 
But anyone who loves God is known by him. 
So concerning the eating of things sacrificed to idols, we know that no idol is anything in the world, and that there is no other God but one. 
Even if there are so-called "gods," whether in the sky or on the land, as there are many "gods" and many "lords", 
yet to us there is one God, the Father, of whom are all things, and we for him; and one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom are all things, and we live through him. 
However, not everyone has this knowledge. But some, still used to the idol, eat the food as food sacrificed to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is made unclean. 
But food won't commend us to God. Neither, if we don't eat are we the worse, nor if we eat are we the better. 
But be careful that this freedom of yours doesn't somehow become a stumbling block to the weak. 
If a man sees you who have knowledge sitting in an idol's temple, won't his conscience, if he is weak, be emboldened to eat things sacrificed to idols? 
And through your knowledge, the one who is weak is broken up, the brother for whose sake Christ died. 
So when you drift against the brothers and sisters, and wound their weak conscience, you are drifting against Christ. 
So, if food traps my brother, I will never eat meat again, so that I don't trap my brother.
