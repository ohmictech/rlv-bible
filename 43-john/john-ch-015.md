"I am the true vine, and my Father is the farmer. 
Every branch in me that doesn't bear fruit, he takes away. Every branch that bears fruit, he prunes, that it might bear more fruit. 
You're already pruned clean because of the word which I have spoken to you. 
Make your home in me, and I in you. As the branch can't bear fruit by itself unless it stays in the vine, so neither can you, unless you make your home in me. 
I'm the vine. You're the branches. Whoever makes their home in me and I in them bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing. 
If anyone doesn't make their home in me, they're thrown out as a branch and is withered; and they gather them, throw them into the fire, and they're burned. 
If you make your home in me, and my words make their home in you, you'll ask whatever you desire, and it'll be done for you. 
"In this my Father is honored, that you bear much fruit; and so you'll be my students. 
Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Make your home in my love. 
If you keep my commandments, you'll make your home in my love, even as I have kept my Father's commandments and make my home in his love. 
I have spoken these things to you, that my joy may make its home in you, and that your joy may be made complete. 
"This is my commandment, that you love one another, even as I have loved you. 
Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends. 
You're my friends if you do whatever I command you. 
No longer do I call you slaves, for the slave doesn't know what his lord does. But I have called you friends, for everything that I heard from my Father, I have made known to you. 
You didn't choose me, but I chose you and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should remain; that whatever you'll ask of the Father in my name, he can give it to you. 
"I command these things to you, that you can love one another. 
If the world hates you, you know that it has hated me before it hated you. 
If you were of the world, the world would love its own. But because you aren't of the world, since I chose you out of the world, the world hates you. 
Remember the word that I said to you: 'A slave isn't greater than his lord.' If they hunted me down, they'll also hunt you down. If they kept my word, they'll also keep yours. 
But they'll do all these things to you for my name's sake, because they don't know him who sent me. 
If I hadn't come and spoken to them, they wouldn't have drifted; but now they have no excuse for their mistakes. 
He who hates me, hates my Father also. 
If I hadn't done among them the deeds which no one else did, they wouldn't have drifted. But now they have seen and also hated both me and my Father. 
But this happened so that the word written in their law would come true: 'They hated me without a cause.' 
"When the one who comes alongside has come, whom I'll send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who comes out from the Father, he'll testify about me. 
You'll also testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.
