Hear, Israel! You're to pass over the Jordan today, to go in to dispossess nations greater and mightier than yourself, cities great and fortified up to the sky, 
a people great and tall, the sons of the Anakim, whom you know, and of whom you have heard say, "Who can stand before the sons of Anak?" 
Know today that your God is he who goes over before you as a devouring fire. He'll destroy them and he'll bring them down before you. So you shall drive them out and make them perish quickly, as God has spoken to you. 
Don't say in your heart, after your God has thrust them out from before you, "For my righteousness God has brought me in to possess this land;" because God drives them out before you because of the guilt of these nations. 
Not for your righteousness or for the uprightness of your heart do you go in to possess their land; but for the guilt of these nations your God does drive them out from before you, and that he may establish the word which God swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob. 
Know that your God doesn't give you this good land to possess for your righteousness, for you're a stiff-necked people. 
Remember, and don't forget, how you provoked your God to wrath in the wilderness. From the day that you left the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have been rebellious against God. 
Also in Horeb you provoked God to wrath, and God was angry with you to destroy you. 
When I had gone up onto the mountain to receive the stone tablets, even the tablets of the covenant which God made with you, then I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights. I neither ate bread nor drank water. 
God delivered to me the two stone tablets written with God's finger. On them were all the words which God spoke with you on the mountain out of the middle of the fire in the day of the assembly. 
It came to pass at the end of forty days and forty nights that God gave me the two stone tablets, even the tablets of the covenant. 
God said to me, "Arise, get down quickly from here; for your people whom you have brought out of Egypt have corrupted themselves. They have quickly turned away from the way which I commanded them. They have made a molten image for themselves!" 
Furthermore God spoke to me, saying, "I have seen these people, and look, they're a stiff-necked people. 
Leave me alone, that I may destroy them, and blot out their name from under the sky; and I'll make of you a nation mightier and greater than they." 
So I turned and came down from the mountain, and the mountain was burning with fire. The two tablets of the covenant were in my two hands. 
I looked, and look, you had wronged your God. You had made yourselves a molded calf. You had quickly turned away from the way which God had commanded you. 
I took hold of the two tablets, and threw them out of my two hands, and broke them before your eyes. 
I fell down before God, as at the first, forty days and forty nights. I neither ate bread nor drank water, because of all your wrong you did, in doing that which was bad in God's sight, to provoke him to anger. 
I was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure with which God was angry against you to destroy you. But God listened to me that time also. 
God was angry enough with Aaron to destroy him. I prayed for Aaron also at the same time. 
I took that wrongdoing of yours, the calf which you had made, and burned it with fire, and crushed it, grinding it very small, until it was as fine as dust. I threw its dust into the brook that descended out of the mountain. 
At Taberah, at Massah, and at Kibroth Hattaavah you provoked God to wrath. 
When God sent you from Kadesh Barnea, saying, "Go up and possess the land which I have given you," you rebelled against the commandment of your God, and you didn't believe him or listen to his voice. 
You have been rebellious against God from the day that I knew you. 
So I fell down before God the forty days and forty nights that I fell down, because God had said he'd destroy you. 
I prayed to God, and said, "God, don't destroy your people and your inheritance that you have bought back through your greatness, that you have brought out of Egypt with a mighty hand. 
Remember your servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Don't look at the stubbornness of this people, nor at their guilt, nor at their wrongdoing, 
lest the land you brought us out from say, 'Because God wasn't able to bring them into the land which he promised to them, and because he hated them, he has brought them out to kill them in the wilderness.' 
Yet they're your people and your inheritance, which you brought out by your great power and by your outstretched arm."
