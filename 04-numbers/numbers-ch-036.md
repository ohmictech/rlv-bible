The heads of the families of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the families of the sons of Joseph, came and spoke in front of Moses and the leaders, the heads of the families of the Israelites. 
They said, "God commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the Israelites, and my lord was commanded by God to give our brother Zelophehad's inheritance to his daughters. 
But if they marry men from another tribe of Israel, their inheritance will be taken away from our fathers' inheritance and added to the tribe they marry into, so it will be taken out of the lot that fell to us. 
And when the jubilee of the Israelites comes, their inheritance will be added to the tribe they belong to, and it will be taken away from our fathers' tribe." 
Moses gave the Israelites God's word: "The tribe of the sons of Joseph are right. 
This is what God commands about the daughters of Zelophehad: 'They may marry anyone they think best, but they must marry within their father's tribe. 
No inheritance in Israel is to pass from tribe to tribe, because each Israelite is to hold on to the inheritance of his ancestral tribe. 
Any daughter who inherits land in a tribe is to marry someone from her father's tribe, so that each Israelite keeps his fathers' inheritance. 
No inheritance is to pass from one tribe to another, because each tribe of Israel is to keep its own inheritance.'" 
The daughters of Zelophehad did as God commanded Moses. 
Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, married their cousins, 
into the families of the sons of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance stayed in the tribe of their father's family. 
These are the commands and decisions God gave through Moses to the Israelites on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
