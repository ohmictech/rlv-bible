A Song of Ascents. God, remember David and all his affliction, 
how he swore to God, and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob: 
"Surely I won't come into the structure of my house, nor go up into my bed; 
I won't give sleep to my eyes, or slumber to my eyelids, 
until I find out a place for God, a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob." 
Look, we heard of it in Ephrathah. We found it in the field of Jaar. 
"We'll go into his dwelling place. We'll worship at his footstool." 
Arise, God, into your resting place, you, and the ark of your strength. 
Let your priests be clothed with righteousness. Let your saints shout for joy! 
Your servant David's sake, don't turn away the face of your anointed one. 
God has sworn to David in truth. He won't turn from it: "I'll set the fruit of your body on your throne. 
If your children will keep my covenant, my testimony that I'll teach them, their children also will sit on your throne for the eras more." 
God has chosen Zion. He has desired it for his habitation. 
"This is my resting place for the eras. I'll live here, for I have desired it. 
I'll abundantly bless her provision. I'll satisfy her poor with bread. 
I'll also clothe her priests with salvation. Her saints will shout aloud for joy. 
I'll make the horn of David to bud there. I have ordained a lamp for my anointed. 
I'll clothe his enemies with shame, but on himself, his crown will shine."
