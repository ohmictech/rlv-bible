On one of those days, as he was teaching the people in the temple courts and preaching the Good News, the priests and the Bible teachers came to him with the elders. 
They asked him, "Tell us: by what authority do you do these things? Or who is giving you this authority?" 
He answered them, "I also will ask you one question. Tell me: 
the immersion of John, was it from the sky, or from people?" 
They reasoned with themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From the sky,' he will say, 'Why didn't you trust him?' 
But if we say, 'From people,' everyone will stone us, for they are persuaded that John was a spokesman." 
They answered that they didn't know where it was from. 
Jesus said to them, "Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things." 
He began to tell the people this story: "A man planted a vineyard and rented it out to some farmers, and went into another country for a long time. 
At the proper season, he sent a slave to the farmers to collect his share of the fruit of the vineyard. But the farmers beat him and sent him away empty. 
He sent yet another slave, and they also beat him and humiliated him, and sent him away empty. 
He sent yet a third, and they also wounded him and threw him out. 
The lord of the vineyard said, 'What should I do? I will send my son, whom I truly love. It may be that seeing him, they will respect him.' 
"But when the farmers saw him, they reasoned among themselves, saying, 'This is the heir. Come, let's kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.' 
Then they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. What will the lord of the vineyard do to them? 
He will come and ruin these farmers, and will give the vineyard to others." When they heard that, they said, "May that never be!" 
But he looked at them and said, "Then what is this that is written, 'The stone which the builders rejected was made the chief cornerstone'? 
Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces, but it will crush whomever it falls on to dust." 
The chief priests and the Bible teachers sought to lay hands on him that very time, but they feared the people, for they knew he had spoken this story against them. 
They watched him and sent out spies, who pretended to be upright, so that they could trap him in something he said, so as to deliver him up to the power and authority of the governor. 
They asked him, "Teacher, we know that you say and teach what is right, and aren't partial to anyone, but truly teach the way of God. 
Is it allowed for us to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?" 
But he perceived their craftiness, and said to them, "Why do you test me? 
Show me a silver coin. Whose image and inscription are on it?" They answered, "Caesar's." 
He said to them, "Then give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's." 
They weren't able to trap him in his words before the people. They were amazed at his answer and were silent. 
Some of the religious elite came to him, those who deny that there is a rising from the dead. 
They asked him, "Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man's brother dies having a wife, and he is childless, his brother should take the wife and raise up children for his brother. 
There were seven brothers. The first took a wife, and died childless. 
The second took her as wife, and he died childless. 
The third took her, and likewise the seven all left no children, and died. 
Afterward the woman also died. 
So in the rising from the dead whose wife of them will she be? All seven had her as a wife." 
Jesus said to them, "The children of this era marry and are given in marriage. 
But those who are considered worthy to attain to that era and the rising from the dead neither marry nor are given in marriage. 
They can't die any more, for they are like the messengers and are children of God, being children of the rising. 
But that the dead are raised, even Moses showed at the burning bush, when he called the Lord 'The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.' 
Now he is not the God of dead bodies, but of the living, for all are alive to him." 
Some of the Bible teachers answered, "Teacher, you speak well." 
They didn't dare to ask him any more questions. 
He said to them, "Why do they say that the Christ is David's son? 
David himself says in the book of Psalms, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand, 
until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet."' 
"David calls him Lord, so how is he his son?" 
In the hearing of everyone, he said to his students, 
"Watch out for those Bible teachers who like to walk in long robes, and love greetings in the marketplaces, the best seats in the gathering places, and the best places at dinners; 
who eat up widows' houses, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive a heavier verdict."
