The descendants of Reuben and Gad had a very large number of livestock, and they saw that the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead were good country for livestock. 
So the descendants of Gad and Reuben came and spoke to Moses, Eleazar the priest and the leaders of the community: 
"Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo and Beon, 
the land God struck down in front of the community of Israel, is good country for livestock, and your servants have livestock." 
They said, "If you are pleased with us, give this land to your servants as our property. Don't make us cross the Jordan." 
Moses said to the descendants of Gad and Reuben, "Are your brothers supposed to go to war while you sit here? 
Why do you discourage the Israelites from crossing into the land God has given them? 
That's what your fathers did when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to look at the land. 
They went up to the Eshcol valley and saw the land, and they discouraged the Israelites from going into the land God had given them. 
God's anger burned that day, and he swore, 
'None of the men who came up out of Egypt, twenty years old and up, will see the land I swore to give Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, because they haven't followed me completely, 
except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, because they followed God completely.' 
God's anger burned against Israel, and he made them wander in the wilderness for forty years, until that whole generation that had done what was bad in God's sight had died out. 
"And here you are, taking your fathers' place, a new batch of people who drift, adding to the fierce anger of God against Israel. 
If you turn away from following him, he will leave these people in the wilderness again, and you will destroy all of them." 
They came to him and said, "We will build sheep pens here for our livestock and towns for our children, 
but we ourselves will be ready and armed to go ahead of the Israelites until we have brought them to their place. Our children will stay in the fortified towns because of the people living in the land. 
We won't go back to our homes until every Israelite has received their inheritance. 
We won't take any inheritance with them on the other side of the Jordan and beyond, because our inheritance has come to us on this side, east of the Jordan." 
Moses said to them, "If you do this, if you arm yourselves and go before God to the war, 
and every one of your armed men crosses the Jordan before God until he has driven his enemies out in front of him, 
and the land is subdued before God, then afterward you may come back, free of any obligation to God and to Israel, and this land will be your property before God. 
"But if you don't, then you have done wrong against God, and you can be sure your wrong will find you out. 
Build towns for your children and pens for your sheep, and do what you said you would." 
The descendants of Gad and Reuben said to Moses, "Your servants will do as my lord commands. 
Our children, our wives, our flocks and all our livestock will stay there in the towns of Gilead, 
but your servants will cross over, every man armed for war, before God to battle, just as my lord says." 
So Moses gave orders about them to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the families of the tribes of Israel. 
Moses said to them, "If the descendants of Gad and Reuben cross the Jordan with you, every man armed for battle before God, and the land is subdued before you, then give them the land of Gilead as their property. 
But if they won't cross over with you armed, then they will have their property among you in the land of Canaan." 
The descendants of Gad and Reuben answered, "What God has said to your servants, that is what we will do. 
We will cross over armed before God into the land of Canaan, and the property we inherit will stay with us on this side of the Jordan." 
So Moses gave to them, to the descendants of Gad, the descendants of Reuben and the half tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph, the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land with its towns and the territory around them. 
The descendants of Gad built Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer, 
Atroth Shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah, 
Beth Nimrah and Beth Haran, fortified towns, and sheep pens. 
The descendants of Reuben built Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim, 
Nebo, Baal Meon (their names were changed) and Sibmah. They gave other names to the towns they built. 
The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, took it and drove out the Amorites living there. 
Moses gave Gilead to Machir son of Manasseh, and he settled there. 
Jair son of Manasseh went and captured its villages and named them Havvoth Jair. 
Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and named it Nobah, after himself.
