Ask of God rain in the spring time, God who makes storm clouds, and he gives rain showers to everyone for the plants in the field. 
The teraphim have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie; and they have told false dreams. They comfort in vain. So they go their way like sheep. They're oppressed, because there's no shepherd. 
My anger is kindled against the shepherds, and I'll punish the male goats, for the God of Armies has visited his flock, the house of Judah, and will make them as his majestic horse in the battle. 
From him will come the cornerstone, from him the tent peg, from him the battle bow, from him every ruler together. 
They'll be as mighty men, treading down muddy streets in the battle. They'll fight, because God is with them. The riders on horses will be confounded. 
"I'll strengthen the house of Judah, and I'll save the house of Joseph. I'll bring them back, for I have mercy on them. They'll be as though I hadn't cast them off, for I'm their God, and I'll hear them. 
Ephraim will be like a mighty man, and their heart will rejoice as through wine. Yes, their children will see it and rejoice. Their heart will be glad in God. 
I'll signal for them and gather them, for I have bought back them. They'll increase as they were before. 
I'll sow them among the peoples. They'll remember me in far countries. They'll live with their children and will return. 
I'll bring them again also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria. I'll bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon; and there won't be room enough for them. 
He'll pass through the sea of affliction, and will strike the waves in the sea, and all the depths of the Nile will dry up; and the pride of Assyria will be brought down, and the scepter of Egypt will depart. 
I'll strengthen them in God. They'll walk up and down in his name," says God.
