Finally, brothers and sisters, we beg you and urge you in the Lord Jesus: just as you learned from us how to live and how to please God (and you do) we ask you to overflow even more. 
You know what instructions we gave you through the Lord Jesus. 
This is what God wants for you: that you be set apart, and that you keep away from sleeping around. 
Each of you should know how to keep his own body in set-apartness and honor, 
not driven by passion and craving the way the nations do, who don't know God, 
and that no one should wrong or take advantage of a brother or sister in this matter, because the Lord is the avenger in all these things, just as we told you before and warned you. 
God didn't call us to uncleanness, but to be set apart. 
So anyone who rejects this isn't rejecting a person but God, who gives you his Holy Spirit. 
About love like family, you don't need anyone to write to you, because God himself has taught you to love one another. 
And you do love all the brothers and sisters throughout Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers and sisters, to overflow even more. 
Make it your aim to live quietly, to mind your own business, and to work with your own hands, just as we instructed you, 
so that your life wins the respect of those outside, and you need nothing from anyone. 
Brothers and sisters, we don't want you to be uninformed about those who have fallen asleep, so that you don't grieve the way the others do, who have no expectation. 
Since we trust that Jesus died and rose again, we also trust that God will bring with him those who have fallen asleep in Jesus. 
What we tell you now is the Lord's own word: we who are alive, who are left until the Lord's arrival, will certainly not go ahead of those who have fallen asleep. 
The Lord himself will come down from the sky with a command shouted out, with the voice of the chief messenger and with God's trumpet, and the dead in Christ will be raised first, 
then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will be with the Lord for the eras. 
So encourage one another with these words.
