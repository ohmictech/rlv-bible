Paul, a sent one of Christ Jesus through the will of God, for the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus, 
to Timothy, my truly loved child: Favor, mercy, and wholeness to you from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord. 
I thank God, whom I serve the way my ancestors did, with a clean conscience, as I remember you constantly in my prayers, night and day. 
I long to see you, remembering your tears, so that I may be full of joy. 
I remember the genuine trust in you, which lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am sure it lives in you too. 
That is why I remind you to fan into flame the gift God gave you when I laid my hands on you. 
God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, love, and good judgment. 
So don't be ashamed of the witness about our Lord, or of me, his prisoner. Instead, share the hardship for the Good News, by the power of God, 
who rescued us and called us with a set-apart calling, not because of our deeds, but because of his own purpose and gift, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before the eras began. 
But now it has been made visible through the appearing of our Restorer, Christ Jesus, who put death out of work and brought life that never decays into the light through the Good News. 
That is what I was appointed for: to announce it, to be a sent one, and to be a teacher of the nations. 
That is why I am going through all this. But I am not ashamed, because I know the one I have trusted, and I am sure he is able to guard what I have put in his care until that day. 
Hold on to the pattern of healthy words you heard from me, in the trust and love that are in Christ Jesus. 
Guard the good thing that was put in your care, through the Holy Spirit who lives in us. 
You know that everyone in Asia has turned away from me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes. 
May the Lord show mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chains, 
When he was in Rome, he searched hard for me and found me 
May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day. And you know very well how much he did for me at Ephesus.
