So, since we have this service, just as we were shown mercy, we don't give up. 
But we have given up the hidden things of shame, not walking in trickery or twisting the word of God, but by showing the truth plainly, commending ourselves to everyone's conscience in God's sight. 
Even if our Good News is covered, it is covered among those who are dying, 
among whom the god of this world has blinded the minds of those who don't trust, so that the light of the Good News of the weight of Christ, who is the image of God, wouldn't shine on them. 
We don't announce ourselves, but Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your servants for Jesus' sake, 
seeing it is God who said, "Light will shine out of darkness," who has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the weight of God in the face of Jesus Christ. 
But we have this treasure in clay jars, so that this great power may be seen to come from God and not from us. 
We are pressed on every side, but not crushed; at a loss, but not in despair; 
hunted down, but not abandoned; struck down, but not broken up; 
always carrying in the body the putting to death of the Lord Jesus, that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body. 
We who live are always delivered to death for Jesus' sake, that the life also of Jesus may be revealed in our mortal flesh. 
So then death works in us, but life in you. 
But having the same spirit of trust, according to what is written, "I trusted, and so I spoke." We also trust, and so we also speak, 
knowing that he who raised the Lord Jesus will raise us also through Jesus, and will present us with you. 
All things are for your sakes, so that the gift, multiplied through many people, may make thanksgiving overflow to the weight of God. 
So we don't give up, but even though our outward person is decaying, our inward person is made new day by day. 
Our light hard times, which last only a moment, are piling up for us a mass of weight beyond all measure that lasts for the eras, 
while we don't look at the things seen, but at the things not seen. The things seen last only a while, but the things not seen last for the eras.
