So then, my brothers and sisters, whom I truly love and long for, my joy and my wreath, stand firm in the Lord this way, my truly loved ones. 
I urge Euodia and I urge Syntyche to think the same way in the Lord. 
Yes, and I ask you too, my true partner, help these women, because they worked alongside me in the Good News, together with Clement and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life. 
Celebrate in the Lord always. I'll say it again: celebrate! 
Let everyone see how gentle you are. The Lord is near. 
Don't worry about anything. Instead, in every situation, tell God what you need, with prayer and requests and thanksgiving. 
And God's wholeness, which goes far beyond anything we can understand, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus. 
Finally, brothers and sisters, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is worth talking about, if there's anything excellent, if there's anything worth praising, think about these things. 
Keep doing the things you learned and received and heard and saw in me, and the God of wholeness will be with you. 
I celebrated greatly in the Lord that you have finally revived your care for me. You were indeed caring for me, but you had no chance to show it. 
I'm not saying this because I'm in need, because I have learned to be content whatever my circumstances. 
I know what it's to be brought low, and I know what it's to overflow. I have learned the secret of being full and of being hungry, of overflowing and of being in need. 
I can do all this through him who gives me strength. 
Still, you did well to share in my troubles. 
And you yourselves know, Philippians, that when the Good News first reached you and I left Macedonia, no gathering shared with me in the matter of giving and receiving except you alone. 
Even while I was in Thessalonica you sent help for my needs more than once. 
Not that I'm looking for a gift; what I'm looking for is the fruit that piles up to your account. 
I have everything I need and more than enough. I'm full, now that I have received from Epaphroditus what you sent, a sweet smell, a sacrifice that God accepts, pleasing to him. 
And my God will fill every need of yours out of his riches in weight, in Christ Jesus. 
To our God and Father be the weight for all the eras! Amen.
