God said to Noah, "Come with all of your household into the ship, for I have seen that you are right before me in this generation. 
Take seven pairs of every clean animal with you, the male and his female. Of the animals that aren't clean, take two, the male and his female. 
Also of the birds of the sky, seven and seven, male and female, to keep seed alive on the face of all the earth. 
In seven days, I will make it rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights. I will wipe out every living thing that I have made from the face of the land." 
Noah did everything that God commanded him. 
Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters came on the earth. 
Noah went into the ship with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives, because of the floodwaters. 
Clean animals, unclean animals, birds, and everything that creeps on the ground 
went by pairs to Noah into the ship, male and female, as God commanded Noah. 
After the seven days, the floodwaters came on the earth. 
In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that day all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the sky opened. 
It rained on the earth forty days and forty nights. 
In the same day Noah, and Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons with him, went into the ship: 
they, and every animal of every kind, all the livestock of every kind, every small crawling thing that crawls on the earth of every kind, and every bird of every kind, every bird of every sort. 
Pairs from all flesh with the breath of life in them went into the ship to Noah. 
Those who went in went in male and female, of all flesh, as God commanded him. Then God shut him in. 
The flood came on the earth for forty days. The waters rose and lifted the ship, and it rose above the earth. 
The waters rose and grew very deep on the earth, and the ship floated on the surface of the water. 
The waters rose so high on the earth that all the high mountains under the whole sky were covered. 
The waters rose twenty-two feet above the mountains, and they were covered. 
All flesh that moved on the earth died: birds, livestock, wild animals, every small crawling thing that crawls on the earth, and every human being. 
Everything on dry land that had the breath of life in its nostrils died. 
Every living thing on the face of the land was wiped out: human beings, livestock, small crawling things, and the birds of the sky. They were wiped off the earth. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ship. 
The waters flooded the earth one hundred fifty days.
