By David. Don't fret because of those who do bad, neither be envious against those who work unrighteousness. 
For they shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither like the green herb. 
Trust in God, and do good. Dwell in the land, and enjoy safe pasture. 
Also delight yourself in God, and he will give you the desires of your heart. 
Commit your way to God. Trust also in him, and he will do this: 
he will make your righteousness shine out like light, and your justice as the noon day sun. 
Rest in God, and wait patiently for him. Don't fret because of him who prospers in his way, because of the man who makes guilty plots happen. 
Cease from anger, and forsake wrath. Don't fret; it leads only to doing bad. 
For those who do bad shall be cut off, but those who wait for God shall inherit the land. 
For yet a little while, and the guilty will be no more. Yes, though you look for his place, he isn't there. 
But the humble shall inherit the land, and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace. 
The guilty plots against the just, and gnashes at him with his teeth. 
The Lord will laugh at him, for he sees that his day is coming. 
The guilty have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, to kill those who are upright on the path. 
Their sword shall enter into their own heart. Their bows shall be broken. 
Better is a little that the righteous has, than the abundance of many guilty. 
For the arms of the guilty shall be broken, but God upholds the righteous. 
God knows the days of the perfect. Their inheritance shall be for the eras. 
They shall not be disappointed in the time of bad. In the days of famine they shall be satisfied. 
But the guilty shall perish. The enemies of God shall be like the beauty of the fields. They will vanish, vanish like smoke. 
The guilty borrow, and don't pay back, but the righteous give generously. 
For such as are blessed by him shall inherit the land. Those who are cursed by him shall be cut off. 
A man's steps are established by God. He delights in his way. 
Though he stumble, he shall not fall, for God holds him up with his hand. 
I have been young, and now am old, yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his children begging for bread. 
All day long he deals graciously, and lends. His offspring is blessed. 
Depart from what is bad, and do good. Live securely for the eras. 
For God loves justice, and doesn't forsake his saints. They are preserved for the eras, but the children of the guilty shall be cut off. 
The righteous shall inherit the land, and live in it for the eras. 
The mouth of the righteous talks of wisdom. His tongue speaks justice. 
The law of his God is in his heart. None of his steps shall slide. 
The guilty watch the righteous, and seek to kill him. 
God will not leave him in his hand, nor condemn him when he is judged. 
Wait for God, and keep his way, and he will exalt you to inherit the land. When the guilty are cut off, you shall see it. 
I have seen the guilty in great power, spreading himself like a green tree in its native soil. 
But he passed away, and behold, he was not. Yes, I sought him, but he could not be found. 
Mark the perfect man, and see the upright, for there is a future for the man of peace. 
As for transgressors, they shall be destroyed together. The future of the guilty shall be cut off. 
But the salvation of the righteous is from God. He is their stronghold in the time of trouble. 
God helps them and rescues them. He rescues them from the guilty and saves them, because they have taken refuge in him.
