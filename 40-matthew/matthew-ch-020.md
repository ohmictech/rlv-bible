"For the reign of the sky is like a man who was the master of a house, who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard. 
When he had agreed with the workers for a silver coin a day, he sent them into his vineyard. 
He went out about mid-morning, and saw others standing idle in the marketplace. 
He said to them, 'You also go into the vineyard, and whatever is right I will give you.' So they went their way. 
Again he went out about noon and mid-afternoon, and did likewise. 
About an hour before evening he went out and found others standing idle. He said to them, 'Why do you stand here all day idle?' 
"They said to him, 'Because no one has hired us.' "He said to them, 'You also go into the vineyard, and you will receive whatever is right.' 
"When evening had come, the lord of the vineyard said to his manager, 'Call the workers and pay them what they earned, beginning from the last to the first.' 
"When those hired about an hour before evening came, they each received a silver coin. 
When the first came, they supposed that they would receive more, and they likewise each received a silver coin. 
When they received it, they grumbled against the master of the house, 
saying, 'These last have spent one hour, and you have made them equal to us who have borne the burden of the day and the scorching heat!' 
"But he answered one of them, 'Friend, I am doing you no wrong. Didn't you agree with me for a silver coin? 
Take what is yours, and go your way. I want to give to this last just as much as to you. 
Isn't it allowed for me to do what I want to with what I own? Or is your eye jealous, because I am good?' 
So the last will be first, and the first will be last. For many are called, but few are chosen." 
As Jesus was going up to Jerusalem, he took the twelve students aside, and on the way he said to them, 
"Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Human One will be handed over to the high priests and Bible teachers, and they will sentence him to death, 
and will hand him over to the nations to mock, to whip, and to crucify, and the third day he will be raised up." 
Then the mother of the sons of Zebedee came to him with her sons, kneeling and asking a certain thing of him. 
He said to her, "What do you want?" She said to him, "Command that these, my two sons, may sit, one on your right hand and one on your left hand, in your reign." 
But Jesus answered, "You don't know what you are asking. Are you able to drink the cup I am about to drink, and be immersed as I am immersed?" They said to him, "We are able." 
He said to them, "You will indeed drink my cup, and be immersed as I am immersed, but to sit on my right hand and on my left hand is not mine to give, but it is for those for whom it has been prepared by my Father." 
When the ten heard it, they were indignant with the two brothers. 
But Jesus summoned them, and said, "You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and their great ones use their authority over them. 
It will not be so among you, but whoever wants to become great among you will be your servant. 
Whoever wants to be first among you will be your slave, 
even as the Human One came not to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom price for everyone." 
As they went out from Jericho, a great crowd followed him. 
Suddenly, two blind men sitting by the road, when they heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out, "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!" 
The crowd rebuked them, telling them that they should be quiet, but they cried out even more, "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!" 
Jesus stopped and called them, and asked, "What do you want me to do for you?" 
They told him, "Lord, that our eyes may be opened." 
Jesus, moved with deep compassion, touched their eyes, and immediately their eyes received their sight, and they followed him.
