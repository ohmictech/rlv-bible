Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, wise men from the east suddenly arrived in Jerusalem, asking, 
"Where is the one who has been born King of the Jews? We saw his star in the east, and have come to worship him." 
When King Herod heard it, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him. 
Gathering together all the chief priests and Bible teachers of the people, he asked them where the Christ would be born. 
They said to him, "In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is what the spokesman wrote: 
'You Bethlehem, land of Judah, are in no way least among the princes of Judah, for out of you'll come a governor who will shepherd my people, Israel.'" 
Then Herod secretly called the wise men, and learned from them exactly what time the star appeared. 
He sent them to Bethlehem, and said, "Go and search carefully for the child. When you have found him, bring me word, so that I also may come and worship him." 
After hearing the king, they went on their way. And the star they had seen in the east went ahead of them until it stopped over the place where the child was. 
When they saw the star, they celebrated with overwhelming joy. 
They went into the house and saw the child with Mary his mother, and they fell down and worshiped him. Then they opened their treasures and offered him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh. 
Being warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went back to their own country another way. 
After they had gone, a messenger of the Lord suddenly appeared to Joseph in a dream and said, "Get up, take the child and his mother, and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, because Herod is going to search for the child to kill him." 
He woke up and took the young child and his mother by night and departed into Egypt, 
and was there until the death of Herod, that it would be fulfilled which was spoken by the Lord through the spokesman, saying, "Out of Egypt I called my son." 
When Herod saw that the wise men had tricked him, he was furious. He sent men and killed all the boys in Bethlehem and the whole surrounding countryside who were two years old and under, matching the time he had learned from the wise men. 
Then what was spoken by the spokesman Jeremiah was fulfilled, saying, 
"A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and great mourning, Rachel crying for her children, and she wouldn't be comforted, because they're no more." 
But when Herod was dead, suddenly, a messenger of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt, saying, 
"Get up, take the child and his mother, and go to the land of Israel, because those who wanted to kill the child are dead." 
He woke up and took the young child and his mother, and came into the land of Israel. 
But when he heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in the place of his father, Herod, he was afraid to go there. Being warned in a dream, he withdrew into the region of Galilee, 
and went and lived in a town called Nazareth. So what was spoken through the spokesmen came true: he'll be called a Nazarene.
