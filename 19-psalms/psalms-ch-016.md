A Poem by David. Preserve me, God, for I take refuge in you. 
My soul, you have said to God, "You're my Lord. Apart from you I have no good thing." 
As for the saints who are on the earth, they're the excellent ones in whom is all my delight. 
Their sorrows shall be multiplied who give gifts to another god. Their drink offerings of blood I won't offer, nor take their names on my lips. 
God assigned my portion and my cup. You made my lot secure. 
The lines have fallen to me in pleasant places. Yes, I have a good inheritance. 
I'll bless God, who has given me counsel. Yes, my heart instructs me in the night seasons. 
I have set God always before me. Because he's at my right hand, I shall not be moved. 
So my heart is glad, and my tongue rejoices. My body shall also dwell in safety. 
You won't leave my life in Hades, neither will you allow your Holy One to see decay. 
You'll show me the path of life. In your presence is fullness of joy. In your right hand there are pleasures for the eras more.
