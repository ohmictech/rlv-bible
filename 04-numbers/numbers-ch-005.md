God spoke to Moses: 
"Command the Israelites to send out of the camp everyone with a serious skin disease, everyone with a discharge, and everyone made unclean by a dead body. 
Send out both men and women, so they don't make the camp unclean, because I live among them." 
The Israelites did this and sent them outside the camp, just as God told Moses. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'When a man or a woman does any of the wrongs people do and breaks faith against God, and that person is guilty, 
he is to confess the wrong he has done, pay back in full what he owes, add a fifth to it, and give it to the person he wronged. 
But if the man has no close relative to pay it back to, the payment belongs to God and goes to the priest, along with the ram used to make things right for him. 
Every contribution of the set apart gifts the Israelites bring to the priest belongs to him. 
Each man's set apart gifts are his own, but whatever a man gives the priest belongs to the priest.'" 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'If a man's wife goes astray and is unfaithful to him, 
and another man has sex with her, and it is hidden from her husband and kept secret, and she has made herself unclean with no witness against her and no one catching her in the act, 
and jealousy comes over him and he is jealous of his wife, whether she has made herself unclean or not, 
then the man is to bring his wife to the priest, along with an offering of a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He is not to pour oil on it or put frankincense on it, because it is a gift offering for jealousy, a reminder offering that brings guilt to mind. 
The priest is to bring her near and set her before God. 
The priest is to take set apart water in a clay pot and put some of the dust from the floor of the tent into the water. 
The priest is to set the woman before God, let her hair down, and put the reminder offering in her hands, the gift offering for jealousy, while he holds the bitter water that brings a curse. 
The priest is to put her under oath and say to the woman, "If no man has had sex with you, and if you have not gone astray and made yourself unclean while married to your husband, may this bitter water that brings a curse do you no harm. 
But if you have gone astray while married to your husband and made yourself unclean, and some man other than your husband has had sex with you" - 
then the priest is to put the woman under the oath of the curse and say to her, "May God make your womb shrivel and your belly swell among your people. 
May this water that brings a curse go into your body, making your belly swell and your womb shrivel." And the woman is to say, "Amen, amen." 
"'The priest is to write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the bitter water. 
He is to make the woman drink the bitter water that brings the curse, and the water that brings the curse will go into her and turn bitter. 
The priest is to take the gift offering for jealousy from the woman's hand, wave it before God and bring it to the altar. 
The priest is to take a handful of the gift offering as its memorial portion and burn it on the altar, and after that make the woman drink the water. 
When he has made her drink the water, then if she has made herself unclean and been unfaithful to her husband, the water that brings the curse will go into her and turn bitter, her belly will swell and her womb will shrivel, and the woman will be a curse word among her people. 
If the woman has not made herself unclean and is clean, she will be free and will have children. 
"'This is the law about jealousy, when a wife goes astray while married to her husband and makes herself unclean, 
or when jealousy comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife. He is to set the woman before God, and the priest is to carry out this whole law on her. 
The man is free of guilt, but the woman will carry her guilt.'"
