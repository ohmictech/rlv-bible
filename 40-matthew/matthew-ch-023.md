Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his students, 
saying, "The Bible teachers and the strict religious ones sit on Moses' seat. 
So whatever they tell you to observe, observe it and do it. But don't do what they do, because they talk but don't act. 
They tie up heavy burdens and lay them on people's shoulders, but they themselves won't lift a finger to move them. 
But they do all their deeds to be seen by people. They make their prayer boxes broad and enlarge the fringes of their garments, 
and love the place of honor at dinners, the best seats in the gathering places, 
the greetings in the marketplaces, and to be called 'Rabbi, Rabbi' by people. 
But you aren't to be called 'Rabbi', for one is your Teacher, the Christ, and all of you're brothers and sisters. 
Call no one on earth your father, for one is your Father, in the sky. 
Neither be called 'master', for one is your master, the Christ. 
But he who's greatest among you'll be your servant. 
Whoever lifts himself up will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be lifted up. 
"How terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! You eat up widows' houses and, for a pretense, make long prayers. So you'll receive a heavier verdict. 
"But how terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! Because you shut up the reign of the sky against people, for you don't enter in yourselves, and you don't allow those who are entering in to enter. 
How terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! You travel around by sea and land to make one convert, and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of Gehenna as yourselves. 
"How terrible for you, you blind guides, who say, 'Whoever swears by the temple, it means nothing, but whoever swears by the gold of the temple is bound by it.' 
You blind fools! Which is greater, the gold or the temple that makes the gold set apart? 
And, 'Whoever swears by the altar, it means nothing, but whoever swears by the gift that's on it's bound by it.' 
You blind fools! Which is greater, the gift, or the altar that makes the gift set apart? 
He who swears by the altar, swears by it and by everything on it. 
He who swears by the temple, swears by it and by the one who lives in it. 
He who swears by the sky, swears by the throne of God and by the one who sits on it. 
"How terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! You give a tenth of mint, dill, and cumin, have left the heavier matters of the law undone: right judgment, mercy, and trust. You should have done these without leaving the others undone. 
You blind guides, who strain out a gnat, and swallow a camel! 
"How terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! You clean the outside of the cup and of the plate, but within they're full of robbery and wrong. 
You blind strict religious man, first clean the inside of the cup and of the plate, so that its outside can become clean also. 
"How terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! You're like whitewashed tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead bones and of all uncleanness. 
Even so you also outwardly appear upright to people, but inwardly you're full of pretending and living as if there were no law. 
"How terrible for you, Bible teachers and strict religious ones, pretenders! You build the tombs of the spokesmen and decorate the tombs of the upright, 
and say, 'If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we wouldn't have been partakers with them in the blood of the spokesmen.' 
So you witness to yourselves that you're children of those who killed the spokesmen. 
Fill up, then, the measure of your fathers. 
You snakes, you offspring of vipers, how will you escape the verdict of Gehenna? 
So, look, I send to you spokesmen, wise men, and Bible teachers. Some of them you'll kill and crucify, and some of them you'll whip in your gathering places and hunt from city to city, 
that on you can come all the blood of the upright shed on the earth, from the blood of upright Abel to the blood of Zachariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the temple and the altar. 
Truly I tell you, all these things will come upon this generation. 
"Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the spokesmen and stones those who are sent to her! How often I'd have gathered your children together, the way a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you wouldn't! 
Look, your house is left to you desolate. 
I tell you, you won't see me from now on, until you say, 'Flourishing is he who comes in the name of the Lord!'"
