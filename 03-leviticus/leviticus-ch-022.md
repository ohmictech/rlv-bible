God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Tell Aaron and his sons to separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel, which they make holy to me, and that they not profane my holy name. I'm God. 
"Tell them, 'If anyone of all your offspring throughout your generations approaches the holy things which the children of Israel make holy to God, having his uncleanness on him, that soul will be cut off from before me. I'm God. 
"'Whoever of the offspring of Aaron is a leper or has a discharge won't eat of the holy things until he's clean. Whoever touches anything that's unclean by the dead, or a man who has a seminal emission, 
or whoever touches any creeping thing by which he can be made unclean, or a man from whom he can become unclean, whatever uncleanness he has 
the person that touches any such will be unclean until the evening, and won't eat of the holy things unless he bathes his body in water. 
When the sun is down, he will be clean; and afterward he will eat of the holy things, because it's his bread. 
He won't eat that which dies of itself or is torn by animals, defiling himself by it. I'm God. 
"'They will follow my commandment, lest they incur guilt for it and die in it, if they profane it. I'm God who sanctifies them. 
"'No stranger will eat of the holy thing: a foreigner living with the priests, or a hired servant, won't eat of the holy thing. 
But if a priest buys a slave, purchased by his money, he will eat of it; and those who are born in his house will eat of his bread. 
If a priest's daughter is married to an outsider, she won't eat of the heave offering of the holy things. 
But if a priest's daughter is a widow, or divorced, and has no child, and has returned to her father's house as in her youth, she can eat of her father's bread; but no stranger will eat any of it. 
"'If a man eats something holy unwittingly, then he will add the fifth part of its value to it, and will give the holy thing to the priest. 
The priests won't profane the holy things of the children of Israel, which they offer to God, 
and so cause them to bear the guilt that brings guilt when they eat their holy things; for I'm God who sanctifies them.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the children of Israel, and say to them, 'Whoever is of the house of Israel, or of the foreigners in Israel, who offers his offering, whether it's any of their vows or any of their free will offerings, which they offer to God for a burnt offering: 
that you can be accepted, you will offer a male without defect, of the bulls, of the sheep, or of the goats. 
But you won't offer whatever has a defect, for it won't be acceptable for you. 
Whoever offers a sacrifice of offerings for wholeness to God to accomplish a vow, or for a free will offering of the herd or of the flock, it will be perfect to be accepted. It will have no defect. 
Don't offer what's blind, is injured, is maimed, has a wart, is festering, or has a running sore to God, nor make an offering by fire of them on the altar to God. 
Either a bull or a lamb that has any deformity or lacking in his parts, that you can offer for a free will offering; but for a vow it won't be accepted. 
You must not offer to God that which has its testicles bruised, crushed, broken, or cut. You must not do this in your land. 
You must not offer any of these as the bread of your God from the hand of a foreigner, because their corruption is in them. There's a defect in them. They won't be accepted for you.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"When a bull, a sheep, or a goat is born, it will remain seven days with its mother. From the eighth day on it will be accepted for the offering of an offering made by fire to God. 
Whether it's a cow or ewe, you won't kill it and its young both in one day. 
"When you sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to God, you will sacrifice it so that you can be accepted. 
It will be eaten on the same day; you will leave none of it until the morning. I'm God. 
"So you will keep my commandments, and do them. I'm God. 
Don't profane my holy name, but I'll be made holy among the children of Israel. I'm God who makes you holy, 
who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God. I'm God."
