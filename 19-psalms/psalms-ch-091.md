He who dwells in the secret place of the Most High will rest in the shadow of the Almighty. 
I'll say of God, "He's my refuge and my fortress; my God, in whom I trust." 
He'll deliver you from the snare of the fowler, and from the deadly pestilence. 
He'll cover you with his feathers. Under his wings you'll take refuge. His faithfulness is your shield and rampart. 
You shall not be afraid of the terror by night, nor of the arrow that flies by day, 
nor of the pestilence that walks in darkness, nor of the destruction that wastes at noonday. 
A thousand may fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand; but it won't come near you. 
You'll only look with your eyes, and see the recompense of the guilty. 
Because you have made God your refuge, and the Most High your dwelling place, 
no bad shall happen to you, neither shall any plague come near your dwelling. 
He'll put his messengers in charge of you, to guard you in all your ways. 
They'll bear you up in their hands, so that you won't dash your foot against a stone. 
You'll tread on the lion and cobra. You'll trample the young lion and the serpent underfoot. 
"Because he has set his love on me, I'll deliver him. I'll set him on high, because he has known my name. 
He'll call on me, and I'll answer him. I'll be with him in trouble. I'll deliver him, and honor him. 
I'll satisfy him with long life, and show him my salvation."
