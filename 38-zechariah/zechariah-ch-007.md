In the fourth year of King Darius, God's word came to Zechariah in the fourth day of the ninth month, the month of Chislev. 
The people of Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem Melech and their men to entreat God's favor, 
and to speak to the priests of the house of the God of Armies and to the spokesmen, saying, "Should I weep in the fifth month, separating myself, as I have done these so many years?" 
Then the word of the God of Armies came to me, saying, 
"Speak to everyone in the land and to the priests, saying, 'When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and in the seventh month for these seventy years, did you at all fast to me, really to me? 
When you eat and when you drink, don't you eat for yourselves and drink for yourselves? 
Aren't these the words which God proclaimed by the former spokesmen when Jerusalem was inhabited and in prosperity, and its cities around her, and the South and the lowland were inhabited?'" 
God's word came to Zechariah, saying, 
"Thus has the God of Armies spoken, saying, 'Execute true judgment, and show kindness and compassion every man to his brother. 
Don't oppress the widow, the fatherless, the foreigner, nor the poor; and let none of you devise bad against his brother in your heart.' 
But they refused to listen, and turned their backs, and stopped their ears, that they might not hear. 
Yes, they made their hearts as hard as flint, lest they might hear the law and the words which the God of Armies had sent by his Spirit by the former spokesmen. So great wrath came from the God of Armies. 
It has come to pass that, as he called and they refused to listen, so they'll call and I won't listen," said the God of Armies; 
"but I'll scatter them with a whirlwind among all the nations which they haven't known. Thus the land was desolate after them, so that no man passed through nor returned; for they made the pleasant land desolate."
