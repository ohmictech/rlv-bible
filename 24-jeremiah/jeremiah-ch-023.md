"Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture!" says God. 
So the God of Israel says against the shepherds who feed my people: "You have scattered my flock, driven them away, and haven't visited them. Look, I'll visit on you the bad of your doings," says God. 
"I'll gather the remnant of my flock out of all the countries where I have driven them, and will bring them again to their folds; and they'll be fruitful and multiply. 
I'll set up shepherds over them who will feed them. They'll no longer be afraid or dismayed, neither will any be lacking," says God. 
"The days are coming," says God, "that I'll raise to David a righteous Branch; and he'll reign as king and deal wisely, and will execute justice and righteousness in the land. 
In his days Judah will be saved, and Israel will dwell safely. This is his name by which he'll be called: God our righteousness. 
"The days are coming," says God, "that they'll no more say, 'As God lives, who brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;' 
but, 'As God lives, who brought up and who led the offspring of the house of Israel out of the north country, and from all the countries where I had driven them.' Then they'll dwell in their own land." 
Concerning the spokesmen: My heart within me is broken. All my bones shake. I'm like a drunken man, and like a man whom wine has overcome, because of God, and because of his holy words. 
"The land is full of adulterers; for because of the curse the land mourns. The pastures of the wilderness have dried up. Their course is bad, and their might isn't right; 
for both spokesman and priest are profane. Yes, in my house I have found their guilt," says God. 
So their way will be to them as slippery places in the darkness. They'll be driven on, and fall therein; for I'll bring bad on them, even the year of their visitation," says God. 
"I have seen folly in the spokesmen of Samaria. They prophesied by Baal, and caused my people Israel to err. 
In the spokesmen of Jerusalem I have also seen a horrible thing: they commit adultery and walk in lies. They strengthen the hands of those who do bad, so that no one returns from his guilt. They have all become to me as Sodom, and its inhabitants as Gomorrah." 
So the God of Armies says concerning the spokesmen: "Look, I'll feed them with wormwood, and make them drink poisoned water; for from the spokesmen of Jerusalem ungodliness has gone out into all the land." 
The God of Armies says, "Don't listen to the words of the spokesmen who prophesy to you. They teach you vanity. They speak a vision of their own heart, and not out of the mouth of God. 
They say continually to those who despise me, 'God has said, "You'll have peace;"' and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his own heart they say, 'No bad will come on you.' 
Who has stood in the council of God, that he should perceive and hear his word? Who has listened to my word, and heard it? 
Look, God's storm, his wrath, has gone out. Yes, a whirling storm! It'll burst on the head of the guilty. 
God's anger won't return until he has executed and performed the intents of his heart. In the latter days, you'll understand it perfectly. 
I didn't send these spokesmen, yet they ran. I didn't speak to them, yet they prophesied. 
But if they had stood in my council, then they'd have caused my people to hear my words, and would have turned them from their bad way, and from the bad of their doings. 
"Am I a God at hand," says God, "and not a God afar off? 
Can anyone hide himself in secret places so that I can't see him?" says God. "Don't I fill sky and earth?" says God. 
"I have heard what the spokesmen have said, who prophesy lies in my name, saying, 'I had a dream! I had a dream!' 
How long will this be in the heart of the spokesmen who prophesy lies, even the spokesmen of the deceit of their own heart? 
They intend to cause my people to forget my name by their dreams which they each tell his neighbor, as their fathers forgot my name because of Baal. 
The spokesman who has a dream, let him tell a dream; and he who has my word, let him speak my word faithfully. What's the straw to the wheat?" says God. 
"Isn't my word like fire?" says God; "and like a hammer that breaks the rock in pieces? 
"Look, I'm against the spokesmen," says God, "who each steal my words from his neighbor. 
Look, I'm against the spokesmen," says God, "who use their tongues, and say, 'He says.' 
Look, I'm against those who prophesy lying dreams," says God, "who tell them, and cause my people to err by their lies, and by their vain boasting; yet I didn't send them or command them. They don't profit this people at all," says God. 
"When this people, or the spokesman, or a priest, asks you, saying, 'What's the message from God?' Then you will tell them, '"What message? I'll cast you off," says God.' 
As for the spokesman, the priest, and the people, who say, 'The message from God,' I'll even punish that man and his household. 
You'll say everyone to his neighbor, and everyone to his brother, 'What has God answered?' and, 'What has God said?' 
You'll mention the message from God no more, for every man's own word has become his message; for you have perverted the words of the living God, of the God of Armies, our God. 
You'll say to the spokesman, 'What has God answered you?' and, 'What has God spoken?' 
Although you say, 'The message from God,' God says: 'Because you say this word, "The message from God," and I have sent to you, telling you not to say, "The message from God," 
Look, I'll utterly forget you, and I'll cast you off with the city that I gave to you and to your fathers, away from my presence. 
I'll bring a perpetual reproach on you, and a perpetual shame, which won't be forgotten.'"
