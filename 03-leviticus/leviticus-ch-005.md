"'If anyone misses the mark, in that he hears a public adjuration to testify, he being a witness, whether he has seen or known, if he doesn't report it, then he shall bear his guilt. 
"'Or if anyone touches any unclean thing, whether it is the carcass of an unclean animal, or the carcass of unclean livestock, or the carcass of unclean creeping things, and it is hidden from him, and he is unclean, then he shall be guilty. 
"'Or if he touches the uncleanness of man, whatever his uncleanness is with which he is unclean, and it is hidden from him; when he knows of it, then he shall be guilty. 
"'Or if anyone swears rashly with his lips to do bad things or to do good, whatever it is that a man might utter rashly with an oath, and it is hidden from him, when he knows of it, then he will be guilty of one of these. 
It shall be, when he is guilty of one of these, he shall confess that in which he has missed the mark; 
and he shall bring his trespass offering to God for his mistake which he has missed the mark: a female from the flock, a lamb or a goat, for an offering for mistakes; and the priest shall make things right for him concerning his mistake. 
"'If he can't afford a lamb, then he shall bring his trespass offering for that in which he has missed the mark, two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, to God; one for an offering for mistakes, and the other for a burnt offering. 
He shall bring them to the priest, who shall first offer the one which is for the offering for mistakes. He shall wring off its head from its neck, but shall not sever it completely. 
He shall sprinkle some of the blood of the offering for mistakes on the side of the altar; and the rest of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar. It is an offering for mistakes. 
He shall offer the second for a burnt offering, according to the ordinance; and the priest shall make things right for him concerning his mistake which he has missed the mark, and he shall be forgiven. 
"'But if he can't afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring as his offering for that in which he has missed the mark, one tenth of an ephah of fine flour for an offering for mistakes. He shall put no oil on it, and he shall not put any frankincense on it, for it is an offering for mistakes. 
He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it as the memorial portion, and burn it on the altar, on the offerings of God made by fire. It is an offering for mistakes. 
The priest shall make things right for him concerning his mistake that he has missed the mark in any of these things, and he will be forgiven; and the rest shall be the priest's, as the meal offering.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"If anyone commits a trespass, and misses the mark unwittingly regarding God's holy things, then he shall bring his trespass offering to God: a ram without defect from the flock, according to your estimation in silver by shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass offering. 
He shall make restitution for that which he has done wrong regarding the holy thing, and shall add a fifth part to it, and give it to the priest; and the priest shall make things right for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and he will be forgiven. 
"If anyone misses the mark, doing any of the things which God has commanded not to be done, though he didn't know it, he is still guilty, and shall bear his guilt. 
He shall bring a ram without defect from of the flock, according to your estimation, for a trespass offering, to the priest; and the priest shall make things right for him concerning the thing in which he missed the mark and didn't know it, and he will be forgiven. 
It is a trespass offering. He is certainly guilty before God."
