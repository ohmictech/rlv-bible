God spoke to Moses: 
"Look, I have chosen Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, from the tribe of Judah. 
I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with skill, understanding, knowledge and every kind of craftsmanship, 
to design works of skill, to work in gold, silver and bronze, 
and in cutting stones for setting and carving wood, in every kind of craftsmanship. 
And I have appointed Oholiab son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, to work with him, and I have given skill to every skilled craftsman, so they can make everything I have commanded you: 
the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the covenant, the mercy seat on it, all the furnishings of the tent, 
the table and its equipment, the pure lampstand with all its equipment, the altar of incense, 
the altar of burnt offering with all its equipment, the basin and its stand, 
the woven garments, the set apart garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments for his sons to serve as priests, 
the anointing oil and the fragrant incense for the Holy Place. They are to make everything just as I have commanded you." 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Tell the Israelites, 'You must keep my Sabbaths, because the Sabbath is a sign between me and you through all your generations, so you will know that I am God, who sets you apart. 
Keep the Sabbath, because it is holy to you. Anyone who treats it as ordinary must be put to death, and whoever does any work on it will be cut off from his people. 
Work is to be done for six days, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, holy to God. Whoever does any work on the Sabbath day must be put to death. 
The Israelites are to keep the Sabbath, observing it through all their generations as a lasting covenant. 
It is a sign between me and the Israelites for the eras, because in six days God made the sky and the earth, and on the seventh day he rested and was refreshed.'" 
When he had finished speaking with Moses on Mount Sinai, he gave him the two tablets of the covenant, stone tablets written by God's own finger.
