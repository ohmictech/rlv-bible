God says: "The sky is my throne, and the earth is my footstool. What kind of house will you build to me? Where will I rest? 
My hand has made all these things, and so all these things came to be," says God: "but I'll look to this man, even to he who's poor and of a contrite spirit, and who trembles at my word. 
He who kills an ox is as he who kills a man; he who sacrifices a lamb, as he who breaks a dog's neck; he who offers an offering, as he who offers pig's blood; he who burns frankincense, as he who blesses an idol. Yes, they have chosen their own ways, and their soul delights in their abominations. 
I also will choose their delusions, and will bring their fears on them, because when I called, no one answered; when I spoke, they didn't listen, but they did that which was bad in my eyes, and chose that in which I didn't delight." 
Hear God's word, you who tremble at his word: "Your brothers who hate you, who cast you out for my name's sake, have said, 'Let God be glorified, that we can see your joy;' but it's those who will be disappointed. 
A voice of tumult from the city, a voice from the temple, a voice of God that repays his enemies what they deserve. 
"Before she travailed, she gave birth. Before her pain came, she delivered a son. 
Who has heard of such a thing? Who has seen such things? Will a land be born in one day? Will a nation be born at once? As soon as Zion travailed, she gave birth to her children. 
Will I bring to the birth, and not cause to be delivered?" says God. "Will I who cause to give birth shut the womb?" says your God. 
"Rejoice with Jerusalem, and be glad for her, all you who love her. Rejoice for joy with her, all you who mourn over her; 
that you can nurse and be satisfied at the comforting breasts; that you can drink deeply, and be delighted with the abundance of her glory." 
God says, "Look, I'll extend peace to her like a river, and the glory of the nations like an overflowing stream, and you'll nurse. You'll be carried on her side, and will be dandled on her knees. 
As one whom his mother comforts, so I'll comfort you. You'll be comforted in Jerusalem." 
You'll see it, and your heart will rejoice, and your bones will flourish like the tender grass. God's hand will be known among his servants; and he'll have indignation against his enemies. 
For, look, God will come with fire, and his chariots will be like the whirlwind; to give his anger with fierceness, and his rebuke with flames of fire. 
God will execute judgment by fire and by his sword on all flesh; and those slain by God will be many. 
"Those who sanctify themselves and purify themselves to go to the gardens, following one in the middle, eating pig's meat, abominable things, and the mouse, they will come to an end together," says God. 
"I know their works and their thoughts. The time comes that I'll gather all nations and languages, and they'll come, and will see my glory. 
"I'll set a sign among them, and I'll send those who escape of them to the nations, to Tarshish, Pul, and Lud, who draw the bow, to Tubal and Javan, to far-away islands, who haven't heard my fame, nor have seen my glory; and they will declare my glory among the nations. 
They will bring all your brothers out of all the nations for an offering to God, on horses, in chariots, in litters, on mules, and on camels, to my holy mountain Jerusalem, says God, as the children of Israel bring their offering in a clean vessel into God's house. 
Of them I'll also select priests and Levites," says God. 
"As the new skies and the new earth, which I'll make, will remain before me," says God, "so your offspring and your name will remain. 
It will happen that from one new moon to another, and from one Sabbath to another, all flesh will come to worship before me," says God. 
"They'll go out, and look at the dead bodies of the men who have transgressed against me; for their ruin is never undone, and their fire can't be interrupted, and everyone will be sickened by the sight of them."
