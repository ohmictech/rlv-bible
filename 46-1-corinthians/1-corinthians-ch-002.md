When I came to you, brothers, I didn't come with great speech or wisdom, announcing to you the witness of God. 
For I decided not to know anything among you except Jesus Christ, and him crucified. 
I was with you in weakness, in fear, and with much trembling. 
My speech and my announcing were not in persuasive words of human wisdom, but in a demonstration of the Spirit and of power, 
so that your trust wouldn't stand in the wisdom of people, but in the power of God. 
We do speak wisdom, however, among those who are grown up, yet a wisdom not of this world nor of the rulers of this world, who are coming to nothing. 
But we speak God's wisdom in a secret, the wisdom that has been hidden, which God marked out ahead of time before the eras for our weight, 
which none of the rulers of this world has known. For if they had known it, they wouldn't have crucified the Lord of weight. 
But as it is written, "Things no eye saw, and no ear heard, and that never entered a human heart, these God has prepared for those who love him." 
But God has unveiled them to us through the Spirit. For the Spirit searches all things, yes, the deep things of God. 
For who among people knows the things of a person except the spirit of the person within him? Even so, no one knows the things of God except God's Spirit. 
But we didn't receive the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, so that we might know the things freely given to us by God. 
We also speak these things, not in words that human wisdom teaches, but in words the Holy Spirit teaches, comparing spiritual things with spiritual things. 
Now the natural person doesn't receive the things of God's Spirit, for they are foolishness to him, and he can't know them, because they are spiritually examined. 
But the one who is spiritual examines all things, and he himself is examined by no one. 
"For who has known the mind of the Lord that he should instruct him?" But we have Christ's mind.
