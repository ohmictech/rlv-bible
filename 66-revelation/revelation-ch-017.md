One of the seven messengers who had the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, "Come here. I'll show you the verdict on the great prostitute who sits on many waters. 
The kings of the land slept around with her, and those who live on the land got drunk on the wine of her sleeping around." 
He carried me away in the Spirit into a desert. I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet beast that was covered with names of slander and had seven heads and ten horns. 
The woman was dressed in purple and scarlet and covered with gold, precious stones, and pearls. In her hand she held a golden cup full of hateful things and the filth of the land's sleeping around. 
On her forehead a name was written: "SECRET, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF PROSTITUTES AND OF THE HATEFUL THINGS OF THE LAND." 
I saw that the woman was drunk with the blood of the set-apart ones and with the blood of those who bore witness to Jesus. When I saw her, I was greatly amazed. 
The messenger said to me, "Why are you amazed? I'll tell you the secret of the woman and of the beast that carries her, which has the seven heads and the ten horns. 
The beast you saw was, and isn't, and is about to come up out of the deep and go to its ruin. Those who live on the land, whose names haven't been written in the book of real life since the founding of the world, will be amazed when they see the beast, because it was, and isn't, and is yet to come. 
This calls for a mind with wisdom. The seven heads are seven hills that the woman sits on. 
They're also seven kings. Five have fallen, one is now, and the other hasn't come yet. When he does come, he must stay for a little while. 
The beast that was and isn't is itself an eighth king. It belongs to the seven, and it goes to its ruin. 
The ten horns you saw are ten kings who haven't yet received a kingdom, but they receive authority as kings along with the beast for one hour. 
These all share one purpose, and they hand their power and authority over to the beast. 
They'll make war on the Lamb, and the Lamb will win the victory over them, because he's Lord of lords and King of kings, and those with him are called, chosen, and trustworthy." 
He said to me, "The waters you saw, where the prostitute sits, are peoples, crowds, nations, and languages. 
The ten horns you saw, they and the beast will hate the prostitute. They'll leave her empty, strip her naked, eat her flesh, and burn her up with fire. 
God has put it into their hearts to carry out his purpose by acting with one purpose and handing their kingdom over to the beast, until God's words are carried out. 
The woman you saw is the great city that rules over the kings of the land."
