So let a person think of us as Christ's servants and managers of God's secrets. 
Here, moreover, what is required of managers is that they be found trustworthy. 
But with me it matters very little that I should be examined by you, or by any human court. Yes, I don't even examine my own self. 
I know nothing against myself. Yet I'm not made right by this, but the one who examines me is the Lord. 
So pass no verdict before the time, until the Lord comes, who will both bring to light the hidden things of darkness and make plain the purposes of hearts. Then each person will get his praise from God. 
Now these things, brothers, I have applied to myself and Apollos as an example for your sakes, so that in us you might learn not to go beyond the things which are written, so that none of you is puffed up in favor of one against another. 
Who makes you different? And what do you have that you didn't receive? But if you did receive it, why do you boast as if you hadn't received it? 
You are already filled. You have already become rich. You have come to reign without us. Yes, and I wish that you did reign, that we also might reign with you! 
I think that God has put us, the sent ones, on display last of all, like people sentenced to death. We have become a spectacle to the world, both to messengers and to people. 
We are fools for Christ's sake, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You have honor, but we have dishonor. 
Even to this present hour we hunger, thirst, are naked, are beaten, and have no place to live. 
We toil, working with our own hands. When people curse us, we bless. When we are hunted down, we stick it out. 
When we are slandered, we speak kindly. We have become the scum of the world, the dirt wiped off by all, even until now. 
I don't write these things to shame you, but to warn you as my truly loved children. 
Though you have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you don't have many fathers. In Christ Jesus, I became your father through the Good News. 
I beg you, follow my example. 
Because of this I have sent Timothy to you, who is my truly loved and trustworthy child in the Lord, who will remind you of my ways in Christ, just as I teach everywhere in every gathering. 
Now some are puffed up, as though I weren't coming to you. 
But I will come to you shortly, if the Lord is willing. And I will know, not the word of those who are puffed up, but the power. 
God's reign isn't in word, but in power. 
What do you want? Should I come to you with a rod, or in love and a spirit of gentleness?
