Who's like the wise man? And who knows the interpretation of a thing? A man's wisdom makes his face shine, and the hardness of his face is changed. 
I say, "Keep the king's command!" because of the oath to God. 
Don't be hasty to go out of his presence. Don't persist in a bad thing, for he does whatever pleases him, 
for the king's word is supreme. Who can say to him, "What are you doing?" 
Whoever keeps the commandment won't come to harm, and his wise heart will know the time and procedure. 
There's a time and procedure for every purpose, although the misery of man is heavy on him. 
He doesn't know that which will be; for who can tell him how it'll be? 
There's no man who has power over the spirit to contain the spirit; neither does he have power over the day of death. There's no discharge in war; neither will guilt deliver those who practice it. 
All this I have seen, and applied my mind to every work that's done under the sun. There's a time in which one man has power over another to his hurt. 
So I saw the guilty buried. Indeed they came also from holiness. They went and were forgotten in the city where they did this. This also is vanity. 
Because sentence against a bad work isn't executed speedily, the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do bad things. 
Though someone who drifts commits crimes a hundred times, and lives long, yet surely I know that it'll be better with those who fear God, who are reverent before him. 
But it won't be well with the guilty, neither will he lengthen days like a shadow, because he doesn't fear God. 
There's a vanity which is done on the earth, that there are righteous men to whom it happens according to the work of the guilty. Again, there are guilty men to whom it happens according to the work of the righteous. I said that this also is vanity. 
Then I commended mirth, because a man has no better thing under the sun than to eat, to drink, and to be joyful: for that will accompany him in his labor all the days of his life which God has given him under the sun. 
When I applied my heart to know wisdom, and to see the business that's done on the earth (even though eyes see no sleep day or night), 
then I saw all the work of God, that man can't find out the work that's done under the sun, because however much a man labors to seek it out, yet he won't find it. Yes even though a wise man thinks he can comprehend it, he won't be able to find it.
