God said to Moses, "I have made you like God to Pharaoh, and your brother Aaron will be your spokesman. 
Tell Aaron everything I command you, and he will speak to Pharaoh so that he lets the Israelites leave his land. 
But I will make Pharaoh's heart stubborn and multiply my signs and wonders in Egypt. 
Pharaoh won't listen to you, so I will lay my hand on Egypt and bring my people the Israelites out of Egypt like an army, by great acts of judgment. 
And the Egyptians will know that I am God when I stretch out my hand over Egypt and bring the Israelites out from among them." 
Moses and Aaron did exactly as God commanded them. 
Moses was eighty years old and Aaron was eighty-three when they spoke to Pharaoh. 
God said to Moses and Aaron, 
"When Pharaoh says to you, 'Prove it with a miracle,' tell Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down in front of Pharaoh, and it will become a snake.'" 
So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and did as God had commanded. Aaron threw his staff down in front of Pharaoh and his officials, and it became a snake. 
Then Pharaoh sent for the wise men and the sorcerers, and the magicians of Egypt did the same thing with their secret arts. 
Each of them threw down his staff and it became a snake, but Aaron's staff swallowed up theirs. 
Pharaoh's heart stayed hard and he wouldn't listen, just as God had said. 
God said to Moses, "Pharaoh is stubborn. He refuses to let the people go. 
Go to Pharaoh in the morning. He goes out to the river, so stand on the riverbank to meet him, and take in your hand the staff that turned into a snake. 
Then say to him, 'The God of the Hebrews sent me to you to say, "Let my people go so they can serve me in the wilderness." Up to now you have not listened. 
God says this: "This is how you will know that I am God. I am going to strike the water in the river with the staff in my hand, and it will turn to blood. 
The fish in the river will die and the river will stink, and the Egyptians will not be able to drink from it."'" 
God said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch your hand out over the waters of Egypt, over its rivers, canals, ponds and every pool of water, and they will turn to blood. There will be blood all through Egypt, even in the wooden and stone jars.'" 
Moses and Aaron did as God commanded. In full view of Pharaoh and his officials, Moses raised the staff and struck the water in the river, and all of it turned to blood. 
The fish in the river died, the river stank, and the Egyptians couldn't drink from it. There was blood all through Egypt. 
But the magicians of Egypt did the same thing with their secret arts, and Pharaoh's heart stayed hard and he wouldn't listen, just as God had said. 
Pharaoh just turned and went home, and didn't give it a second thought. 
The Egyptians dug along the river for water to drink, because they couldn't drink from the river. 
And seven days passed after God struck the river.
