God's word came to me again, saying, 
"What do you mean, that you use this proverb concerning the land of Israel, saying, 'The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge'? 
"As I live," says God, "you won't use this proverb any more in Israel. 
Look, every living person is mine, father and son alike. The one who drifts is the one who dies. 
"But if a man is just, and does that which is lawful and right, 
and hasn't eaten on the mountains, hasn't lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, hasn't defiled his neighbor's wife, hasn't come near a woman in her impurity, 
and hasn't wronged any, but has restored to the debtor his pledge, has taken nothing by robbery, has given his bread to the hungry, and has covered the naked with a garment; 
he who hasn't lent to them with interest, hasn't taken any increase from them, who has withdrawn his hand from guilt, has executed true justice between man and man, 
has walked in my statutes, and has kept my ordinances, to deal truly; he's just, he will surely live," says God. 
"If he fathers a son who's a robber who sheds blood, and who does any one of these things, 
or who doesn't do any of those things but has eaten at the mountain shrines and defiled his neighbor's wife, 
has wronged the poor and needy, has taken by robbery, hasn't restored the pledge, and has lifted up his eyes to the idols, has committed abomination, 
has lent with interest, and has taken increase from the poor, will he then live? He won't live. He has done all these abominations. He will surely die. His blood will be on him. 
"Now, look, if he fathers a son who sees all his father's mistakes which he has done, and fears, and doesn't do likewise, 
who hasn't eaten on the mountains, hasn't lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, hasn't defiled his neighbor's wife, 
hasn't wronged any, hasn't taken anything to pledge, hasn't taken by robbery, but has given his bread to the hungry, and has covered the naked with a garment; 
who has withdrawn his hand from the poor, who hasn't received interest or increase, has executed my ordinances, has walked in my statutes; he won't die for the guilt of his father. He will surely live. 
As for his father, because he cruelly oppressed, robbed his brother, and did that which isn't good among his people, look, he'll die in his guilt. 
"Yet you say, 'Why doesn't the son bear the guilt of the father?' When the son has done that which is lawful and right, and has kept all my statutes, and has done them, he'll surely live. 
The one who drifts is the one who dies. The son won't bear the guilt of the father, neither will the father bear the guilt of the son. The righteousness of the righteous will be on him, and the guilt of the guilty will be on him. 
"But if the guilty turns from all his mistakes that he has committed, and keeps all my statutes, and does that which is lawful and right, he will surely live. He won't die. 
None of his rebellion that he has committed will be remembered against him. In his righteousness that he has done, he will live. 
Have I any pleasure in the death of the guilty?" says God, "and not rather that he should return from his way, and live? 
"But when the righteous turns away from his righteousness, and commits guilt, and does according to all the abominations that the guilty man does, should he live? None of his righteous deeds that he has done will be remembered. In his trespass that he has trespassed, and in the wrong he has done, in them he will die. 
"Yet you say, 'The way of the Lord isn't equal.' Hear now, house of Israel: Is my way not equal? Aren't your ways unequal? 
When the righteous man turns away from his righteousness, and commits guilt, and dies in it, then he dies in his guilt that he has done. 
Again, when the guilty man turns away from his guilt that he has committed, and does that which is lawful and right, he'll save his soul alive. 
Because he considers, and turns away from all his rebellion that he has committed, he will surely live. He won't die. 
Yet the house of Israel says, 'The way of the Lord isn't fair.' House of Israel, aren't my ways fair? Aren't your ways unfair? 
"So I'll judge you, house of Israel, everyone according to his ways," says God. "Return, and turn yourselves from all your rebellion, so guilt won't be your ruin. 
Cast away from you all your rebellion in which you have transgressed; and make yourself a new heart and a new spirit. Why will you die, house of Israel? 
I have no pleasure in the death of him who dies," says God. "So turn yourselves, and live!
