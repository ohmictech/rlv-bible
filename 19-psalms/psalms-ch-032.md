By David. A contemplative psalm. Blessed is he whose disobedience is forgiven, whose wrongdoing is covered. 
Blessed is the man to whom God doesn't impute guilt, in whose spirit there's no deceit. 
When I kept silence, my bones wasted away through my groaning all day long. 
Day and night your hand was heavy on me. My strength was sapped in the heat of summer. Selah. 
I acknowledged my wrongdoing to you. I didn't hide my guilt. I said, I'll confess my rebellion to God, and you forgave the guilt of my wrongdoing. Selah. 
This, let everyone who's godly pray to you in a time when you may be found. Surely when the great waters overflow, they won't reach to him. 
You're my hiding place. You'll preserve me from trouble. You'll surround me with songs of deliverance. Selah. 
I'll instruct you and teach you in the way which you will go. I'll counsel you with my eye on you. 
Don't be like the horse, or like the mule, which have no understanding, who are controlled by bit and bridle, or else they won't come near to you. 
Many sorrows come to the guilty, but loving kindness will surround him who trusts in God. 
Be glad in God, and rejoice, you righteous! Shout for joy, all you who are upright in heart!
