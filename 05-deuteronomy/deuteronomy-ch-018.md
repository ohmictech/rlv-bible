"The priests, the whole tribe of Levi, will have no share or inheritance with Israel. They will eat the offerings made by fire to God, which are his portion. 
They will have no inheritance among their brothers. God is their inheritance, as he told them. 
This is what the priests are owed by the people, by those who offer a sacrifice, ox or sheep: the shoulder, the two cheeks and the inner parts. 
You must give him the firstfruits of your grain, your new wine and your oil, and the first wool from your sheep, 
because your God has chosen him out of all your tribes to stand and serve in the name of God, him and his sons for the eras. 
If a Levite leaves any of your towns anywhere in Israel where he lives, and comes with all his heart's desire to the place God will choose, 
then he may serve in the name of his God like all his brothers the Levites who stand there before God. 
He will get an equal share to eat, besides what he gets from the sale of his family property. 
When you come into the land your God is giving you, don't learn to copy the disgusting practices of those nations. 
There must not be anyone among you who passes his son or daughter through the fire, or who practices divination, or tells fortunes, or reads omens, or works sorcery, 
or casts spells, or consults the spirits of the dead, or is a medium, or asks the dead for advice. 
Whoever does these things is disgusting to God, and it is because of these disgusting practices that your God is driving them out ahead of you. 
You must be blameless before your God. 
Those nations you are taking over listen to fortune tellers and diviners, but your God hasn't allowed you to do that. 
Your God will raise up a prophet for you from among your brothers, like me. You must listen to him. 
This is what you asked your God for at Horeb on the day of the gathering, when you said, 'Don't let me hear the voice of my God again, and don't let me see this great fire anymore, or I will die.' 
God said to me, 'What they have said is good. 
I will raise up a prophet for them from among their brothers, like you. I will put my words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him. 
Whoever won't listen to my words that he speaks in my name, I myself will hold him accountable. 
But a prophet who dares to speak in my name a word I haven't commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must die.' 
You may say to yourself, 'How will we know when a word hasn't come from God?' 
When a prophet speaks in God's name and the thing doesn't happen or come true, that is a word God hasn't spoken. The prophet has spoken it on his own. Don't be afraid of him.
