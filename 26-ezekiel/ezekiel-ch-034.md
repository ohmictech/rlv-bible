God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy, and tell them, even the shepherds, 'The God says: "Woe to the shepherds of Israel who feed themselves! Shouldn't the shepherds feed the sheep? 
You eat the fat. You clothe yourself with the wool. You kill the fatlings, but you don't feed the sheep. 
You haven't strengthened the diseased. You haven't healed that which was sick. You haven't bound up that which was broken. You haven't brought back that which was driven away. You haven't sought that which was lost, but you have ruled over them with force and with rigor. 
They were scattered, because there was no shepherd. They became food to all the animals of the field, and were scattered. 
My sheep wandered through all the mountains and on every high hill. Yes, my sheep were scattered on all the surface of the earth. There was no one who searched or sought." 
"'So, you shepherds, hear God's word: 
"As I live," says God, "surely because my sheep became a prey, and my sheep became food to all the animals of the field, because there was no shepherd, and my shepherds didn't search for my sheep, but the shepherds fed themselves, and didn't feed my sheep, 
So, you shepherds, hear God's word!" 
The God says: "Look, I'm against the shepherds. I'll require my sheep at their hand, and cause them to cease from feeding the sheep. The shepherds won't feed themselves any more. I'll deliver my sheep from their mouth, that they can't be food for them." 
"'God says: "Look, I myself, even I, will search for my sheep, and will seek them out. 
As a shepherd seeks out his flock in the day that he's among his sheep that are scattered abroad, so I'll seek out my sheep. I'll deliver them out of all places where they have been scattered in the cloudy and dark day. 
I'll bring them out from the peoples, and gather them from the countries, and will bring them into their own land. I'll feed them on the mountains of Israel, by the watercourses, and in all the inhabited places of the country. 
I'll feed them with good pasture, and their fold will be on the mountains of the height of Israel. There they'll lie down in a good fold. They'll feed on rich pasture on the mountains of Israel. 
I myself will be the shepherd of my sheep, and I'll cause them to lie down," says God. 
"I'll seek that which was lost, and will bring back that which was driven away, and will bind up that which was broken, and will strengthen that which was sick; but I'll destroy the fat and the strong. I'll feed them in justice."' 
"As for you, O my flock, God says: 'Look, I judge between sheep and sheep, the rams and the male goats. 
Does it seem a small thing to you to have fed on the good pasture, but you must tread down with your feet the residue of your pasture? And to have drunk of the clear waters, but must you foul the residue with your feet? 
As for my sheep, they eat that which you have trodden with your feet, and they drink that which you have fouled with your feet.' 
"So God says to them: 'Look, I, even I, will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep. 
Because you thrust with side and with shoulder, and push all the diseased with your horns, until you have scattered them abroad, 
So I'll save my flock, and they'll no more be a prey. I'll judge between sheep and sheep. 
I'll set up one shepherd over them, and he'll feed them, even my servant David. He'll feed them, and he'll be their shepherd. 
I, God, will be their God, and my servant David prince among them. I, God, have spoken it. 
"'I'll make with them a covenant of peace, and will cause bad animals to cease out of the land. They'll dwell securely in the wilderness and sleep in the woods. 
I'll make them and the places around my hill a blessing. I'll cause the shower to come down in its season. There will be showers of blessing. 
The tree of the field will yield its fruit, and the earth will yield its increase, and they'll be secure in their land. Then they'll know that I'm God, when I have broken the bars of their yoke, and have delivered them out of the hand of those who made slaves of them. 
They'll no more be a prey to the nations, neither will the animals of the earth devour them; but they'll dwell securely, and no one will make them afraid. 
I'll raise up to them a plantation for renown, and they'll no more be consumed with famine in the land, and not bear the shame of the nations any more. 
They'll know that I, God, their God am with them, and that they, the house of Israel, are my people, says God. 
You my sheep, the sheep of my pasture, are men, and I'm your God,' says God."
