He said to them, "Truly I tell you, there are some standing here who will in no way taste death until they see God's reign come with power." 
After six days Jesus took Peter, James, and John with him, and brought them up a high mountain privately by themselves, and he was changed into another form in front of them. 
His clothing became glistening, dazzling white, like snow, such as no one on earth could bleach them. 
Elijah and Moses appeared to them, and they were talking with Jesus. 
Peter answered Jesus, "Rabbi, it's good for us to be here. Let's make three tents: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah." 
He didn't know what to say, because they were very afraid. 
A cloud came, overshadowing them, and a voice came out of the cloud, "This is my Son, whom I truly love. Listen to him." 
Suddenly they looked around and saw no one with them anymore, except Jesus alone. 
As they were coming down from the mountain, he commanded them to tell no one what they had seen, until after the Human One had been raised from the dead. 
They kept this saying to themselves, questioning what "being raised from the dead" meant. 
They asked him, "Why do the Bible teachers say that Elijah must come first?" 
He said to them, "Elijah does come first and restores all things. And how is it written about the Human One, that he should suffer many things and be despised? 
But I tell you that Elijah has come, and they have also done to him whatever they wanted to, even as it's written about him." 
Coming to the students, he saw a great crowd around them, and Bible teachers questioning them. 
Immediately all the crowd, when they saw him, were greatly amazed, and they ran to him and greeted him. 
He asked the Bible teachers, "What are you arguing with them about?" 
One of the crowd answered, "Teacher, I brought my son to you. He has a spirit that keeps him from speaking. 
And wherever it seizes him, it throws him down, and he foams at the mouth, grinds his teeth, and becomes rigid. I asked your students to drive it out, and they couldn't." 
He answered him, "You generation with no trust, how long must I be with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring him to me." 
They brought him to him, and when the spirit saw him, it immediately threw him into convulsions, and he fell on the ground, rolling around and foaming at the mouth. 
He asked his father, "How long has this been happening to him?" He said, "Since childhood. 
Often it has thrown him both into fire and into water to kill him. But if you can do anything, have compassion on us and help us." 
Jesus said to him, "If you can trust, all things are possible for the one who trusts." 
Immediately the child's father cried out with tears, "I do trust. Help my lack of trust!" 
When Jesus saw that a crowd came running together, he rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, "You spirit that keeps him from speaking and hearing, I command you, come out of him, and never enter him again!" 
After crying out and throwing him into terrible convulsions, it came out of him. The boy became like one dead, so that most of them said, "He's dead." 
But Jesus took him by the hand and lifted him up, and he stood up. 
When he had gone into the house, his students asked him privately, "Why couldn't we drive it out?" 
He said to them, "This kind can come out only by prayer and going without food." 
They went out from there and passed through Galilee. He didn't want anyone to know it, 
for he was teaching his students and said to them, "The Human One is being handed over into the hands of people, and they'll kill him, and after he's killed, he'll rise again on the third day." 
But they didn't understand the saying, and were afraid to ask him. 
He came to Capernaum, and when he was in the house he asked them, "What were you arguing about on the way?" 
But they were silent, for they had argued with one another on the way about who was the greatest. 
He sat down and called the twelve, and said to them, "If anyone wants to be first, he must be last of all and slave of all." 
He took a little child and set him in the middle of them. Taking him in his arms, he said to them, 
"Whoever welcomes one such little child in my name welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me doesn't welcome me, but the one who sent me." 
John said to him, "Teacher, we saw someone who doesn't follow us driving out unclean spirits in your name, and we told him to stop, because he doesn't follow us." 
But Jesus said, "Don't stop him, because no one who does a powerful work in my name will soon speak maliciously of me. 
Whoever isn't against us is on our side. 
Whoever gives you a cup of water to drink in my name because you belong to Christ, truly I tell you, he'll in no way lose his earned result. 
"Whoever causes one of these little ones who trust in me to be trapped would be better off to have a millstone hung around his neck and be thrown into the sea. 
If your hand traps you, cut it off. It's better for you to enter real life maimed than to have two hands and go into Gehenna, into the fire that can't be interrupted, 
'where their worm doesn't die, and the fire isn't put out.' 
If your foot traps you, cut it off. It's better for you to enter real life lame than to have two feet and be thrown into Gehenna, into the fire that can't be interrupted, 
'where their worm doesn't die, and the fire isn't put out.' 
If your eye traps you, throw it out. It's better for you to enter God's reign with one eye than to have two eyes and be thrown into the Gehenna of fire, 
'where their worm doesn't die, and the fire isn't put out.' 
Everyone will be salted with fire, and every sacrifice will be seasoned with salt. 
Salt is good, but if the salt has lost its saltiness, what will you season it with? Have salt in yourselves, and keep wholeness with one another."
