"Don't pass a verdict, so that you won't be given a verdict. 
With whatever verdict you give, you'll be given a verdict. And with whatever measure you measure, it'll be measured to you. 
Why do you see the speck that's in your brother's eye, but don't consider the beam that's in your own eye? 
Or how can you say to your brother, 'Let me take the speck out of your eye,' when there's a beam in your own eye? 
You pretender! First remove the beam out of your own eye, and then you can see clearly to remove the speck out of your brother's eye. 
"Don't give what's set apart to the dogs, and don't throw your pearls in front of the pigs, or they'll trample them under their feet, and turn and tear you to pieces. 
"Ask, and it'll be given to you. Seek, and you'll find. Knock, and it'll be opened for you. 
Everyone who asks receives. He who seeks finds. To him who knocks it'll be opened. 
Or who's there among you who, if his son asks him for bread, will give him a stone? 
Or if he asks for a fish, who will give him a snake? 
If you then, being malicious, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in the sky give good things to those who ask him! 
So, whatever you want people to do to you, you'll also do to them, for this is the law and the prophets. 
"Enter through the narrow gate. The gate is wide and the road is broad that leads to ruin, and there are many who enter through it. 
How narrow is the gate, and how hard is the road that leads to real life! There are few who find it. 
"Beware of false spokesmen, who come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly are ravenous wolves. 
By their fruits you'll know them. Do you gather grapes from thorns or figs from thistles? 
Even so, every good tree produces good fruit, but the rotten tree produces bad fruit. 
A good tree can't produce bad fruit, neither can a rotten tree produce good fruit. 
Every tree that doesn't produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire. 
So by their fruits you'll know them. 
"Not everyone who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter the reign of the sky, but only the one who does the will of my Father in the sky. 
Many will tell me in that day, 'Lord, Lord, didn't we speak for God in your name, in your name drive out unclean spirits, and in your name do many powerful works?' 
Then I'll tell them, 'I never knew you. Get away from me, you who live as if there were no law.' 
"So everyone who hears these words of mine and does them is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. 
The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house, and it didn't fall, for it was founded on the rock. 
Everyone who hears these words of mine and doesn't do them will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand. 
The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house, and it fell, and its fall was great." 
When Jesus had finished saying these things, the crowds were astonished at his teaching, 
for he taught them with authority, and not like the Bible teachers.
