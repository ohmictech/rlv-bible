"To the messenger of the gathering in Ephesus write: These are the words of the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand, who walks among the seven golden lampstands: 
I know your deeds, your hard work, and your staying power. I know that you can't put up with bad people, and that you tested those who call themselves sent ones but aren't, and found them to be liars. 
You have staying power, and you have endured for my name's sake without growing tired. 
But I have this against you: you have left the love you had at first. 
Remember how far you have fallen. Have a change of heart and do the deeds you did at first. If you don't, I'll come to you and move your lampstand out of its place, unless you have a change of heart. 
But you have this in your favor: you hate the deeds of the Nicolaitans, and I hate them too. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings. To the one who wins the victory I'll give the right to eat from the tree of real life, which is in the paradise of my God. 
"To the messenger of the gathering in Smyrna write: These are the words of the first and the last, who was dead and came back to life: 
I know your hard times and your poverty, but you're rich. I know the slander of those who say they're Jewish and aren't, but are a gathering of the Adversary. 
Don't be afraid of what you're about to suffer. Look, the Slanderer is about to throw some of you into prison, so that you'll be tested, and you'll have hard times for ten days. Be trustworthy even to death, and I'll give you the wreath of real life. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings. The one who wins the victory won't be hurt by the second death. 
"To the messenger of the gathering in Pergamum write: These are the words of the one who has the sharp double-edged sword: 
I know where you live, where the Adversary's throne is. Yet you hold firmly to my name, and you didn't disown your trust in me even in the days of Antipas, my witness, my trustworthy one, who was killed among you, where the Adversary lives. 
But I have a few things against you: you have some there who hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to put a trap in front of the people of Israel, so that they'd eat food sacrificed to idols and sleep around. 
In the same way, you also have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans. 
So have a change of heart. If you don't, I'll come to you soon and fight against them with the sword of my mouth. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings. To the one who wins the victory I'll give some of the hidden manna. I'll also give them a white stone, and on the stone a new name written, which no one knows except the one who receives it. 
"To the messenger of the gathering in Thyatira write: These are the words of the Son of God, whose eyes are like a flame of fire and whose feet are like polished bronze: 
I know your deeds, your love, your trust, your service, and your staying power, and I know that your last deeds are more than your first. 
But I have this against you: you put up with that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a spokeswoman. She teaches and leads my slaves astray, so that they sleep around and eat food sacrificed to idols. 
I gave her time to have a change of heart, but she refuses to turn from her sleeping around. 
Look, I'll throw her onto a bed of great suffering, and I'll throw those who cheat with her into great hard times with her, unless they turn from what she's doing. 
I'll put her children to death, and all the gatherings will know that I am the one who searches minds and hearts. I'll pay each of you back for what you have done. 
But to the rest of you in Thyatira, who don't hold this teaching and haven't learned what some call "the deep things of the Adversary", I'm not putting any other burden on you. 
Only hold on to what you have until I come. 
To the one who wins the victory and keeps doing my deeds to the end, I'll give authority over the nations. 
They'll shepherd them with an iron rod and smash them like clay pots, just as I also received from my Father. 
And I'll give them the morning star. 
Whoever has ears should listen to what the Spirit says to the gatherings.
