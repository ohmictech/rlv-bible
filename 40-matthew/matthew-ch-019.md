When Jesus had finished these words, he departed from Galilee and came into the region of Judea beyond the Jordan. 
Great crowds followed him, and he healed them there. 
strict religious ones came to him, testing him and saying, "Is it allowed for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?" 
He answered, "Haven't you read that he who made them from the beginning made them male and female, 
and said, 'That's why a man will leave his father and mother, and will be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh'? 
So that they're no more two, but one flesh. What God has joined together, don't let a person tear apart." 
They asked him, "Why then did Moses command us to give her a certificate of divorce and split from her?" 
He said to them, "Moses, because of the hardness of your hearts, allowed you to split from your wives, but from the beginning it hasn't been so. 
I tell you that whoever splits from his wife, except for sleeping around, and marries another, cheats, and he who marries her when she has been split from also cheats." 
His students said to him, "If this is how it's between a man and his wife, it's better not to marry." 
But he said to them, "Not everyone can accept this saying, but those to whom it's given. 
There are eunuchs who were born that way from their mother's womb, and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by people, and there are eunuchs who made themselves eunuchs for the sake of the reign of the sky. He who's able to accept it, let him accept it." 
Then little children were brought to him that he should lay his hands on them and pray, and the students rebuked them. 
But Jesus said, "Let the little children come, and don't forbid them, for the reign of the sky belongs to ones like these." 
He laid his hands on them, and departed from there. 
Look, one came to him and said, "Teacher, what good thing must I do, that I can have the real life of the era?" 
He said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good but one, that is, God. But if you want to enter into real life, keep the commands." 
He said to him, "Which ones?" Jesus said, "'You won't murder.' 'You won't cheat.' 'You won't steal.' 'You won't give false witness.' 
'Honor your father and your mother.' And, 'You will love your neighbor as yourself.'" 
The young man said to him, "All these things I have observed from my youth. What do I still lack?" 
Jesus said to him, "If you want to be complete, go, sell what you have, and give to the destitute, and you'll have treasure in the sky, and come, follow me." 
But when the young man heard this, he went away sad, for he was one who had great possessions. 
Jesus said to his students, "Truly I say to you, a rich man will enter into the reign of the sky with difficulty. 
Again I tell you, it's easier for a camel to go through the eye of a sewing needle than for a rich man to enter into God's reign." 
When the students heard it, they were very astonished, saying, "Who then can be rescued?" 
Looking at them, Jesus said, "With people this is impossible, but with God all things are possible." 
Then Peter answered, "Look, we have left everything and followed you. What then will we have?" 
Jesus said to them, "Truly I tell you that you who have followed me, in the renewal of all things, when the Human One will sit on the throne of his weight, you also will sit on twelve thrones, passing a verdict on the twelve tribes of Israel. 
Everyone who has left houses, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my name's sake, will receive a hundred times as much, and will be given charge of the real life of the era. 
But many will be last who are first, and first who are last.
