What should we say then? Should we continue in the drift, so that favor may overflow? 
Never! We who died to the drift, how could we live in it any longer? 
Don't you know that all of us who were immersed into Christ Jesus were immersed into his death? 
We were buried with him through immersion into death, so that just as Christ was raised from the dead through the weight of the Father, we also would walk in new life. 
If we have become united with him in the way of his death, we will also be part of his rising; 
knowing this, that our old man was crucified with him, so that the body of the drift would be put out of work, so that we would no longer be slaves to the drift. 
The person who died has been freed from the drift. 
But if we died with Christ, we trust that we will also live with him, 
knowing that Christ, being raised from the dead, dies no more. Death no longer dominates him! 
For the death he died, he died to the drift once; but the life that he lives, he lives to God. 
So consider yourselves dead to the drift, but alive to God in Christ Jesus our Lord. 
So don't let the drift reign in your mortal body, to obey it in its cravings. 
Also, don't give your hands and feet to the drift as tools of wrong, but present yourselves to God as alive from the dead, and your limbs as tools of rightness to God. 
The drift will not dominate you, because you are not under law, but under favor. 
What then? Should we drift because we are not under law but under favor? Never! 
Don't you know that when you present yourselves as slaves and obey someone, you are the slaves of whoever you obey, whether of the drift to death, or of obedience to rightness? 
But thanks be to God that, even though you were slaves of the drift, you became obedient from the heart to that form of teaching you were given to. 
Being made free from the drift, you became slaves of rightness. 
I speak in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. Just as you once handed your bodies over as slaves to what is unclean and to living as if there were no law, which led to more of the same, now hand your bodies over as slaves to rightness, which leads to being set apart. 
When you were slaves of the drift, you were free from rightness. 
What fruit then did you have at that time in the things of which you are now ashamed? For the end of those things is death. 
But now, being made free from the drift and having become slaves of God, you have your fruit of set-apartness and the result of the real life of the era. 
For what the drift owes is death, but the free gift of God is the real life of the era in Christ Jesus our Lord.
