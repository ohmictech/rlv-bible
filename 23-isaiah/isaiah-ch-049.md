Listen, islands, to me. Listen, you peoples, from afar: God has called me from the womb; from the inside of my mother, he has mentioned my name. 
He has made my mouth like a sharp sword. He has hidden me in the shadow of his hand. He has made me a polished shaft. He has kept me close in his quiver. 
He said to me, "You're my servant, Israel, in whom I'll be glorified." 
But I said, "I have labored in vain. I have spent my strength in vain for nothing; yet surely the justice due to me is with God, and my reward with my God." 
Now God, he who formed me from the womb to be his servant, says to bring Jacob again to him, and to gather Israel to him, for I'm honorable in God's eyes, and my God has become my strength. 
Indeed, he says, "It's too light a thing that you should be my servant to raise up the tribes of Jacob, and to restore the preserved of Israel. I'll also give you as a light to the nations, that you can be my salvation to the end of the earth." 
God, the one who buys back of Israel, and his Holy One, says to him whom man despises, to him whom the nation abhors, to a servant of rulers: "Kings will see and rise up, princes, and they will worship, because of God who's faithful, even the Holy One of Israel, who has chosen you." 
God says, "I have answered you in an acceptable time. I have helped you in a day of salvation. I'll preserve you and give you for a covenant of the people, to raise up the land, to make them inherit the desolate heritage, 
saying to those who are bound, 'Come out!'; to those who are in darkness, 'Show yourselves!' "They will feed along the paths, and their pasture will be on all treeless heights. 
They won't hunger nor thirst; neither will the heat nor sun strike them, for he who has mercy on them will lead them. He'll guide them by springs of water. 
I'll make all my mountains a road, and my highways will be exalted. 
Look, these will come from afar, and look, these from the north and from the west, and these from the land of Sinim." 
Sing, skies, and be joyful, earth! Break out into singing, mountains! God has comforted his people, and will have compassion on his afflicted. 
But Zion said, "God has forsaken me, and the Lord has forgotten me." 
"Can a woman forget her nursing child, that she shouldn't have compassion on the son of her womb? Yes, these may forget, yet I won't forget you! 
Look, I have engraved you on the palms of my hands. Your walls are continually before me. 
Your children hurry. Your destroyers and those who devastated you'll leave you. 
Lift up your eyes all around, and see: all these gather themselves together, and come to you. As I live," says God, "you will surely clothe yourself with them all as with an ornament, and dress yourself with them, like a bride. 
"For, as for your waste and your desolate places, and your land that has been destroyed, surely now that land will be too small for the inhabitants, and those who swallowed you up will be far away. 
The children of your bereavement will say in your ears, 'This place is too small for me. Give me a place to live in.' 
Then you'll say in your heart, 'Who has conceived these for me, since I have been bereaved of my children and am alone, an exile, and wandering back and forth? Who has brought these up? Look, I was left alone. Where were these?'" 
The God says, "Look, I'll lift up my hand to the nations, and lift up my banner to the peoples. They will bring your sons in their bosom, and your daughters will be carried on their shoulders. 
Kings will be your foster fathers, and their queens your nursing mothers. They'll bow down to you with their faces to the earth, and lick the dust of your feet. Then you'll know that I'm God; and those who wait for me won't be disappointed." 
Will the plunder be taken from the mighty, or the lawful captives be delivered? 
But God says, "Even the captives of the mighty will be taken away, and the plunder retrieved from the fierce, for I'll contend with him who contends with you and I'll save your children. 
I'll feed those who oppress you with their own flesh; and they'll be drunk on their own blood, as with sweet wine. Then all flesh will know that I, God, am your Savior and the one who buys you back, the Mighty One of Jacob."
