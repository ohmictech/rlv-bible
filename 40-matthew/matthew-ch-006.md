"Be careful not to do your right works in front of people so that they'll see them. If you do, you have no pay from your Father in the sky. 
So when you give to the poor, don't sound a trumpet in front of you, the way the pretenders do in the gathering places and in the streets, so that people will honor them. Truly I tell you: they already have their pay in full. 
When you give to the poor, don't let your left hand know what your right hand is doing, 
so that your giving is in secret. And your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you out in the open. 
And when you pray, don't be like the pretenders, because they love to stand and pray in the gathering places and on the street corners, so that people will see them. Truly I tell you: they already have their pay in full. 
When you pray, go into your room, close the door, and pray to your Father in secret. And your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you out in the open. 
When you pray, don't babble on the way the outsiders do, because they think they'll be heard for their many words. 
Don't be like them. Your Father knows what you need before you ask him. 
Pray, then, like this: Our Father in the sky, let your name be held in honor. 
Let your reign come. Let your will be done on earth as it's in the sky. 
Give us today our bread for the coming day. 
Release us from what we owe, as we also release those who owe us. 
And don't bring us into the test, but rescue us from the Slanderer. Yours is the reign and the power and the honor, into the eras. Amen. 
If you release people from their false steps, your Father in the sky will release you too. 
But if you don't release people, your Father won't release your false steps." 
"And when you go without food, don't put on a gloomy face the way the pretenders do, because they make their faces look miserable so that people will see that they're going without food. Truly I tell you: they already have their pay in full. 
When you go without food, wash your face and anoint your head, 
so that your going without food is seen not by people but by your Father in secret. And your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you out in the open. 
Don't store up treasures for yourselves on the land, where moth and rust ruin and where thieves break in and steal. 
Store up treasures for yourselves in the sky, where neither moth nor rust ruins and where thieves don't break in and steal. 
Where your treasure is, there your heart will be too. 
The lamp of the body is the eye. So if your eye is healthy, your whole body will be full of light. 
But if your eye is sick, your whole body will be full of darkness. If the light in you is darkness, how great that darkness is. 
No one can serve two masters. He'll either hate the one and love the other, or stand by the one and look down on the other. You can't serve God and Money. 
So I tell you: don't worry about your life, what you'll eat or what you'll drink, or about your body, what you'll wear. Isn't life more than food, and the body more than clothing? 
Look at the birds of the sky. They don't sow or reap or gather into barns, and your Father in the sky feeds them. Are you not worth more than they are? 
Who among you, by worrying, can add one hour to his life? 
Why do you worry about clothing? Learn from the wildflowers of the field, how they grow. They don't work or spin thread. 
But I tell you: not even Solomon in all his splendor was dressed like one of these. 
If God dresses the grass of the field like this, grass that's here today and thrown into the oven tomorrow, will he not much more clothe you, you who don't trust? 
So don't worry, saying, 'What will we eat?' or 'What will we drink?' or 'What will we wear?' 
The outsiders chase after all these things, and your Father in the sky knows that you need them all. 
Chase after his reign and his rightness first, and all these things will be added to you. 
So don't worry about tomorrow. Tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own."
