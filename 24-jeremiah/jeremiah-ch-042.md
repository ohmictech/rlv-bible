Then all the captains of the forces, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and Jezaniah the son of Hoshaiah, and everyone from the least even to the greatest, came near, 
and said to Jeremiah the spokesman, "Please let our supplication be presented before you, and pray for us to your God, even for all this remnant, for we're left but a few of many, as your eyes see us, 
that your God may show us the way in which we should walk, and the things that we should do." 
Then Jeremiah the spokesman said to them, "I have heard you. Look, I'll pray to your God according to your words; and it'll happen that whatever thing God answers you, I'll declare it to you. I'll keep nothing back from you." 
Then they said to Jeremiah, "May God be a true and faithful witness among us, if we don't do according to all the word with which your God sends you to tell us. 
Whether it's good, or whether it's bad, we'll obey the voice of our God, to whom we send you; that it might be well with us, when we obey the voice of our God." 
After ten days, God's word came to Jeremiah. 
Then he called Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces who were with him, and everyone from the least even to the greatest, 
and said to them, "The God of Israel, to whom you sent me to present your supplication before him, says: 
'If you'll still live in this land, then I'll build you, and not pull you down, and I'll plant you, and not pluck you up; for I grieve over the distress that I have brought on you. 
Don't be afraid of the king of Babylon, of whom you're afraid. Don't be afraid of him,' says God, 'for I'm with you to save you, and to deliver you from his hand. 
I'll grant you mercy, that he can have mercy on you, and cause you to return to your own land. 
"'But if you say, "We won't dwell in this land," so that you don't obey your God's voice, 
saying, "No, but we'll go into the land of Egypt, where we'll see no war, nor hear the sound of the trumpet, nor have hunger of bread; and there we'll dwell;"' 
now hear God's word, O remnant of Judah! The God of Armies, the God of Israel says, 'If you indeed set your faces to enter into Egypt, and go to live there, 
then it'll happen that the sword, which you fear, will overtake you there in the land of Egypt; and the famine, about which you're afraid, will follow close behind you there in Egypt; and you'll die there. 
So will it be with all the men who set their faces to go into Egypt to live there. They'll die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence. None of them will remain or escape from the bad that I'll bring on them.' 
The God of Armies, the God of Israel says: 'As my anger and my wrath has been poured out on the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so my wrath will be poured out on you, when you enter into Egypt; and you'll be an object of horror, an astonishment, a curse, and a reproach; and you'll see this place no more.' 
"God has spoken concerning you, remnant of Judah, 'Don't go into Egypt!' Know certainly that I have testified to you today. 
You have dealt deceitfully against your own souls; for you sent me to your God, saying, 'Pray for us to our God; and according to all that our God says, so declare to us, and we'll do it.' 
I have declared it to you today; but you haven't obeyed your God's voice in anything for which he has sent me to you. 
Now know certainly that you'll die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence in the place where you desire to go to live."
