The Chief Musician. Set to "The Death of the Son." A Psalm by David. I'll give thanks to God with my whole heart. I'll tell of all your marvelous works. 
I'll be glad and rejoice in you. I'll sing praise to your name, O Most High. 
When my enemies turn back, they stumble and perish in your presence. 
You have maintained my just cause. You sit on the throne judging righteously. 
You have rebuked the nations. You have destroyed the guilty. You have blotted out their name through all the eras. 
The enemy is overtaken by lasting ruin. The very memory of the cities which you have overthrown has perished. 
But God reigns for the eras. He has prepared his throne for judgment. 
He'll judge the world in righteousness. He'll administer judgment to the peoples in uprightness. 
God will also be a high tower for the oppressed; a high tower in times of trouble. 
Those who know your name will put their trust in you, for you, God, haven't forsaken those who seek you. 
Sing praises to God, who dwells in Zion, and declare among the people what he has done. 
He who avenges blood remembers them. He doesn't forget the cry of the afflicted. 
Have mercy on me, God. See my affliction by those who hate me, and lift me up from the gates of death, 
that I can show all of your praise. I'll rejoice in your salvation in the gates of the daughter of Zion. 
The nations have sunk down in the pit that they made. In the net which they hid, their own foot is taken. 
God has made himself known. He has executed judgment. The guilty is snared by the work of his own hands. Meditation. Selah. 
The guilty will be turned back to Hades, even all the nations that forget God. 
The needy won't always be forgotten, nor the hope of the poor perish for the eras. 
Arise, God! Don't let man prevail. Let the nations be judged in your sight. 
Put them in fear, God. Let the nations know that they're only men. Selah.
