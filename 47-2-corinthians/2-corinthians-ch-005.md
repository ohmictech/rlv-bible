We know that if the tent we live in on land is taken down, we have a building from God, a house not made with hands, that lasts for the eras, in the sky. 
In this one we groan, longing to put on our dwelling from the sky, 
if indeed being clothed, we will not be found naked. 
We who are in this tent groan, weighed down, not because we want to be unclothed, but because we want to be clothed, so that what dies may be swallowed up by real life. 
Now he who made us for this very thing is God, who also gave to us the down payment of the Spirit. 
So we are always confident and know that while we are at home in the body, we are absent from the Lord; 
for we walk by trust, not by sight. 
We are courageous, I say, and are willing rather to be absent from the body and to be at home with the Lord. 
So we make it our aim, whether at home or away, to be pleasing to him. 
We must all appear before the platform of Christ, so that each one may receive what he did in the body, whether good or bad. 
So, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade people, but we are plainly known to God, and I hope we are plainly known in your consciences too. 
We are not commending ourselves to you again, but speak as giving you a reason to take pride on our behalf, so that you have something to answer those who boast in appearance and not in heart. 
If we are out of our minds, it is for God. If we are of sound mind, it is for you. 
The love of Christ drives us on, because we have concluded this: one died for all, therefore all died. 
He died for all, that those who live should no longer live to themselves, but to him who for their sakes died and rose again. 
So we know no one according to the flesh from now on. Even though we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we know him so no more. 
So if anyone is in Christ, they are a new creation. The old things have passed away. Look, all things have become new. 
But all things are from God, who made friends with us through Jesus Christ, and gave us the service of making friends; 
that is, God was in Christ making friends with the world, not counting their false steps against them, and he has handed us the message of making friends. 
So we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were appealing through us. We beg you for Christ's sake: make friends with God. 
God made the one who had not drifted to be the drift on our behalf, so that in him we might become the rightness of God.
