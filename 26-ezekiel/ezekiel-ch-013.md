God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, prophesy against the spokesmen of Israel who prophesy, and say to those who prophesy out of their own heart, 'Hear God's word: 
The God says, "Woe to the foolish spokesmen, who follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing! 
Israel, your spokesmen have been like foxes in the waste places. 
You haven't gone up into the gaps or built up the wall for the house of Israel, to stand in the battle in God's day. 
They have seen falsehood and lying divination, who say, 'God says;' but God hasn't sent them. They have made men to hope that the word would be confirmed. 
Haven't you seen a false vision, and haven't you spoken a lying divination, in that you say, 'God says;' but I haven't spoken?" 
"'Therefore God says: "Because you have spoken falsehood and seen lies, look, I'm against you," says God. 
"My hand will be against the spokesmen who see false visions and who utter lying divinations. They won't be in the council of my people, neither will they be written in the writing of the house of Israel, neither will they enter into the land of Israel. Then you'll know that I'm God." 
"'Because, even because they have seduced my people, saying, "Peace;" and there's no peace. When one builds up a wall, look, they plaster it with whitewash. 
Tell those who plaster it with whitewash that it'll fall. There will be an overflowing shower; and you, great hailstones, will fall. A stormy wind will tear it. 
Look, when the wall has fallen, won't it be said to you, "Where is the plaster with which you have plastered it?" 
"'Therefore God says: "I'll even tear it with a stormy wind in my wrath. There will be an overflowing shower in my anger, and great hailstones in wrath to consume it. 
So I'll break down the wall that you have plastered with whitewash, and bring it down to the ground, so that its foundation will be uncovered. It'll fall, and you'll be consumed in the middle of it. Then you'll know that I'm God. 
Thus I'll accomplish my wrath on the wall, and on those who have plastered it with whitewash. I'll tell you, 'The wall is no more, nor those who plastered it 
to wit, the spokesmen of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem, and who see visions of peace for her, and there's no peace,'" says God.'" 
You, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people, who prophesy out of their own heart; and prophesy against them, 
and say, "The God says: 'Woe to the women who sew magic bands on all elbows and make veils for the head of persons of every stature to hunt souls! Will you hunt the souls of my people and save souls alive for yourselves? 
You have profaned me among my people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, to kill the souls who shouldn't die and to save the souls alive who shouldn't live, by your lying to my people who listen to lies.' 
"Therefore God says: 'Look, I'm against your magic bands, with which you hunt the souls to make them fly, and I'll tear them from your arms. I'll let the souls fly free, even the souls whom you ensnare like birds. 
I'll also tear your veils and deliver my people out of your hand; and they'll no longer be in your hand to be ensnared. Then you'll know that I'm God. 
Because with lies you have grieved the heart of the righteous, whom I haven't made sad; and strengthened the hands of the guilty, that he shouldn't return from his guilty way, and be saved alive. 
So you will no more see false visions nor practice divination. I'll deliver my people out of your hand. Then you'll know that I'm God.'"
