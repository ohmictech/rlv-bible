Paul, a sent one of Christ Jesus, by the command of God our Restorer and of Christ Jesus our expectation, 
to Timothy, my true child in the trust: Favor, mercy, and wholeness to you from God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord. 
Just as I urged you when I was on my way to Macedonia, stay on at Ephesus so that you can tell certain people to stop teaching something different, 
and to stop paying attention to old stories and endless family trees, which only stir up arguments instead of the household plan of God, which works through trust. 
The goal of this instruction is love that comes from a clean heart, a good conscience, and a genuine trust, 
Some have drifted on these and turned aside into empty talk, 
wanting to be teachers of the law, without understanding either what they're saying or what they're so sure about. 
We know that the law is good, if a person uses it the way it's meant to be used. 
knowing this: the law isn't laid down for someone who's right, but for those who live as if there were no law, for those who won't be told what to do, for those with no regard for God and those who have drifted, for those who are irreverent and worldly, for men who kill their fathers or their mothers, for murderers, 
for those who sleep around, for men who lie with men, for those who kidnap people and sell them, for liars, for those who break their oaths, and for anything else that's against healthy teaching, 
in keeping with the Good News of the weight of the flourishing God, which was put in my care. 
I give thanks to Christ Jesus our Lord, who gave me strength, because he counted me trustworthy and put me into service, 
even though I used to speak slander, hunt people down, and treat them with violence. But I was shown mercy, because I did it in ignorance, without trust. 
The favor of our Lord overflowed far more, along with the trust and love that are in Christ Jesus. 
This saying is trustworthy and deserves to be accepted without question: Christ Jesus came into the world to rescue those who have drifted, and I'm the worst of them. 
But this is why I was shown mercy: so that in me, the worst of them, Christ Jesus could show how patient he's, as an example for those who would trust in him for the real life of the era. 
Now to the King of the eras, undying, unseen, to the only God, who alone is wise, be honor and weight for all the eras. Amen. 
Timothy, my child, I put this instruction in your hands, in keeping with the words spoken for God about you earlier, so that by them you can fight the good fight, 
holding on to trust and a good conscience. Some have pushed those aside and wrecked their trust, 
Hymenaeus and Alexander are among them. I have handed them over to the Adversary, so that they can be taught not to speak slander.
