Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. 
"Honor your father and mother." This is the first command with a promise attached: 
"so that things go well with you and you live a long time on the land." 
And fathers, don't make your children angry, but bring them up with the Lord's discipline and teaching. 
Slaves, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling and with all your heart, as you would Christ, 
not only when they are watching you, as if you were trying to please people, but as slaves of Christ, doing God's will from the heart, 
serving willingly, as to the Lord and not to people, 
because you know that whatever good anyone does, he will get the same back from the Lord, whether he is a slave or free. 
And you who are masters, do the same for them, and give up threats, because you know that their Master and yours is in the sky, and he plays no favorites. 
Finally, be strong in the Lord, in his powerful might. 
Put on God's whole armor, so that you can stand against the Slanderer's schemes. 
For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the ruling powers, against the authorities, against the world rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of malice in the sky. 
That is why you must put on God's whole armor, so that you can hold your ground on the bad day, and after you have done everything, stand. 
So stand, with the belt of truth buckled around your waist and the breastplate of rightness in place, 
and your feet fitted with the readiness that comes from the Good News of wholeness, 
And above all, take up the shield of trust, with which you can put out all the burning arrows of the malicious one. 
And take the helmet of rescue, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God. 
Pray at all times in the Spirit, with every kind of prayer and request. Keep awake and keep at it, praying for all the set-apart ones, 
and pray for me too, that words may be given to me when I open my mouth, so that I can speak out in the open and make known the secret of the Good News, 
for which I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may speak out in the open as I should. 
Tychicus, our truly loved brother and trustworthy servant in the Lord, will tell you everything, so that you also may know how I am doing. 
I am sending him to you for this very reason, so that you may know how we are doing and so that he may encourage your hearts. 
Wholeness to the brothers and sisters, and love with trust, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
Favor be with everyone who truly loves our Lord Jesus Christ with a love that never decays. Amen.
