God spoke to Moses and Aaron: 
"The Israelites are to camp each man under his own banner, with the banners of their families, and they are to camp around the Tent of Meeting, facing it from a distance. 
"On the east side, toward the sunrise, is the camp of Judah under its banner, by their units. The leader of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab. 
His unit numbered seventy-four thousand six hundred. 
"Next to him is the tribe of Issachar. The leader of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar. 
His unit numbered fifty-four thousand four hundred. 
"Then the tribe of Zebulun. The leader of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon. 
His unit numbered fifty-seven thousand four hundred. 
"All those in the camp of Judah, counted by their units, numbered one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred. They are to set out first. 
"On the south side is the camp of Reuben under its banner, by their units. The leader of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur. 
His unit numbered forty-six thousand five hundred. 
"Next to him is the tribe of Simeon. The leader of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai. 
His unit numbered fifty-nine thousand three hundred. 
"Then the tribe of Gad. The leader of Gad is Eliasaph son of Reuel. 
His unit numbered forty-five thousand six hundred fifty. 
"All those in the camp of Reuben, counted by their units, numbered one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty. They are to set out second. 
"Then the Tent of Meeting is to set out, with the camp of the Levites in the middle of the camps. As they camp, so they set out, each man in his place under his banner. 
"On the west side is the camp of Ephraim under its banner, by their units. The leader of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud. 
His unit numbered forty thousand five hundred. 
"Next to him is the tribe of Manasseh. The leader of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur. 
His unit numbered thirty-two thousand two hundred. 
"Then the tribe of Benjamin. The leader of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni. 
His unit numbered thirty-five thousand four hundred. 
"All those in the camp of Ephraim, counted by their units, numbered one hundred eight thousand one hundred. They are to set out third. 
"On the north side is the camp of Dan under its banner, by their units. The leader of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai. 
His unit numbered sixty-two thousand seven hundred. 
"Next to him is the tribe of Asher. The leader of Asher is Pagiel son of Ochran. 
His unit numbered forty-one thousand five hundred. 
"Then the tribe of Naphtali. The leader of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan. 
His unit numbered fifty-three thousand four hundred. 
"All those in the camp of Dan numbered one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They are to set out last, under their banners." 
These are the Israelites counted by their families. All those in the camps, counted by their units, numbered six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty. 
But the Levites were not counted with the Israelites, just as God commanded Moses. 
The Israelites did just as God commanded Moses. They camped under their banners and set out the same way, each man with his clan and family.
