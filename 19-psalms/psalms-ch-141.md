A Psalm by David. God, I have called on you. Come to me quickly! Listen to my voice when I call to you. 
Let my prayer be set before you like incense; the lifting up of my hands like the evening sacrifice. 
Set a watch, God, before my mouth. Keep the door of my lips. 
Don't incline my heart to any bad thing, to practice deeds of guilt with men who work guilt. Don't let me eat of their delicacies. 
Let the righteous strike me, it's kindness; let him reprove me, it's like oil on the head; don't let my head refuse it; Yet my prayer is always against bad deeds. 
Their judges are thrown down by the sides of the rock. They'll hear my words, for they're well spoken. 
"As when one plows and breaks up the earth, our bones are scattered at the mouth of Hades." 
My eyes are on you, God, the Lord. I take refuge in you. Don't leave my soul destitute. 
Keep me from the snare which they have laid for me, from the traps of the workers of guilt. 
Let the guilty fall together into their own nets while I pass by.
