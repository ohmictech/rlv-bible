A second time they said, "Hallelujah! Her smoke rises for all the eras." 
The twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and bowed down to God who sits on the throne, saying, "Amen! Hallelujah!" 
Then a voice came from the throne, saying, "Praise our God, all you his slaves, all you who fear him, small and great!" 
Then I heard what sounded like the voice of a great crowd, like the sound of rushing waters, and like loud peals of thunder, saying, "Hallelujah! The Lord our God, the one who holds sway over all, has begun to reign! 
Let us celebrate and be glad and give him the weight, because the wedding of the Lamb has come, and his bride has made herself ready." 
She was allowed to dress in bright, clean, fine linen, and the fine linen is the right deeds of the set-apart ones. 
Then he said to me, "Write this down: 'Flourishing are those who are invited to the wedding feast of the Lamb.'" He said to me, "These are the true words of God." 
I fell at his feet to bow down to him, but he said to me, "Don't do that! I am a fellow slave with you and with your brothers and sisters who hold to the witness Jesus gave. Bow down to God! The witness Jesus gave is the spirit that speaks for God." 
Then I saw the sky standing open, and there was a white horse, and the one sitting on it is called Trustworthy and True. He passes a verdict and makes war in rightness. 
His eyes are a flame of fire, and on his head are many crowns. He has a name written that no one knows except himself. 
He is dressed in a robe dipped in blood, and his name is called the Word of God. 
The armies in the sky, dressed in white, clean, fine linen, followed him on white horses. 
Out of his mouth comes a sharp, double-edged sword, and with it he strikes down the nations. He will shepherd them with an iron rod, and he treads the winepress of the furious heat of God, the one who holds sway over all. 
On his robe and on his thigh he has a name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS. 
Then I saw a messenger standing in the sun, and he called out in a loud voice to all the birds flying in the sky, "Come! Gather for the great feast of God, 
so that you can eat the flesh of kings, the flesh of commanders, the flesh of the mighty, the flesh of horses and their riders, and the flesh of everyone, free and slave, small and great." 
Then I saw the beast, the kings of the land, and their armies gathered together to make war on the one sitting on the horse and on his army. 
The beast was captured, and with it the false spokesman who had worked the signs for it, the signs by which it had led astray those who received the mark of the beast and those who bowed down to its image. The two of them were thrown alive into the lake of fire that burns with sulfur. 
The rest were killed with the sword that came out of the mouth of the one sitting on the horse, and all the birds gorged themselves on their flesh.
