"You, son of man, take a sharp sword. You will take it as a barber's razor to yourself, and will cause it to pass over your head and over your beard. Then take balances to weigh and divide the hair. 
A third part you will burn in the fire in the middle of the city, when the days of the siege are fulfilled. You will take a third part, and strike with the sword around it. A third part you will scatter to the wind, and I'll draw out a sword after them. 
You will take a small number of these and bind them in the folds of your robe. 
Of these again you will take, and cast them into the middle of the fire, and burn them in the fire. From it a fire will come out into all the house of Israel. 
"The God says: 'This is Jerusalem. I have set her in the middle of the nations, and countries are around her. 
She has rebelled against my ordinances in doing guilt more than the nations, and against my statutes more than the countries that are around her; for they have rejected my ordinances, and as for my statutes, they haven't walked in them.' 
"Therefore God says: 'Because you're more turbulent than the nations that are around you, and haven't walked in my statutes, neither have kept my ordinances, neither have followed the ordinances of the nations that are around you; 
So God says: 'Look, I, even I, am against you; and I'll execute judgments among you in the sight of the nations. 
I'll do in you that which I haven't done, and which I won't do anything like it any more, because of all your abominations. 
So the fathers will eat the sons within you, and the sons will eat their fathers. I'll execute judgments on you; and I'll scatter the whole remnant of you to all the winds. 
So as I live,' says God, 'surely, because you have defiled my sanctuary with all your detestable things, and with all your abominations, I'll also diminish you. My eye won't spare, and I'll have no pity. 
A third part of you'll die with the pestilence, and they'll be consumed with famine within you. A third part will fall by the sword around you. A third part I'll scatter to all the winds, and will draw out a sword after them. 
"'Thus my anger will be accomplished, and I'll cause my wrath toward them to rest, and I'll be comforted. They'll know that I, God, have spoken in my passion, when I have accomplished my wrath on them. 
"'Moreover I'll make you a desolation and a reproach among the nations that are around you, in the sight of all that pass by. 
So it'll be a reproach and a taunt, an instruction and an astonishment, to the nations that are around you, when I execute judgments on you in anger and in wrath, and in wrathful rebukes, I, God, have spoken it 
when I send on them the bad arrows of famine that are for destruction, which I'll send to destroy you. I'll increase the famine on you and will break your staff of bread. 
I'll send on you famine and bad animals, and they'll bereave you. Pestilence and blood will pass through you. I'll bring the sword on you. I, God, have spoken it.'"
