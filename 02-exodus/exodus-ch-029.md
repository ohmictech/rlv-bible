"This is what you are to do to set them apart, so they can serve me as priest: take one young bull and two rams without defect, 
bread without yeast, flatbread without yeast mixed with oil, and thin wafers without yeast brushed with oil, all made from fine wheat flour. 
Put them in a basket and bring them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams. 
Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and wash them with water. 
Take the garments and dress Aaron in the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod itself and the breastplate, and tie the woven band of the ephod around him. 
Put the turban on his head and the set apart crown on the turban. 
Then take the anointing oil, pour it on his head and anoint him. 
Bring his sons and dress them in tunics. 
Put belts on Aaron and his sons and headbands on them, and the priesthood will be theirs by a lasting rule. So you will ordain Aaron and his sons. 
"Bring the bull to the front of the Tent of Meeting, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on its head. 
Kill the bull before God at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 
Take some of the bull's blood and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour the rest out at the base of the altar. 
Take all the fat that covers the inner organs, the long lobe of the liver, the two kidneys and the fat on them, and burn them on the altar. 
But the bull's meat, its hide and its dung you are to burn outside the camp. It is an offering for wrongdoing. 
"Take one of the rams, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on its head. 
Kill the ram, take its blood and splash it against all sides of the altar. 
Cut the ram into pieces, wash its inner organs and its legs, and lay them with the pieces and the head. 
Burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to God, a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to God. 
"Take the other ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on its head. 
Kill it, take some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear and on the right earlobes of his sons, on the thumbs of their right hands and on the big toes of their right feet, and splash the rest of the blood against all sides of the altar. 
Take some of the blood on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle them on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and their garments, so he and his garments and his sons and their garments become set apart. 
Take the fat of the ram, the fat tail, the fat covering the inner organs, the long lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat on them and the right thigh, because this is the ram of ordination, 
and one loaf of bread, one cake of bread mixed with oil and one wafer from the basket of bread without yeast set before God. 
Put all of this in the hands of Aaron and his sons and present it before God as a wave offering. 
Take it from their hands and burn it on the altar on top of the burnt offering, a soothing aroma before God, an offering made by fire to God. 
"Take the breast of Aaron's ram of ordination and present it before God as a wave offering. It will be your share. 
Set apart the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution, the parts presented and lifted up from the ram of ordination, the one that belongs to Aaron and the one that belongs to his sons. 
It will be the share of Aaron and his sons for the eras from the Israelites, because it is a contribution. It will be a contribution from the Israelites out of their offerings for wholeness, their contribution to God. 
"The set apart garments of Aaron will belong to his sons after him, so they can be anointed and ordained in them. 
The son who becomes priest in his place will wear them for seven days when he comes into the Tent of Meeting to serve in the Holy Place. 
"Take the ram of ordination and boil its meat in a set apart place. 
Aaron and his sons are to eat the meat of the ram and the bread in the basket at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 
They are to eat what was offered to make things right at their ordination and setting apart, but no outsider may eat it, because it is holy. 
If any of the meat of the ordination, or of the bread, is left until morning, burn the rest. It must not be eaten, because it is holy. 
"Do all this for Aaron and his sons, just as I have commanded you, ordaining them over seven days. 
Each day offer the bull of the offering for wrongdoing to make things right, and cleanse the altar by making things right for it, and anoint it to set it apart. 
For seven days make things right for the altar and set it apart, and the altar will be most holy. Whatever touches the altar will be holy. 
"This is what you are to offer on the altar: two lambs a year old, every day, without fail. 
Offer one lamb in the morning and the other lamb at dusk, 
and with the first lamb a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of beaten oil, and a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering. 
Offer the second lamb at dusk, with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to God. 
It is a burnt offering that never stops, through all your generations, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before God, where I will meet with you and speak to you. 
There I will meet with the Israelites, and the place will be set apart by my splendor. 
I will set apart the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and I will set apart Aaron and his sons to serve me as priest. 
I will live among the Israelites and be their God. 
They will know that I am God, who brought them out of Egypt to live among them. I am their God.
