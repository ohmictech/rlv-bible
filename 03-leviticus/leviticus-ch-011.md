God spoke to Moses and Aaron: 
"Speak to the Israelites and tell them, 'These are the animals you may eat from all the animals on the earth. 
You may eat any animal that has a split hoof, divided all the way through, and that chews the cud. 
"'But from those that chew the cud or have a divided hoof, you are not to eat these: the camel, because it chews the cud but doesn't have a divided hoof, is unclean to you; 
the rock badger, because it chews the cud but doesn't have a divided hoof, is unclean to you; 
the hare, because it chews the cud but doesn't have a divided hoof, is unclean to you; 
and the pig, because it has a divided hoof and the hoof is split through, but it doesn't chew the cud, is unclean to you. 
You are not to eat their meat or touch their carcasses. They are unclean to you. 
"'From all the creatures in the water, you may eat these: anything in the seas or the rivers that has fins and scales. 
But anything in the seas or the rivers that doesn't have fins and scales, everything that swarms in the water or is a living creature in the water, is disgusting to you. 
You are to treat them as disgusting. You are not to eat their meat, and you are to treat their carcasses as disgusting. 
Anything in the water that has no fins and scales is disgusting to you. 
"'These are the birds you are to treat as disgusting and not eat, because they are disgusting: the eagle, the vulture, the black vulture, 
the red kite, any kind of black kite, 
any kind of raven, 
the horned owl, the screech owl, the gull, any kind of hawk, 
the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl, 
the white owl, the desert owl, the osprey, 
the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe, and the bat. 
"'All winged insects that walk on four legs are disgusting to you. 
But you may eat these among the winged insects that walk on four legs: those that have jointed legs for jumping on the ground. 
From these you may eat any kind of locust, any kind of katydid, any kind of cricket, and any kind of grasshopper. 
But every other winged insect with four legs is disgusting to you. 
"'By these you will become unclean. Whoever touches their carcass will be unclean until evening, 
and whoever carries any part of their carcass is to wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. 
"'Every animal that has a split hoof not divided all the way through, or that doesn't chew the cud, is unclean to you. Everyone who touches them will be unclean. 
Any animal that walks on its paws, among all the animals that walk on four legs, is unclean to you. Whoever touches their carcass will be unclean until evening, 
and whoever carries their carcass is to wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. They are unclean to you. 
"'These are the ones that are unclean to you among the small animals that crawl on the ground: the weasel, the rat, any kind of great lizard, 
the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink, and the chameleon. 
These are the ones that are unclean to you among all that crawl. Whoever touches them when they are dead will be unclean until evening. 
Anything they fall on when they are dead becomes unclean, whether a wooden utensil, clothing, hide or sackcloth, any utensil used for work. It must be put in water and will be unclean until evening. Then it will be clean. 
Any clay pot they fall into becomes unclean, and everything in it, and you are to break it. 
Any food that may be eaten becomes unclean if water from such a pot touches it, and any drink that may be drunk from such a vessel becomes unclean. 
Anything their carcass falls on becomes unclean. An oven or a cooking stove must be broken in pieces. They are unclean, and they will stay unclean to you. 
But a spring or a cistern where water is gathered stays clean, though anyone who touches their carcass will be unclean. 
If part of their carcass falls on seed that is about to be sown, the seed stays clean. 
But if water has been put on the seed and part of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean to you. 
"'If an animal that you may eat dies, whoever touches its carcass will be unclean until evening. 
Whoever eats from its carcass is to wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening, and whoever carries its carcass is to wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. 
"'Every small animal that crawls on the ground is disgusting. It is not to be eaten. 
Whatever moves on its belly, whatever walks on four legs, and whatever has many legs, among all the small animals that crawl on the ground, you are not to eat them, because they are disgusting. 
Do not make yourselves disgusting with any small animal that crawls. Do not make yourselves unclean with them and become unclean through them. 
I am your God, so set yourselves apart and be set apart, because I am set apart. Do not make yourselves unclean with any small animal that moves on the ground. 
I am God, who brought you up out of Egypt to be your God. So be set apart, because I am set apart. 
"'This is the law about the animals, the birds, every living creature that moves in the water, and every creature that crawls on the ground, 
so you can tell the difference between unclean and clean, and between the living things you may eat and the living things you may not eat.'"
