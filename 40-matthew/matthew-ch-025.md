"Then the reign of the sky will be like ten bridesmaids who took their lamps and went out to meet the bridegroom. 
Five of them were foolish, and five were sensible. 
The foolish ones took no oil with them when they took their lamps, 
but the sensible ones took oil in their jars along with their lamps. 
While the bridegroom was delayed, they all became drowsy and slept. 
But at midnight there was a shout, 'Look! The bridegroom is coming! Come out to meet him!' 
Then all those bridesmaids woke up and trimmed their lamps. 
The foolish ones said to the sensible ones, 'Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out.' 
But the sensible ones answered, 'What if there isn't enough for us and you? Go instead to those who sell, and buy some for yourselves.' 
While they went away to buy, the bridegroom came, and those who were ready went in with him to the wedding feast, and the door was shut. 
Afterward the other bridesmaids also came, saying, 'Lord, Lord, open the door to us.' 
But he answered, 'Truly I tell you, I don't know you.' 
Stay awake, then, for you don't know the day or the hour when the Human One is coming. 
"It's like a man going to another country, who called his own slaves and handed over to them what he owned. 
To one he gave five bags of silver, to another two, to another one, to each according to his own ability. Then he went on his journey. 
Immediately the one who had received the five bags of silver went and traded with them, and gained another five. 
In the same way, the one who had received the two gained another two. 
But the one who had received the one bag of silver went away and dug a hole in the ground and hid his lord's money. 
"Now after a long time the lord of those slaves came and settled accounts with them. 
The one who had received the five bags of silver came and brought another five, saying, 'Lord, you handed me five bags of silver. Look, I have gained five more.' 
"His lord said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful slave. You have been faithful over a few things, so I'll set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.' 
"The one who had received the two bags of silver also came and said, 'Lord, you handed me two bags of silver. Look, I have gained two more.' 
"His lord said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful slave. You have been faithful over a few things, so I'll set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.' 
"The one who had received the one bag of silver also came and said, 'Lord, I knew that you're a hard man, reaping where you didn't sow, and gathering where you didn't scatter. 
I was afraid, and went away and hid your bag of silver in the ground. Look, you have what's yours.' 
"But his lord answered him, 'You malicious and lazy slave. You knew that I reap where I didn't sow, and gather where I didn't scatter. 
So you ought to have given my money to the bankers, and when I came I'd have received back my own with interest. 
So take the bag of silver away from him and give it to the one who has the ten. 
To everyone who has, more will be given, and he'll have plenty, but from the one who doesn't have, even what he has will be taken away. 
Throw the useless slave out into the outer darkness, where there will be crying and grinding of teeth.' 
"But when the Human One comes in his weight, and all the set-apart messengers with him, then he'll sit on the throne of his weight. 
All the nations will be gathered before him, and he'll separate them from one another, the way a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats. 
He'll put the sheep on his right, and the goats on the left. 
Then the King will say to those on his right, 'Come, you who are flourishing from my Father, be given charge of the reign prepared for you from the founding of the world, 
for I was hungry and you gave me food. I was thirsty and you gave me a drink. I was a stranger and you took me in. 
I was naked and you clothed me. I was sick and you visited me. I was in prison and you came to me.' 
"Then the upright will answer him, 'Lord, when did we see you hungry and give you food, or thirsty and give you a drink? 
When did we see you as a stranger and take you in, or naked and clothe you? 
When did we see you sick or in prison and come to you?' 
"The King will answer them, 'Truly I tell you, because you did it to one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did it to me.' 
Then he'll also say to those on the left, 'Get away from me, you who are cursed, into the fire of the era, which is prepared for the Slanderer and his messengers. 
I was hungry, and you didn't give me food. I was thirsty, and you gave me nothing to drink. 
I was a stranger, and you didn't take me in. Naked, and you didn't clothe me. Sick, and in prison, and you didn't visit me.' 
"Then they'll also answer, 'Lord, when did we see you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and didn't help you?' 
"Then he'll answer them, 'Truly I tell you, because you didn't do it to one of the least of these, you didn't do it to me.' 
These will go away into the correction of the era, but the upright into the real life of the era."
