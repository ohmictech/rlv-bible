Then we turned and set out into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea, as God told me, and we went around Mount Seir for many days. 
God spoke to me: 
"You have gone around this mountain long enough. Turn north. 
Command the people: 'You are about to pass through the territory of your brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be afraid of you, so be very careful. 
Don't start a fight with them, because I won't give you any of their land, not even enough for a footstep, since I have given Mount Seir to Esau as his property. 
Buy food from them with money so you can eat, and buy water from them with money so you can drink.'" 
Your God has blessed you in everything you have done. He has watched over your journey through this great wilderness. For forty years now your God has been with you, and you have lacked nothing. 
So we passed by our brothers, the descendants of Esau who live in Seir, along the Arabah road from Elath and Ezion Geber, and we turned and went by the way of the Moab wilderness. 
God said to me, "Don't trouble Moab or start a fight with them, because I won't give you any of their land as your property, since I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot." 
(The Emim lived there before, a great and numerous people, as tall as the Anakim. 
They too are counted as Rephaim, like the Anakim, but the Moabites call them Emim. 
The Horites also lived in Seir before, but the descendants of Esau drove them out and took their place, just as Israel did in the land God gave them.) 
"Now get up and cross the Zered valley." So we crossed the Zered valley. 
The time it took us to come from Kadesh Barnea until we crossed the Zered valley was thirty-eight years, until that whole generation of fighting men had died out from the camp, just as God had sworn to them. 
God's hand was against them too, wiping them out from the camp until they were gone. 
When all the fighting men had died out from among the people, 
God spoke to me: 
"Today you are to cross the border of Moab at Ar. 
When you come near the Ammonites, don't trouble them or start a fight with them, because I won't give you any of the Ammonites' land as your property. I have given it to the descendants of Lot." 
(That too is counted as a land of Rephaim. Rephaim lived there before, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim, 
a great and numerous people, as tall as the Anakim. But God wiped them out ahead of Israel, and they took their place and lived there, 
just as he did for the descendants of Esau in Seir, when he wiped out the Horites ahead of them, and they took their place and live there to this day. 
As for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim who came from Caphtor wiped them out and lived in their place.) 
"Get up, set out, and cross the Arnon valley. Look, I have given Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land into your hand. Begin to take it, and fight him. 
Today I will begin to put the fear and dread of you on the peoples under the whole sky. When they hear the report about you, they will tremble and be in anguish because of you." 
I sent messengers from the Kedemoth wilderness to Sihon king of Heshbon with an offer of peace: 
"Let me pass through your land. I will stay on the main road and not turn right or left. 
Sell me food for money so I can eat, and give me water for money so I can drink. Just let me walk through, 
as the descendants of Esau in Seir and the Moabites in Ar let me, until I cross the Jordan into the land our God is giving us." 
But Sihon king of Heshbon wouldn't let us pass, because your God made his spirit stubborn and his heart hard, so that he could hand him over to you, as happened today. 
God said to me, "Look, I have begun to give Sihon and his land over to you. Start taking it, so you can take his land as your own." 
Then Sihon came out against us with all his people to fight at Jahaz. 
Our God handed him over to us, and we struck him down with his sons and all his people. 
We captured all his cities at that time and completely destroyed every town, with the women and children. We left no one alive. 
Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, along with what we took from the cities. 
From Aroer on the edge of the Arnon valley, and the town in the valley, all the way to Gilead, there was no city too strong for us. Our God gave us all of it. 
You didn't go near the land of the Ammonites, or any of the Jabbok riverbank, or the towns in the hill country, or anywhere our God told us not to go.
