"So love your God and always keep his requirements, his rules, his decisions and his commands. 
Remember today that I am not speaking to your children, who have never known and have never seen the discipline of your God, his greatness, his strong hand and his outstretched arm, 
his signs and his works, which he did in the middle of Egypt to Pharaoh the king of Egypt and to all his land, 
and what he did to the army of Egypt, to their horses and chariots, how he flooded them with the water of the Red Sea when they chased you, and how God has destroyed them right up to this day, 
and what he did for you in the wilderness until you came to this place, 
and what he did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab son of Reuben, when the ground opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households, their tents and every living thing that belonged to them, right in the middle of all Israel. 
But your own eyes have seen all the great work God did. 
So keep the whole command I am giving you today, so that you may be strong and go in and take the land you are crossing over to take, 
and so that you may live long in the land God swore to your fathers and to their descendants, a land flowing with milk and honey. 
The land you are going in to take isn't like the land of Egypt you came out of, where you planted your seed and watered it by foot, like a vegetable garden. 
The land you are crossing over to take is a land of hills and valleys that drinks water from the rain of the sky, 
a land your God cares for. The eyes of your God are on it all year long, from the beginning of the year to the end of it. 
If you carefully listen to my commands that I am giving you today, to love your God and serve him with all your heart and all your life, 
then I will give your land rain in its season, the early rain and the late rain, so you can gather in your grain, your new wine and your oil. 
I will give grass in your fields for your livestock, and you will eat and be full. 
Be careful that your heart isn't fooled and you turn away to serve other gods and bow down to them, 
and God's anger burns against you and he shuts up the sky so there is no rain and the land doesn't produce its fruit, and you quickly die off the good land God is giving you. 
So store up these words of mine in your heart and in your life. Tie them as a sign on your hand, and let them be a mark on your forehead. 
Teach them to your children, talking about them when you sit at home, when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up. 
Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates, 
so that your days and the days of your children may be many in the land God swore to your fathers to give them, as long as the sky lasts above the earth. 
If you carefully keep all these commands I am giving you, and do them, loving your God, walking in all his ways and holding on to him, 
then God will drive all these nations out ahead of you, and you will take over nations greater and stronger than yourselves. 
Every place your foot steps on will be yours. Your border will run from the wilderness to Lebanon, and from the Euphrates River to the western sea. 
No one will be able to stand against you. Your God will put the fear and the dread of you on all the land you step on, just as he has said to you. 
Look, today I am setting before you a blessing and a curse: 
the blessing, if you listen to the commands of your God that I am giving you today, 
and the curse, if you don't listen to the commands of your God and you turn off the road I am giving you today to go after other gods you have never known. 
When your God brings you into the land you are going to take, you are to set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal. 
Aren't they across the Jordan, beyond the road that runs west, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah near Gilgal, beside the oaks of Moreh? 
You are about to cross the Jordan to go in and take the land your God is giving you. You will take it and live in it. 
So be careful to do all the rules and decisions I am setting before you today."
