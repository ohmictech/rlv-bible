God said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them, 'A priest won't defile himself for the dead among his people, 
except for his relatives that are near to him: for his mother, for his father, for his son, for his daughter, for his brother, 
and for his virgin sister who's near to him, who has had no husband; for her he may defile himself. 
He won't defile himself, being a chief man among his people, to profane himself. 
"'They won't shave their heads or shave off the corners of their beards or make any cuttings in their flesh. 
They will be holy to their God, and not profane the name of their God, for they offer the offerings of God made by fire, the bread of their God. So they will be holy. 
"'They won't marry a woman who's a prostitute, or profane. A priest won't marry a woman divorced from her husband; for he's holy to his God. 
So you will sanctify him, for he offers the bread of your God. He will be holy to you, for I God, who sanctify you, am holy. 
"'The daughter of any priest, if she profanes herself by playing the prostitute, she profanes her father. She will be burned with fire. 
"'He who's the high priest among his brothers, upon whose head the anointing oil is poured, and who's consecrated to put on the garments, won't let the hair of his head hang loose, or tear his clothes. 
He must not go in to any dead body, or defile himself for his father or for his mother. 
He won't go out of the sanctuary, nor profane the sanctuary of his God; for the crown of the anointing oil of his God is upon him. I'm God. 
"'He will take a wife in her virginity. 
He won't marry a widow, or one divorced, or a woman who has been defiled, or a prostitute. He will take a virgin of his own people as a wife. 
He won't profane his offspring among his people, for I'm God who sanctifies him.'" 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Say to Aaron, 'None of your offspring throughout their generations who has a defect may approach to offer the bread of his God. 
Whatever man he's that has a defect, he won't draw near: a blind man, or a lame, or he who has a flat nose, or any deformity, 
or a man who has an injured foot, or an injured hand, 
or hunchbacked, or a dwarf, or one who has a defect in his eye, or an itching disease, or scabs, or who has damaged testicles. 
No man of the offspring of Aaron the priest who has a defect will come near to offer the offerings of God made by fire. Since he has a defect, he won't come near to offer the bread of his God. 
He will eat the bread of his God, both of the most holy, and of the holy. 
He won't come near to the veil, nor come near to the altar, because he has a defect; that he may not profane my sanctuaries, for I'm God who sanctifies them.'" 
So Moses spoke to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the children of Israel.
