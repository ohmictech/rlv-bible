Peter and John were going up into the temple courts at the hour of prayer, mid-afternoon. 
A certain man who was unable to walk from his mother's womb was being carried, whom they laid daily at the door of the temple which is called Beautiful, to ask for gifts for the needy from those entering the temple. 
Seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple courts, he asked for gifts for the needy. 
Peter looked straight at him, as did John, and said, "Look at us." 
He listened to them, expecting to receive something from them. 
But Peter said, "I have no silver or gold, but what I have, that I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk!" 
He took him by the right hand and raised him up. Immediately his feet and his ankle bones received strength. 
He jumped up, stood, and began to walk. He entered with them into the temple courts, walking, leaping, and praising God. 
Everyone saw him walking and praising God. 
They recognized him, that it was he who used to sit begging for gifts for the needy at the Beautiful Gate of the temple. They were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him. 
As the lame man who was healed held on to Peter and John, everyone ran together to them in the covered walkway that is called Solomon's, greatly wondering. 
When Peter saw it, he responded to the people, "Men of Israel, why are you amazed at this? Why are you staring at us, as though by our own power or devotion to God we had made him walk? 
The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has given honor to his Servant Jesus, whom you handed over and disowned in the presence of Pilate, when he had determined to release him. 
But you disowned the Holy and Upright One and asked for a murderer to be granted to you, 
and killed the Author of life, whom God raised from the dead, to which we are witnesses. 
By trust in his name, his name has made this man strong, whom you see and know. Yes, the trust which is through him has given him this complete healing in the presence of you all. 
"Now, brothers and sisters, I know that you did this in ignorance, as did also your rulers. 
But the things which God announced by the mouth of all his spokesmen, that Christ should suffer, he thus fulfilled. 
"Have a change of heart, and turn again, that your mistakes may be blotted out, so that there may come times of refreshing from the presence of the Lord, 
and that he may send Christ Jesus, who was ordained for you before, 
whom the sky must receive until the times of restoration of all things, which God spoke long ago by the mouth of his set-apart spokesmen. 
Moses indeed said to the fathers, 'The Lord God will raise up a spokesman for you from among your brothers, like me. You shall listen to him in all things whatever he says to you. 
It will be that every person that won't listen to that spokesman will be rooted out from among the people.' 
Yes, and all the spokesmen from Samuel and those who followed after, as many as have spoken, also told of these days. 
You are the children of the spokesmen, and of the binding arrangement which God made with our ancestors, saying to Abraham, 'All the families of the land will be blessed through your offspring.' 
God, having raised up his servant Jesus, sent him to you first to bless you, in turning away every one of you from your malice."
