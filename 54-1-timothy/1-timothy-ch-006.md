Everyone who is a slave under the yoke should count their own masters worthy of full honor, so that God's name and the teaching are not slandered. 
Those who have masters who trust should not look down on them because they are brothers and sisters. They should serve them all the better, because those who benefit from their work trust and are truly loved. Teach these things and urge them. 
If anyone teaches something different and does not agree with healthy words, the words of our Lord Jesus Christ, and with the teaching that leads to devotion to God, 
he is swollen with conceit and knows nothing. He is sick with a craving for arguments and fights over words, and out of that come envy, quarreling, insults, and malicious suspicions, 
and endless friction between people whose minds are rotted and who have been robbed of the truth, who think that devotion to God is a way to make money. Keep away from people like that. 
But devotion to God with contentment is great wealth. 
We brought nothing into the world, and we certainly can't carry anything out. 
So if we have food and clothing, we will be content with that. 
But those who are set on getting rich fall into temptation and a trap, and into many foolish and harmful cravings that drag people down into ruin and brokenness. 
For the love of money is a root of all kinds of bad. Some have reached for it and been led astray from the trust, and have run themselves through with many sorrows. 
But you, man of God, run from all that, and go after rightness, devotion to God, trust, love, staying power, and gentleness. 
Fight the good fight of trust. Take hold of the real life of the era, to which you were called when you made your good confession in front of many witnesses. 
Before God, who gives life to everything, and before Christ Jesus, who in front of Pontius Pilate made the good confession, I command you 
to keep the command unstained and above reproach until our Lord Jesus Christ appears, 
which he will make visible at the right time, he who is the flourishing and only Ruler, the King of kings and Lord of lords. 
He alone is undying and lives in light no one can come near, whom no one has seen or can see. To him be honor and might for the eras. Amen. 
Tell those who are rich in this present era not to look down on others, and not to set their expectation on riches, which are so uncertain, but on the living God, who gives us everything richly to enjoy; 
tell them to do good, to be rich in good deeds, to be generous, and ready to share. 
In this way they store up a good foundation for the time to come, so that they may take hold of the real life. 
Timothy, guard what has been put in your care. Turn away from the empty talk and the contradictions of what is falsely called knowledge, 
which some have claimed and then missed the way of the trust. Favor be with you. Amen.
