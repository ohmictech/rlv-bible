Praise God! Praise God from the sky! Praise him in the heights! 
Praise him, all his messengers! Praise him, all his army! 
Praise him, sun and moon! Praise him, all you shining stars! 
Praise him, you skies above the skies, you waters that are above the sky. 
Let them praise God's name, for he commanded, and they were created. 
He has also established them through all the eras. He has made a decree which won't pass away. 
Praise God from the earth, you great sea creatures, and all depths, 
lightning and hail, snow and clouds, stormy wind, fulfilling his word, 
mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars, 
wild animals and all livestock, small creatures and flying birds, 
kings of the earth and all peoples, princes and all judges of the earth, 
both young men and maidens, old men and children. 
Let them praise God's name, for his name alone is exalted. His glory is above the earth and the sky. 
He has lifted up the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints, even of the children of Israel, a people near to him. Praise God!
