Now God's word came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying, 
"Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and preach against it, for their guilt has come up before me." 
But Jonah rose up to flee to Tarshish from the presence of God. He went down to Joppa, and found a ship going to Tarshish; so he paid its fare, and went down into it, to go with them to Tarshish from the presence of God. 
But God sent out a great wind on the sea, and there was a mighty storm on the sea, so that the ship was likely to break up. 
Then the mariners were afraid, and every man cried to his god. They threw the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten the ship. But Jonah had gone down into the innermost parts of the ship and he was laying down, and was fast asleep. 
So the ship master came to him, and said to him, "What do you mean, sleeper? Arise, call on your God! Maybe your God will notice us, so that we won't perish." 
They all said to each other, "Come! Let's cast lots, that we can know who's responsible for this bad that's on us." So they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah. 
Then they asked him, "Tell us, please, for whose cause this bad is on us. What's your occupation? Where do you come from? What's your country? Of what people are you?" 
He said to them, "I'm a Hebrew, and I fear the God of the sky, who has made the sea and the dry land." 
Then the men were exceedingly afraid, and said to him, "What have you done?" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the presence of God, because he had told them. 
Then they said to him, "What will we do to you, that the sea may be calm to us?" For the sea grew more and more stormy. 
He said to them, "Take me up, and throw me into the sea. Then the sea will be calm for you; for I know that because of me this great storm is on you." 
Nevertheless the men rowed hard to get them back to the land; but they couldn't, for the sea grew more and more stormy against them. 
So they cried to God, and said, "We beg you, God, we beg you, don't let's die for this man's life, and don't lay on us innocent blood; for you, God, have done as it pleased you." 
So they took up Jonah and threw him into the sea; and the sea ceased its raging. 
Then the men feared God exceedingly; and they offered a sacrifice to God and made vows. 
God prepared a huge fish to swallow up Jonah, and Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.
