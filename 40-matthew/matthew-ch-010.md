He called his twelve students to himself, and gave them authority over unclean spirits, to drive them out, and to heal every disease and every sickness. 
Now the names of the twelve sent ones are these. The first, Simon, who's called Peter. Andrew, his brother. James the son of Zebedee. John, his brother. 
Philip. Bartholomew. Thomas. Matthew the tax collector. James the son of Alphaeus. Lebbaeus, who was also called Thaddaeus. 
Simon the Zealot. And Judas Iscariot, who also handed him over. 
Jesus sent these twelve out and commanded them, saying, "Don't go among the nations, and don't enter into any city of the Samaritans. 
Rather, go to the lost sheep of the house of Israel. 
As you go, preach, saying, 'The reign of the sky is at hand!' 
Heal the sick, make the men with leprosy clean, and drive out unclean spirits. Freely you received, so freely give. 
Don't take any gold, silver, or brass in your money belts. 
Take no bag for your journey, nor two coats, nor sandals, nor staff, for the worker is worthy of his food. 
Into whatever city or village you enter, find out who in it's worthy, and stay there until you go on. 
As you enter into the household, greet it. 
If the household is worthy, let your wholeness come on it, but if it isn't worthy, let your wholeness return to you. 
Whoever doesn't receive you or hear your words, as you go out of that house or that city, shake the dust off your feet. 
Truly I tell you, it'll be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah in the day of verdict than for that city. 
"Look, I send you out as sheep among wolves. So be as wise as snakes and as harmless as doves. 
But beware of people, for they'll hand you over to councils, and in their gathering places they'll whip you. 
Yes, and you'll be brought before governors and kings for my sake, as a witness to them and to the nations. 
But when they hand you over, don't worry about how or what you'll say, because what you're to say will be given to you in that hour. 
It isn't you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father who speaks in you. 
"Brother will hand over brother to death, and the father his child. Children will rise up against parents and cause them to be put to death. 
You'll be hated by everyone for my name's sake, but he who sticks it out to the end will be rescued. 
But when they hunt you down in this city, flee into the next, for truly I tell you, you won't have gone through the cities of Israel until the Human One has come. 
"A student isn't above his teacher, nor a slave above his lord. 
It's enough for the student that he be like his teacher, and the slave like his lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more those of his household! 
So don't be afraid of them, for there's nothing covered that won't be unveiled, or hidden that won't be known. 
What I tell you in the darkness, speak in the light, and what you hear whispered in the ear, proclaim on the housetops. 
Don't be afraid of those who kill the body but can't kill the life. Fear instead the one who can break up both the life and the body in Gehenna. 
"Aren't two sparrows sold for a small coin? Not one of them falls to the ground without your Father. 
But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. 
So don't be afraid. You're of more value than many sparrows. 
So everyone who acknowledges me in front of people, I'll also acknowledge in front of my Father in the sky. 
But whoever disowns me in front of people, I'll also disown him in front of my Father in the sky. 
"Don't think that I came to send wholeness on the earth. I didn't come to send wholeness, but a sword. 
I came to set a man at odds against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. 
A man's enemies will be those of his own household. 
Whoever loves father or mother more than me isn't worthy of me, and whoever loves son or daughter more than me isn't worthy of me. 
Whoever doesn't take up his cross and follow me isn't worthy of me. 
He who seeks his life will lose it, and he who loses his life for my sake will find it. 
"He who receives you receives me, and he who receives me receives the one who sent me. 
He who receives a spokesman because he's a spokesman will receive a spokesman's earned result. He who receives an upright man because he's upright will receive an upright man's earned result. 
Whoever gives one of these little ones just a cup of cold water to drink because he's a student, truly I tell you, he'll in no way lose his earned result."
