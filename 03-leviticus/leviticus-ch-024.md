God spoke to Moses: 
"Command the Israelites to bring you pure olive oil, pressed for the light, so the lamp keeps burning all the time. 
Outside the curtain of the Testimony, in the Tent of Meeting, Aaron is to keep it in order from evening to morning before God continually. This is a perpetual rule for all your generations. 
He is to keep the lamps in order on the pure gold lampstand before God continually. 
"Take fine flour and bake twelve loaves from it, two tenths of an ephah for each loaf. 
Set them out in two rows, six to a row, on the pure gold table before God. 
Put pure frankincense on each row, to be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering made by fire to God. 
Every Sabbath day he is to set it out before God continually. It is a perpetual covenant on behalf of the Israelites. 
It belongs to Aaron and his sons, who are to eat it in a set apart place, because it is most holy to him from God's offerings made by fire, by a perpetual rule." 
A man whose mother was an Israelite and whose father was an Egyptian went out among the Israelites, and this son of the Israelite woman got into a fight with an Israelite man in the camp. 
The son of the Israelite woman spoke badly of the Name and cursed it, so they brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan. 
They kept him in custody until God's will was made clear to them. 
God spoke to Moses: 
"Take the one who cursed outside the camp, and let everyone who heard him put their hands on his head, and let the whole community stone him. 
Say to the Israelites, 'Anyone who curses his God will carry his guilt. 
Anyone who speaks badly of God's name is to be put to death. The whole community is to stone him. The foreigner as well as the native born is to be put to death for speaking badly of the Name. 
"'Anyone who strikes another person so that he dies is to be put to death. 
Anyone who strikes an animal so that it dies is to make it good, a life for a life. 
If anyone injures his neighbor, it is to be done to him as he did: 
a break for a break, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth. Whatever injury he gave someone is to be given to him. 
Whoever kills an animal is to make it good, and whoever kills a person is to be put to death. 
You are to have the same law for the foreigner and the native born, because I am your God.'" 
Moses spoke to the Israelites, and they took the one who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him. The Israelites did just as God commanded Moses.
