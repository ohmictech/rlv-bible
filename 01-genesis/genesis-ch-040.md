Some time later, the king of Egypt's cupbearer and his baker did something wrong against their master, the king of Egypt. 
Pharaoh was angry with these two officials, the chief cupbearer and the chief baker. 
So he put them under guard in the house of the captain of the guard, in the same prison where Joseph was being held. 
The captain of the guard assigned Joseph to look after them, and he served them. They spent a good while in custody. 
One night the cupbearer and the baker of the king of Egypt, who were locked up in the prison, each had a dream, and each dream had its own meaning. 
When Joseph came in to them the next morning, he saw they looked miserable. 
So he asked Pharaoh's officials who were in custody with him in his master's house, "Why do you look so down today?" 
They said, "We both had dreams, and there's nobody here who can tell us what they mean." Joseph said, "Doesn't interpretation belong to God? Tell me your dreams." 
So the chief cupbearer told Joseph his dream. He said, "In my dream there was a vine right in front of me, 
and on the vine were three branches. It budded, blossomed, and its clusters ripened into grapes. 
I was holding Pharaoh's cup, so I took the grapes and squeezed them into it, and put the cup in Pharaoh's hand." 
Joseph said, "Here's what it means. The three branches are three days. 
In three days Pharaoh will lift up your head and give you back your position, and you'll hand Pharaoh his cup again, just as you did when you were his cupbearer. 
But when things go well for you, remember me. Do me a kindness and mention me to Pharaoh, and get me out of this place. 
I was kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews, and even here I've done nothing to deserve being thrown in a dungeon." 
When the chief baker saw that Joseph had given a good interpretation, he said, "I had a dream too. There were three baskets of bread on my head, 
and in the top basket was every kind of pastry for Pharaoh, and the birds were eating it out of the basket on my head." 
Joseph answered, "Here's what it means. The three baskets are three days. 
In three days Pharaoh will lift your head off you and hang you on a tree, and the birds will eat your flesh." 
Three days later it was Pharaoh's birthday, and he held a feast for all his servants. He brought the chief cupbearer and the chief baker out in front of them, 
and he gave the chief cupbearer his position back, so that he handed the cup to Pharaoh again. 
But he hanged the chief baker, just as Joseph had said. 
Still, the chief cupbearer didn't remember Joseph. He forgot all about him.
