Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the gathering of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: 
Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
Brothers and sisters, we have to keep giving thanks to God for you, as is only right, because your trust is growing by leaps and bounds and the love each of you has for one another keeps overflowing. 
So we take pride in you among the gatherings of God, because of your staying power and trust in all the persecutions and hard times you're putting up with. 
This is clear proof of God's right verdict, that you'll be counted worthy of God's reign, and it's for that reign that you suffer. 
God does what's right when he pays back hard times to those who give you hard times, 
and gives relief to you who are in hard times, along with us. This will happen when the Lord Jesus is unveiled from the sky with his powerful messengers in blazing fire, 
bringing payback on those who don't know God and on those who won't listen to the Good News of our Lord Jesus. 
They'll pay the penalty: the ruin of the era, coming from the face of the Lord and from the weight of his might, 
when he comes on that day to be honored among his set-apart ones and to be admired by everyone who has trusted, and you trusted our witness to you. 
That's what we always pray for you: that our God may count you worthy of the calling he gave you, and may bring to completion with power every good intent and every work of trust, 
so that the name of our Lord Jesus may be honored in you, and you in him, by the favor of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
