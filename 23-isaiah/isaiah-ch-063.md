Who's this who comes from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah? Who's this who's glorious in his clothing, marching in the greatness of his strength? "It's I who speak in righteousness, mighty to save." 
Why is your clothing red, and your garments like him who treads in the wine vat? 
"I have trodden the wine press alone. Of the peoples, no one was with me. Yes, I trod them in my anger and trampled them in my wrath. Their lifeblood is sprinkled on my garments, and I have stained all my clothing. 
The day of vengeance was in my heart, and the year of my bought back has come. 
I looked, and there was no one to help; and I wondered that there was no one to uphold. So my own arm brought salvation to me. My own wrath upheld me. 
I trod down the peoples in my anger and made them drunk in my wrath. I poured their lifeblood out on the earth." 
I'll tell of the loving kindnesses of God and the praises of God, according to all that God has given to us, and the great goodness toward the house of Israel, which he has given to them according to his mercies, and according to the multitude of his loving kindnesses. 
He said, "Surely, they're my people, children who won't deal falsely;" so he became their Savior. 
In all their affliction he was afflicted, and the messenger of his presence saved them. In his love and in his pity he bought back them. He bore them, and carried them all the days of old. 
But they rebelled and grieved his Holy Spirit. So he turned and became their enemy, and he himself fought against them. 
Then he remembered the days of old, Moses and his people, saying, "Where is he who brought them up out of the sea with the shepherds of his flock? Where is he who put his Holy Spirit among them?" 
Who caused his glorious arm to be at Moses' right hand? Who divided the waters before them, to make himself a perpetual name? 
Who led them through the depths, like a horse in the wilderness, so that they didn't stumble? 
As the livestock that go down into the valley, God's Spirit caused them to rest. So you led your people to make yourself a glorious name. 
Look down from the sky, and see from the habitation of your holiness and of your glory. Where are your passion and your mighty acts? The yearning of your heart and your compassion is restrained toward me. 
You're our Father, though Abraham doesn't know us, and Israel doesn't acknowledge us. You, God, are our Father. the one who buys us back from perpetual is your name. 
O God, why do you make us wander from your ways, and harden our heart from your fear? Return for your servants' sake, the tribes of your inheritance. 
Your holy people possessed it but a little while. Our adversaries have trodden down your sanctuary. 
We have become like those over whom you never ruled, like those who weren't called by your name.
