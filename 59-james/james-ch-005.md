Now listen, you rich people: weep and wail, because misery is coming on you. 
Your wealth has rotted, and your clothes are full of moth holes. 
Your gold and your silver have tarnished, and that tarnish will stand as a witness against you and will eat your flesh like fire. You have piled up treasure in the last days. 
Look, the pay of the workers who mowed your fields, the pay you held back by cheating, is crying out, and the cries of those who reaped have reached the ears of the Lord of Armies. 
You have lived in luxury and pleasure on the earth. You have fattened your hearts for the day of slaughter. 
You have passed sentence on the upright one and murdered them, and they do not resist you. 
So be patient, brothers and sisters, until the arrival of the Lord. Look how the farmer waits for the precious crop from the ground, patient with it until the early and the late rain fall. 
You also be patient. Make your hearts firm, because the arrival of the Lord is near. 
Brothers and sisters, don't grumble against one another, so that you are not judged. Look, the Judge is standing at the door. 
Brothers and sisters, take the spokesmen who spoke in the Lord's name as your example of suffering and of staying power. 
We call those who held on flourishing. You have heard of the staying power of Job, and you have seen how the Lord ended it, and that the Lord is full of deep compassion and mercy. 
But above all things, my brothers and sisters, don't swear, not by the sky, or by the earth, or by any other oath; but let your "yes" be "yes", and your "no", "no", so that you don't fall into pretending. 
Is any among you in trouble? Let them pray. Is any cheerful? Let them sing praises. 
Is any among you sick? Let them call for the elders of the gathering, and let them pray over them, anointing them with oil in the Lord's name; 
and the prayer of trust will heal the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise them up. If they have committed mistakes, they will be released. 
Confess your mistakes to one another, and pray for one another, so that you may be healed. The earnest prayer of an upright person is powerful and at work. 
Elijah was a person just like us, and he prayed earnestly that it might not rain, and it didn't rain on the land for three years and six months. 
Then he prayed again, and the sky gave rain, and the land produced its crop. 
My brothers and sisters, if anyone among you wanders away from the truth and someone brings them back, 
remember this: whoever turns a person who keeps drifting away from the error of their way will rescue their life from death and will cover over a great many mistakes.
