Sing to God a new song! Sing to God, all the earth. 
Sing to God! Bless his name! Proclaim his salvation from day to day! 
Declare his glory among the nations, his marvelous works among everyones. 
God is great, and greatly to be praised! He's to be feared above all gods. 
All the gods of the peoples are idols, but God made the sky. 
Honor and majesty are before him. Strength and beauty are in his sanctuary. 
Ascribe to God, you families of nations, ascribe to God glory and strength. 
Ascribe to God the glory due to his name. Bring an offering, and come into his courts. 
Worship God in holy array. Tremble before him, all the earth. 
Say among the nations, "God reigns." The world is also established. It can't be moved. He'll judge the peoples with equity. 
Let the sky be glad, and let the earth rejoice. Let the sea roar, and its fullness! 
Let the field and all that's in it exult! Then all the trees of the woods will sing for joy 
before God; for he comes, for he comes to judge the earth. He'll judge the world with righteousness, the peoples with his truth.
