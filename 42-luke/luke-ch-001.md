Since many have undertaken to set in order a narrative concerning those matters which have been fulfilled among us, 
even as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed them down to us, 
it seemed good to me also, having carefully traced everything from the first, to write it out for you in order, most excellent Theophilus; 
so that you would know the certainty of the things in which you were taught. 
In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a priest named Zacharias, of the priestly division of Abijah. His wife was one of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth. 
They were both upright before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and rules of the Lord. 
But they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they both were well advanced in years. 
Now while he executed the priest's office before God in the order of his division 
according to the custom of the priestly office, he was chosen by lot to go into the temple of the Lord and burn incense. 
The whole crowd of the people was praying outside at the time of the incense offering. 
A messenger of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense. 
Zacharias was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him. 
But the messenger said to him, "Don't be afraid, Zacharias, because your request has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you are to name him John. 
You will have joy and gladness, and many will celebrate at his birth. 
For he will be great in the sight of the Lord, and he will drink no wine nor strong drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit, even from his mother's womb. 
He will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God. 
He will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, 'to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children,' and to turn those who refuse to obey to the wisdom of the upright, to make ready a people prepared for the Lord." 
Zacharias said to the messenger, "How can I be sure of this? For I am an old man, and my wife is well advanced in years." 
The messenger answered him, "I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God. I was sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news. 
Look, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day that these things will happen, because you didn't trust my words, which will be fulfilled in their proper time." 
The people were waiting for Zacharias, and they were amazed that he stayed so long in the temple. 
When he came out, he could not speak to them. They realized that he had seen a vision in the temple. He kept making signs to them and stayed unable to speak. 
When the days of his service were over, he went home. 
After these days Elizabeth his wife conceived, and she hid herself five months, saying, 
"This is what the Lord has done for me in the days when he looked on me, to take away my disgrace among people." 
Now in the sixth month, the messenger Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth, 
to a virgin pledged to be married to a man named Joseph, of David's house. The virgin's name was Mary. 
Having come in, the messenger said to her, "Celebrate, you who are highly favored! The Lord is with you. Flourishing are you among women!" 
But when she saw him, she was greatly troubled by his words and wondered what kind of greeting this might be. 
The messenger said to her, "Don't be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. 
Look, you will conceive in your womb and give birth to a son, and you are to name him 'Jesus.' 
He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his father David, 
and he will reign over the house of Jacob into the eras. There will be no end to his reign." 
Mary said to the messenger, "How can this be, since I am a virgin?" 
The messenger answered her, "The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. So the set-apart one born from you will be called the Son of God. 
Look, Elizabeth your relative has also conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month for her who was called barren. 
For nothing spoken by God is impossible." 
Mary said, "Look, I am the Lord's slave. Let it be done to me just as you have said." Then the messenger departed from her. 
Mary got up in those days and hurried into the hill country, into a city of Judah, 
and went into the house of Zacharias and greeted Elizabeth. 
When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit. 
She called out with a loud voice and said, "Flourishing are you among women, and flourishing is the fruit of your womb! 
Why am I so favored, that the mother of my Lord should come to me? 
For look, as soon as the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the baby leaped in my womb for joy! 
Flourishing is she who trusted, for there will be a fulfillment of the things which have been spoken to her from the Lord!" 
Mary said, "My life makes the Lord great. 
My spirit has celebrated in God my Restorer, 
for he has looked at the humble state of his slave. For look, from now on, all generations will call me flourishing. 
For he who is mighty has done great things for me. Holy is his name. 
His mercy is for generations and generations on those who fear him. 
He has shown strength with his arm. He has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts. 
He has brought rulers down from their thrones, and has lifted up the lowly. 
He has filled the hungry with good things. He has sent the rich away empty. 
He has helped Israel, his servant, remembering mercy, 
as he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and his offspring forever." 
Mary stayed with her about three months, and then returned to her house. 
Now the time came for Elizabeth to give birth, and she gave birth to a son. 
Her neighbors and her relatives heard that the Lord had shown her great mercy, and they celebrated with her. 
On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child; and they were going to name him Zacharias, after his father. 
His mother answered, "No, he is to be called John." 
They said to her, "There is no one among your relatives who is called by this name." 
They made signs to his father to find out what he wanted him called. 
He asked for a writing tablet, and wrote, "His name is John." They were all amazed. 
His mouth was opened immediately and his tongue freed, and he spoke, blessing God. 
Fear came on everyone who lived around them, and all these sayings were talked about throughout the whole hill country of Judea. 
Everyone who heard them kept them in their heart, saying, "What then will this child be?" The hand of the Lord was with him. 
His father Zacharias was filled with the Holy Spirit, and spoke for God, saying, 
"Praised be the Lord, the God of Israel, for he has come to his people and set them free. 
and has raised up a mighty deliverer for us in the house of his servant David 
(as he spoke by the mouth of his set-apart spokesmen who have been from of old), 
rescue from our enemies and from the hand of everyone who hates us, 
to show mercy toward our fathers, to remember his set-apart binding arrangement, 
the oath which he swore to Abraham our father, 
to grant to us that we, having been rescued out of the hand of our enemies, should serve him without fear, 
in set-apartness and rightness before him all the days of our life. 
And you, child, will be called a spokesman of the Most High; for you will go before the Lord to prepare his ways, 
to give his people the knowledge of healing and wholeness by the release of their mistakes, 
because of the tender mercy of our God, by which the sunrise from on high will visit us, 
to shine on those who sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, and to guide our feet into the way of wholeness." 
The child was growing and becoming strong in spirit, and was in the wilderness until the day of his public appearance to Israel.
