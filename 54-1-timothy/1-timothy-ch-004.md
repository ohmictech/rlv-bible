But the Spirit says plainly that in later times some will walk away from the trust, paying attention to spirits that lead people astray and to teachings that come from unclean spirits, 
through the pretending of liars whose own conscience has been seared as with a hot iron. 
They forbid marriage and tell people to keep away from foods that God made to be received with thanksgiving by those who trust and know the truth. 
For everything God made is good, and nothing is to be thrown away if it is received with thanksgiving. 
It is set apart by God's word and by prayer. 
If you put these things before the brothers and sisters, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, fed on the words of the trust and of the good teaching you have followed. 
Have nothing to do with worldly old women's stories. Train yourself instead for devotion to God. 
Training the body has some value, but devotion to God has value in every way, since it carries the promise of life now and of the life to come. 
This saying is trustworthy and deserves to be accepted without question. 
That is what we work and struggle for, because we have set our expectation on the living God, who is the Restorer of everyone, and most of all of those who trust. 
Command these things and teach them. 
Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young. Be an example to those who trust, in what you say, in how you live, in love, in spirit, in trust, and in purity. 
Until I come, give your attention to reading, to encouragement, and to teaching. 
Don't neglect the gift in you, which was given to you through words spoken for God when the elders laid their hands on you. 
Keep at these things; give yourself to them completely, so that everyone can see your progress. 
Watch your own life and your teaching. Keep at them, because by doing this you will rescue both yourself and those who listen to you.
