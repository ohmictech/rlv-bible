"Hear, Israel! Today you are crossing the Jordan to go in and take over nations greater and stronger than you, cities that are great and walled up to the sky, 
a people great and tall, the Anakim, whom you know about and have heard people say, 'Who can stand up to the sons of Anak?' 
Know today that your God is the one who goes ahead of you like a fire that consumes everything. He will destroy them and bring them down in front of you. So you will drive them out and finish them off quickly, as God has said to you. 
Don't say to yourself, after your God has pushed them out ahead of you, 'God brought me in to take this land because I am in the right.' No, God is driving those nations out ahead of you because of their guilt. 
It isn't because you are in the right or because your heart is honest that you are going in to take their land. It is because of the guilt of those nations that your God is driving them out ahead of you, and to keep the word God swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob. 
Know then that your God isn't giving you this good land to take because you are in the right. You are a stubborn, stiff-necked people. 
Remember this and never forget how you made your God angry in the wilderness. From the day you left Egypt until you arrived here, you have been rebelling against God. 
Even at Horeb you made God angry, and God was angry enough with you to destroy you. 
I had gone up the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant God made with you, and I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights. I ate no bread and drank no water. 
God gave me the two stone tablets written by God's own finger. On them was everything God said to you on the mountain out of the middle of the fire, on the day of the gathering. 
At the end of those forty days and forty nights, God gave me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant, 
and God said to me, 'Get up, go down quickly from here. Your people, the ones you brought out of Egypt, have ruined themselves. They have quickly turned off the road I commanded them. They have made themselves a metal image.' 
God also said to me, 'I have seen these people, and they are a stubborn, stiff-necked people. 
Leave me alone, and I will destroy them and wipe out their name from under the sky, and I will make a nation out of you that is stronger and greater than they are.' 
So I turned and came down the mountain, and the mountain was burning with fire, and the two tablets of the covenant were in my two hands. 
I looked, and there you had sinned against your God. You had made yourselves a calf out of melted metal. You had quickly turned off the road God commanded you. 
I grabbed the two tablets, threw them out of my hands and smashed them in front of your eyes. 
Then I lay face down before God, as at the first, forty days and forty nights. I ate no bread and drank no water, because of all the wrong you had done, doing what was bad in God's sight and making him angry. 
I was afraid of the anger and the fierce fury God had against you, enough to destroy you. But God listened to me that time too. 
God was angry enough with Aaron to destroy him, and I prayed for Aaron at the same time. 
Then I took that wrong thing of yours, the calf you had made, and burned it in the fire and crushed it and ground it down fine, until it was as fine as dust, and I threw the dust into the stream that runs down from the mountain. 
You made God angry at Taberah, at Massah and at Kibroth Hattaavah too. 
And when God sent you out from Kadesh Barnea and said, 'Go up and take the land I have given you,' you rebelled against the command of your God. You didn't believe him and you didn't listen to his voice. 
You have been rebelling against God from the day I first knew you. 
So I lay face down before God for those forty days and forty nights, because God had said he would destroy you. 
I prayed to God and said, 'Lord God, don't destroy your people, your own inheritance, the ones you bought back by your greatness, the ones you brought out of Egypt with a strong hand. 
Remember your servants Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Don't look at how stubborn this people is, or at their guilt, or at their wrong, 
or the land you brought us out of will say, "God wasn't able to bring them into the land he promised them, and he hated them, so he brought them out to kill them in the wilderness." 
But they are your people and your inheritance, the ones you brought out by your great power and your outstretched arm.'
