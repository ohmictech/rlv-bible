Then some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat before me. 
God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, these men have taken their idols into their heart, and put the stumbling block of their guilt before their face. Should I be inquired of at all by them? 
So speak to them and tell them, 'The God says: "Every man of the house of Israel who takes his idols into his heart and puts the stumbling block of his guilt before his face then comes to the spokesman, I God will answer him there according to the multitude of his idols, 
that I may take the house of Israel in their own heart, because they're all estranged from me through their idols."' 
"Therefore tell the house of Israel, 'The God says: "Return, and turn yourselves from your idols! Turn away your faces from all your abominations. 
"'"For everyone of the house of Israel, or of the strangers who live in Israel, who separates himself from me and takes his idols into his heart, and puts the stumbling block of his guilt before his face, and comes to the spokesman to inquire for himself of me, I God will answer him by myself. 
I'll set my face against that man and will make him an astonishment, for a sign and a proverb, and I'll cut him off from among my people. Then you'll know that I'm God. 
"'"If the spokesman is deceived and speaks a word, I, God, have deceived that spokesman, and I'll stretch out my hand on him, and will destroy him from among my people Israel. 
They'll bear their guilt. The guilt of the spokesman will be even as the guilt of him who seeks him, 
that the house of Israel may no more go astray from me, neither defile themselves any more with all their rebellion; but that they may be my people, and I may be their God," says God.'" 
God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, when a land wrongs me by committing a trespass, and I stretch out my hand on it, and break the staff of its bread and send famine on it, and cut off from it man and animal 
though these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, they'd deliver only their own souls by their righteousness," says God. 
"If I cause bad animals to pass through the land, and they ravage it and it's made desolate, so that no man can pass through because of the animals 
though these three men were in it, as I live," says God, "they'd deliver neither sons nor daughters. They only would be delivered, but the land would be desolate. 
"Or if I bring a sword on that land, and say, 'Sword, go through the land, so that I cut off from it man and animal' 
though these three men were in it, as I live," says God, "they'd deliver neither sons nor daughters, but they only would be delivered themselves. 
"Or if I send a pestilence into that land, and pour out my wrath on it in blood, to cut off from it man and animal 
though Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, as I live," says God, "they'd deliver neither son nor daughter; they'd deliver only their own souls by their righteousness." 
God says: "How much more when I send my four severe judgments on Jerusalem, the sword, the famine, the bad animals, and the pestilence, to cut off from it man and animal! 
Yet, look, there will be left a remnant in it that will be carried out, both sons and daughters. Look, they'll come out to you, and you'll see their way and their doings. Then you'll be comforted concerning the bad that I have brought on Jerusalem, even concerning all that I have brought on it. 
They'll comfort you, when you see their way and their doings; then you'll know that I haven't done all that I have done in it without cause," says God.
