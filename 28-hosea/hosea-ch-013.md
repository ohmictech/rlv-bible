When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling. He exalted himself in Israel, but when he became guilty through Baal, he died. 
Now they drift more and more, and have made themselves molten images of their silver, even idols according to their own understanding, everyone the work of the craftsmen. They say of them, 'They offer human sacrifice and kiss the calves.' 
Therefore they'll be like the morning mist, like the dew that passes away early, like the chaff that's driven with the whirlwind out of the threshing floor, and like the smoke out of the chimney. 
"Yet I'm your God from the land of Egypt; and you shall acknowledge no god but me, and besides me there's no savior. 
I knew you in the wilderness, in the land of great drought. 
According to their pasture, so were they filled; they were filled, and their heart was exalted. So they have forgotten me. 
So I'm like a lion to them. Like a leopard, I'll lurk by the path. 
I'll meet them like a bear that's bereaved of her cubs, and will tear the covering of their heart. There I'll devour them like a lioness. The wild animal will tear them. 
You're destroyed, Israel, because you're against me, against your helper. 
Where is your king now, that he may save you in all your cities? And your judges, of whom you said, 'Give me a king and princes'? 
I have given you a king in my anger, and have taken him away in my wrath. 
The guilt of Ephraim is stored up. His wrongdoing is stored up. 
The sorrows of a travailing woman will come on him. He's an unwise son, for when it's time, he doesn't come to the opening of the womb. 
I'll ransom them from the power of Hades. I'll buy them back from death! Death, where are your plagues? Hades, where is your destruction? "Compassion will be hidden from my eyes. 
Though he's fruitful among his brothers, an east wind will come, the breath of God coming up from the wilderness; and his spring will become dry, and his fountain will be dried up. He'll plunder the storehouse of treasure. 
Samaria will bear her guilt, for she has rebelled against her God. They'll fall by the sword. Their infants will be dashed in pieces, and their pregnant women will be ripped open."
