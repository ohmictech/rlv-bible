God spoke to Moses: 
"Take Aaron and his sons with him, the garments, the anointing oil, the bull of the offering for wrongdoing, the two rams, and the basket of bread made without yeast, 
and gather the whole community at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting." 
Moses did as God commanded, and the community gathered at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. 
Moses said to the community, "This is what God has commanded to be done." 
Moses brought Aaron and his sons and washed them with water. 
He put the tunic on Aaron, tied the sash on him, dressed him in the robe, put the ephod on him, and tied the woven band of the ephod and fastened it to him. 
He placed the breastplate on him, and put the Urim and Thummim in the breastplate. 
He set the turban on his head, and on the front of the turban he set the gold plate, the set apart crown, just as God commanded Moses. 
Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tent and everything in it, and set them apart. 
He splashed some of it on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand, to set them apart. 
He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him, to set him apart. 
Moses brought Aaron's sons, dressed them in tunics, tied sashes on them and put headbands on them, just as God commanded Moses. 
He brought the bull of the offering for wrongdoing, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 
He killed it, and Moses took the blood and put some of it on the horns of the altar with his finger, and cleansed the altar, and poured out the blood at the base of the altar, and set the altar apart, to make things right for it. 
He took all the fat that was on the inner organs, the covering of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and Moses burned it on the altar. 
But the bull, its skin, its meat and its dung he burned with fire outside the camp, just as God commanded Moses. 
He brought the ram of the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 
He killed it, and Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar. 
He cut the ram into pieces, and Moses burned the head, the pieces and the fat. 
He washed the inner organs and the legs with water, and Moses burned the whole ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering, a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to God, just as God commanded Moses. 
He brought the other ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head. 
He killed it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the tip of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot. 
He brought Aaron's sons, and Moses put some of the blood on the tip of their right ear, on the thumb of their right hand and on the big toe of their right foot, and Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar. 
He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat on the inner organs, the covering of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh, 
and from the basket of bread made without yeast that was before God he took one cake made without yeast, one cake of bread mixed with oil and one wafer, and put them on the fat and on the right thigh. 
He put all of it in Aaron's hands and in his sons' hands and waved them as a wave offering before God. 
Moses took them back from their hands and burned them on the altar, on top of the burnt offering. They were the ordination offering, a soothing aroma, an offering made by fire to God. 
Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before God. It was Moses' share of the ram of ordination, just as God commanded Moses. 
Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar and splashed it on Aaron, on his garments, on his sons, and on his sons' garments with him, and set Aaron apart, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons' garments with him. 
Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and eat it there with the bread from the basket of ordination, as I was commanded: 'Aaron and his sons will eat it.' 
Burn with fire whatever is left of the meat and the bread. 
Don't leave the entrance of the Tent of Meeting for seven days, until the days of your ordination are complete, because it takes seven days to ordain you. 
What has been done today is what God commanded, to make things right for you. 
You are to stay at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting day and night for seven days and do what God requires, so you don't die, because that is what I was commanded." 
Aaron and his sons did everything God commanded through Moses.
