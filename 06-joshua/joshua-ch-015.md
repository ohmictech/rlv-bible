The land allotted to the tribe of Judah, clan by clan, reached the border of Edom, as far as the wilderness of Zin in the far south. 
Their southern border began at the bay at the far end of the Salt Sea, looking south, 
and ran south of the ascent of Akrabbim, on past Zin, up south of Kadesh Barnea, past Hezron, up to Addar and around to Karka, 
and on to Azmon, coming out at the brook of Egypt and ending at the sea. That is your southern border. 
The eastern border was the Salt Sea as far as the mouth of the Jordan. The northern border began at the bay of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan, 
went up to Beth Hoglah, passed north of Beth Arabah and up to the stone of Bohan son of Reuben, 
then up to Debir from the valley of Achor, turning north toward Gilgal, opposite the ascent of Adummim south of the ravine. It went on to the waters of En Shemesh and ended at En Rogel. 
Then it went up the valley of the son of Hinnom to the south slope of the Jebusite city, that is, Jerusalem, and up to the top of the mountain west of the valley of Hinnom, at the north end of the valley of Rephaim. 
From the top of the mountain it went to the spring at Nephtoah, out to the towns of Mount Ephron, and on to Baalah, that is, Kiriath Jearim. 
Then it turned west from Baalah to Mount Seir, along the north side of Mount Jearim, that is, Chesalon, down to Beth Shemesh and on past Timnah. 
It went out to the north side of Ekron, on to Shikkeron, past Mount Baalah and out at Jabneel, ending at the sea. 
The western border was the shore of the Great Sea. These are the borders of the tribe of Judah all the way around, clan by clan. 
Joshua gave Caleb son of Jephunneh a share among the people of Judah, as God had commanded: Kiriath Arba, named after the father of Anak, that is, Hebron. 
Caleb drove out the three sons of Anak from there: Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai, the children of Anak. 
From there he went up against the people of Debir, which used to be called Kiriath Sepher. 
Caleb said, "Whoever attacks Kiriath Sepher and takes it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as his wife." 
Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, took it, so Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as his wife. 
When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She got off her donkey, and Caleb asked her, "What do you want?" 
She said, "Give me a blessing. Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me springs of water too." So he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs. 
This is the inheritance of the tribe of Judah, clan by clan.
