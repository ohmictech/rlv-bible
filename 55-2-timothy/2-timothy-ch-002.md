You, then, my child, be strong in the favor that is in Christ Jesus. 
What you heard from me in front of many witnesses, hand on to trustworthy people who will be able to teach others too. 
Share hardship with me, like a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 
No soldier on duty gets tangled up in the affairs of everyday life; he wants to please the one who enlisted him. 
And if someone competes as an athlete, he isn't given the wreath unless he competes by the rules. 
The farmer who does the hard work should be the first to get a share of the crops. 
Think about what I am saying, and the Lord will give you understanding in everything. 
Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, from the family line of David, that is my Good News. 
That is what I am suffering for, right up to being chained like a criminal. But God's word isn't chained. 
So I put up with everything for the sake of the chosen ones, so that they too may receive the rescue that is in Christ Jesus, with weight that lasts for the eras. 
This saying is trustworthy: "If we died with him, we will also live with him. 
If we hold on, we will also reign with him. If we disown him, he will disown us too. 
If we have no trust, he still stays trustworthy, because he can't disown himself." 
Keep reminding them of this, and warn them before the Lord not to fight over words. It does no good and only ruins those who hear. 
Do your best to present yourself to God as someone who has passed the test, a worker with nothing to be ashamed of, handling the word of truth the right way. 
But keep away from empty talk, because it only leads people further into disregard for God, 
and their talk will spread like gangrene. Hymenaeus and Philetus are among them. 
They have missed the way of the truth, saying that the rising again has already happened, and they are wrecking the trust of some. 
But God's firm foundation stands, with this stamp on it: "The Lord knows who belongs to him," and, "Everyone who names the name of the Lord must turn away from wrong." 
In a large house there aren't only dishes of gold and silver but also of wood and clay. Some are for honor and some for dishonor. 
So if anyone keeps himself clean from these, he will be a dish for honor, set apart, useful to the master, and ready for every good deed. 
Run from the cravings of youth, and go after rightness, trust, love, and wholeness, along with those who call on the Lord from a clean heart. 
Have nothing to do with foolish and ignorant arguments; you know they only start quarrels. 
The Lord's slave must not quarrel; he must be gentle with everyone, able to teach, and patient when wronged, 
correcting his opponents gently, in case God gives them a change of heart that leads them to know the truth fully, 
and they come to their senses and escape from the Slanderer's trap, now that they have been caught alive by him to do what he wants.
