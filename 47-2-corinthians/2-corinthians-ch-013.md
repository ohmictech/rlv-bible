This is the third time I'm coming to you. "At the mouth of two or three witnesses will every word be established." 
I warned you before, and I warn you again now, as I did when I was present the second time: to those who drifted before and to everyone else, if I come again, I won't spare you. 
seeing that you seek a proof of Christ who speaks in me who isn't weak, but is powerful in you. 
He was crucified through weakness, yet he lives through the power of God. We also are weak in him, but we'll truly live with him through the power of God toward you. 
Examine yourselves, to see whether you're in the trust. Test yourselves. Or don't you know that Jesus Christ is in you? Unless you have failed the test. 
But I hope you'll know that we haven't failed the test. 
Now I pray to God that you do nothing bad, not so that we appear to have passed the test, but so that you do what's right, even if we seem to have failed. 
We can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth. 
We celebrate when we're weak and you're strong. We also pray for this: that you become complete. 
That's why I write these things while absent, that I may not deal sharply when present, according to the authority which the Lord gave me for building up and not for tearing down. 
Finally, brothers, be glad. Be made complete. Be comforted. Be of the same mind. Live in wholeness, and the God of love and wholeness will be with you. 
Greet one another with a set-apart kiss. 
All the set-apart ones greet you. 
The favor of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the sharing of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen.
