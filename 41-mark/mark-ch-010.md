He left there and came into the region of Judea and beyond the Jordan. Crowds gathered to him again, and as he usually did, he taught them again. 
strict religious ones came to him, testing him, and asked, "Is it allowed for a man to divorce his wife?" 
He answered, "What did Moses command you?" 
They said, "Moses allowed a man to write a certificate of divorce and to divorce her." 
But Jesus said to them, "He wrote you this commandment because your hearts were hard. 
But from the beginning of the creation, God made them male and female. 
That is why a man will leave his father and mother, and will join to his wife, 
and the two will become one flesh, so that they are no longer two, but one flesh. 
So what God has joined together, let no one separate." 
In the house, his students asked him again about this. 
He said to them, "Whoever divorces his wife and marries another cheats on her. 
If a woman herself divorces her husband and marries another, she cheats." 
They were bringing little children to him so that he would touch them, but the students rebuked those who were bringing them. 
But when Jesus saw it, he was indignant and said to them, "Let the little children come to me! Don't forbid them, for God's reign belongs to people like these. 
Truly I tell you, whoever won't welcome God's reign like a little child will in no way enter it." 
He took them in his arms and blessed them, laying his hands on them. 
As he was going out on the road, one man ran to him, knelt before him, and asked him, "Good Teacher, what must I do to be given charge of the real life of the era?" 
Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except one: God. 
You know the commandments: 'Don't murder,' 'Don't cheat on your spouse,' 'Don't steal,' 'Don't give false witness,' 'Don't cheat anyone out of what is theirs,' 'Honor your father and mother.'" 
He said to him, "Teacher, I have kept all these things since my youth." 
Jesus looked at him and loved him, and said to him, "One thing you are missing. Go, sell whatever you have and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in the sky. Then come, follow me, taking up the cross." 
But his face fell at that saying, and he went away grieved, for he had many possessions. 
Jesus looked around and said to his students, "How hard it is for those who have riches to enter God's reign!" 
The students were amazed at his words. But Jesus answered again, "Children, how hard it is for those who trust in riches to enter God's reign! 
It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a sewing needle than for a rich man to enter God's reign." 
They were utterly astonished, saying to him, "Then who can be rescued?" 
Jesus looked at them and said, "With people it is impossible, but not with God, for all things are possible with God." 
Peter began to say to him, "Look, we have left everything and followed you." 
Jesus said, "Truly I tell you, there is no one who has left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or land, for my sake and for the sake of the Good News, 
who won't receive a hundred times as much now in this present time: houses, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and land, with persecutions, and in the era to come, the real life of the era. 
But many who are first will be last, and the last first." 
They were on the road, going up to Jerusalem, and Jesus was going ahead of them, and they were amazed, and those who followed were afraid. He again took the twelve aside and began to tell them what was going to happen to him. 
"Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Human One will be handed over to the chief priests and the Bible teachers. They will sentence him to death and hand him over to the nations. 
They will mock him, spit on him, whip him, and kill him. On the third day he will rise again." 
James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came near to him, saying, "Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we will ask." 
He said to them, "What do you want me to do for you?" 
They said to him, "Let us sit, one at your right and one at your left, in your weight." 
But Jesus said to them, "You don't know what you are asking. Can you drink the cup that I drink, or be immersed with the immersion that I am immersed with?" 
They said to him, "We can." Jesus said to them, "You will indeed drink the cup that I drink, and you will be immersed with the immersion that I am immersed with, 
but to sit at my right hand and at my left isn't mine to give. It is for those for whom it has been prepared." 
When the ten heard it, they became indignant with James and John. 
Jesus called them and said to them, "You know that those who are recognized as rulers over the nations lord it over them, and their great ones use their authority over them. 
But it isn't to be so among you. Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant. 
Whoever of you wants to be first must be slave of all. 
The Human One also came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom price for everyone." 
They came to Jericho. As he went out from Jericho with his students and a great crowd, Bartimaeus, the son of Timaeus, a blind beggar, was sitting by the roadside. 
When he heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began to cry out and say, "Jesus, son of David, have mercy on me!" 
Many rebuked him and told him to be quiet, but he cried out all the more, "Son of David, have mercy on me!" 
Jesus stopped and said, "Call him." They called the blind man, saying, "Cheer up! Get up. He is calling you!" 
He threw off his cloak, sprang up, and came to Jesus. 
Jesus asked him, "What do you want me to do for you?" The blind man said to him, "Rabboni, that I may see again." 
Jesus said to him, "Go your way. Your trust has healed you and made you whole." Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus on the way.
