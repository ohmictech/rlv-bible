Hear this word that God has spoken against you, children of Israel, against the whole family which I brought up out of the land of Egypt, saying: 
"I have chosen only you of all the families of the earth. So I'll punish you for all of your mistakes." 
Do two walk together, unless they have agreed? 
Will a lion roar in the thicket, when he has no prey? Does a young lion cry out of his den, if he has caught nothing? 
Can a bird fall in a trap on the earth, where no snare is set for him? Does a snare spring up from the ground, when there's nothing to catch? 
Does the trumpet alarm sound in a city, without the people being afraid? Does bad happen to a city, and God hasn't done it? 
Surely God will do nothing, unless he reveals his secret to his servants the spokesmen. 
The lion has roared. Who won't fear? The God has spoken. Who can but prophesy? 
Proclaim in the palaces at Ashdod, and in the palaces in the land of Egypt, and say, "Assemble yourselves on the mountains of Samaria, and see what unrest is in her, and what oppression is among them." 
"Indeed they don't know to do right," says God, "Who hoard plunder and loot in their palaces." 
So God says: "An adversary will overrun the land; and he'll pull down your strongholds, and your fortresses will be plundered." 
God says: "As the shepherd rescues out of the mouth of the lion two legs, or a piece of an ear, so will the children of Israel be rescued who sit in Samaria on the corner of a couch, and on the silken cushions of a bed." 
"Listen, and testify against the house of Jacob," says the God of Armies. 
"In the day that I visit the rebellion of Israel on him, I'll also visit the altars of Bethel; and the horns of the altar will be cut off, and fall to the ground. 
I'll strike the winter house with the summer house; and the houses of ivory will perish, and the great houses will have an end," says God.
