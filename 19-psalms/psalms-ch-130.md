A Song of Ascents. Out of the depths I have cried to you, God. 
Lord, hear my voice. Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my petitions. 
If you, God, kept a record of mistakes, Lord, who could stand? 
But there is forgiveness with you, therefore you are feared. 
I wait for God. My soul waits. I hope in his word. 
My soul longs for the Lord more than watchmen long for the morning, more than watchmen for the morning. 
Israel, hope in God, for there is loving kindness with God. Abundant buying back is with him. 
He will buy back Israel from all their mistakes.
