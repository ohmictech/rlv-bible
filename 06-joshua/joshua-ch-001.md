After Moses the servant of God died, God spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant, and said, 
"Moses my servant is dead. Now get up and cross this Jordan, you and all these people, into the land I am giving them, the Israelites. 
I have given you every place your foot steps on, just as I told Moses. 
Your territory will run from the wilderness and Lebanon to the great river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and on to the Great Sea in the west. 
No one will be able to stand against you as long as you live. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I won't fail you or leave you. 
Be strong and brave, because you will lead this people in to take the land I swore to their fathers to give them. 
Only be strong and very brave. Be careful to do everything in the law my servant Moses commanded you. Don't turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you succeed wherever you go. 
Don't let this scroll of the law out of your mouth. Think about it day and night, so that you are careful to do everything written in it, because then you will make your way succeed and then you will do well. 
Haven't I commanded you? Be strong and brave. Don't be afraid. Don't lose heart, because your God is with you wherever you go." 
Then Joshua gave orders to the officials of the people: 
"Go through the middle of the camp and give the people this order: 'Get your food ready, because in three days you will cross this Jordan to go in and take the land your God is giving you.'" 
Joshua also spoke to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half tribe of Manasseh: 
"Remember what Moses the servant of God commanded you: 'Your God is giving you rest and will give you this land.' 
Your wives, your children and your livestock will stay in the land Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan, but all of you fighting men must cross over armed ahead of your brothers and help them, 
until God gives your brothers rest, as he has given you, and they too have taken the land your God is giving them. Then you may come back to your own land and take it, the land Moses the servant of God gave you on the east side of the Jordan." 
They answered Joshua, "We will do everything you command us, and we will go wherever you send us. 
Just as we listened to Moses in everything, so we will listen to you. Only may your God be with you as he was with Moses. 
Anyone who rebels against your orders and won't listen to what you say, whatever you command him, will be put to death himself. Only be strong and brave."
