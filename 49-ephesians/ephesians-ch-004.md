So I, a prisoner in the Lord, beg you to live in a way that is worthy of the calling you were given, 
with all lowliness and humility, with patience, putting up with one another in love, 
eager to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of wholeness. 
There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to one expectation when you were called. 
There is one Lord, one trust, one immersion, 
one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all. 
But each one of us was given a gift, measured out by Christ's own giving. 
That is why it says, "When he went up on high, he led a crowd of captives and gave gifts to people." 
Now what does "he went up" mean, except that he also first came down into the lower parts of the land? 
The one who came down is the same one who went up far above all the skies, so that he could fill everything. 
And he gave some as sent ones, some as spokesmen, some as announcers of good news, and some as shepherds and teachers, 
to get the set-apart ones ready for the work of serving, for building up the body of Christ, 
until we all come together into one trust and one knowing of the Son of God, into a mature person, up to the full height of the fullness of Christ, 
so that we are no longer children, tossed around and blown here and there by every wind of teaching, by people's trickery and sly scheming to lead us astray. 
Instead, speaking the truth in love, we grow up in every way into him who is the head, Christ. 
From him the whole body, joined and knit together by every joint that supplies it, works as each single part does its own part, and so the body grows and builds itself up in love. 
So I say this, and I insist in the Lord: you must no longer live the way the other nations live, in the emptiness of their minds, 
with their understanding darkened, shut out from God's real life because of the ignorance in them, because their hearts have gone hard. 
Having lost all feeling, they gave themselves over to shamelessness, doing every kind of uncleanness with greed. 
But that isn't how you learned Christ, 
if indeed you heard him and were taught in him, as the truth is in Jesus. 
You were taught to put off, as far as your former way of life goes, the old person that is rotting away with its deceitful cravings, 
and to be made new in the spirit of your mind, 
and to put on the new person, created to be like God in true rightness and set-apartness. 
So put away lying, and let each of you speak the truth with his neighbor, because we are parts of one another. 
"Be angry, but don't drift." Don't let the sun go down on your temper, 
and don't give the Slanderer an opening. 
Anyone who has been stealing must steal no more, but must work hard instead, doing something good with his own hands, so that he has something to share with anyone in need. 
Don't let any rotten talk come out of your mouth, but only what is good and needed to build others up, so that it gives a gift to those who hear. 
And don't make the Holy Spirit of God sad, because you were stamped with him for the day of release. 
Get rid of all bitterness, flare, temper, shouting, and slander, along with all ill will. 
Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, releasing each other, just as God in Christ has released you.
