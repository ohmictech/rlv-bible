Simon Peter, a slave and sent one of Jesus Christ, to those who have been given the same precious trust as we have, through the rightness of our God and Restorer, Jesus Christ: 
Favor and wholeness to you in abundance, as you come to know God and Jesus our Lord, 
because his divine power has given us everything we need for real life and devotion to God, through knowing the one who called us by his own weight and goodness. 
By these he has given us his precious and very great promises, so that through them you can share in the divine nature, having escaped the decay that's in the world because of craving. 
This very reason, make every effort to add to your trust goodness, and to goodness, knowing, 
and to knowing, good judgment, and to good judgment, staying power, and to staying power, devotion to God, 
and to devotion to God, affection for your brothers and sisters, and to that affection, love. 
If you have these things and they're growing in you, they'll keep you from being useless and fruitless in knowing our Lord Jesus Christ. 
Anyone who lacks them is blind and short-sighted, and has forgotten that they were made clean from their old mistakes. 
So, brothers and sisters, work all the harder to make your calling and your being chosen certain. If you do these things, you'll never trip up. 
That's how you'll be given a rich welcome into the reign of the eras of our Lord and Restorer, Jesus Christ. 
So I won't be careless about reminding you of these things, even though you know them and are firmly grounded in the truth you have. 
I think it right, as long as I'm in this tent of a body, to keep stirring you up by reminding you, 
because I know that my tent will soon be taken down, just as our Lord Jesus Christ showed me. 
And I'll make every effort to see that you can always remember these things after I'm gone. 
We didn't follow cleverly made-up old stories when we told you about the power and the arrival of our Lord Jesus Christ. We saw his greatness with our own eyes. 
He received honor and weight from God the Father when the voice came to him from the magnificent weight: "This is my Son, whom I truly love, and I'm well pleased with him." 
We ourselves heard that voice come from the sky when we were with him on the set-apart mountain. 
We have the words God spoke through the spokesmen, and they're completely reliable. You'll do well to pay attention to them, like a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day breaks and the morning star rises in your hearts. 
Above all, you must understand that no word of Scripture spoken for God comes from someone's own interpretation. 
No such word ever came from what a person wanted. People spoke from God as the Holy Spirit carried them along.
