The main point of what we are saying is this: we have a high priest like that, and he sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the skies. 
He serves in the Holy Place and in the true tent, the one the Lord set up, not a human being. 
Every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices, so this one must have something to offer too. 
If he were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, because there are already priests who offer the gifts according to the law. 
They serve at a copy and a shadow of what is in the sky, just as Moses was warned when he was about to build the tent: "See that you shall make everything according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain." 
But now he has been given a far better service, just as he is the mediator of a better binding arrangement, which is set up on better promises. 
For if that first arrangement had been without fault, there would have been no need to look for a second one. 
But God found fault with them, and he says, "The days are coming", says the Lord, "when I will make a new binding arrangement with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah. 
It will not be like the arrangement I made with their fathers on the day I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt. They did not keep their side of my arrangement, so I stopped caring about them," says the Lord. 
"This is the binding arrangement I will make with the house of Israel after those days," says the Lord: "I will put my laws into their minds; I will also write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people. 
No one will teach their neighbor or their brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,' because they will all know me, from the least of them to the greatest. 
For I will be merciful toward their wrongs. I will remember their mistakes and their living as if there were no law no more." 
By saying "a new arrangement", he has made the first one old. And what is growing old and worn out is close to disappearing.
