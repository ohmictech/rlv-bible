A Psalm by David. I'll sing of loving kindness and justice. To you, God, I'll sing praises. 
I'll be careful to live a blameless life. When will you come to me? I'll walk within my house with a blameless heart. 
I'll set no vile thing before my eyes. I hate the deeds of faithless men. They won't cling to me. 
A perverse heart will be far from me. I'll have nothing to do with bad. 
I'll silence whoever secretly slanders his neighbor. I won't tolerate one who's arrogant and conceited. 
My eyes will be on the faithful of the land, that they can dwell with me. He who walks in a perfect way, he'll serve me. 
He who practices deceit won't dwell within my house. He who speaks falsehood won't be established before my eyes. 
Morning by morning, I'll destroy all the guilty of the land, to cut off all the workers of guilt from God's city.
