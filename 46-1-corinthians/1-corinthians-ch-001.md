Paul, called to be a sent one of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes, 
to the gathering of God that is at Corinth, those who are set apart in Christ Jesus, called to be set-apart ones, with everyone who calls on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, both theirs and ours: 
Favor and wholeness to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 
I always thank my God for you, for the gift of God that was given you in Christ Jesus, 
that in everything you were made rich in him, in all speech and all knowledge, 
just as the witness about Christ was made firm in you, 
so that you come behind in no gift, waiting for the unveiling of our Lord Jesus Christ, 
who will also make you firm to the end, blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ. 
God is trustworthy, through whom you were called into the sharing of his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord. 
Now I beg you, brothers, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all say the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be made complete together in the same mind and in the same thinking. 
It has been reported to me concerning you, my brothers, by those who are from Chloe's household, that there are quarrels among you. 
Now I mean this, that each one of you says, "I follow Paul," "I follow Apollos," "I follow Cephas," and, "I follow Christ." 
Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you immersed into the name of Paul? 
I thank God that I immersed none of you except Crispus and Gaius, 
so that no one should say that I had immersed you into my own name. 
(I also immersed the household of Stephanas; besides them, I don't know whether I immersed any other.) 
Christ sent me not to immerse, but to announce the Good News, not in wisdom of words, so that the cross of Christ wouldn't be put out of work. 
The word of the cross is foolishness to those who are dying, but to us who are being made whole it is the power of God. 
It is written, "I will break up the wisdom of the wise. I will bring the discernment of the discerning to nothing." 
Where is the wise? Where is the Bible teacher? Where is the debater of this era? Hasn't God made the wisdom of this world foolish? 
Since, in the wisdom of God, the world through its wisdom didn't know God, God was delighted, through the foolishness of the announcement, to make whole those who trust. 
Jewish people ask for signs, and Greeks look for wisdom, 
but we announce Christ crucified, a trap to Jewish people and foolishness to Greeks, 
but to those who are called, both Jewish people and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God; 
because the foolishness of God is wiser than people, and the weakness of God is stronger than people. 
Think about your calling, brothers: not many of you are wise according to the flesh, not many powerful, not many of noble birth; 
but God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise. God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong. 
God chose the lowly things of the world, and the things people look down on, and the things that are nothing at all, to bring to nothing the things that are, 
that no flesh should boast before God. 
Because of him, you are in Christ Jesus, who has become wisdom for us from God, and rightness and being set apart, and release, 
that, as it is written, "The one who takes pride, let him take pride in the Lord."
