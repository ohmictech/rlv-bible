"'The God says: "The gate of the inner court that looks toward the east will be shut the six working days; but on the Sabbath day it will be opened, and on the day of the new moon it will be opened. 
The prince will enter by the way of the porch of the gate outside, and will stand by the post of the gate; and the priests will prepare his burnt offering and his offerings for wholeness, and he will worship at the threshold of the gate. Then he will go out, but the gate won't be shut until the evening. 
The people of the land will worship at the door of that gate before God on the Sabbaths and on the new moons. 
The burnt offering that the prince will offer to God will be on the Sabbath day, six lambs without defect and a ram without defect; 
and the meal offering will be an ephah for the ram, and the meal offering for the lambs as he's able to give, and a hin of oil to an ephah. 
On the day of the new moon it will be a young bull without defect, six lambs, and a ram. They will be without defect. 
He will prepare a meal offering: an ephah for the bull, and an ephah for the ram, and for the lambs according as he's able, and a hin of oil to an ephah. 
When the prince enters, he will go in by the way of the porch of the gate, and he will go out by its way. 
"'"But when the people of the land come before God in the appointed feasts, he who enters by the way of the north gate to worship will go out by the way of the south gate; and he who enters by the way of the south gate will go out by the way of the north gate. He won't return by the way of the gate by which he came in, but will go out straight before him. 
The prince will go in with them when they go in. When they go out, he will go out. 
"'"In the feasts and in the appointed holidays, the meal offering will be an ephah for a bull, and an ephah for a ram, and for the lambs as he's able to give, and a hin of oil to an ephah. 
When the prince prepares a free will offering, a burnt offering or offerings for wholeness as a free will offering to God, one will open for him the gate that looks toward the east; and he will prepare his burnt offering and his offerings for wholeness, as he does on the Sabbath day. Then he will go out; and after his going out one will shut the gate. 
"'"You will prepare a lamb a year old without defect for a burnt offering to God daily. Morning by morning you will prepare it. 
You will prepare a meal offering with it morning by morning, the sixth part of an ephah, and the third part of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour; a meal offering to God continually by a perpetual ordinance. 
Thus they will prepare the lamb, the meal offering, and the oil, morning by morning, for a continual burnt offering." 
"'The God says: "If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it's his inheritance. It will belong to his sons. It's their possession by inheritance. 
But if he gives of his inheritance a gift to one of his servants, it will be his to the year of liberty; then it will return to the prince; but as for his inheritance, it will be for his sons. 
Moreover the prince won't take of the people's inheritance, to thrust them out of their possession. He will give inheritance to his sons out of his own possession, that my people not each be scattered from his possession."'" 
Then he brought me through the entry, which was at the side of the gate, into the holy rooms for the priests, which looked toward the north. Look, there was a place on the back part westward. 
He said to me, "This is the place where the priests will boil the trespass offering and the offering for wrongdoing, and where they will bake the meal offering, that they not bring them out into the outer court, to sanctify the people." 
Then he brought me out into the outer court and caused me to pass by the four corners of the court; and look, in every corner of the court there was a court. 
In the four corners of the court there were courts enclosed, forty cubits long and thirty wide. These four in the corners were the same size. 
There was a wall around in them, around the four, and boiling places were made under the walls all around. 
Then he said to me, "These are the boiling houses, where the ministers of the house will boil the sacrifice of the people."
