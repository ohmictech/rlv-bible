The fifth messenger sounded, and I saw a star that had fallen from the sky to the land. The key to the pit of the deep was given to him. 
He opened the pit of the deep, and smoke rose out of it like the smoke from a huge furnace. The sun and the air were darkened by the smoke from the pit. 
Then out of the smoke came locusts on the land, and they were given power like the power the scorpions of the land have. 
They were told not to hurt the grass of the land, nor any green thing, nor any tree, but only those people who don't have God's seal on their foreheads. 
They were given power, not to kill them, but to put them in anguish for five months. Their anguish was like the anguish of a scorpion sting. 
In those days people will look for death and won't find it. They will long to die, and death will run away from them. 
The shapes of the locusts were like horses ready for battle. On their heads were something like gold wreaths, and their faces were like human faces. 
They had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth. 
They wore breastplates like breastplates of iron, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of many chariots and horses rushing into battle. 
They have tails like scorpions, with stingers, and in their tails is their power to harm people for five months. 
They have as their king the messenger of the deep. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek he has the name Apollyon. 
The first how terrible is past. Look, two more are still coming after this. 
The sixth messenger sounded. I heard a voice from the horns of the golden altar in front of God, 
saying to the sixth messenger who had the trumpet, "Release the four messengers who are tied up at the great river Euphrates!" 
The four messengers were released, the ones who had been kept ready for that very hour and day and month and year, so that they could kill a third of humanity. 
The number of the mounted troops was two hundred million. I heard how many there were. 
This is how I saw the horses in the vision, and those riding them: they wore breastplates the color of fire, of blue, and of yellow sulfur, and the horses' heads were like lions' heads. Out of their mouths came fire, smoke, and sulfur. 
A third of humanity was killed by these three plagues: by the fire, the smoke, and the sulfur that came out of their mouths. 
The power of the horses is in their mouths and in their tails, because their tails are like snakes and have heads, and with those they cause harm. 
The rest of humanity, who weren't killed by these plagues, didn't have a change of heart about the deeds of their hands. They didn't stop bowing down to unclean spirits and to idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood, which can't see or hear or walk. 
And they didn't have a change of heart about their murders, their drug-magic, their sleeping around, or their stealing.
