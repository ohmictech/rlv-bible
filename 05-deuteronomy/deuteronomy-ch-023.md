"No one who has been castrated by crushing or cutting may enter the assembly of God. 
No one born from a forbidden union may enter the assembly of God. Even to the tenth generation none of his descendants may enter the assembly of God. 
No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of God. Even to the tenth generation none of their descendants may ever enter the assembly of God, 
because they didn't meet you with bread and water on the road when you came out of Egypt, and because they hired Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Mesopotamia to curse you. 
But your God wouldn't listen to Balaam. Your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because your God loves you. 
Never seek their peace or their prosperity, as long as you live. 
Don't hate an Edomite, because he is your brother. Don't hate an Egyptian, because you lived as a foreigner in his land. 
The children born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of God. 
When you go out to camp against your enemies, keep away from everything bad. 
If there is a man among you who becomes unclean because of what happens to him at night, he must go outside the camp and not come in. 
When evening comes, he must wash himself in water, and after sunset he may come back into the camp. 
Have a place outside the camp where you can go to relieve yourself. 
Keep a spade among your equipment. When you relieve yourself, dig a hole with it and cover up what you leave behind. 
Your God walks through the middle of your camp to rescue you and to hand your enemies over to you, so your camp must be set apart, and he must not see anything indecent among you and turn away from you. 
Don't hand back to his master a slave who has escaped from his master and come to you. 
Let him live with you, wherever he chooses in any of your towns, wherever he likes best. Don't mistreat him. 
No woman of Israel is to become a shrine prostitute, and no man of Israel is to become a shrine prostitute. 
Don't bring the pay of a prostitute or the wages of a male prostitute into the house of your God to pay any vow, because both are disgusting to your God. 
Don't charge your brother interest, whether on money or food or anything else that can be lent at interest. 
You may charge a foreigner interest, but not your brother, so that your God may bless you in everything you put your hand to in the land you are going in to take. 
When you make a vow to your God, don't be slow to pay it, because your God will certainly require it from you, and it would be guilt on you. 
But if you don't make a vow, there is no guilt in you. 
Whatever your lips have said you must be careful to do. Whatever you have promised to your God as a freewill offering with your own mouth, you must do. 
When you go into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat as many grapes as you want, but don't put any in your basket. 
When you go into your neighbor's standing grain, you may pick the heads of grain with your hand, but don't use a sickle on your neighbor's standing grain.
