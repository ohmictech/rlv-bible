Don't see your brother's ox or his sheep go astray and hide yourself from them. You will surely bring them again to your brother. 
If your brother isn't near to you, or if you don't know him, then you will bring it home to your house, and it will be with you until your brother comes looking for it, and you will restore it to him. 
So you will do with his donkey. So you will do with his garment. So you will do with every lost thing of your brother's, which he has lost and you have found. You may not hide yourself. 
Don't see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen down by the way, and hide yourself from them. You will surely help him to lift them up again. 
A woman won't wear men's clothing, neither will a man put on women's clothing; for whoever does these things is an abomination to your God. 
If you come across a bird's nest on the way, in any tree or on the ground, with young ones or eggs, and the hen sitting on the young, or on the eggs, you won't take the hen with the young. 
You will surely let the hen go, but the young you may take for yourself, that it may be well with you, and that you may prolong your days. 
When you build a new house, then you will make a railing around your roof, so that you don't bring blood on your house if anyone falls from there. 
Don't sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, lest all the fruit be defiled, the seed which you have sown, and the increase of the vineyard. 
Don't plow with an ox and a donkey together. 
Don't wear clothes of wool and linen woven together. 
You will make yourselves fringes on the four corners of your cloak with which you cover yourself. 
If any man takes a wife, and goes in to her, hates her, 
accuses her of shameful things, gives her a bad name, and says, "I took this woman, and when I came near to her, I didn't find in her the tokens of virginity;" 
then the young lady's father and mother will take and bring the tokens of the young lady's virginity to the elders of the city in the gate. 
The young lady's father will tell the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, and he hates her. 
Look, he has accused her of shameful things, saying, 'I didn't find in your daughter the tokens of virginity;' and yet these are the tokens of my daughter's virginity." They will spread the cloth before the elders of the city. 
The elders of that city will take the man and chastise him. 
They will fine him one hundred shekels of silver, and give them to the father of the young lady, because he has given a bad name to a virgin of Israel. She will be his wife. He may not put her away all his days. 
But if this thing is true, that the tokens of virginity weren't found in the young lady, 
then they will bring out the young lady to the door of her father's house, and the men of her city will stone her to death with stones, because she has done folly in Israel, to play the prostitute in her father's house. So you will remove the bad from among you. 
If a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, then they will both die, the man who lay with the woman and the woman. So you will remove the bad from Israel. 
If there's a young lady who's a virgin pledged to be married to a husband, and a man finds her in the city, and lies with her, 
then you will bring them both out to the gate of that city, and you will stone them to death with stones; the lady, because she didn't cry, being in the city; and the man, because he has humbled his neighbor's wife. So you will remove the bad from among you. 
But if the man finds the lady who's pledged to be married in the field, and the man forces her and lies with her, then only the man who lay with her will die; 
but to the lady you will do nothing. There's in the lady no wrongdoing worthy of death; for as when a man rises against his neighbor and kills him, even so is this matter; 
for he found her in the field, the pledged to be married lady cried, and there was no one to save her. 
If a man finds a lady who's a virgin, who isn't pledged to be married, grabs her and lies with her, and they're found, 
then the man who lay with her will give to the lady's father fifty shekels of silver. She will be his wife, because he has humbled her. He may not put her away all his days. 
A man won't take his father's wife, and won't uncover his father's skirt.
