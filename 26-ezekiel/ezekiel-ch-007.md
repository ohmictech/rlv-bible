Moreover God's word came to me, saying, 
"You, son of man, God says to the land of Israel, 'An end! The end has come on the four corners of the land. 
Now the end is on you, and I'll send my anger on you, and will judge you according to your ways. I'll bring on you all your abominations. 
My eye won't spare you, neither will I have pity; but I'll bring your ways on you, and your abominations will be among you. Then you'll know that I'm God.' 
"The God says: 'A disaster! A unique disaster! Look, it comes. 
An end has come. The end has come! It awakes against you. Look, it comes. 
Your doom has come to you, inhabitant of the land! The time has come! The day is near, a day of tumult, and not of joyful shouting, on the mountains. 
Now I'll shortly pour out my wrath on you, and accomplish my anger against you, and will judge you according to your ways. I'll bring on you all your abominations. 
My eye won't spare, neither will I have pity. I'll punish you according to your ways. Your abominations will be among you. Then you'll know that I, God, strike. 
"'Look, the day! Look, it comes! Your doom has gone out. The rod has blossomed. Pride has budded. 
Violence has risen up into a rod of guilt. None of them will remain, nor of their multitude, nor of their wealth. There will be nothing of value among them. 
The time has come! The day draws near. Don't let the buyer rejoice, nor the seller mourn; for wrath is on all its multitude. 
The seller won't return to that which is sold, although they're still alive; for the vision concerns the whole multitude of it. None will return. None will strengthen himself in the guilt of his life. 
They have blown the trumpet, and have made all ready; but no one goes to the battle, for my wrath is on all its multitude. 
"'The sword is outside, and the pestilence and the famine within. He who's in the field will die by the sword. He who's in the city will be devoured by famine and pestilence. 
But of those who escape, they'll escape and will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, everyone moaning, everyone in his guilt. 
All hands will be feeble, and all knees will be weak as water. 
They'll also clothe themselves with sackcloth, and horror will cover them. Shame will be on all faces, and baldness on all their heads. 
They'll cast their silver in the streets, and their gold will be as an unclean thing. Their silver and their gold won't be able to deliver them in the day of God's wrath. They won't satisfy their souls or fill their bellies; because it has been the stumbling block of their guilt. 
As for the beauty of his ornament, he set it in majesty; but they made the images of their abominations and their detestable things therein. So I have made it to them as an unclean thing. 
I'll give it into the hands of the strangers for a prey, and to the guilty of the earth for a plunder; and they'll profane it. 
I'll also turn my face from them, and they'll profane my secret place. Robbers will enter into it, and profane it. 
"'Make chains, for the land is full of bloody crimes, and the city is full of violence. 
So I'll bring the worst of the nations, and they'll possess their houses. I'll also make the pride of the strong to cease. Their holy places will be profaned. 
Destruction comes! They'll seek peace, and there will be none. 
Mischief will come on mischief, and rumor will be on rumor. They'll seek a vision of the spokesman; but the law will perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders. 
The king will mourn, and the prince will be clothed with desolation. The hands of the people of the land will be troubled. I'll do to them after their way, and according to their own judgments I'll judge them. Then they'll know that I'm God.'"
