In the third month after the Israelites had left Egypt, on that very day, they came to the wilderness of Sinai. 
They had set out from Rephidim and come to the wilderness of Sinai, and there they camped in the wilderness, Israel camping in front of the mountain. 
Moses went up to God, and God called to him from the mountain: "This is what you are to tell the house of Jacob, and say to the Israelites: 
'You have seen what I did to the Egyptians, and how I carried you on eagles' wings and brought you to myself. 
Now if you obey me and keep my covenant, you will be my own possession out of all the peoples, because the whole earth is mine. 
You will be for me a kingdom of priests and a set apart nation.' Those are the words you are to speak to the Israelites." 
So Moses came and called the elders of the people together and set before them everything God had commanded him. 
All the people answered together, "We will do everything God has said." And Moses brought their answer back to God. 
God said to Moses, "I am coming to you in a thick cloud so the people can hear me speaking with you and will always trust you." Then Moses told God what the people had said. 
God said to Moses, "Go to the people and set them apart today and tomorrow, and have them wash their clothes. 
Be ready for the third day, because on the third day God will come down on Mount Sinai where all the people can see. 
Put a boundary around the mountain for the people and tell them, 'Be careful not to go up the mountain or even touch its edge. Anyone who touches the mountain must be put to death. 
No one is to lay a hand on him. He must be stoned or shot with arrows, whether animal or human, and he will not live.' When the trumpet sounds a long blast, they may come up to the mountain." 
So Moses went down from the mountain to the people, set them apart, and they washed their clothes. 
He said to the people, "Be ready by the third day. Don't sleep with a woman." 
On the morning of the third day there was thunder and lightning, a thick cloud on the mountain, and a very loud trumpet blast, and everyone in the camp trembled. 
Moses led the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stood at the foot of the mountain. 
Mount Sinai was covered in smoke, because God came down on it in fire. The smoke rose like smoke from a furnace, and the whole mountain shook violently. 
As the trumpet blast grew louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him in a voice. 
God came down on the top of Mount Sinai and called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up. 
God said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people not to break through to look at God, or many of them will die. 
Even the priests who come near to God must set themselves apart, or God will break out against them." 
Moses said to God, "The people can't come up Mount Sinai, because you warned us yourself, 'Put a boundary around the mountain and set it apart.'" 
God said to him, "Go down, and then come back up with Aaron. But don't let the priests or the people break through to come up to God, or he will break out against them." 
So Moses went down to the people and told them.
