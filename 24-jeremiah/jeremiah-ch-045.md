The message that Jeremiah the spokesman spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying, 
"The God of Israel says to you, Baruch: 
'You said, "Woe is me now! God has added sorrow to my pain! I'm weary with my groaning, and I find no rest."' 
"You shall tell him, God says: 'Look, that which I have built, I'll break down, and that which I have planted I'll pluck up; and this in the whole land. 
Do you seek great things for yourself? Don't seek them; for, look, I'll bring bad on all flesh,' says God, 'but I'll let you escape with your life wherever you go.'"
