Not to us, God, not to us, but to your name give glory, for your loving kindness, and for your truth's sake. 
Why should the nations say, "Where is their God, now?" 
But our God is in the sky. He does whatever he pleases. 
Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men's hands. 
They have mouths, but they don't speak. They have eyes, but they don't see. 
They have ears, but they don't hear. They have noses, but they don't smell. 
They have hands, but they don't feel. They have feet, but they don't walk, neither do they speak through their throat. 
Those who make them will be like them; yes, everyone who trusts in them. 
Israel, trust in God! He is their help and their shield. 
House of Aaron, trust in God! He is their help and their shield. 
You who fear God, trust in God! He is their help and their shield. 
God remembers us. He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel. He will bless the house of Aaron. 
He will bless those who fear God, both small and great. 
May God increase you more and more, you and your children. 
Blessed are you by God, who made the sky and the earth. 
The sky are God's skies, but he has given the earth to the children of men. 
The dead don't praise God, nor any who go down into silence, 
but we will bless God, from this time forward and for the eras more. Praise God!
