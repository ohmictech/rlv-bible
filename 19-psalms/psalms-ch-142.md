A contemplation by David, when he was in the cave. A Prayer. I cry with my voice to God. With my voice, I ask God for mercy. 
I pour out my complaint before him. I tell him my troubles. 
When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, you knew my route. On the path in which I walk, they have hidden a snare for me. 
Look on my right, and see; for there's no one who's concerned for me. Refuge has fled from me. No one cares for my soul. 
I cried to you, God. I said, "You're my refuge, my portion in the land of the living." 
Listen to my cry, for I'm in desperate need. Deliver me from my persecutors, for they're too strong for me. 
Bring my soul out of prison, that I can give thanks to your name. The righteous will surround me, for you'll be good to me.
