God spoke to Moses: "Leave this place and go up, you and the people you brought up out of Egypt, to the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, saying, 'I will give it to your descendants.' 
I will send a messenger ahead of you and drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites. 
Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey, but I won't go up with you, because you are a stubborn people, and I might destroy you on the way." 
When the people heard this bad news they mourned, and no one put on any jewelry. 
God had said to Moses, "Tell the Israelites, 'You are a stubborn people. If I went up with you even for a moment, I would destroy you. So take off your jewelry, and I will decide what to do with you.'" 
So the Israelites took off their jewelry from Mount Horeb onward. 
Moses used to take a tent and set it up outside the camp, some distance away, and he called it the Tent of Meeting. Everyone who wanted to ask God anything went out to the Tent of Meeting outside the camp. 
When Moses went out to the tent, everyone stood up at the entrance of their own tent and watched him until he had gone in. 
When Moses entered, the pillar of cloud came down and stood at the entrance of the tent, and God spoke with Moses. 
When all the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, they stood up and worshiped, each at the entrance of his own tent. 
God spoke to Moses face to face, the way a man speaks to his friend. Then Moses returned to the camp, but his young aide Joshua son of Nun never left the tent. 
Moses said to God, "Look, you keep telling me, 'Lead this people up,' but you haven't told me who you will send with me. Yet you have said, 'I know you by name, and you have found favor with me.' 
If I have found favor with you, show me your way so I can know you and go on finding favor with you. Remember that this nation is your people." 
God said, "My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest." 
Moses said, "If your presence doesn't go with me, don't take us up from here. 
How else would anyone know that I and your people have found favor with you, except that you go with us? That is what sets us apart from every other people on the face of the earth." 
God said to Moses, "I will do this thing you have asked too, because you have found favor with me and I know you by name." 
Moses said, "Please show me your splendor." 
God said, "I will make all my goodness pass in front of you and proclaim my name before you, and I will be gracious to whom I am gracious and show mercy to whom I show mercy." 
God said, "You cannot see my face, because no one can see me and live." 
God said, "There is a place near me. Stand on the rock there, 
and when my splendor passes by, I will put you in a gap in the rock and cover you with my hand until I have passed. 
Then I will take my hand away, and you will see my back. But my face will not be seen."
