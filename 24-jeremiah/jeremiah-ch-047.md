God's word that came to Jeremiah the spokesman concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza. 
God says: "Look, waters rise up out of the north, and will become an overflowing stream, and will overflow the land and all that's therein, the city and those who dwell therein. The men will cry, and all the inhabitants of the land will wail. 
At the noise of the stamping of the hoofs of his strong ones, at the rushing of his chariots, at the rumbling of his wheels, the fathers don't look back for their children because their hands are so feeble, 
because of the day that comes to destroy all the Philistines, to cut off from Tyre and Sidon every helper who remains; for God will destroy the Philistines, the remnant of the isle of Caphtor. 
Baldness has come on Gaza; Ashkelon is brought to nothing. You remnant of their valley, how long will you cut yourself? 
"'You sword of God, how long will it be before you're quiet? Put yourself back into your scabbard; rest, and be still.' 
"How can you be quiet, since God has given you a command? Against Ashkelon, and against the seashore, there he has appointed it."
