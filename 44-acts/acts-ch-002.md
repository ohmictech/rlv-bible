Now when the day of Pentecost had come, they were all together in one place. 
Suddenly there came from the sky a sound like the rushing of a mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting. 
Tongues like fire appeared and were distributed to them, and one sat on each of them. 
They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak with other languages, as the Spirit gave them the ability to speak. 
Now there were dwelling in Jerusalem Jewish people, devout men, from every nation under the sky. 
When this sound was heard, a crowd came together and were bewildered, because everyone heard them speaking in their own language. 
They were all amazed and astonished, saying to one another, "Look, aren't all these who speak Galileans? 
How do we hear, everyone in our own native language? 
Parthians, Medes, Elamites, and people from Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, Asia, 
Phrygia, Pamphylia, Egypt, the parts of Libya around Cyrene, visitors from Rome, both Jewish people and converts, 
Cretans and Arabians, we hear them speaking in our languages the mighty deeds of God!" 
Everyone was amazed and confused, saying to one another, "What does this mean?" 
Others, mocking, said, "They have had too much new wine." 
But Peter, standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice and spoke out to them, "Men of Judea and all you who dwell at Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and listen carefully to my words. 
These aren't drunken, as you suppose, since it's only mid-morning. 
But this is what has been spoken through the spokesman Joel: 
'It'll be in the last days, says God, that I'll pour out my Spirit on all flesh. Your sons and your daughters will speak for God. Your young men will see visions. Your old men will dream dreams. 
Yes, and on my slaves, both men and women, in those days, I'll pour out my Spirit, and they'll speak for God. 
I'll show wonders in the sky above, and signs on the earth beneath: blood, and fire, and billows of smoke. 
The sun will be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and splendid day of the Lord comes. 
It'll be that whoever will call on the name of the Lord will be made whole.' 
"Men of Israel, hear these words! Jesus of Nazareth, a man whom God approved for you by powerful deeds and wonders and signs which God did through him among you, even as you yourselves know, 
him, being handed over by the determined counsel and foreknowledge of God, you have taken by the hand of people who live as if there were no law, and you crucified and killed him; 
whom God raised up, having freed him from the agony of death, because it wasn't possible that he should be held by it. 
David says concerning him, 'I saw the Lord always before my face, for he's on my right hand, that I shouldn't be moved. 
So my heart was glad, and my tongue celebrated. Moreover my flesh also will dwell in hope, 
because you won't leave my life in Hades, neither will you allow your Holy One to see decay. 
You made known to me the ways of life. You'll make me full of gladness with your presence.' 
"Brothers and sisters, I can tell you freely of the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day. 
So, being a spokesman, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him that of the fruit of his body, according to the flesh, he'd raise up the Christ to sit on his throne, 
he foreseeing this, spoke about the rising of the Christ, that his life wasn't left in Hades, and his flesh didn't see decay. 
This Jesus God raised up, to which we all are witnesses. 
Being exalted by the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, he has poured out this which you now see and hear. 
David didn't ascend into the sky, but he says himself, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit by my right hand 
until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet."' 
"Let all the house of Israel know certainly that God has made him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom you crucified." 
Now when they heard this, they were cut to the heart, and said to Peter and the rest of the sent ones, "Brothers and sisters, what should we do?" 
Peter said to them, "Have a change of heart and be immersed, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, so that your mistakes are released, and you'll receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. 
The promise is to you and to your children, and to everyone who's far off, even as many as the Lord our God will call to himself." 
With many other words he testified and urged them, saying, "Rescue yourselves from this crooked generation!" 
Then those who gladly received his word were immersed. There were added that day about three thousand people. 
They continued steadfastly in the teaching of the sent ones and in sharing, in the breaking of bread, and prayer. 
Awe came on every person, and many wonders and signs were done through the sent ones. 
Everyone who trusted were together, and had all things in common. 
They sold their possessions and goods, and distributed them to all, as anyone had need. 
Day by day, continuing steadfastly together in the temple courts, and breaking bread at home, they took their food with gladness and singleness of heart, 
praising God and having favor with everyone. The Lord added to the gathering day by day those who were being made whole.
