Sarah lived a hundred and twenty-seven years. 
She died at Kiriath Arba, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan, and Abraham went there to mourn for Sarah and to cry over her. 
Then Abraham got up from beside his dead wife and spoke to the Hittites: 
"I'm an outsider and a foreigner living among you. Sell me some land here so I can bury my dead." 
The Hittites answered Abraham, 
"Listen, my lord. You're a mighty prince among us. Bury your dead in the best of our tombs. Not one of us will refuse you his tomb for burying your dead." 
Abraham got up and bowed down to the people of the land, the Hittites. 
He spoke with them and said, "If you're willing to let me bury my dead, then listen to me and speak to Ephron son of Zohar for me, 
so that he'll sell me the cave of Machpelah that belongs to him, at the end of his field. Let him sell it to me for the full price, in front of you all, as my burial place." 
Ephron was sitting among the Hittites. He answered Abraham in front of all the Hittites who were there at the city gate: 
"No, my lord, listen. I'll give you the field, and the cave in it too. In front of my people I give it to you. Bury your dead." 
Abraham bowed down before the people of the land. 
Then he spoke to Ephron so everyone could hear, "Please, listen to me. I'll pay the price of the field. Take it from me, and I'll bury my dead there." 
Ephron answered Abraham, 
"My lord, listen to me. A plot of land like that is worth four hundred shekels of silver. What does that matter between you and me? Go ahead and bury your dead." 
Abraham agreed with Ephron and weighed out the silver he had named in front of the Hittites, four hundred shekels of silver at the going rate among merchants. 
So Ephron's field at Machpelah, near Mamre, along with the cave in it and all the trees on the land within its borders, was handed over 
to Abraham as his property in front of the Hittites, before everyone at the city gate. 
After that, Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan. 
So the field and the cave in it were handed over to Abraham by the Hittites as a burial place.
