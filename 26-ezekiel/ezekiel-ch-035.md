Moreover God's word came to me, saying, 
"Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir, and prophesy against it, 
and tell it, 'The God says: "Look, I'm against you, Mount Seir, and I'll stretch out my hand against you. I'll make you a desolation and an astonishment. 
I'll lay your cities waste, and you'll be desolate. Then you'll know that I'm God. 
"'"Because you have had a perpetual hostility, and have given over the children of Israel to the power of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time of the guilt of the end, 
So, as I live," says God, "I'll prepare you for blood, and blood will pursue you. Since you haven't hated blood, blood will pursue you. 
Thus I'll make Mount Seir an astonishment and a desolation. I'll cut off from it him who passes through and him who returns. 
I'll fill its mountains with its slain. The slain with the sword will fall in your hills and in your valleys and in all your watercourses. 
I'll make you a perpetual desolation, and your cities won't be inhabited. Then you'll know that I'm God. 
"'"Because you have said, 'These two nations and these two countries will be mine, and we'll possess it,' although God was there, 
So, as I live," says God, "I'll do according to your anger, and according to your envy which you have shown out of your hatred against them; and I'll make myself known among them when I judge you. 
You'll know that I, God, have heard all your insults which you have spoken against the mountains of Israel, saying, 'They have been laid desolate. They have been given to us to devour.' 
You have magnified yourselves against me with your mouth, and have multiplied your words against me. I have heard it." 
The God says: "When the whole earth rejoices, I'll make you desolate. 
As you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it was desolate, so I'll do to you. You'll be desolate, Mount Seir, and all Edom, even all of it. Then they'll know that I'm God.'"
