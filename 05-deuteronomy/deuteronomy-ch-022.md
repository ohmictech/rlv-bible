Don't look the other way when you see your brother's ox or sheep wandering off. Bring it back to your brother. 
If your brother doesn't live near you or you don't know who he is, take the animal home with you and keep it until he comes looking for it, and then give it back. 
Do the same with his donkey and with his clothing. Do the same with anything your brother has lost and you have found. Don't look the other way. 
Don't look the other way when you see your brother's donkey or ox fallen down on the road. Help him get it back on its feet. 
A woman must not wear men's clothing and a man must not put on women's clothing, because anyone who does that is disgusting to your God. 
If you come across a bird's nest on the road, in a tree or on the ground, with young birds or eggs and the mother sitting on them, don't take the mother with the young. 
Let the mother go, but you may take the young for yourself, so that things go well for you and you live a long life. 
When you build a new house, put a low wall around your roof, so you don't bring blood guilt on your house if someone falls from it. 
Don't plant two kinds of seed in your vineyard, or the whole crop will be made unclean, both the seed you planted and the produce of the vineyard. 
Don't plow with an ox and a donkey yoked together. 
Don't wear cloth woven from wool and linen together. 
Put tassels on the four corners of the cloak you wrap around yourself. 
If a man marries a woman and after going to her he turns against her, 
and accuses her of shameful things and gives her a bad name, saying, 'I married this woman, and when I went to her I found no proof that she was a virgin,' 
then the young woman's father and mother are to bring the proof of her virginity to the elders at the gate of the city. 
The young woman's father will say to the elders, 'I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, and he has turned against her. 
Now he has accused her of shameful things, saying, "I found no proof that your daughter was a virgin," but here is the proof of my daughter's virginity.' And they will spread the sheet out before the elders of the city. 
Then the elders of that city must take the man and punish him. 
They must fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give it to the young woman's father, because he has given a bad name to a virgin of Israel. She will stay his wife, and he can never divorce her. 
But if it is true and no proof of the young woman's virginity was found, 
then they must bring the young woman out to the door of her father's house, and the men of her city must stone her to death, because she has done something shameful in Israel by sleeping around in her father's house. So get rid of the evil among you. 
If a man is found sleeping with a woman who is married, then both of them must die, the man who slept with her and the woman. So get rid of the evil from Israel. 
If a young woman who is a virgin is engaged to be married, and a man finds her in the city and sleeps with her, 
then you must bring both of them out to the gate of that city and stone them to death: the young woman, because she didn't cry out although she was in the city, and the man, because he took advantage of his neighbor's wife. So get rid of the evil among you. 
But if the man finds the engaged young woman out in the country, and he forces her and sleeps with her, then only the man who slept with her must die. 
Do nothing to the young woman. She has done nothing that deserves death. This case is like that of a man who attacks his neighbor and kills him, 
because he found her out in the country, and the engaged young woman cried out, and there was no one to rescue her. 
If a man finds a young woman who is a virgin and not engaged, grabs her and sleeps with her, and they are found out, 
then the man who slept with her must give the young woman's father fifty shekels of silver. She will be his wife, because he took advantage of her, and he can never divorce her. 
A man must not marry his father's wife, because that would uncover what belongs to his father.
