Therefore I urge you, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice, set apart, acceptable to God, which is your spiritual service. 
Don't be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may prove what is the good, well-pleasing, and complete will of God. 
For I say through the gift that was given me, to everyone who is among you, not to think of yourself more highly than you ought to think; but to think reasonably, as God has apportioned to each person a measure of trust. 
For even as we have many members in one body, and all the members don't have the same function, 
so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another, 
having gifts that differ according to the gift given to us: if it is speaking as a spokesman, let's do it in proportion to our trust; 
or service, let's give ourselves to service; or he who teaches, to his teaching; 
or he who exhorts, to his exhorting; he who gives, let him do it with generosity; he who rules, with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness. 
Let love be without hypocrisy. Hate that which is bad. Cling to that which is good. 
In love of the brothers be tenderly affectionate to one another; in honor prefer one another, 
not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord, 
celebrating in expectation, enduring in hard times, continuing steadfastly in prayer, 
contributing to the needs of the set-apart ones, and given to hospitality. 
Bless those who hunt you down; bless, and don't curse. 
Celebrate with those who celebrate. Weep with those who weep. 
Be of the same mind one toward another. Don't set your mind on high things, but associate with the humble. Don't be wise in your own conceits. 
Pay no one back with bad for bad. Respect what is honorable in the sight of everyone. 
If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, keep wholeness with everyone. 
Don't take payback into your own hands, truly loved, but leave room for God's heat. For it is written, "Payback belongs to me; I will repay, says the Lord." 
Therefore "If your enemy is hungry, feed him. If he is thirsty, give him a drink; for in doing so, you will heap coals of fire on his head." 
Don't be overcome by what is bad, but overcome what is bad with good.
