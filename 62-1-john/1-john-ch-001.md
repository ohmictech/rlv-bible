That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our own eyes, which we looked at and our hands touched, this is about the Word of life. 
(the life appeared, and we have seen it. We bear witness and tell you about the real life of the era that was with the Father and appeared to us); 
what we have seen and heard we tell you too, so that you also may share with us. And the sharing we have is with the Father and with his Son, Jesus Christ. 
We write these things to you so that our joy may be complete. 
This is the message we have heard from him and announce to you: God is light, and there is no darkness in him at all. 
If we say we share with him and still walk in the darkness, we are lying and not living the truth. 
But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we share with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son makes us clean from every wrongdoing. 
If we say we have no wrongdoing in us, we are fooling ourselves, and the truth is not in us. 
If we confess our mistakes, he is trustworthy and upright, and he will release us from our mistakes and make us clean from everything wrong. 
If we say we have never drifted, we make him out to be a liar, and his word is not in us.
