In that hour the students came to Jesus, saying, "Who then is greatest in the reign of the sky?" 
Jesus called a little child to himself, and set him in the middle of them 
and said, "Truly I tell you, unless you turn and become like little children, you will in no way enter into the reign of the sky. 
Whoever therefore humbles himself like this little child is the greatest in the reign of the sky. 
Whoever receives one such little child in my name receives me, 
but whoever traps one of these little ones who trust in me would be better off to have a heavy millstone hung around his neck and be drowned in the depths of the sea. 
"How terrible for the world because of traps! For it must be that the traps come, but how terrible for the person through whom the trap comes! 
If your hand or your foot traps you, cut it off and throw it away from you. It is better for you to enter into real life maimed or crippled, rather than having two hands or two feet and be thrown into the fire of the era. 
If your eye traps you, pluck it out and throw it away from you. It is better for you to enter into real life with one eye, rather than having two eyes and be thrown into the Gehenna of fire. 
See that you don't look down on one of these little ones, for I tell you that in the sky their messengers always see the face of my Father in the sky. 
For the Human One came to rescue what was lost. 
"What do you think? If a person has one hundred sheep, and one of them wanders off, doesn't he leave the ninety-nine on the mountains and go to look for the one that wandered off? 
If he finds it, truly I tell you, he celebrates over it more than over the ninety-nine that did not wander off. 
Even so it is not the will of your Father in the sky that one of these little ones should be lost. 
"If your brother misses the mark against you, go, show him his fault between you and him alone. If he listens to you, you have gained back your brother. 
But if he doesn't listen, take one or two more with you, so that at the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established. 
If he refuses to listen to them, tell it to the gathering. If he refuses to hear the gathering also, let him be to you as an outsider or a tax collector. 
Truly I tell you, whatever you forbid on earth will have been forbidden in the sky, and whatever you permit on earth will have been permitted in the sky. 
Again, truly I tell you, that if two of you agree on earth about anything that they ask, it will be done for them by my Father in the sky. 
For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there I am among them." 
Then Peter came and said to him, "Lord, how often will my brother miss the mark against me, and I release him? Up to seven times?" 
Jesus said to him, "I don't tell you up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven. 
Therefore the reign of the sky is like a certain king who wanted to settle accounts with his slaves. 
When he had begun to settle, one was brought to him who owed him ten thousand bags of silver. 
But because he couldn't pay, his lord commanded him to be sold, with his wife, his children, and all that he had, and payment to be made. 
The slave therefore fell down and knelt before him, saying, 'Lord, have patience with me, and I will pay you back all of it!' 
The lord of that slave, moved with deep compassion, released him and let the debt go. 
"But that slave went out and found one of his fellow slaves who owed him a hundred silver coins, and he grabbed him and took him by the throat, saying, 'Pay me what you owe!' 
"So his fellow slave fell down at his feet and begged him, saying, 'Have patience with me, and I will pay you back!' 
He would not, but went and threw him into prison until he should pay back what was due. 
So when his fellow slaves saw what was done, they were very sorry, and came and told their lord all that was done. 
Then his lord called him in and said to him, 'You malicious slave! I let go all that debt because you begged me. 
Shouldn't you also have had mercy on your fellow slave, even as I had mercy on you?' 
His lord was angry, and handed him over to the jailers until he should pay all that was due to him. 
So my Father in the sky will also do to you, if you don't each release your brother from your heart for his false steps."
