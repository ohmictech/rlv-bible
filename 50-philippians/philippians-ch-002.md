So if there is any encouragement in Christ, any comfort from love, any partnership of the Spirit, any deep compassion and mercy, 
then make my joy complete by thinking the same way, having the same love, being of one heart and one mind, 
doing nothing out of selfish ambition or vanity, but in humility counting others as more important than yourselves, 
and not each of you looking out for your own interests only, but also for the interests of others. 
Have this way of thinking among you, which was also in Christ Jesus: 
though he existed in the form of God, he did not think that being equal with God was something to grab hold of, 
but emptied himself, taking the form of a slave, coming to be like a human being. 
And being found in human form, he humbled himself and became obedient all the way to death, even death on a cross. 
That is why God raised him to the highest place and gave him the name that is above every name, 
so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in the sky, on the land, and under the land, 
and every tongue should agree that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the weight of God the Father. 
So then, my truly loved ones, just as you have always obeyed, not only when I was with you, but now much more while I am away, keep working out your own healing and wholeness with fear and trembling. 
because it is God at work in you, giving you both the wanting and the doing, for the sake of what delights him. 
Do everything without grumbling and arguing, 
so that you may be blameless and honest, children of God without defect in the middle of a twisted and warped generation, among whom you shine like lights in the world, 
as you hold firmly to the word of life. Then on the day of Christ I will have something to take pride in, because I did not run for nothing or work for nothing. 
But even if I am poured out on the sacrifice and service that your trust brings, I am glad and I celebrate with all of you. 
In the same way you too should be glad and celebrate with me. 
I expect in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I too may be cheered up when I learn how you are doing. 
For I have no one else of the same heart, who will genuinely care about you. 
For everyone else looks out for their own interests, not for the interests of Jesus Christ. 
But you know what he is worth: as a child serves a father, so he has served with me in the Good News. 
So I expect to send him as soon as I see how things turn out for me. 
And I am confident in the Lord that I myself will come soon too. 
But I thought it necessary to send back to you Epaphroditus, my brother, my fellow worker, my fellow soldier, and your sent one who served my need, 
because he has been longing for all of you and was distressed because you heard he was sick. 
He was indeed sick, nearly to death, but God had mercy on him, and not on him only, but on me too, so that I would not have sorrow piled on sorrow. 
So I am sending him all the more eagerly, so that when you see him again you may be glad, and I may have less sorrow. 
So welcome him in the Lord with all joy, and hold people like him in honor, 
because he came close to death for the work of Christ, risking his life to make up for what was lacking in your service to me.
