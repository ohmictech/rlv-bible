From the elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I truly love, and not only I, but also everyone who knows the truth, 
because of the truth that makes its home in us and will be with us for the eras. 
Favor, mercy, and wholeness will be with us, from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love. 
I celebrated greatly to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as the Father commanded us. 
And now I ask you, dear lady, not as though I were writing you a new command, but the one we have had from the beginning, that we love one another. 
This is love: that we walk according to his commands. And this is the command, just as you heard from the beginning, that you should walk in it. 
Many deceivers have gone out into the world, people who don't openly declare that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. That's the deceiver and the one against Christ. 
Watch yourselves, so that you don't lose what we have worked for, but receive a full reward. 
Anyone who goes too far and doesn't make their home in the teaching of Christ doesn't have God. Whoever makes their home in the teaching has both the Father and the Son. 
If anyone comes to you and doesn't bring this teaching, don't take them into your house and don't welcome them, 
because anyone who welcomes them shares in their evil deeds. 
I have many things to write to you, but I'd rather not do it with paper and ink. I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that our joy may be complete. 
The children of your chosen sister send you greetings. Amen.
