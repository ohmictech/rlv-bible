You foolish Galatians! Who has put a spell on you? Before your very eyes Jesus Christ was openly shown to you as crucified. 
This is all I want to learn from you: did you receive the Spirit by deeds of the law, or by hearing with trust? 
Are you so foolish? Having begun in the Spirit, are you now completed in the flesh? 
Did you suffer so many things for nothing, if it really was for nothing? 
So does the one who supplies the Spirit to you and does powerful works among you do it by deeds of the law, or by hearing with trust? 
Even so, Abraham "trusted God, and it was counted to him as rightness." 
Know therefore that those who are of trust are children of Abraham. 
The Scripture, foreseeing that God would make the nations right by trust, announced the Good News beforehand to Abraham, saying, "All the nations will be blessed in you." 
So those who are of trust are blessed along with Abraham, who trusted. 
For as many as are of the deeds of the law are under a curse. For it is written, "Everyone who doesn't keep to everything written in the book of the law, to do it, is cursed." 
Now it is clear that no one is made right by the law before God, for, "The one who is right will live by trust." 
The law is not of trust, but, "The one who does them will live by them." 
Christ bought us out from the curse of the law, having become a curse for us. For it is written, "Everyone who hangs on a tree is cursed," 
so that the blessing of Abraham might come on the nations in Christ Jesus, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through trust. 
Brothers, let me put it in human terms: even a person's binding arrangement, once it is confirmed, no one sets it aside or adds to it. 
Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his offspring. He doesn't say, "To descendants", as of many, but as of one, "To your offspring", which is Christ. 
What I mean is this: the law, which came four hundred thirty years later, doesn't cancel a binding arrangement confirmed beforehand by God, so as to make the promise useless. 
For if the charge handed down comes by the law, it is no longer by promise; but God granted it to Abraham by promise. 
Then why is there the law? It was added because of people stepping over the line, until the offspring should come to whom the promise was made. It was put in place through messengers by the hand of a mediator. 
Now a mediator isn't for one party only, but God is one. 
Is the law then against God's promises? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that could make alive, then rightness really would come by the law. 
But the Scripture locked everything up under the mistake, so that the promise, by trust in Jesus Christ, might be given to those who trust. 
But before trust came, we were kept in custody under the law, locked up until the trust that was to come should be unveiled. 
So the law became our guardian to bring us to Christ, so that we might be made right by trust. 
But now that trust has come, we are no longer under a guardian. 
For you are all children of God, through trust in Christ Jesus. 
For as many of you as were immersed into Christ have put on Christ. 
There is neither Jewish person nor Greek, neither slave nor free, neither male nor female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus. 
If you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's offspring, heirs by promise.
