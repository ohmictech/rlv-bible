Then he showed me the river of the water of real life, clear as crystal, flowing out from the throne of God and of the Lamb, 
down the middle of its street. On each side of the river stood the tree of real life, bearing twelve kinds of fruit, giving its fruit every month. The leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations. 
There will be no curse any more. The throne of God and of the Lamb will be in the city, and his slaves will serve him. 
They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads. 
There will be no night any more, and they will not need lamplight or sunlight, because the Lord God will shine on them, and they will reign through all the eras. 
He said to me, "These words are trustworthy and true. The Lord, the God of the spirits of the spokesmen, sent his messenger to show his slaves what must happen soon." 
"Look, I am coming soon! Flourishing is the one who keeps the words spoken for God in this scroll." 
I, John, am the one who heard and saw these things. When I heard and saw them, I fell down to bow at the feet of the messenger who had shown them to me. 
But he said to me, "Don't do that! I am a fellow slave with you and with your brothers and sisters the spokesmen, and with those who keep the words of this scroll. Bow down to God." 
He said to me, "Don't seal up the words spoken for God in this scroll, because the time is near. 
Let the one who does wrong keep on doing wrong, and the one who is filthy keep on being filthy. Let the one who is right keep on doing right, and the one who is set apart keep on being set apart." 
"Look, I am coming soon, and my reward is with me, to pay each person back for what they have done. 
I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End. 
Flourishing are those who do his commands, so that they may have the right to the tree of real life and may enter the city through its gates. 
Outside are the dogs, the drug-magicians, those who sleep around, the murderers, the idol worshipers, and everyone who loves and tells lies. 
I, Jesus, have sent my messenger to give you this witness for the gatherings. I am the root and the offspring of David, the bright morning star." 
The Spirit and the bride say, "Come!" Let everyone who hears say, "Come!" Let anyone who is thirsty come. Let anyone who wants take the water of real life freely. 
I bear witness to everyone who hears the words spoken for God in this scroll: if anyone adds anything to them, God will add to them the plagues that are written in this scroll. 
And if anyone takes away from the words of this scroll of prophecy, God will take away their share in the tree of real life and in the holy city, which are described in this scroll. 
The one who bears witness to these things says, "Yes, I am coming soon." Amen! Come, Lord Jesus! 
May the favor of the Lord Jesus Christ be with all the set-apart ones. Amen.
