"When your God brings you into the land you are going to take, and drives out many nations ahead of you, the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites, seven nations greater and stronger than you, 
and your God hands them over to you and you defeat them, then you are to destroy them completely. Make no treaty with them and show them no mercy. 
Don't marry into them. Don't give your daughter to their son or take their daughter for your son, 
because that would turn your sons away from following me to serve other gods, and God's anger would burn against you and he would destroy you quickly. 
Instead, this is what you are to do to them: tear down their altars, smash their sacred stones, cut down their Asherah poles and burn their carved images with fire. 
You are a people set apart to your God. Your God has chosen you to be his own people out of all the peoples on the face of the earth. 
God didn't set his love on you or choose you because you were more numerous than any other people. You were the fewest of all peoples. 
It was because God loves you and because he kept the oath he swore to your fathers that God brought you out with a strong hand and bought you back out of the house of slavery, out of the grip of Pharaoh king of Egypt. 
So know that your God is God, the faithful God who keeps his covenant and his loyal love to a thousand generations with those who love him and keep his commands, 
and who pays back those who hate him to their face, destroying them. He won't be slow with anyone who hates him. He pays him back to his face. 
So keep the commands, the rules and the decisions I am giving you today, and do them. 
"If you listen to these decisions and keep them and do them, then your God will keep his covenant and his loyal love with you, as he swore to your fathers. 
He will love you, bless you and multiply you. He will bless your children and your crops, your grain, your new wine and your oil, the young of your cattle and your flocks, in the land he swore to your fathers to give you. 
You will be blessed above every other people. No man or woman among you will be childless, and none of your livestock will fail to breed. 
God will take away all sickness from you. He won't put on you any of the terrible diseases of Egypt that you know, but will put them on everyone who hates you. 
You are to destroy every people your God hands over to you. Don't look on them with pity and don't serve their gods, because that would be a trap for you. 
If you say to yourself, 'These nations are more numerous than I am. How can I drive them out?' 
don't be afraid of them. Remember what your God did to Pharaoh and to all of Egypt, 
the great tests your eyes saw, the signs and wonders, the strong hand and the outstretched arm by which your God brought you out. Your God will do the same to all the peoples you are afraid of. 
Your God will even send hornets among them, until those who are left and are hiding are gone from in front of you. 
Don't be terrified of them, because your God is among you, a great and fearsome God. 
Your God will drive those nations out ahead of you little by little. You can't destroy them all at once, or the wild animals would multiply around you. 
But your God will hand them over to you and throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed. 
He will hand their kings over to you, and you will wipe out their names from under the sky. No one will be able to stand against you until you have destroyed them. 
Burn the carved images of their gods with fire. Don't covet the silver or gold on them or take it for yourself, or you will be trapped by it, because it is disgusting to your God. 
Don't bring anything disgusting into your house, or you will be given over to destruction like it. Treat it as utterly disgusting and hate it completely, because it has been given over to destruction.
