Moses called to all Israel, and said to them, "Hear, Israel, the statutes and the ordinances which I speak in your ears today, that you can learn them, and observe to do them." 
Our God made a covenant with us in Horeb. 
God didn't make this covenant with our fathers, but with us, even us, who are all of us here alive today. 
God spoke with you face to face on the mountain out of the middle of the fire, 
(I stood between God and you at that time, to show you God's word; for you were afraid because of the fire, and didn't go up onto the mountain) saying, 
"I'm your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. 
"Don't have any other gods before me. 
"Don't make a carved image for yourself, any likeness of what's in the sky above, or what's on the earth beneath, or that's in the water under the earth. 
Don't bow yourself down to them, nor serve them, for I, your God, am a jealous God, visiting the guilt of the fathers on the children and on the third and on the fourth generation of those who hate me 
and showing loving kindness to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments. 
"Don't misuse the name of your God; for God won't hold him guiltless who misuses his name. 
"Observe the Sabbath day, to keep it holy, as your God commanded you. 
You will labor six days, and do all your work; 
but the seventh day is a Sabbath to your God, in which you won't do any work, neither you, nor your son, nor your daughter, nor your male servant, nor your female servant, nor your ox, nor your donkey, nor any of your livestock, nor your stranger who's within your gates; that your male servant and your female servant may rest as well as you. 
You will remember that you were a servant in the land of Egypt, and your God brought you out of there by a mighty hand and by an outstretched arm. So your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day. 
"Honor your father and your mother, as your God commanded you, that your days may be long and that it may go well with you in the land which your God gives you. 
"Don't murder. 
"Don't commit adultery. 
"Don't steal. 
"Don't give false testimony against your neighbor. 
"Don't covet your neighbor's wife. Neither will you desire your neighbor's house, his field, or his male servant, or his female servant, his ox, or his donkey, or anything that's your neighbor's." 
God spoke these words to all your assembly on the mountain out of the middle of the fire, of the cloud, and of the thick darkness, with a great voice. He added no more. He wrote them on two stone tablets, and gave them to me. 
When you heard the voice out of the middle of the darkness, while the mountain was burning with fire, you came near to me, even all the heads of your tribes, and your elders; 
and you said, "Look, our God has shown us his glory and his greatness, and we have heard his voice out of the middle of the fire. We have seen today that God does speak with man, and he lives. 
Now, why should we die? This great fire will consume us. If we hear our God's voice any more, then we will die. 
Who's there of all flesh who has heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the middle of the fire, as we have, and lived? 
Go near, and hear all that our God will say, and tell us all that our God tells you; and we'll hear it, and do it." 
God heard the voice of your words when you spoke to me; and God said to me, "I have heard the voice of the words of this people which they have spoken to you. They have well said all that they have spoken. 
Oh that there were such a heart in them that they'd fear me and keep all my commandments always, that it might be well with them and with their children for the eras! 
"Go tell them, 'Return to your tents.' 
But as for you, stand here by me, and I'll tell you all the commandments, and the statutes, and the ordinances, which you will teach them, that they can do them in the land which I give them to possess." 
You will observe to do as your God has commanded you. Don't turn away to the right hand or to the left. 
You will walk in all the way which your God has commanded you, that you can live and that it might be well with you, and that you can prolong your days in the land which you will possess.
