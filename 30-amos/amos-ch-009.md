I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and he said, "Strike the tops of the pillars, that the thresholds may shake. Break them in pieces on the head of everyone. I'll kill the last of them with the sword. Not one of them will flee away. Not one of them will escape. 
Though they dig into Hades, there my hand will take them; and though they climb up to the sky, there I'll bring them down. 
Though they hide themselves in the top of Carmel, I'll search and take them out from there; and though they be hidden from my sight in the bottom of the sea, there I'll command the serpent, and it'll bite them. 
Though they go into captivity before their enemies, there I'll command the sword, and it'll kill them. I'll set my eyes on them for bad, and not for good. 
The Lord, the GOD of Armies, is he who touches the land and it melts, and everyone who dwells in it'll mourn; and it'll rise up wholly like the River, and will sink again, like the River of Egypt. 
It's he who builds his rooms in the sky, and has founded his vault on the earth; he who calls for the waters of the sea, and pours them out on the surface of the earth, God is his name. 
Are you not like the children of the Ethiopians to me, children of Israel?" says God. "Haven't I brought up Israel out of the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Syrians from Kir? 
Look, the eyes of God are on the sinful kingdom, and I'll destroy it from off the surface of the earth, except that I won't utterly destroy the house of Jacob," says God. 
"Look, I'll command, and I'll sift the house of Israel among all the nations as grain is sifted in a sieve, yet not the least kernel will fall on the earth. 
All the those who have drifted of my people will die by the sword, who say, 'Bad won't overtake nor meet us.' 
In that day I'll raise up the tent of David who's fallen and close up its breaches, and I'll raise up its ruins, and I'll build it as in the days of old, 
that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the nations who are called by my name," says God who does this. 
"Look, the days come," says God, "that the plowman will overtake the reaper, and the one treading grapes him who sows seed; and sweet wine will drip from the mountains, and flow from the hills. 
I'll bring my people Israel back from captivity, and they'll rebuild the ruined cities, and inhabit them; and they'll plant vineyards, and drink wine from them. They will also make gardens, and eat their fruit. 
I'll plant them on their land, and they'll no more be plucked up out of their land which I have given them," says your God.
