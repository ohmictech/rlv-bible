God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Speak to Aaron, and tell him, 'When you light the lamps, the seven lamps will give light in front of the lamp stand.'" 
Aaron did so. He lit its lamps to light the area in front of the lamp stand, as God commanded Moses. 
This was the workmanship of the lamp stand, beaten work of gold. From its base to its flowers, it was beaten work. He made the lamp stand according to the pattern which God had shown Moses. 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"Take the Levites from among the children of Israel, and cleanse them. 
You will do this to them to cleanse them: sprinkle the water of cleansing on them, let them shave their whole bodies with a razor, let them wash their clothes, and cleanse themselves. 
Then let them take a young bull and its meal offering, fine flour mixed with oil; and another young bull you will take for an offering for wrongdoing. 
You will present the Levites before the Tent of Meeting. You will assemble the whole congregation of the children of Israel. 
You will present the Levites before God. The children of Israel will lay their hands on the Levites, 
and Aaron will offer the Levites before God for a wave offering on the behalf of the children of Israel, that it might be theirs to do the service of God. 
"The Levites will lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you will offer the one for an offering for wrongdoing and the other for a burnt offering to God, to make things right for the Levites. 
You will set the Levites before Aaron and before his sons, and offer them as a wave offering to God. 
Thus you will separate the Levites from among the children of Israel, and the Levites will be mine. 
"After that, the Levites will go in to do the service of the Tent of Meeting. You will cleanse them, and offer them as a wave offering. 
They're wholly given to me from among the children of Israel; instead of everyone who opens the womb, even the firstborn of all the children of Israel, I have taken them to me. 
All the firstborn among the children of Israel are mine, both man and animal. On the day that I struck all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified them for myself. 
I have taken the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel. 
I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and to his sons from among the children of Israel, to do the service of the children of Israel in the Tent of Meeting, and to make things right for the children of Israel, so that there will be no plague among the children of Israel when the children of Israel come near to the sanctuary." 
Moses, and Aaron, and all the congregation of the children of Israel did so to the Levites. According to all that God commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so the children of Israel did to them. 
The Levites purified themselves from wrongdoing, and they washed their clothes; and Aaron offered them for a wave offering before God and Aaron made things right for them to cleanse them. 
After that, the Levites went in to do their service in the Tent of Meeting before Aaron and before his sons: as God had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so they did to them. 
God spoke to Moses, saying, 
"This is what's assigned to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward they will go in to wait on the service in the work of the Tent of Meeting; 
and from the age of fifty years they will retire from doing the work, and will serve no more, 
but will assist their brothers in the Tent of Meeting, to perform the duty, and will perform no service. This is how you will have the Levites do their duties."
