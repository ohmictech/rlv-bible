"When you come into the land your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you take it and live in it, 
take some of the firstfruits of everything your ground produces, from the land your God is giving you, put them in a basket and go to the place your God will choose for his name to be. 
Come to the priest in office at that time and say to him, 'I declare today before your God that I have come into the land God swore to our fathers to give us.' 
The priest will take the basket from your hand and set it down in front of the altar of your God. 
Then say before your God, 'My father was a wandering Aramean, and he went down to Egypt and lived there with only a few people, and there he became a great, strong and numerous nation. 
The Egyptians treated us badly, made us suffer and forced hard labor on us. 
Then we cried out to the God of our fathers, and God heard our voice and saw our suffering, our hard work and our oppression. 
God brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror, with signs and with wonders, 
and he brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey. 
Now, look, I have brought the firstfruits of the ground you have given me, God.' Then set it down before your God and bow down before your God. 
Then celebrate all the good things your God has given you and your household, you, the Levite and the foreigner living among you. 
When you have finished setting aside the whole tenth of your crops in the third year, the year of the tenth, give it to the Levite, the foreigner, the orphan and the widow, so they can eat inside your towns and be satisfied. 
Then say before your God, 'I have taken the holy part out of my house and given it to the Levite, the foreigner, the orphan and the widow, just as you commanded me. I haven't broken any of your commands or forgotten them. 
I haven't eaten any of it while mourning, I haven't taken any of it while unclean, and I haven't given any of it to the dead. I have listened to the voice of my God and done everything you commanded me. 
Look down from your holy dwelling place in the sky and bless your people Israel and the ground you have given us, as you swore to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.' 
Today your God commands you to keep these rules and decisions. Be careful to do them with all your heart and all your life. 
Today you have declared that God is your God and that you will walk in his ways, keep his rules, his commands and his decisions, and listen to his voice. 
And today God has declared that you are his own treasured people, as he promised you, and that you must keep all his commands. 
He will set you high above all the nations he has made, in praise, in name and in honor, and you will be a people set apart to your God, as he has spoken.
