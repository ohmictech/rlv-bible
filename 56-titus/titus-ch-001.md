Paul, a slave of God and sent of Christ Jesus, for the trust of God's chosen ones and so that they will know the truth that leads to devotion to God, 
in the expectation of the real life of the era, which God, who can't lie, promised before the eras began. 
At the right time he made his word visible in the announcement that was put in my care, by the command of God our Restorer. 
to Titus, my true child in the trust we share: Favor, mercy, and wholeness to you from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Restorer. 
The reason I left you in Crete was so that you could put right what was still lacking and appoint elders in every city, just as I told you. 
An elder must be above reproach, faithful to one wife, with children who trust and aren't accused of wild living or of refusing to be told what to do. 
The overseer, since he manages God's household, must be above reproach: not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not a heavy drinker, not a fighter, not out for shameful gain, 
but welcoming to strangers, a lover of what is good, sensible, right, set apart, and self-controlled, 
He must hold firmly to the trustworthy word just as he was taught, so that he can encourage others with healthy teaching and show those who argue against it where they are wrong. 
There are many who refuse to be told what to do, who talk empty nonsense and lead people astray, especially those from the circumcision group. 
They must be silenced, because they are wrecking whole households by teaching what they shouldn't, for the sake of shameful gain. 
One of their own spokesmen said, "Cretans are always liars, vicious animals, and lazy bellies." 
That witness is true. So correct them sharply, so that they may be healthy in the trust, 
and stop paying attention to Jewish old stories and to the commands of people who turn their backs on the truth. 
To those who are clean, everything is clean; but to those who are stained and have no trust, nothing is clean. Their minds and their consciences are stained too. 
They claim to know God, but they disown him by what they do. They are hateful, they refuse to be told what to do, and they are useless for any good deed.
