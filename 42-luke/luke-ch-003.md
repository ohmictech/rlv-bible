Now in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, and Herod was district ruler of Galilee, and his brother Philip district ruler of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias district ruler of Abilene, 
during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John, the son of Zacharias, in the wilderness. 
He came into all the region around the Jordan, preaching an immersion that marked a change of heart for the release of mistakes. 
As it's written in the book of the words of Isaiah the spokesman, "The voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Get the way of the Lord ready. Make his paths straight. 
Every valley will be filled. Every mountain and hill will be brought low. The crooked will become straight, and the rough ways smooth. 
All flesh will know the healing and wholeness of God.'" 
So he said to the crowds who went out to be immersed by him, "You offspring of vipers, who warned you to run away from the heat to come? 
So produce fruit that shows your change of heart is real, and don't begin to say among yourselves, 'We have Abraham for our father;' for I tell you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham from these stones! 
The ax is already lying at the root of the trees. Every tree that doesn't produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire." 
The crowds asked him, "What then must we do?" 
He answered them, "Whoever has two coats should share with the one who has none, and whoever has food should do the same." 
Tax collectors also came to be immersed, and they said to him, "Teacher, what must we do?" 
He said to them, "Collect no more than what you have been told to collect." 
Soldiers also asked him, saying, "What about us? What must we do?" He said to them, "Don't take money from anyone by force or by false accusation. Be content with your pay." 
As the people were in expectation, and everyone was wondering in their hearts concerning John, whether perhaps he was the Christ, 
John answered them all, "I indeed immerse you with water, but one mightier than I is coming, the strap of whose sandals I'm not worthy to loosen. He'll immerse you in the Holy Spirit and fire. 
His winnowing fork is in his hand, and he'll clean out his threshing floor completely, and will gather the wheat into his barn; but he'll burn the chaff with fire that never goes out." 
Then with many other appeals he preached good news to the people, 
but Herod the district ruler, being rebuked by him for Herodias, his brother's wife, and for all the bad things which Herod had done, 
added this also to them all, that he locked John up in prison. 
Now when everyone was immersed, Jesus also had been immersed and was praying. The sky was opened, 
and the Holy Spirit came down on him in bodily form like a dove; and a voice came out of the sky, saying "You're my Son, whom I truly love. I'm delighted with you."
