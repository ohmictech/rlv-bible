But there were also false spokesmen among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you. They'll quietly bring in destructive factions, and they'll even deny the Master who bought them, bringing quick ruin on themselves. 
Many will follow their shameless ways, and because of them the way of truth will be slandered. 
In their greed they'll make money off you with made-up stories. Their verdict was decided long ago and isn't idle, and their ruin never sleeps. 
God didn't spare the messengers when they made their wrongdoing, but threw them into the deepest darkness and kept them there for the verdict; 
and he didn't spare the ancient world either, but he kept Noah, who announced what was right, along with seven others, when he brought the flood on a world of people who disregarded God, 
and turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes, he passed sentence on them, making them an example to those who would later disregard God, 
and rescued upright Lot, who was worn down by the shameless way those lawbreakers lived 
(for that upright man, living among them, was in anguish day after day in his upright life, seeing and hearing what they did as if there were no law), 
then the Lord knows how to rescue people who are devoted to God out of testing, and how to hold the wrongdoers for punishment until the day of the verdict. 
This is especially true of those who follow the flesh in its craving for what's unclean, and who despise authority. Bold and self-willed, they aren't afraid to slander glorious beings, 
whereas even messengers, who are greater in strength and power, don't bring a slandering verdict against them before the Lord. 
But these people are like animals without reason, born to be caught and killed. They slander what they don't understand, and like those animals they'll be destroyed, 
and they'll be paid back for the wrong they have done. They think it a pleasure to live it up in broad daylight. They're stains and blemishes, enjoying their own lies while they feast with you. 
Their eyes are full of adultery, and they never stop making mistakes. They lure people who aren't steady. Their hearts are trained in greed. They're children under a curse! 
They have left the straight road and gone astray. They have followed the way of Balaam the son of Beor, who loved the pay of doing wrong, 
but he was corrected for his own lawbreaking. A donkey that couldn't speak talked with a human voice and stopped the spokesman's madness. 
These people are springs without water, mists driven by a storm. The deepest darkness has been kept for them for the eras. 
They talk big and say empty things, and by the cravings of the flesh and by shameless living they lure back those who are just now escaping from people who live in error. 
They promise freedom while they themselves are slaves of decay. You're a slave to whatever has defeated you. 
If people have escaped the filth of the world by knowing our Lord and Restorer Jesus Christ, and then get tangled up in it again and are defeated, they end up worse off than they were at first. 
It would have been better for them never to have known the way of rightness than to know it and then turn their backs on the set-apart command they were given. 
What the true proverb says has happened to them: "A dog goes back to its own vomit," and, "A pig that has been washed goes back to roll in the mud."
