Abraham traveled from there toward the southern country and settled between Kadesh and Shur. He lived as a foreigner in Gerar. 
Abraham said of his wife Sarah, "She's my sister." So Abimelech, king of Gerar, sent for Sarah and took her. 
But God came to Abimelech in a dream one night and said, "You're a dead man, because of the woman you've taken. She's someone's wife." 
Abimelech hadn't gone near her. He said, "Lord, would you kill an innocent nation? 
Didn't he tell me, 'She's my sister'? And she herself said, 'He's my brother.' I've done this with a clear conscience and clean hands." 
God said to him in the dream, "Yes, I know you did this with a clear conscience, and I'm the one who kept you from wronging me. That's why I didn't let you touch her. 
Now give the man back his wife. He's a spokesman, and he'll pray for you, and you'll live. But if you don't give her back, you can be sure you'll die, you and everyone with you." 
Abimelech got up early in the morning, called all his servants, and told them everything. The men were terrified. 
Then Abimelech called Abraham and said to him, "What have you done to us? How did I wrong you, that you've brought this great wrongdoing on me and on my kingdom? You've done things to me that should never be done!" 
Abimelech said to Abraham, "What did you see, that made you do this?" 
Abraham said, "I thought, 'There's no respect for God in this place, and they'll kill me because of my wife.' 
Besides, she really is my sister, my father's daughter though not my mother's, and she became my wife. 
When God sent me wandering from my father's house, I said to her, 'This is how you can show kindness to me: everywhere we go, say of me, "He's my brother."'" 
So Abimelech took sheep and cattle, male and female servants, and gave them to Abraham, and gave Sarah his wife back to him. 
Abimelech said, "Look, my land is open to you. Make yourself at home wherever you like." 
To Sarah he said, "Look, I've given your brother a thousand pieces of silver. That settles it in the eyes of everyone with you. You're cleared in front of them all." 
Then Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife, and his female servants, so that they could have children again. 
God had made every woman in Abimelech's household unable to have children, because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
